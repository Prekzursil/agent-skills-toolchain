# Phenibut "weak GABA-A activity (locate the site)" — Adversarial Adjudication

**Date:** 2026-06-25
**Verdict:** Site-locatability REFUTED; high-concentration functional GABA-A activity INSUFFICIENT. OHAT very-low certainty.

## Decisive primary evidence
- ChEMBL CHEMBL315818 (phenibut) has exactly ONE primary GABA-A binding record: target CHEMBL343 (GABRA1, rat α1 subunit), assay CHEMBL5291800, **AC50 ">30000 nM" (>30 µM) — NEGATIVE at tested ceiling**. Source: Sutherland/Yonchev/Fekete/Urban 2023, Nat Commun 14:3989, PMID 37468498 (Secondary Pharmacology Database, 1958 drugs × 200 safety-panel assays). This is an inactive screen result, not a demonstration of weak binding, and localizes no site (broad α1-subunit binding panel, radioligand not specified in record).
- Phenibut ABSENT from any orthosteric/BZ-site GABA-A functional or electrophysiology study in corpus. No site ever localized in any source.

## Origin of the claim = review propagation, NOT data
- Lapin 2001 (CNS Drug Rev, PMC6494145) abstract: "primarily GABA-B and, to some extent, GABA-A" — uncited assertion. **Internal contradiction:** Lapin's own striatal-DA experiment is bicuculline-INSENSITIVE = explicitly NOT GABA-A. The origin source's own data point the other way.
- Downstream reviews/case reports (Gurley 2024 bcpt.14075; Martin 2023 JCP; Esin 2025) repeat "to a lesser extent GABA-A" — all trace to Lapin, none add primary GABA-A binding/EP data. Martin 2023 "targeting GABA-A helped clinically" is therapeutic inference, not target activity.
- Shulgina 2013 (DOI 10.33225/ppc/13.06.71, Problems of Psychology in 21st Century, Scientia Socialis) labels phenibut "non-selective GABA-A and GABA-B agonist" — DOI is REAL (verified CrossRef), but it is a behavioral fear-conditioning study in a low-impact psychology journal, asserted label not a measurement. Citation valid, evidentiary weight low.

## Conflation traps avoided
- Lapin's report that phenibut WITHDRAWAL increases GABA-A/BZ receptor density = downstream adaptation, NOT a direct phenibut GABA-A site.

## Bias note (Volgograd/Tyurenkov)
For THIS claim the GABA-A assertion does NOT originate from the Tyurenkov/Volgograd cluster — it is Lapin→Western-review propagation. Cluster concentration is a corpus-wide risk but not the driver here.

## No fabricated citations found
All cited DOIs/PMIDs resolve. Concern is evidentiary weight (assertions in reviews) not fabrication.
