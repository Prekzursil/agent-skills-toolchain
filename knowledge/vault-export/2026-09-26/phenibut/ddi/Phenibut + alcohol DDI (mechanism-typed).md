# Phenibut + Alcohol DDI (mechanism-typed, ToolUniverse-sourced 2026-06)

Mechanism-typed DDI only; no regimen. Phenibut ABSENT from ChEMBL/BindingDB (re-confirmed) -> use baclofen as GABA-B proxy.

## Dominant axis: PD additivity (CNS depression)
- R-phenibut = GABA-B agonist; ethanol = GABA-A potentiation + NMDA antagonism -> ADDITIVE CNS/respiratory depression, sedation, ataxia, LOC.
- Baclofen openFDA label (set_id 00c6c924...): explicit "CNS effects of baclofen may be additive to those of alcohol and other CNS depressants"; pharm_class GABA A + GABA B Agonists [MoA]; overdose = "coma, respiratory depression, seizures".
- FAERS phenibut top reactions (count): DRUG INTERACTION 14, TOXICITY TO VARIOUS AGENTS 9, UNRESPONSIVE TO STIMULI 9, LOSS OF CONSCIOUSNESS 7, SOMNOLENCE 5, WITHDRAWAL SYNDROME 4 -> CNS-depressant signature.
- FAERS phenibut+ALCOHOL combo: 2 serious fatal reports, both polydrug (+clonazepam / +salicylates), "Intentional overdose / Completed suicide / Toxicity to various agents". Confounded by other CNS depressants -> co-ingestion danger real but not isolatable.
- FAERS baclofen+ALCOHOL combo: many serious reports (FR) -> Somnolence, Coma, Falls/Fractures. Validates GABA-B+ethanol additive-depression class effect.

## PK axis: NULL with reason (not a gap)
- Phenibut: renally excreted largely unchanged (literature; EPMC direct PK pull returned empty - record once, no retry). NOT a meaningful CYP substrate -> NO CYP-mediated DDI. CYP2E1 hypothesis REFUTED for phenibut.
- Mechanistic homolog: baclofen label states "excreted primarily by the kidney in unchanged form" - same renal route -> supports PK-interaction-unlikely conclusion.

## NEW angle: bidirectional / triad PD (not one-directional)
1. ADDITIVE acute CNS/respiratory depression (danger).
2. GABA-B-mediated SUPPRESSION of alcohol reinforcement/craving - baclofen is an active AUD pharmacotherapy candidate (Burnette 2022 Drugs PMID 35133639; lists baclofen among AUD agents). So GABA-B agonism opposes alcohol reward.
3. CROSS-TOLERANCE / shared withdrawal - phenibut withdrawal phenotypically = baclofen/benzo/alcohol withdrawal (GABA-B + glutamate); seizures/delirium (Bonnet 2024 PMID 38377332; Gurley 2024 PMID 39197876).

## Key refs
- Gurley & Koturbash 2024 Basic Clin Pharmacol Toxicol, PMID 39197876 - phenibut review, GABA-B/A/PEA activity, polydrug co-ingestion common.
- Bonnet 2024 Dtsch Arztebl Int, PMID 38377332 - phenibut = gabapentinoid/benzo-like; combo with opiates raises coma/resp-depression risk.
- Peterkin 2022 J Addict Med, PMID 35120058 - phenibut self-detox toxicity case.
