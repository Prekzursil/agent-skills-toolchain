## Key mechanistic findings
- **Question B (dopamine puzzle) — STRONGEST hit:** "Phenomenon of Aminophenylbutyric Acid" (Latipova/Usmanova 2022) explicitly describes GABA-B tonic disinhibition: GABA-B on GABAergic interneurons = HIGH sensitivity; GABA-B on dopaminergic neurons = LOW sensitivity. Therapeutic dose → hits sensitive GABA-B on interneurons that tonically inhibit DA neurons → disinhibition → ↑DA (activating component). High dose → also hits GABA-B on DA neurons → inhibits DA release. This IS the tonic VTA disinhibition model. Review-grade (weak/moderate).
- **Question A (prosocial):** Poshivalov 1981 (Pharmacol Biochem Behav 14 Suppl): phenibut 50-100 mg/kg ↑intraspecies sociability + ↓aggression in isolated mice (GABAergic resocialization); amphetamine/L-DOPA POTENTIATE desocialization (catecholamine ≠ prosocial). Strong primary-data animal.
- GABA-B = metabotropic Gi, activates K+, inhibits Ca2+ (Esin 2020 sanogenesis; Esin 2025 GABAergic review).
- GABA-B downregulation/internalization under chronic GABA (epilepsy/tolerance context) — Esin 2025.

## SAR / dosing (Volgograd Tyurenkov series) — measured-corpus
- phenibut = 4-amino-3-phenylbutanoic acid (β-phenyl-GABA); R-isomer ~2× racemate, S largely inactive (Brodzka 2013 citing Dambrova 2008).
- Reference doses (1/10 MW, ~equimolar): phenibut 18-25 mg/kg; RGPU-197 (glutamic acid deriv) 22 mg/kg; RSPU-149 (phenibut succinate) 48 mg/kg.
- salifen = 4-amino-3-phenylbutanoic acid + 2-hydroxybenzoic(salicylic) acid 2:1, 7.5 mg/kg.
- succicard/citrocard = 4-phenylpiracetam + ethane-1,2-dicarboxylic(succinic) acid 2:1, 22 mg/kg.
- RGPU-197 surpasses phenibut in anxiolytic+activating; antidepressant ~ melipramine (Bagmetova 2012).
- RGPU-147, RGPU-189 = phenibut derivatives, > phenibut anti-amnestic (Tyurenkov 2008).
