# Phenibut + amphetamine/lisdexamfetamine DDI (mechanism-typed)

Source: ToolUniverse run 2026-06-22. Mechanism/research framing only — NO regimen.

## CYP / metabolic axis = LOW direct DDI potential
- Lisdexamfetamine (Vyvanse): prodrug, NOT metabolized by CYP450. Hydrolyzed by RBC esterases -> dextroamphetamine + L-lysine. Rate-limiting step is enzymatic hydrolysis, peripheral. [FDA label 12.3]
- Dextroamphetamine clearance: ~half urinary as alpha-hydroxy-amphetamine derivs, ~30-40% as parent amphetamine; CYP2D6 forms 4-hydroxyamphetamine (minor pathway). Highly urine-pH dependent (pKa 9.9). [FDA label DEXTROAMPHETAMINE]
- Phenibut: GABA analog, predominantly RENAL clearance, no published CYP metabolism (Europe PMC: no PK/CYP paper found; consistent with corpus). Not a CYP substrate/inhibitor of note.
- => No shared CYP route. Phenibut does NOT inhibit CYP2D6, so it will not raise dextroamphetamine exposure via the labeled CYP2D6-inhibitor mechanism. Metabolic DDI = not expected.

## Transporter / renal axis = the plausible PK lever
- Both undergo significant renal elimination; amphetamine renal recovery is urinary-pH dependent (active tubular secretion implicated). Phenibut is renally cleared. Theoretical competition at renal organic-cation transport / pH effects is the only PK-typed lever, and it is weak/unquantified. No DAT-level PK interaction (DAT is a CNS target, not a clearance route).

## PD-additivity axis = the real interest (opposing + overlapping)
- Functional OPPOSITION on arousal: amphetamine = CNS stimulant (NE/DA release, monoamine); phenibut = GABA-B agonist CNS depressant/sedative. Net subjective state is a tug-of-war, not simple additivity.
- Overlapping cardiovascular/sympathetic load: amphetamine raises HR/BP; phenibut withdrawal/high-dose states show autonomic instability (tachycardia, agitation per case reports). Co-exposure -> additive sympathetic/cardiac strain is the mechanistically coherent hazard class.
- Phenibut's GABA-B-mediated VTA disinhibition (program hypothesis B) is a DA-tone mechanism distinct from amphetamine's DAT-reversal release — they reach DA by different routes, so a DA-overlap (not additive at the transporter) is conceivable but unproven.

## FAERS empirical (openFDA, real)
- PHENIBUT total reports present: drugcharacterization Suspect 49 / Concomitant 18 / Interacting 12.
- Top phenibut reactions: DRUG INTERACTION 14, DRUG ABUSE 14, MULTIPLE ORGAN DYSFUNCTION 9, PURPURA FULMINANS 9, UNRESPONSIVE TO STIMULI 9, WITHDRAWAL SYNDROME 4, SOMNOLENCE/SEDATION, ANXIETY 5.
- PHENIBUT+AMPHETAMINE co-reports: cluster of serious/fatal cases BUT confounded — same IV polydrug-abuse case series (cocaine + levamisole + buprenorphine/naloxone + clonazepam), endocarditis/purpura fulminans driven by IV use + levamisole, NOT a clean phenibut-amphetamine PD signal. Treat as non-causal for the dyad.

## Bottom line
- DDI type = primarily PHARMACODYNAMIC (opposing arousal + additive sympathetic/cardiac), NOT pharmacokinetic. No CYP2D6 or strong renal-transporter mechanism that would raise amphetamine levels. FAERS dyad signal is confounded by polydrug IV abuse.
