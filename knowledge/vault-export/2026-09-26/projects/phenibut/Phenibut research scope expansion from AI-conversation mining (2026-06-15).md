- [decision] NEW core question **G — molecular mechanism & reversibility of GABA-B tolerance** (most compute-tractable): GRK4/5 scaffolding (phosphorylation-independent), KCTD12/16 Gβγ scavenging, NMDA→CaMKIIβ→Ser867→MIB2/MARCH1 K63-ubiquitination, GSK3β/DARPP-32 phenotype; structure-tractable via GABA-B1/B2 + GRK/KCTD interfaces #scope
- [finding] **B (dopamine puzzle) reframed**: GABA-B-mediated VTA *disinhibition* (tonic), NOT direct DA release. Testable wedge from user's lived data: MPH (reuptake-blocker) clears post-phenibut fog but 2-FMA/LDX (releasers) don't (need intact VMAT2 stores) #mechanism
- [data] Affinity anchors to verify: R-phenibut α2δ Ki ~23µM / GABA-B ~92µM vs (−)-baclofen ~40nM, pregabalin ~6nM (phenibut's gabapentinoid arm is genuinely weak) #data
- [correction] Documented human withdrawal treatment = **baclofen taper** (GABA-B substitution) + benzo/phenobarbital/gabapentin for severe — NOT fasoracetam (racetam folklore). 2024 systematic review ~15 cases, mean ~13.6 g/day #correction
- [scope] NEW sub-study 7 — polypharmacy interaction-safety map of the user's real stack (phenibut/LDX/2-FMA/pregabalin/zopiclone/ketamine/agmatine/alcohol/opioids); sub-studies 1+3 gain enantiomer split + GABA-B molecular-tolerance arm; sub-study 2 adds GABA-B PAMs (ASP8062) + bromantane #scope
