# grant-proposal-architecture

This skill designs the proposal architecture before rhetoric takes over. It focuses on review criteria, feasibility, and novelty that can survive scrutiny.

## When to use

- The user is preparing a grant or fellowship proposal.
- The proposal needs stronger logic, reviewer positioning, or work-package structure.

## Workflow

1. Identify the funder lens: novelty, impact, feasibility, capability, or translational value.
2. Map the proposal around objectives, work packages, milestones, and risks.
3. Stress-test the logic for overclaiming, hidden dependencies, or weak feasibility.
4. Rewrite the proposal spine around reviewer decision points.

## Configuration and access

- Save templates, funder notes, and deadline trackers only.
- Keep submission credentials and portal sessions user-controlled.

## Output standard

- Deliver a proposal spine, not just polished prose.
- Surface the three biggest reviewer objections and how to answer them.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise outcomes the design cannot support.
- Do not bury risk under marketing language.
