# J04 — “While you were away” without becoming noise

## Executive decision

Do **not** build another dreaming daemon, memory store, notification service, or hook. Upgrade the already-built `background-digest/reader.py` **in place** into a typed, evidence-bearing, thread-safe return brief. The default surface should be a maximum of three ranked cards at the first main-session return boundary, with routine cards batched and only genuinely urgent state changes promoted. “Surfaced” must mean **seen**, never **resolved**.

This is an evolution, not a greenfield design. The unpacked subject contains a live `background-digest/reader` entry in the local SessionStart dispatcher (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/_dispatch/manifest.json:45-69`). The reader already says it is folded into the dispatcher rather than adding a hook, is fail-open, guards against subagents stealing the digest, archives before truncation, and surfaces entries once (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/background-digest/reader.py:2-43,205-224`). It also already has fixture coverage for silent empty state, surface-once, FIFO overflow, concurrent append preservation, fail-open garbage, opt-out, and archive rotation (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/background-digest/test_background_digest.py:88-180`). Reimplementation would violate the brief’s dedup and quality bars.

The present surface nevertheless has a presentation/semantics gap that this unit is responsible for: entries are untyped lines, the oldest 12 are shown FIFO, every surfaced line is immediately archived and removed, and the footer offers an unverified proposed command (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/background-digest/reader.py:12-22,67-72,138-158,161-202`). That is a transport, not yet a trustworthy partner digest.

## Grounding and detector discipline

The corpus is suitable for positive claims about observed counts: it distilled 10.62 GB from 11,633 files with zero file failures, one bad line, and capped samples (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/corpus.json:2-11`). It is **not** suitable for hook-frequency claims: its hook detector explicitly failed a known-positive control and marks the entire hooks section as no data (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/corpus.json:13260-13264`). I therefore make no claim that the digest did or did not fire historically.

The owner-correction detector is also recall-biased: 2,268 of 8,441 messages are candidates, but its own smoke sample found 41/41 false positives (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/corpus.json:10371-10375`). I use only individually readable examples and structural counts, not the candidate total as a correction rate. The readable evidence is still highly relevant:

* Background/teammate chatter is being mixed into the stream: one idle-notification pattern occurs 18 times in the distilled top patterns (`corpus.json:10404-10410`), and samples contain successive `golden-journey`, `hook-gems`, `skills-verify-seed`, and `gem-mutation-check` idle notices (`corpus.json:11308-11336`).
* A background agent lost in-process state when the prior process exited, and a later task notification explicitly said no completion record existed and told the operator to inspect partial output (`corpus.json:11224-11234`). A stopped workflow sample later supplied a resumable run ID rather than a terminal result (`corpus.json:11350-11354`).
* The owner said “yeah ive already told you to do that” after several cross-session notices (`corpus.json:11338-11342`), while the top patterns also include 12 “why did you stop proceed until you finish everything” corrections (`corpus.json:10424-10426`). These are direct, individually readable signals for continuity and low repetition; they do not prove a universal preference.
* The harness itself already defines the event ledger as objective history, `note.py` as the durable “why,” SessionStart replay as recovery ground truth, and basic-memory as a separate semantic store (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/AGENTS.md:28-34,85-91`). The open-loop resurface module already selects 1–3 relevant loops, deliberately avoids duplicating recovery replay, and is passive until a loop is explicitly closed (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/open-loops/resurface.py:2-25,38-40,51-95`).

The known-defects file supplies the controlling epistemic requirement: six confident wrong conclusions came from single unvalidated probes, followed by a seventh detector error in which a known-positive control separated a broken hooks detector from working skill and agent detectors (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/INPUT-known-harness-defects.md:111-129`). Therefore every “noticed,” “prepared,” “broken,” and “done” card below closes on a signal outside the generating context.

## Proposed return brief

Compact default rendering (three cards maximum, approximately 12 lines):

```text
While you were away — 2 changes, 1 thread needs you

[NEEDS-YOU · high · thread T-184] Export workflow stopped, not completed.
Evidence: workflow terminal state=stopped + output artifact lacks completion receipt.
Next: Resume cached run wf_507…  [snooze] [close only after receipt]

[PREPARED · medium] Router regression reproduced and a patch draft is ready.
Evidence: eval receipt 0.71<0.78 + patch hash 8b2…; no config changed.
Next: review draft path …

[NOTICED · low] HexStrike upstream changed; no local compatibility failure observed.
Evidence: pinned revision A != remote revision B. Recheck queued for weekly review.
```

The heading reports counts, not personality theatre. Each card answers: **what changed; what did the harness actually do; what external evidence supports it; what happens next; which open thread owns it**. Detail and full provenance remain behind a path/ID, not in the prompt.

## Finding 1 — Replace FIFO prose with a typed “delta contract” (inferred surprise)

**Improvement.** Migrate the existing queue in place from `[date] [producer] prose | PROPOSE: command` to a versioned JSONL envelope, while retaining a compatibility parser for old lines until the queue drains.

**What it is.** Each record has: `schema_version`, stable `notice_id`, `created_at`, `producer`, `project_key`, `thread_id?`, `kind` (`NOTICED|PREPARED|NEEDS_YOU|BLOCKED|RESOLVED`), `subject`, `delta`, `previous_state?`, `current_state`, `priority`, `confidence_band`, `evidence_refs[]`, `verification_state`, `artifact_refs[]`, `autonomy`, `expires_at?`, `dedup_key`, `seen_at?`, `closed_at?`, and `supersedes[]`. Human text is rendered from these fields; it is never the source of truth.

**Why inferred.** The current queue cannot distinguish a fact from a proposal and embeds a proposed command directly in prose (`reader.py:12-18`). The corpus contains stopped and resumable background work whose terminal distinctions matter (`corpus.json:11225-11234,11350-11354`). The owner did not ask for a schema, but reliable “noticed/prepared” language is impossible without one.

**How to build.** Refactor `reader.py`’s `_is_entry`, `build_message`, archive, and truncate functions around a small `DigestRecord` validator. Preserve the existing filenames and manifest registration; do not create `background-digest-v2`. On read, parse JSON first and legacy lines second. On write, producers use one shared `append_digest(record)` helper with an exclusive lock and atomic replace. Reject malformed records to a quarantine archive without blocking SessionStart. Keep the top-level `try/except` and exit 0. Estimated change is 150–190 lines plus fixtures.

**External signal that verifies it.** A producer test writes one legacy line and one JSON record; an independent reader process must render both, archive exact IDs, and leave a third concurrently appended record untouched. A schema-negative fixture must exit 0 and preserve the bad payload in quarantine. Reopen the archive with a separate process and compare record IDs and SHA-256s; the generator’s own in-memory assertion does not count.

**SOTA.** Microsoft’s validated Human-AI guidelines call for making capability/error boundaries clear, explaining why behavior occurred, remembering recent interaction, adapting cautiously, and notifying users of changes ([Amershi et al., CHI 2019](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf), Table 1). NIST likewise calls for documented human oversight and objective, repeatable TEVV ([AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/)).

**Value × effort.** transformative × medium. It is **very likely (80-90%)** to eliminate ambiguity between “observed,” “prepared,” and “completed,” because those states become schema-valid and independently receipted; this does not assert that owner satisfaction will improve.

**Novelty.** inferred-surprise.

**Autonomy.** just-do-it (internal, reversible queue migration with legacy compatibility).

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD.** BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).

**Anti-overfit gate.** Passes: it replaces an untyped file format on the existing bus; it adds no runtime, model, vector store, KG, SaaS, or framework.

## Finding 2 — Make “seen” different from “done”: thread escrow, not notification consumption (inferred surprise)

**Improvement.** Stop deleting actionable records merely because they were surfaced. Bind every actionable card to the existing open-loop/`OPEN-THREADS.md` identity and retain it until an external closure receipt exists.

**What it is.** Presentation becomes a state transition from `pending` to `seen`; only `closed`, `superseded`, or explicit owner dismissal removes a thread from the active set. Informational notices can still expire after being seen. Actionable cards retain stable IDs across sessions and become one-line “still open” reminders only when their state has not changed.

**Why inferred.** The present reader archives and truncates immediately after emission (`reader.py:161-220`). That provides surface-once behavior but equates attention delivery with obligation completion. The owner’s “already told you” and “why did you stop” examples (`corpus.json:11338-11342,10424-10426`) point to the opposite requirement. The already-built open-loop module is explicitly passive and requires explicit close (`open-loops/resurface.py:14-25,90-95`), so the digest should reference it rather than compete with it.

**How to build.** Add `thread_id` and `seen_at` to digest records. For `NEEDS_YOU|BLOCKED`, call the existing open-loop add path or reference an existing canonical thread; never create two thread objects for the same `dedup_key`. At render, omit unchanged seen cards from routine returns except (a) due date crossed, (b) state changed, or (c) the weekly reconciliation. Closing uses the existing loop close operation and appends a `RESOLVED` digest delta. OPEN-THREADS remains canonical; the digest is its change surface.

**External signal that verifies it.** An end-to-end fixture creates thread T, surfaces it twice across process restarts, and proves the canonical open-thread record persists after `seen_at`. A separate simulated task completion with a passing receipt closes T and produces one resolution delta. A reconciliation test compares canonical open-thread IDs with digest-linked active IDs and must report zero silently dropped IDs.

**SOTA.** The Human-AI guidelines separate efficient dismissal/correction from remembering recent interactions and granular feedback ([Amershi et al.](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf), G8–G9 and G12–G15). This is also classic mixed initiative: dialogue should resolve key uncertainty while considering the cost of needlessly bothering the user ([Horvitz, 1999](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/chi99horvitz.pdf)).

**Value × effort.** transformative × medium. It is **almost certain (90-99%)** to prevent the specific state-machine error “surfaced therefore resolved,” because closure becomes receipt-dependent; preventing every forgotten thread is **UNVERIFIED — settle with a 30-day reconciliation of created versus closed/superseded/open IDs and manual audit of any unmatched ID**.

**Novelty.** inferred-surprise.

**Autonomy.** confirm-first (the in-place migration is reversible, but any reconciliation that would close ambiguous existing threads requires the owner).

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD.** BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).

**Anti-overfit gate.** Passes: OPEN-THREADS/open-loops remain canonical; there is no second memory system.

## Finding 3 — Evidence badges must be earned outside the dreaming context (inferred surprise)

**Improvement.** Every card carries a mechanically enforced evidence class; the digest never reports the dreamer’s same-context self-assessment as a fact.

**What it is.** Four display states: `VERIFIED` (external terminal/receipt or two independent observations), `OBSERVED` (one external observation; no causal conclusion), `PREPARED` (artifact exists and its hash was reopened by another process, but effectiveness is not claimed), and `UNVERIFIED` (explicit hypothesis plus settling experiment). Words such as “fixed,” “broken,” “absent,” and “done” are illegal unless the record contains the required fresh receipt class.

**Why inferred.** The known-defects report records six single-probe wrong conclusions and a seventh detector failure caught only by a known-positive control (`INPUT-known-harness-defects.md:111-129`). The current digest accepts any date-prefixed producer prose (`reader.py:70-72,124-158`), so a producer can currently smuggle same-context confidence into the return surface.

**How to build.** Reuse event-ledger receipt IDs and the existing truth-verify/fresh-context rails. The append helper validates `evidence_refs` against ledger receipt type, timestamp, project, and mutation freshness. For absence/broken claims require both (1) a detector known-positive control and (2) an independent field/tool/result. For prepared artifacts require path, content hash, and a separate-process reopen. Rendering emits the exact confidence band and evidence shorthand. If evidence is missing, downgrade automatically to `UNVERIFIED` and show the settling experiment; never block the session.

**External signal that verifies it.** Mutation tests attempt to append `DONE` with no receipt, stale receipt, wrong-project receipt, and same-context prose; all must render `UNVERIFIED`. A real test command exit 0 plus independently parsed result may render `VERIFIED`. A known-positive/known-negative detector pair must be included for any absence fixture.

**SOTA.** Lee and See frame trust design as supporting appropriate reliance rather than maximal trust ([Trust in automation, Human Factors 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15151155/)). NIST says validation is confirmation through objective evidence and recommends independent review to mitigate internal bias ([AI RMF trustworthiness characteristics](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/3-sec-characteristics/); [AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/)).

**Value × effort.** high × medium. It is **very likely (80-90%)** to improve trust calibration because the display distinguishes receipt-backed claims from hypotheses, supported by the local seven-error class and trust literature; the magnitude is not predicted.

**Novelty.** inferred-surprise.

**Autonomy.** just-do-it.

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD.** BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus). Reuse the already-built receipt validator; the required interface probe decides the adapter shape, not the tag.

**Anti-overfit gate.** Passes if it reuses receipts and fresh-context verification. A trained critic is explicitly rejected.

## Finding 4 — Rank by actionable delta; coalesce chatter before it reaches attention (inferred surprise)

**Improvement.** Replace FIFO/12 with deterministic coalescing and a hard three-card attention budget.

**What it is.** Stable `dedup_key = project + producer + subject + thread`. Repeated identical states increment `observations` without creating cards. A state transition supersedes the old record. Rank by a transparent tuple: safety/irreversibility, owner-blocking status, due time, externally verified impact, novelty, then age. Never use an LLM to decide whether another LLM deserves attention.

**Why inferred.** The current default is 12 oldest entries and overflow on another session (`reader.py:67,138-158`); open-loops already demonstrates a deliberate 1–3 item cap (`open-loops/resurface.py:3-5,38-40`). Corpus samples show idle teammate notices arriving serially (`corpus.json:11308-11336`). A partner surface should synthesize these into “four agents idle; one produced a terminal result,” not replay four transport events.

**How to build.** Add a pure `coalesce(records)` and `rank(records, now)` to the reader. Suppress `idle_notification` unless it changes a parent thread’s terminal readiness. Collapse resource warnings by subject and retain first-seen, last-seen, peak, current state, and evidence count. Show at most: one urgent/needs-you card, one prepared-value card, one noticed/watch card. Put `+N changes` behind a pointer to the archive. Deterministic tie-breaking by record ID makes tests stable.

**External signal that verifies it.** Feed fixtures containing 100 identical idle notices, three resource measurements, a stopped workflow, and a verified prepared artifact. A separate golden renderer must output at most three cards, one coalesced idle summary at most, current resource state, and both higher-value deltas. Compare selected IDs to a hand-authored expected set; do not ask the ranking code to grade itself.

**SOTA.** Attention-sensitive alerting explicitly balances the cost of deferring information against interruption cost and prioritizes by expected criticality ([Horvitz, Jacobs & Hovel](https://erichorvitz.com/attend.htm)). Human-centered proactive-agent research warns that initiative without thoughtful design becomes intrusive and proposes intelligence, adaptivity, and civility as distinct dimensions ([Deng et al., SIGIR 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12670)).

**Value × effort.** high × low. It is **very likely (80-90%)** to reduce raw notification volume on repeated-state workloads because deduplication and the cap guarantee that mechanically; whether three cards is the owner’s optimal cognitive budget is UNVERIFIED and is tested in Finding 8.

**Novelty.** inferred-surprise.

**Autonomy.** just-do-it.

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD.** BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).

**Anti-overfit gate.** Passes: deterministic rules replace, rather than add, model orchestration. A 2026 preprint proposes a trained temporal-graph trigger, but adopting it would violate the rejected trained-component/minimality gate; it is documented as a researched dead end, not a recommendation ([Liu et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30152)).

## Finding 5 — UNVERIFIED: return-bound batching, not clock-driven interruption (inferred surprise)

**Improvement.** Default to one routine digest at a natural return boundary, with no routine push notifications.

**What it is.** Proposed starting policy: queue continuously, surface only in the first **main** session after either (a) at least four hours of owner inactivity or (b) a local calendar-day change; then suppress another routine digest for 12 hours. A new `NEEDS_YOU` state may surface at the next main SessionStart even inside the 12-hour window, but still does not push while the owner is away. Weekly, include a compact unresolved-thread reconciliation. Empty queue means silence.

**Why inferred.** The existing subagent guard already protects the main session from digest theft (`reader.py:28-33,109-121,213-220`), giving a natural owner-return boundary without a new hook. The brief makes prompt fatigue first-class. A randomized field experiment found three predictable notification batches improved several attention/control outcomes while zero notifications increased anxiety/FoMO; transfer from smartphones to an intensive coding harness is **UNVERIFIED — settle with the shadow/A-B experiment in Finding 8** ([Fitz et al., 2019](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/57a40c19414fb54f51f8095f/t/614a55faa7b89e25f4e48ad1/1632261627146/2019%2BFitz%2BBatching.pdf)).

**How to build.** Store only `last_main_activity_at` and `last_routine_digest_at` beside the existing digest archive. SessionStart reads these timestamps and the typed queue; subagent starts never update them. `BG_DIGEST_RETURN_AFTER_HOURS=4` and `BG_DIGEST_MIN_INTERVAL_HOURS=12` are transparent config values. A `/digest now` or equivalent existing invocation path can bypass timing; do not ship an unwired UI button. Preserve opt-out. No scheduler changes are required.

**External signal that verifies it.** A fake-clock integration suite across main/subagent starts proves: four-hour threshold, date boundary, 12-hour suppression, urgent-delta exception, empty silence, and no subagent consumption. The owner-facing hypothesis is tested by randomized weeks or alternating-day shadow comparison of one/day versus return-bound policy, measuring cards shown, explicit useful/dismiss/snooze responses, repeated owner reminders, and unresolved-thread misses.

**SOTA.** Horvitz’s mixed-initiative principles require attention-aware timing and explicitly account for costs/benefits of deferral ([Horvitz, 1999](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/chi99horvitz.pdf), principles 3–5). Amershi et al. likewise say to time services based on current task/environment and support efficient dismissal ([CHI 2019](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf), G3 and G8).

**Value × effort.** high × low. It is **likely (55-80%)** that return-bound batching will be less disruptive than every-session FIFO, based on notification batching and attention-aware alerting; the exact 4h/12h values are **UNVERIFIED** until the specified owner study.

**Novelty.** inferred-surprise.

**Autonomy.** confirm-first (shadow measurement may be prepared without changing cadence, but the live frequency change requires owner confirmation).

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD.** CONFIG (settings/JSON only) after the reader accepts timing fields.

**Anti-overfit gate.** Passes: this is a timestamp gate inside the existing SessionStart reader, not a scheduler/daemon.

## Finding 6 — Separate “noticed,” “prepared,” and “acted”; background autonomy must be legible (inferred surprise)

**Improvement.** Make the card verb a contractual statement about side effects and authority.

**What it is.** `NOTICED` means read-only observation only. `PREPARED` means an artifact/draft exists, independently reopened and hashed, but no live config/repo/external state was changed. `ACTED` is allowed only for pre-authorized, reversible operations and must include before/after receipts plus rollback. `NEEDS_YOU` requests a genuine authority or trade-off decision. Producers cannot choose a stronger verb than their receipts support.

**Why inferred.** The current implementation correctly states “LOG + PROPOSE ONLY” (`reader.py:16-18,154-157`), but the owner’s desired surface explicitly says “noticed/prepared,” which invites scope creep unless those words carry enforceable meanings. The corpus includes destructive-scope sensitivity (“wait do not delete the installers”) in an individually readable sample (`corpus.json:11201`), reinforcing the need to distinguish preparation from action.

**How to build.** Add `side_effect_class`, `authorization_ref?`, `before_receipt?`, `after_receipt?`, and `rollback_ref?`. The append validator derives the display verb: a missing artifact hash downgrades PREPARED to NOTICED; a missing authorization or postcondition downgrades ACTED to NEEDS_YOU/UNVERIFIED. Default background producers remain read-only. No command in a digest is auto-executed. The renderer states “no live changes” on PREPARED cards when that is externally confirmed.

**External signal that verifies it.** Run producers in a throwaway repo with filesystem/process/network mutation logging. A PREPARED fixture must show exactly one allowed artifact write and zero config/git/external mutations; an ACTED fixture must fail to earn that verb without an authorization token, after-state receipt, and tested rollback. A separate filesystem diff supplies the external signal.

**SOTA.** Mixed-initiative design calls for expected-value decisions under uncertainty and efficient dialogue for key uncertainty ([Horvitz, 1999](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/chi99horvitz.pdf)). Human-AI guidelines call for scoping services when in doubt, explaining why, conveying consequences, and providing global controls ([Amershi et al.](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf), G10–G11, G16–G17).

**Value × effort.** high × low. It is **very likely (80-90%)** to reduce ambiguity about autonomous side effects because verbs are receipt-derived; whether it changes subjective trust is not claimed.

**Novelty.** inferred-surprise.

**Autonomy.** confirm-first (the schema/validator can be prepared read-only, but this finding must not grant any new background mutation authority implicitly).

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD.** CONFIG (settings/JSON only) on top of Finding 1’s typed contract.

**Anti-overfit gate.** Passes: this constrains existing producers; it does not introduce a guardrail framework.

## Finding 7 — Progressive disclosure plus explicit, reversible personalization (user-asked surface)

**Improvement.** Show outcome first, evidence second, control third; learn cadence/topic preferences only from explicit feedback and reversible behavior.

**What it is.** Default cards are three lines. Full evidence lives at a stable archive/thread pointer. Controls are real CLI actions: `useful`, `mute producer/topic`, `snooze thread`, `show less/more`, and `close with receipt`. Global settings include maximum cards, routine interval, quiet mode, and allowed producer classes. Silence is not interpreted as dislike.

**Why inferred.** Prompt fatigue is a hard constraint, while the existing reader can emit 12 full raw lines and a footer (`reader.py:67,148-158`). The owner asked specifically how to report without noise, so progressive disclosure and controls are directly requested. The current opt-out (`BG_DIGEST=off`) is useful but all-or-nothing (`reader.py:75-81`); granular controls build beyond it.

**How to build.** Extend the same reader CLI with `--show <id>`, `--feedback <id> useful|noise`, `--snooze <thread> <duration>`, and `--mute <producer|subject>`; wire help text only after tests and manifest invocation exist. Persist a small JSON preference file with provenance (`explicit_owner`), timestamp, and undo history. After three explicit same-direction ratings, suggest—do not silently apply—a cadence/topic change. Never derive sensitive interests or personality traits.

**External signal that verifies it.** CLI integration tests must prove each displayed action mutates the intended preference/thread state and is reversible. The owner is the independent signal for usefulness/noise. Weekly preference reconciliation shows exactly what changed and offers one-command undo.

**SOTA.** Amershi et al. recommend efficient dismissal/correction, granular feedback, conveying consequences, and global controls ([CHI 2019](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf), G8–G9, G15–G17). Human-centered proactive-agent work treats adaptivity and civility as separate from raw intelligence ([Deng et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12670)).

**Value × effort.** high × medium. It is **likely (55-80%)** that explicit controls will calibrate the surface better than implicit inference, supported by the guideline evidence; the optimal feedback threshold is **UNVERIFIED — test 2 versus 3 versus 5 consistent ratings in shadow and ask the owner which changes felt premature**.

**Novelty.** user-asked.

**Autonomy.** confirm-first (persistent preference changes are proposed and reversible, never silently inferred).

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD.** BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).

**Anti-overfit gate.** Passes: a small explicit preference file is configuration, not a new personalization model or memory service. Basic-memory is not duplicated.

## Finding 8 — UNVERIFIED: promote only after a shadow precision/continuity trial (user-asked surface)

**Improvement.** Instrument the existing digest’s usefulness before changing live presentation, then promote only if it reduces noise without losing threads.

**What it is.** For 14 active days, generate the proposed ranked digest in shadow alongside the current reader without showing it. Record only IDs, selected ranks, suppression reasons, and receipt classes in the event ledger. Then conduct a seven-day visible canary with easy feedback. Success requires both attention and continuity metrics.

**Why inferred.** The corpus cannot measure historical hook behavior because its hook detector is broken (`corpus.json:13260-13264`), and the defects report says hook telemetry across the corpus is presently unmeasurable pending separate ledger/transcript probes (`INPUT-known-harness-defects.md:133-147`). Therefore any claim that the current or proposed digest is quiet/useful is **UNVERIFIED — this trial is the settling experiment**.

**How to build.** Reuse the event ledger; append `digest_candidate`, `digest_surface`, `digest_feedback`, and `digest_thread_reconcile` events containing no raw sensitive text. Metrics: cards per active day; duplicate-state suppression; percent `useful`, `noise`, `snooze`, `acted`; median time to owner action; number of owner reminders matching an already-open thread; created/open/closed/superseded thread conservation; false “done/broken/absent” cards; SessionStart latency p50/p95; hook errors (which remain fail-open). Promotion gates: zero silently lost thread IDs, zero evidence-policy violations in a hand audit, p95 added reader latency under 100 ms on the local file sizes, and a visible-card rate selected with the owner. Do not optimize a single “engagement” score.

**External signal that verifies it.** Ledger events are parsed by a separate evaluation process; a fresh reviewer receives only candidate IDs, source records, and owner feedback, not the generator rationale. The owner decides whether the canary is less noisy. Thread conservation is independently reconciled against OPEN-THREADS/open-loop canonical state. This satisfies the law against same-context self-correction.

**SOTA.** NIST calls for context-specific measurement, end-user feedback integrated into evaluation, independent review, and documented performance change ([AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/), Measure 2.9, 3.3, 4.1–4.3). Amershi et al. validated their guidelines through multiple evaluation rounds rather than treating design intuitions as proof ([Microsoft Research publication page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/)).

**Value × effort.** high × medium. It is **almost certain (90-99%)** that this will produce a falsifiable comparison because every candidate and owner response is externally logged; it is **roughly even (45-55%)** that the initial 3-card/4h/12h defaults will pass unchanged, because no workspace-specific preference data currently settles those thresholds.

**Novelty.** user-asked.

**Autonomy.** confirm-first (the owner should approve even privacy-minimized shadow telemetry before collection; the visible canary and promotion remain separately gated).

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD.** BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).

**Anti-overfit gate.** Passes: event-ledger telemetry and a standalone evaluator reuse current rails. No observability SaaS or trained critic.

## Frequency recommendation, in one place
