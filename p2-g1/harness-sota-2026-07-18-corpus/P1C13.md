# P1C13 — Normalized audit findings B09–B12

## Scope and method

This unit normalizes the findings already written in `B09.md`, `B10.md`, `B11.md`, and `B12.md`. It does **not** inspect the cited SUBJECT files, rerun commands, or validate URLs. Accordingly, every source claim below is **UNVERIFIED against the real system**; the experiment that settles each claim is to open every cited range and replay every cited bounded command against an isolated/read-only snapshot in the later verification phase.

`SUPPORTED`, `PARTIAL`, and `UNSUPPORTED` assess internal coherence only: assuming the evidence statement is accurate, does it entail the one-sentence claim? These judgments are **almost certain (90-99%)** to reproduce under a fresh-context textual review because they compare the literal claim and literal evidence fields, but they are not estimates that the underlying system claim is true. “Claimed severity” is the value component of each source finding's `value x effort` field (`high` or `med`), not a new severity assigned by this unit.

Stable IDs are `P1C13-<source>-<finding>`. No exact duplicate appears across the four files. Two material overlaps are marked: `B11-F6` and `B12-F2` discuss the same Codex plugin but make tension-bearing availability claims; `B10-F3` and `B12-F1` both describe one-way cross-provider rails but concern different mechanisms. This no-exact-duplicate assessment is **very likely (80-90%)**, based on one-to-one comparison of all 33 claim fields; semantic equivalence remains somewhat subjective.

No recommendation is introduced here, so no NATIVE-vs-BUILD classification applies. Source `FIX`/`BUILD`/`PRUNE` tags are deliberately not promoted into this unit's proposals.
