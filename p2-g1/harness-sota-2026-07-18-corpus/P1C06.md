# P1C06 — Normalized audit findings A21–A24

## Scope and method

This unit normalizes the 35 titled finding blocks in `A21.md`, `A22.md`, `A23.md`, and `A24.md` (8 + 9 + 10 + 8). A finding is one `### F#` block; A22-F3's embedded 15-skill scorecard remains evidence inside that one finding rather than becoming 15 findings with invented severities.

`SUPPORTED`, `PARTIAL`, and `UNSUPPORTED` assess only internal coherence: whether the cited evidence, **taken at face value**, supports the claim. Every underlying filesystem fact, command result, and external-currentness assertion remains **UNVERIFIED against the real subject** in this phase; the settling experiment is the later verification phase re-running the cited command or reading the cited path/URL. This limitation is inline for the entire table and JSON dataset and is not a claim that any source finding is externally true.

Confidence in the normalization and coherence classifications is **almost certain (90-99%)**, based on direct, line-numbered reading of all four audit artifacts and a one-to-one count of their titled finding blocks. Where a source claim goes beyond its stated evidence, the record is `PARTIAL` and identifies the unsupported leap. No record is `UNSUPPORTED`: every finding supplies at least some evidence relevant to its claim.

The `native_vs_build` field applies only when the source finding proposes an action. It uses the mandated values `NATIVE`, `CONFIG`, `BUILD-SMALL`, or `BUILD-LARGE`; `null` means the finding is observational and contains no implementation proposal. None proposes a rejected second runtime, vector/KG memory, durable-execution engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, or guardrail framework, so all pass the anti-overfit gate as written.
