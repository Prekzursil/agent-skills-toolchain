## Scope and method

This artifact normalizes every finding in the four assigned audit files and assesses internal coherence only: whether the evidence cited by the finding, taken at face value, supports its one-sentence claim. It does not inspect or verify the cited subject files. The underlying subject claims are **UNVERIFIED**; the settling experiment is the later verification phase, which must inspect each cited location and reproduce each cited command or probe.

The support judgments are **almost certain (90-99%)**, because all 37 records preserve the exact audit evidence field and each evidence field directly names implementation/test locations, command output, a native contract, or a combination of those signals that addresses the stated mechanism. This confidence applies only to the internal-coherence classification, not to whether the cited evidence is factually accurate in the real subject.

Claimed severity is normalized from the first component of each audit `value x effort` field (`high` or `med`); it is not a new severity assessment. Stable IDs use `P1C04-<audit>-F<zero-padded-number>`.
