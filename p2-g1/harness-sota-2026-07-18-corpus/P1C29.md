This unit normalizes the 36 findings in `E03.md`, `E04.md`, `E05.md`, and `E06.md`. It performs **internal-coherence review only**: cited evidence is taken at face value and was not checked against the subject files. `SUPPORTED` means the evidence as written entails the normalized claim; `PARTIAL` means it supports only part of a compound claim or leaves a consequential inference untested; `UNSUPPORTED` means it does not establish the claim. The assessment itself is **almost certain (90-99%)**, because all four source files were read in full and their nine finding boundaries were independently enumerated by heading; this does not imply that the underlying audit claims are true.

“Claimed severity” preserves the source's `value x effort` severity component (`high` or `med`), not an independently assigned severity. “Proposal class” normalizes the action in each source finding to the mandated architecture vocabulary. These tags classify the proposal; they do not endorse it. All proposals ride an existing hook/skill/MCP/configuration bus and none proposes a rejected second runtime, vector/KG memory, durable-execution engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, or guardrail framework.
