## Executive judgment

The useful borrow is **not** “score every memory by truth and retain the highest score.” It is a narrower bundle of intelligence-tradecraft mechanisms:

1. Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH) makes alternatives explicit, evaluates each evidence item across all alternatives, privileges diagnostic and disconfirming evidence, tests sensitivity to critical evidence, and names future observations that would change the judgment.
2. Intelligence grading keeps the reliability of a source separate from the credibility of this particular report, preserves “cannot be judged” as a legitimate state, and revises the grades as evidence accumulates.
3. Quality-of-information checks preserve source limitations, information gaps, age, contradictory reporting, and the distinction between observations, assumptions, and judgments.
4. Sherman-Kent-style probability bands standardize the uncertainty expressed by judgments; modern doctrine additionally separates likelihood from confidence in the evidentiary basis.

These mechanisms solve a real information-selection problem in their own field: they prevent a concise finished assessment from laundering its evidence base into a single fluent conclusion. Current UK standards require the main evidence and contradictory information affecting a judgment to remain referenced, require a discoverable record of alternative-hypothesis analysis, and require the most important sources and critical gaps to be identified ([PHIA Common Analytical Standards, “Comprehensive,” “Auditable,” and “Rigorous”](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/phia-common-analytical-standards/phia-common-analytical-standards)). The CIA’s quality-of-information check warns specifically that judgments can remain anchored to weak information while old caveats are forgotten over time ([CIA, *A Tradecraft Primer*, pp. 9–10](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf)). That is almost exactly the failure mode of repeated agent summarization.

My verdict is **partial transfer**. It is very likely (80-90%) that an ACH-inspired, contradiction-preserving compaction record will retain task-critical qualifications better than an ordinary narrative rolling summary; the evidence is structural alignment with the cited tradecraft rules plus the demonstrated sensitivity of LLMs to context position, not a harness trial. This is **UNVERIFIED; settle it with the paired, seeded compaction benchmark specified in “Evaluation gate” below**. It is unlikely (20-45%) that full ACH, including its conclusion-scoring rule, improves general agent accuracy; the evidence is a 2024 critical review of six experiments finding little-to-no overall benefit and possible harm, plus a 2024 experimental paper reporting no reduction in confirmation bias ([Crawford et al., critical review](https://doi.org/10.1080/02684527.2024.2304934); [Crawford et al., task-structure experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11169332/)). Therefore the proposal borrows **representation and retention disciplines**, not ACH’s claim to be a general debiasing or inference algorithm.

## What intelligence tradecraft has actually solved

“Solved” needs care. Intelligence tradecraft has standardized how analysts expose the structure and uncertainty of a judgment to other humans. It has not solved inference accuracy, eliminated bias, or supplied an objectively correct compression function.

### 1. ACH: retain what discriminates, not what merely supports

#### How it works in intelligence analysis

Richards Heuer’s canonical ACH procedure is an eight-step method for difficult judgments:

1. Enumerate reasonable alternative hypotheses.
2. List significant evidence and arguments for and against each.
3. Put hypotheses in columns and evidence in rows; assess each item’s **diagnosticity**, meaning how much it helps distinguish the alternatives.
4. Refine the matrix and remove evidence with no diagnostic value.
5. Seek to disprove hypotheses rather than accumulate support for a favorite.
6. Test sensitivity to critical evidence being wrong, misleading, or differently interpreted.
7. Report the relative likelihood of all hypotheses, not only the leader.
8. Identify future milestones that would show events taking a different course.

Those steps appear together in Heuer’s *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis* ([CIA edition, pp. 95–97](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/9a5f1162fd0932c29bfed1c030edf4ae/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf)). The important unit of value is not “evidence consistent with the current answer.” It is evidence whose pattern differs across live alternatives. Heuer explicitly notes that the same confirming evidence may fit multiple hypotheses, while one inconsistent item can sometimes reject a hypothesis ([ibid., pp. 102–104](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/9a5f1162fd0932c29bfed1c030edf4ae/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf)). The CIA’s shorter tradecraft primer summarizes ACH as evaluating evidence that disconfirms rather than confirms hypotheses ([CIA, *A Tradecraft Primer*, pp. 14–16](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf)).

ACH also externalizes the case. Instead of relying on an analyst to remember why an item mattered, the matrix records how every evidence item bears on every hypothesis. This leaves an audit trail and makes it possible to revisit a judgment when an evidentiary interpretation changes. The 2025 UK cross-government standards independently institutionalize the same surrounding requirements: systematically evaluate differing hypotheses, conceive of observations that would contradict a hypothesis, preserve the method in a discoverable audit trail, and reference contradictory information that affects a judgment ([PHIA standards](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/phia-common-analytical-standards/phia-common-analytical-standards)).

#### What is genuinely relevant to context selection

A narrative summarizer typically optimizes for centrality, coherence, and compression. ACH supplies a different loss function: **preserve information with high decision diagnosticity, especially contradictions to the currently leading plan**. A repeated but generic supporting fact is cheap to reconstruct and contributes little discrimination. A one-line exception that rules out the favorite implementation can be worth more than several pages of confirming narrative.

This maps to finite context because both settings must turn a large, noisy record into a smaller decision substrate while preserving the possibility of revision. The mapping is functional, not metaphorical:

| Intelligence object | Harness object | Retention implication |
|---|---|---|
| Intelligence requirement | Current user goal and acceptance test | Context has value only relative to the live requirement. |
| Competing hypothesis | Competing diagnosis, implementation plan, or interpretation of user intent | Do not let the current plan erase the strongest surviving alternative. |
| Evidence item | User statement, file observation, test result, tool output, or external source claim | Store an atomic item with provenance rather than only a blended prose summary. |
| Inconsistency cell | Observation that conflicts with a plan/claim | Pin while the affected alternative remains live. |
| Diagnosticity | Ability to distinguish live alternatives or alter the next action | Prefer over repetition, rhetorical salience, or mere recency. |
| Critical evidence / linchpin | Fact or assumption on which a decision depends | Preserve with its caveat and revalidation method. |
| Indicator/signpost | Test, file change, reply, event, or tool result that would reverse a judgment | Use as a rehydration trigger. |
| Fully referenced assessment | Cold case file outside the prompt, with stable IDs/pointers represented in working context | Evict tokens without severing provenance. |

### 2. Admiralty/NATO grading: source reputation is not claim credibility

#### How it works in intelligence analysis

The NATO Intelligence Grading System, often called the Admiralty Code, records two separate judgments in an alphanumeric pair. Source reliability is graded A–F; information credibility is graded 1–6. A usually reliable source can provide improbable information (B5), and an unreliable source can provide independently confirmed information (E1). F and 6 mean that reliability or truth cannot be judged; they do not mean “useless” ([UK Ministry of Defence, *JDP 2-00*, 4th ed., paras. 3.40 and table 3.1](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/653a4b0780884d0013f71bb0/JDP_2_00_Ed_4_web.pdf)). Doctrine also says grades should be revisited as more reporting, broader context, or deeper expertise arrives.

The separation prevents two common errors:

- **halo transfer:** “this source is usually good, therefore this item is true”; and
- **reverse halo:** “this source is poor or new, therefore this particular item cannot matter.”

The method is not behaviorally foolproof. Recent experimental work notes a tendency for users to assign matching diagonal codes and shows that accuracy judgments, rather than every dimension of information quality, consistently predicted intended use across two experiments ([Mandel et al., 2025](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/judgment-and-decision-making/article/effect-of-source-reliability-and-information-credibility-on-judgments-of-information-quality-in-intelligence-analysis/E67548E8010A47345C3439D45D9EC6B3)). Thus the transferable principle is **factor separation and explicit unknowns**, not the literal A–F/1–6 labels.

#### Translation to context

For an agent, “source” might be a user, a generated summary, a test runner, a particular documentation site, a tool, or a model-generated inference. “Item” is the particular content emitted. A flaky tool can still produce one result corroborated by a second mechanism. A usually authoritative document can be stale for a specific version. A user correction has authority over desired behavior even when it is not evidence about physical reality. These distinctions must survive compaction.

The direct context rule is: **never collapse authority/provenance, observed reliability, and item-level credibility into one importance score**. They are distinct typed fields and have different consequences. “Unknown” also needs its own value; otherwise first-seen but decisive evidence is automatically evicted.

### 3. Quality-of-information checks: prevent caveat decay

The CIA describes information-quality checking as continuous. It says multiple sources are not a substitute for good information, and warns that old caveats can be forgotten while a major analytic line remains anchored to weak evidence ([CIA, *A Tradecraft Primer*, pp. 9–11](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf)). ODNI’s binding analytic standard requires discussion of accuracy, completeness, denial/deception, age/currency, collection details, access, validation, motivation, bias, and expertise, and asks products to explain which sources matter most to key judgments ([ICD 203, pp. 3–4](https://www.dni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203.pdf)). The 2025 UK standard additionally requires information to be distinguished from assumptions, linchpin assumptions to be visible, and implications of a false assumption to be understood ([PHIA standards, “Clear”](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/phia-common-analytical-standards/phia-common-analytical-standards)).

This is the most direct long-horizon borrow. Repeated summarization tends to turn:

> test X failed under environment Y; cause Z is an inference; output was truncated; user rejected workaround W

into:

> Z caused the failure, so use W.

The shorter sentence is fluent and catastrophically changes epistemic type. An information-quality record prevents that by keeping observation, inference, assumption, decision, and user constraint separate.

### 4. Sherman-Kent bands: useful annotation, wrong eviction metric

Sherman Kent argued that estimative words need stable odds meanings because readers otherwise attach different probabilities to the same phrase ([Kent, “Words of Estimative Probability,” 1964](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/studies-in-intelligence/archives/vol-8-no-4/words-of-estimative-probability/)). Modern ICD 203 uses standardized likelihood ranges and separately requires an explanation of confidence in the evidentiary basis; it explicitly warns against mixing likelihood and confidence in the same sentence ([ICD 203, p. 4](https://www.dni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203.pdf)). The UK’s 2025 framework likewise separates a seven-band probability yardstick from an Analytical Confidence Rating based on information base, analytic rigor, and complexity/volatility ([PHIA, “Explaining Uncertainty”](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/explaining-uncertainty-in-uk-intelligence-assessment/explaining-uncertainty-in-uk-intelligence-assessment)).

The bands transfer as compact metadata on consequential forecasts and inferences. They do **not** transfer as retention priority. A low-probability safety hazard, an unresolved contradiction, or a low-confidence linchpin can deserve more context than a near-certain but irrelevant fact. “Probability” answers whether a proposition is thought true; “context value” answers the expected damage if the agent cannot access it. Conflating them would recreate the very category error the two-axis doctrine tries to prevent.

### Proposal A — requirement-scoped epistemic context ledger — UNVERIFIED

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

Add a pre-compaction/continuation hook that writes and refreshes a small, plain-text or JSON ledger. It is not a new memory runtime. It uses the existing context-compaction hook/bus and ordinary workspace storage. Each retained record is atomic:

```json
{
  "id": "E17",
  "type": "observation|instruction|assumption|inference|decision|open_question|indicator",
  "text": "...",
  "requirement": ["R1"],
  "provenance": {"event": "tool-call-id or message-id", "locator": "path:line or URL"},

```

The ledger must begin with explicit requirements: current goal, acceptance tests, user prohibitions/preferences, environment facts that affect execution, and unresolved questions. Relevance is evaluated against these requirements; it is not a universal semantic-similarity score.

It is very likely (80-90%) that typed fields reduce the specific transformation of assumptions into facts during compaction, because the schema forces those types to remain distinct and both ICD 203 and PHIA require that distinction. ### Proposal B — contradiction-preserving compaction policy — UNVERIFIED

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

At compaction, retain in the active prompt, in descending class priority:

1. Exact current user instruction, acceptance condition, correction, prohibition, safety boundary, and authorization boundary.
2. Current state needed for the next irreversible or externally visible action.
3. The leading plan or diagnosis **and the most diagnostic contradiction to it**.
4. Each unresolved linchpin assumption, its evidence, consequence if false, and cheapest recheck.
5. Decisions with rationale, rejected alternative, and reversal condition.
6. Unresolved errors, partial/truncated outputs, and negative test results that bear on a live alternative.
7. Independent provenance pointers and freshness/expiry conditions.
8. Expected indicators or signposts that should trigger re-evaluation or rehydration.

Prefer eviction of:

1. verbatim duplication and repeated confirmations from the same causal source;
2. non-diagnostic evidence equally compatible with every live alternative;
3. resolved exploration whose conclusion, rationale, and archive pointer are preserved;
4. superseded plans, provided the supersession and any still-relevant constraint remain;
5. tool narration and conversational glue.

This is deliberately a **class policy**, not a learned scalar. A scalar invites a high “source reliability” score to hide low item credibility, or high recency to erase old user constraints. Within a class, a compact heuristic can consider requirement relevance, diagnostic spread across alternatives, dependency count, cost of reacquisition, staleness, and token cost, but no field may silently stand in for another.

The hook should emit a brief “compaction manifest” into the new prompt:

```text
REQUIREMENT R1: ...
LEADING H1: ...
LIVE ALTERNATIVE H2: ...
KEY CONTRADICTION E17: ... [observed; source mixed; item corroborated]
LINCHPIN A4: ... ; if false => D3 invalid ; recheck => ...
USER CORRECTION U2: ...
DECISION D3: ... ; rejected H2 because E9 ; reverse if I5
GAPS: ...
ARCHIVE: ledger path + stable event IDs
```

It is likely (55-80%) that this policy catches more late reversals and avoids repeating rejected approaches than an ordinary narrative summary; the evidence is ACH’s diagnostic representation, the quality-check warning about forgotten caveats, and experimental evidence that domain experts can conflate supportive evidence with importance ([Cook & Smallman, 2008](https://doi.org/10.1518/001872008X354183)). This is **UNVERIFIED; seeded late-reversal and rejected-option replay cases settle it**.

### Proposal C — signpost-triggered rehydration — UNVERIFIED

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

For every consequential decision, record one or more observable reversal indicators: “if test T changes,” “if file F changes,” “if owner answers Q,” “if package version differs,” or “if independent source S contradicts E.” The CIA’s indicators/signposts technique creates observable events expected under a developing situation and periodically reviews them to detect change ([CIA, *A Tradecraft Primer*, pp. 11–13](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf)). In the harness, the indicator is also an index key: when it fires, rehydrate the linked evidence and decision rationale before acting.

This is likely (55-80%) to outperform time/recency-only retrieval for decisions with explicit invalidation conditions, because the trigger is causally linked to the decision rather than merely lexically similar. This is **UNVERIFIED; mutate the linked file/test/user answer after compaction and measure correct decision reversal**.

## What this would catch that ordinary context compaction misses

These are candidate failure classes, **not claims that this specific local harness exhibits them**, because this ideation unit was explicitly barred from local grounding.

1. **Caveat decay.** “Output says X, but it was truncated/stale/collected under condition Y” becomes “X.” The ledger retains the condition and credibility separately.
2. **Confirmation-preserving summaries.** A summary preserves five reasons for the chosen diagnosis but drops the single failed prediction that points to another cause. Diagnostic contradiction is pinned.
3. **Source laundering.** A model inference is compacted as if it were a tool observation; a secondary summary is cited as if independent. Type and provenance prevent the silent promotion.
4. **False corroboration.** Three pages repeat the same upstream claim and are treated as three sources. Independent provenance is recorded; duplicate causal lineage is cheap to evict.
5. **Reputation halo.** An authoritative source is trusted even though the particular page is stale, or a flaky tool’s independently corroborated result is discarded. Source reliability and item credibility remain separate.
6. **Unknown → false.** Novel or ungraded information disappears because “no confidence” is interpreted as low probability. Explicit unknown preserves it until it can be judged.
7. **Assumption hardening.** “Likely cause” becomes “cause”; “planned” becomes “done”; “requested” becomes “authorized.” Epistemic type and completion evidence remain separate.
8. **Lost negative results.** A failed attempt disappears as non-progress, causing the agent to repeat it. Negative evidence stays while it discriminates among live plans.
9. **Decision without rationale.** The chosen approach survives, but the condition that made it preferable does not; later state changes without triggering revision. Decision plus reversal signpost survives.
10. **Stale truth.** A once-correct version, path, or service state persists indefinitely. Currency and recheck condition are first-class.
11. **Serial-position loss.** Relevant evidence in the middle of a long prompt is technically present but functionally weak. Controlled experiments find substantial performance changes based solely on where relevant information appears and warn that more context can reduce accuracy ([Liu et al., *Lost in the Middle*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172)). The manifest moves critical constraints and contradictions into a small, predictable working set while preserving archive pointers.
12. **Irrecoverable compression.** A prose summary drops the only handle for retrieving a detail. Stable event/file/URL pointers are mandatory even when verbatim content leaves the prompt.

It is very likely (80-90%) that the policy can mechanically detect missing provenance, missing epistemic type, missing reversal condition, and absence of a contradiction for a live alternative, because those are schema invariants. This is **UNVERIFIED as an end-to-end benefit; inject each defect and test manifest validation plus downstream task success**. It is only roughly even (45-55%) that an LLM will correctly assign diagnosticity without a second signal, because ACH experiments do not establish reliable general judgment improvement and the model that compresses the record shares failure modes with the model that generated it. This is **UNVERIFIED; compare model-assigned relations with benchmark ground truth and require abstention when agreement is low**.

## Adversarial analysis: where the analogy breaks

### Disanalogy 1: intelligence cases naturally have hypotheses; many agent tasks do not

ACH fits diagnosis, forecasting, ambiguous requirements, and plan choice. It is unnecessary overhead for “rename this variable” or a deterministic lookup. Forcing every transcript into competing hypotheses creates fake alternatives and consumes the very context it is meant to save.

**Design consequence:** activate the matrix only when there is a consequential unresolved choice, a diagnosis, conflicting evidence, a forecast, or a long-horizon decision. Otherwise use the typed ledger without hypotheses.

### Disanalogy 2: intelligence evidence is about a world; agent context also contains commands

A user instruction is not merely a possibly true proposition. It can define authority, preference, or the desired artifact. A safety rule is not evidence for a hypothesis. A test observation and a source-code token also have different semantics. Applying credibility grades indiscriminately would be incoherent and dangerous.

**Design consequence:** instruction/authority and exact-state records occupy protected types. Source/item grading applies to descriptive claims, not to whether a valid user preference is “true.” Exact code, identifiers, hashes, commands, and error strings should be retained verbatim or by lossless pointer, not paraphrased into an intelligence judgment.

### Disanalogy 3: an analyst usually retains the case file; a model may lose the evidence entirely

Finished intelligence can be concise because referenced reporting remains available. A context window without an accessible archive cannot emulate that. An archive pointer is also worthless if the next agent lacks permission, the source mutates, or the locator is unstable.

**Design consequence:** eviction is allowed only when there is a validated, accessible, stable rehydration pointer or when the content is demonstrably disposable. Destructive loss must be budgeted separately from prompt eviction. It is very likely (80-90%) that pointer validation is necessary for safe aggressive compaction; evidence is the direct difference between referenced intelligence products and an isolated prompt. This is **UNVERIFIED; revoke archive access or mutate targets in fault-injection tests and confirm the compactor refuses eviction**.

### Disanalogy 4: evidence items are not independent

Tool outputs, summaries, web pages, and model conclusions often descend from the same upstream text. Counting them as separate corroboration creates false confidence. Admiralty grades are themselves vulnerable to source/claim cue contamination; recent behavioral work documents the concern ([Mandel et al., 2025](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/judgment-and-decision-making/article/effect-of-source-reliability-and-information-credibility-on-judgments-of-information-quality-in-intelligence-analysis/E67548E8010A47345C3439D45D9EC6B3)).

**Design consequence:** “independent” requires distinct causal provenance, not distinct URLs or messages. When lineage is unknown, mark it unknown rather than corroborated.

### Disanalogy 5: ACH’s normative story is empirically weak

Heuer described ACH as effective, but later evidence does not justify treating the whole method as proven. A 2019 study found ACH users more often identified all relevant evidence items (92% versus 68% in its sample), but the broader critical review found little-to-no overall judgment-quality benefit and possible harm ([Dhami et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1002/acp.3550); [Crawford et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1080/02684527.2024.2304934)). Another experiment found graphical evidence layout reduced skew toward confirming evidence even though peer assessments did not, suggesting the representation may matter more than the ritual ([Cook & Smallman, 2008](https://doi.org/10.1518/001872008X354183)).

**Design consequence:** borrow the matrix as a compact audit/selection representation; do not use “fewest inconsistencies wins” as an automated truth engine. Do not claim debiasing until the context-specific trial shows it.

### Disanalogy 6: intelligence optimizes a finished assessment; an agent optimizes future action

Context value depends on expected future use, cost of reacquisition, dependency structure, and reversibility. A low-credibility clue may be crucial because checking it is the next action. A perfectly credible historical fact may be irrelevant. ACH diagnosticity covers only part of this value function.

**Design consequence:** diagnosticity is a retention class, not the whole eviction score. Protect current requirements, irreversible-action prerequisites, user corrections, and exact operational state independently.

### Disanalogy 7: the compressor can be the source of the bias

Intelligence workflows can separate collector, evaluator, analyst, reviewer, and consumer. In a simple harness, one model may create the claim, grade it, compress it, and later rely on it. The metadata can become self-authored theater.

**Design consequence:** use mechanical fields and provenance extraction where possible; treat model-assigned relations as fallible; and validate via seeded ground truth. Do not add a trained critic or second orchestration runtime merely to imitate organizational separation.

## Anti-overfit gate

The proposal passes the gate conditionally:

- It rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus as one small compaction hook plus a compact schema.
- It uses ordinary text/JSON and stable pointers, not a vector database, knowledge graph, durable-execution engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, guardrail framework, or second orchestration runtime.
- It is task-scoped and turns the hypothesis matrix off for simple deterministic work.
- It makes a measurable representational change rather than adding a permanent “agent organization.”

The following tempting expansions are explicitly rejected:

1. **Full-time ACH agent or review committee — rejected.** It is a second orchestration layer, ACH’s aggregate empirical benefit is weak, and the overhead scales poorly.
2. **Vector database / knowledge graph memory — rejected.** The proposed value is epistemic typing and contradiction retention, not semantic retrieval infrastructure. This is also a previously rejected component under the program’s gate.
3. **Learned retention critic — rejected.** It would add training/calibration burden and correlated judgment without first proving that the small schema helps.
4. **Literal Admiralty A–F/1–6 scoring — rejected.** The ordinal code is easy to misuse, “diagonal” contamination is documented, and agent records need authority, exactness, freshness, and reacquisition cost in addition to reliability/credibility.
5. **Probability-ranked eviction — rejected.** Truth likelihood is not expected cost of forgetting.
6. **Keep all context because windows are larger — rejected.** Long-context experiments show that information position affects performance and that adding context can reduce downstream accuracy even when it fits ([Liu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172)).

## Bottom line

The analogy transfers at the level of **epistemic bookkeeping under compression**:

- preserve contradictions, not just conclusions;
- preserve diagnostic evidence, not repeated support;
- preserve source provenance separately from claim credibility;
- preserve unknowns, caveats, assumptions, freshness, and reversal conditions;
- preserve an auditable route back to verbatim evidence.

It does not transfer at the level of “run full ACH and trust its winner,” “grade every token like a field report,” or “probability equals retention priority.” The best harness intervention is therefore a small, requirement-scoped, contradiction-preserving compaction manifest with a cold referenced case file—not a new memory platform. Adoption remains **UNVERIFIED until the paired replay benchmark above demonstrates better critical-information retention without harming ordinary task completion**.
