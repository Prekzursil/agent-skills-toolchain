## Executive finding

The borrow **does transfer, but only partially**. The transferable mechanism is not “hold an AI trial,” nor is it jury voting. It is a two-part evidentiary control:

1. **Sequester before counting.** Federal Rule of Evidence 615 prevents a witness from hearing other witnesses' testimony, and its Advisory Committee notes identify the purpose as discouraging and exposing fabrication, inaccuracy, collusion, and later testimony tailored to earlier testimony ([FRE 615 and notes](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_615); the Judiciary's official 2023 amendment package contains the operative text at pp. 2–3, [U.S. Courts PDF](https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2023_congressional_package_april_24_2023_0.pdf)). In harness terms: workers whose agreement will be treated as corroboration must produce their first-pass claim ledgers without seeing peers' answers. Agreement after answer-sharing is synthesis, not independent evidence.
2. **Discount and stop cumulative proof.** Federal Rule of Evidence 403 permits exclusion when probative value is substantially outweighed by delay, wasted time, or needlessly cumulative evidence; Rule 611 directs the judge to control presentation to determine truth effectively and avoid wasting time ([official current Federal Rules of Evidence page and PDF](https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure/federal-rules-evidence); [FRE 611 and notes](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_611)). In harness terms: the next worker is justified by expected *new, decision-relevant, independently sourced information*, not by another vote. Stop adding generalist workers once the latest admissible result adds no material claim, source lineage, contradiction, or risk-relevant test.

This yields an **Evidence-Diversity Gate**: a small orchestration hook that records claim provenance, distinguishes independent corroboration from common-source repetition, and launches an additional worker only for a named unresolved proposition or missing evidence class.

**Transfer verdict:** **very likely (80-90%)** that the structural mechanism—sequestered first passes plus a cumulative-evidence gate—improves the information gained per worker in fan-out tasks. Evidence: Rule 615 directly targets contamination between nominally separate witnesses; Rules 403 and 611 directly target marginally redundant presentations; and AI workers cloned from one model/prompt are especially capable of sharing a common failure mode. The last proposition is an inference about agent systems, not a fact established by the cited legal authorities. The remaining uncertainty is whether the owner's actual task mix contains enough decision-stage fan-out for the savings to exceed the gate's bookkeeping cost.

**Non-transfer verdict:** It is **almost certain (90-99%)** that evidence law, by itself, cannot determine a universally optimal numeric worker count. Evidence: the text of Rule 403 is a contextual balancing standard and Rule 611's notes assign many presentation questions to judicial “common sense and fairness in view of the particular circumstances,” rather than specifying a witness cap ([FRE 403 in the official rules PDF](https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/restyled_federal_rules_of_evidence.pdf); [FRE 611 notes](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_611)). The law supplies the shape of a stopping decision, not calibrated token economics or a statistical estimator.

## 1. The specific mechanism in its own field

### 1.1 Relevance is incremental, not a headcount

Federal Rule of Evidence 401 defines relevant evidence by whether it has any tendency to make a consequential fact more or less probable. Rule 402 generally admits relevant evidence. Rule 403 then creates a discretionary gate: even relevant evidence may be excluded if its probative value is substantially outweighed by specified risks, including undue delay, wasting time, and needlessly presenting cumulative evidence. These rules appear together in the Judiciary's current rules publication ([official Federal Rules of Evidence landing page](https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure/federal-rules-evidence); [official rules PDF, Rules 401–403](https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/restyled_federal_rules_of_evidence.pdf)).

The important mechanism is **marginal evaluation in an evidentiary context**. A second item is not worthless merely because it addresses the same fact, but its probative contribution can be discounted when the fact is already established through a less costly or less risky alternative. In *Old Chief v. United States*, 519 U.S. 172 (1997), the Supreme Court treated probative value as something that can be evaluated against evidentiary alternatives and the full evidentiary context, rather than as an intrinsic property of an isolated item ([official U.S. Reports record on GovInfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USREPORTS-519/USREPORTS-519-172); [full opinion text](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/519/172/)). The Court also cautioned that two items going to the same point do not automatically imply that only one may be used; narrative richness and distinct evidentiary force can matter. That qualification is central to a faithful transfer.

Rule 611 supplies the process authority around that balance. It tells the court to exercise reasonable control over the mode and order of presenting evidence so that truth determination is effective, time is not wasted, and witnesses are protected ([FRE 611](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_611)). Thus the legal mechanism is neither “accept every relevant item” nor “allow N witnesses.” It is supervised, context-sensitive marginal admission.

### 1.2 Sequestration protects the independence that makes corroboration valuable

Rule 615(a) says that, at a party's request, the court must exclude witnesses so they cannot hear other witnesses' testimony, subject to listed exceptions. Rule 615(b), added in 2023, makes clear that a court may also prohibit disclosure of or access to trial testimony outside the courtroom ([FRE 615](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_615); [official 2023 amendment package](https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2023_congressional_package_april_24_2023_0.pdf)).

The field's rationale is explicit. The original Advisory Committee note says sequestration has long been used to discourage and expose fabrication, inaccuracy, and collusion. The 2023 note says the core purpose is to prevent witnesses from tailoring testimony to trial evidence, and recognizes that merely moving exposure outside the courtroom would defeat that purpose ([FRE 615 Committee Notes](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_615)). The Supreme Court likewise described broad power to sequester nonparty witnesses to restrain tailoring and aid detection of less-than-candid testimony in *Geders v. United States*, 425 U.S. 80 (1976) ([opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/425/80)).

This is more than a courtroom etiquette rule. It preserves **diagnostic disagreement**. If later witnesses can conform to the first account, agreement ceases to reveal whether multiple observers independently reached the same account. Sequestration does not guarantee honesty or independence—witnesses may share prior influences—but it blocks one concrete contamination path and makes inconsistencies available to the factfinder.

### 1.3 Hearsay-within-hearsay is a lineage rule, not a ban on secondhand information

The hearsay rules address a different failure: an assertion may arrive through layers whose declarants cannot be tested. Rule 805 provides that hearsay within hearsay escapes exclusion only if **each layer** satisfies an exception ([FRE 805 in Article VIII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/28a/courtrules-Evid/article-VIII)). Rule 806 allows the credibility of an admitted hearsay declarant to be attacked as though that declarant had testified ([same Article VIII source](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/28a/courtrules-Evid/article-VIII)). The Article VIII Advisory Committee material explains the classic concerns in terms of perception, memory, narration, and the inability to cross-examine, while recognizing exceptions with circumstantial guarantees of trustworthiness ([FRE Article VIII and notes](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/article_VIII)).

The usable abstraction is: **do not flatten a derived assertion into a source count**. “Worker B reports that Worker A found X” is not a second observation of X. It is another transport layer over A's assertion. Each layer needs provenance, and credibility attacks against the underlying source must propagate to dependent claims.

This is deliberately not a literal import of the legal hearsay exclusions. Agent research often must rely on summaries, generated transformations, API outputs, or secondary authorities. The valuable part is the lineage accounting: derived claims cannot silently become independent corroboration.

### 1.4 Authentication and chain of custody answer “is this the item claimed?”, not “is it true?”

Rule 901(a) requires sufficient evidence to support a finding that an item is what the proponent claims. Its examples include testimony from a knowledgeable witness, distinctive characteristics, and evidence describing a process or system and showing that it produces an accurate result ([FRE 901](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_901)). The notes specifically discuss custody and computer-generated results.

That distinction prevents an overclaim in the translation. A complete log showing which agent emitted a claim, from which prompt and source, authenticates lineage. It does **not** prove the claim's truth. Chain-of-custody metadata is therefore a prerequisite for assessing independence and reproducibility, not a confidence score.

### 1.5 Burdens and standards of proof allocate error risk by consequence

Standards of proof are not worker quotas. They express how decisively the factfinder must be persuaded, with the demanded level changing according to the social cost of error. *In re Winship*, 397 U.S. 358 (1970), requires proof beyond a reasonable doubt for every fact necessary to juvenile criminal liability and connects that standard to reducing erroneous convictions ([opinion and syllabus](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/397/358/)). *Addington v. Texas*, 441 U.S. 418 (1979), adopts clear and convincing evidence for involuntary civil commitment; the opinion explains that a preponderance standard ordinarily allocates error risk roughly equally, while heightened standards reflect more important interests ([official U.S. Reports PDF](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-441/pdf/USREPORTS-441-418.pdf)).

The transferable mechanism is consequence-sensitive **burden selection**: a reversible formatting choice does not deserve the same evidence budget as deleting data, spending money, or making a public accusation. The non-transferable temptation is turning verbal legal standards into fake numeric probabilities. *Victor v. Nebraska*, 511 U.S. 1 (1994), notes that reasonable doubt defies easy explication and does not require a particular formula of words ([opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/92-8894.ZO.html)). Nothing in these authorities licenses mapping “beyond reasonable doubt” to, for example, 95% model confidence.

## 2. Concrete translation: the Evidence-Diversity Gate

### Proposal A — sequestered first-pass fan-out — UNVERIFIED; experiment X077-E1 would settle outcome benefit

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only).**

For any fan-out whose outputs will be treated as corroborating votes, give each first-pass worker:

- the same task record and admissible source package;
- a distinct investigative question or method when diversity is desired;
- no peer answer, synthesis, vote tally, or “most workers think” message;
- an output contract containing claims, source locators, observation method, material uncertainties, and falsifiers.

Workers may see one another after first-pass sealing, during an explicit adversarial or synthesis stage. The rule is not “agents must never communicate.” It is “do not call post-contact convergence independent corroboration.”

**Expected catch:** false consensus produced when later workers anchor on the earliest answer; cosmetic paraphrases counted as separate findings; a single hallucinated citation spreading across the pool; and dissent erased before the orchestrator sees it.

It is **very likely (80-90%)** that this configuration preserves more diagnostic disagreement than sequentially showing every worker the current draft. Evidence: Rule 615's stated purpose is precisely to prevent earlier testimony from shaping later testimony ([FRE 615 notes](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_615)), and no additional inference is required to see that hidden peer outputs remove that direct exposure path. Whether this improves final task accuracy rather than merely increasing disagreement is **UNVERIFIED; experiment X077-E1 (defined in §6) would settle it**.

### Proposal B — claim/source lineage envelope — UNVERIFIED; experiment X077-E2 would settle gate accuracy

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

Require every fan-out result to emit a small sidecar envelope:

```json
{
  "worker_id": "w3",
  "sealed_first_pass": true,
  "claims": [
    {
      "claim_id": "c17",
      "proposition": "...",
      "disposition_impact": "blocking|material|context",
      "basis": [
        {"kind": "primary_source|tool_observation|peer_claim|inference",
         "locator": "...", "observed_by": "w3", "parent_claim": null}
      ],
      "falsifier": "...",
      "status": "support|contradict|uncertain"
    }
  ]
}
```

The gate hashes or normalizes locators only to find shared lineage; it does not pretend that text similarity proves dependence. A claim based on `peer_claim: c17` inherits c17's lineage and cannot increase the independent-source count. Two workers citing the same underlying report are one source family for corroboration, even if one encountered it through a different web page. Two workers applying genuinely different tests to the same artifact can be separate observation methods even though the target is shared.

The gate should retain all raw outputs. “Excluded as cumulative” means “not grounds for spawning or confidence multiplication,” not deletion from the audit record.

**Expected catch:** hearsay cascades (“three agents say the blog says…”), circular citation, same API response treated as independent confirmation, summaries that obscure the primary source, and untraceable claims that win solely through repetition.

It is **likely (55-80%)** that a compact envelope catches materially misleading consensus at acceptable overhead. Evidence: Rule 805 demonstrates why layered assertions require layer-by-layer treatment ([FRE 805](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/28a/courtrules-Evid/article-VIII)); Rule 901 supplies a process/authentication analogue ([FRE 901](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_901)). The wide confidence band reflects that workers may omit or misclassify lineage and that URL identity is only a weak proxy for causal dependence. The net value is **UNVERIFIED; experiment X077-E2 would settle it**.

### Proposal C — marginal-evidence launch/stop gate — UNVERIFIED; experiment X077-E3 would settle the threshold

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

After each sealed batch, compute a qualitative evidence delta for each unresolved proposition. A worker is **non-cumulative** if it contributes at least one of:

1. a new material proposition that could change the disposition;
2. an independently originated primary source or direct tool observation bearing on a material proposition;
3. a genuinely different test or reasoning method with a recorded falsifier;
4. a contradiction that changes uncertainty or exposes an unresolved branch;
5. coverage of a predeclared risk class not yet tested (for example, destructive side effects, permissions, backward compatibility, or adversarial input).

A worker is **cumulative for launch purposes** when it only restates already-supported propositions from the same source lineage and supplies no new test, contradiction, or risk coverage.

The orchestrator then follows this state machine:

```text
classify consequence / set burden
             |
launch smallest sealed batch justified by task decomposition
             |
admit claim envelopes + build provenance families
             |
material contradiction or uncovered risk? --yes--> launch TARGETED worker/test
             | no                                  (sealed from conclusion)
new independent decision-relevant delta? --yes--> update record; reassess
             | no
burden met and no blocking contradiction? --yes--> stop fan-out; synthesize
             | no
can name evidence that another worker could obtain? --yes--> launch that worker
             | no
stop and report unresolved uncertainty (do not buy generic votes)
```

This is where Rule 403 transfers most directly. The gate assesses the next worker against the **full current record**, just as *Old Chief* permits evidentiary alternatives to affect probative-value analysis ([*Old Chief*, GovInfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USREPORTS-519/USREPORTS-519-172)). It also preserves *Old Chief*'s warning: same-topic evidence can remain valuable when it adds distinct force. A new independent reproduction is not “needlessly cumulative” merely because it reaches the same result.

Initial parameters should be deliberately modest:

- one worker for low-consequence, easily reversible work with direct verification;
- two sealed workers when independent corroboration is actually useful;
- additional workers only by a named evidence request, not by a fixed ensemble size;
- one adversarial worker when the consequence class calls for testing a leading hypothesis;
- stop after a batch adds no material independent delta and no named missing evidence remains.

These numeric defaults are **UNVERIFIED; experiment X077-E3 would settle them**. It is **roughly even (45-55%)** that “two sealed workers” is the best default across this owner's task distribution. Evidence for two is only structural—one result cannot reveal disagreement, while two can—and there is no measured local task distribution in this IDEATION unit. The mechanism does not depend on two being optimal; the number belongs in configuration and must be calibrated.

**Expected catch:** runaway parallelism after evidentiary saturation; majority voting among correlated clones; adding generalists when the missing item is a specific execution test; premature stopping when a minority worker supplies unique primary evidence; and the opposite failure, repeatedly paying for agreement without narrowing a live uncertainty.

### Proposal D — consequence-sensitive burdens, expressed as required evidence classes — UNVERIFIED; experiment X077-E4 would settle class boundaries

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only).**

Define burdens operationally, without legal labels or pseudo-probabilities:

| Harness consequence class | Required evidence before action | Parallelism implication |
|---|---|---|
| Low: reversible, local, cheaply tested | One direct observation or deterministic test | Usually no corroborating fan-out |
| Medium: broad code/config effect but recoverable | Direct test plus source review or independent reproduction; resolve material contradiction | Sealed pair only if methods can differ |
| High: destructive, externally visible, costly, security/legal/safety-sensitive | Authenticated target; direct test where possible; independent provenance family; adversarial check; explicit residual uncertainty | Add targeted workers until each evidence class is filled, or stop without action |

This borrows the error-allocation principle of *Winship* and *Addington*, not their doctrinal names or verbal thresholds ([*Winship*](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/397/358/); [*Addington*, official U.S. Reports](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-441/pdf/USREPORTS-441-418.pdf)). The burden is satisfied by **kinds of evidence**, not agent confidence values or vote shares.

It is **likely (55-80%)** that evidence-class burdens allocate worker effort better than one global maximum-agent setting. Evidence: the Supreme Court authorities expressly vary proof demands with the consequence and allocation of error; the harness mapping follows the same structure. The exact class boundaries and required evidence are **UNVERIFIED; experiment X077-E4, a retrospective cost/error simulation, would settle them**.

### Proposal E — provenance-aware synthesizer instruction — UNVERIFIED; experiment X077-E2 would settle synthesis impact

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only).**

The synthesizer receives all sealed ledgers and the provenance-family grouping. Its instruction should say:

> Count reasons, sources, and tests—not workers. Agreement sharing a source lineage is one corroborative family. Preserve a uniquely sourced dissent until its source or test is resolved. A trace proves origin, not truth. State which burden elements are satisfied and which remain open.

This inexpensive instruction is the part most directly supported by the hearsay/authentication distinctions. It is **likely (55-80%)** to prevent at least some false vote-counting when lineage metadata is accurate. Evidence: Rule 805 refuses to treat a layered statement as though its layers disappear, while Rule 901 distinguishes identity/process foundation from substantive truth ([FRE 805](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/28a/courtrules-Evid/article-VIII); [FRE 901](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_901)). ## 3. What this catches that naïve orchestration misses

This section describes failure modes of a generic naïve orchestrator, not measured defects in the owner's current harness.

### 3.1 Correlated consensus masquerading as confidence

Suppose six workers all cite the same secondary article, or workers two through six see worker one's answer. A raw 6–0 vote looks strong. The evidence model represents it as one source family plus five dependent transmissions. If one worker independently executes the artifact and contradicts the article, that minority result can carry greater decision relevance than the five echoes.

**Confidence:** **very likely (80-90%)** that lineage-aware counting ranks this case more sensibly than raw majority voting. Evidence: the conclusion follows from the constructed example's stipulated dependence, and Rule 615/805 supply the independently developed mechanisms for contamination and layered assertions. This does not establish the frequency of the case in real harness operation.

### 3.2 More workers launched when only a specific experiment can help

If three code reviewers agree that a race is possible but none runs a concurrency test, more generic reviewers are cumulative. The gate can name the unmet evidence class—direct reproduction—and launch a test worker. If no environment can run the test, it stops and reports the gap rather than manufacturing certainty through votes.

**Confidence:** **likely (55-80%)** that a named-evidence launch rule reduces redundant generalist fan-out. Evidence: Rule 403 explicitly balances probative value against cumulative presentation and waste; the proposed rule makes the missing evidence type explicit. Frequency and savings are **UNVERIFIED; experiment X077-E3 would settle them**.

### 3.3 A unique dissent discarded because it loses the vote

Five workers reason from documentation; one worker inspects actual runtime output and finds a version mismatch. The latter supplies a new observation method and material contradiction. It survives the cumulative gate even if its conclusion is unpopular. The next worker is tasked with reproducing the mismatch, not voting on which prose sounds better.

**Confidence:** **very likely (80-90%)** that this rule preserves the dissent in the stipulated example, because preservation is an explicit gate condition. Whether real workers correctly label the contradiction is **UNVERIFIED; experiment X077-E2 would settle extraction reliability**.

### 3.4 Provenance mistaken for truth

A perfect chain from agent to tool response proves what response was received and by whom. It does not prove the server was correct or the interpretation valid. Separating Rule 901-style authentication from truth forces a later direct check or source evaluation where consequence warrants it.

**Confidence:** **almost certain (90-99%)** that this conceptual distinction is valid. Evidence: Rule 901's operative question is whether the item is what the proponent claims, while substantive admissibility and weight remain separate inquiries ([FRE 901](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_901)).

### 3.5 Premature stopping after superficial duplication

Two workers can reach the same conclusion with different evidentiary force: one repeats documentation, while another independently reproduces behavior. A text-deduplication stop rule may see duplicate answers. An evidentiary gate sees a new observation method and admits it as non-cumulative.

**Confidence:** **likely (55-80%)** that explicit basis types outperform answer-text similarity on such cases. Evidence: *Old Chief* says same-point items need not be treated as interchangeable and directs attention to evidentiary context ([opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/519/172/)). The band is not higher because basis labels may be inaccurate.

## 4. Adversarial analysis: where the analogy breaks

### 4.1 A court optimizes legitimacy and fairness, not token-normalized answer accuracy

Rule 403 contains dangers—unfair prejudice, confusing or misleading a jury—that have no clean equivalent in most agent workflows. It also says exclusion is permitted when dangers **substantially outweigh** probative value, an admissibility-favoring formulation. A harness may rationally stop when marginal expected value merely falls below marginal cost. Copying the legal asymmetry would overspend.

**Consequence:** transfer “evaluate marginal information in context,” but do not copy Rule 403's legal weighting or remedies.

### 4.2 Witnesses have separately situated perception; cloned workers often do not

Human witnesses may observe an event from different physical locations and possess different memories. Parallel agents often share model weights, system instructions, task prompt, context, search engine, and benchmark-induced priors. Sequestration blocks direct peer anchoring but does not create causal independence. Two isolated workers can still make the same systematic error.

**Confidence:** **very likely (80-90%)** that sequestration alone is insufficient to establish agent independence. Evidence: Rule 615 only regulates access to others' testimony and lists its purpose accordingly; it does not authenticate the underlying observation or guarantee unrelated prior influences ([FRE 615](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_615)). In the target domain, shared model and prompt are stipulated features of typical same-harness fan-out, but their measured effect here is not locally grounded.

**Design response:** record model/prompt/tool/source families and deliberately vary investigative method. Call the result “less contaminated,” not statistically independent, unless an experiment establishes independence.

### 4.3 Cross-examination has incentives and powers agents lack

Legal cross-examination occurs in an adversarial institution, under oath, before a factfinder, with sanctions and procedural rights. Asking another agent to “critique” an answer is not equivalent: both may share priors, neither bears a cost for evasion, and the critic may be anchored by the draft.

**Consequence:** an adversarial worker is a different test method, not a legal cross-examiner. It should receive a proposition and falsification task, not merely the leading draft, whenever preserving independence matters.

### 4.4 Hearsay rules embody confrontation and policy choices, not pure epistemology

The hearsay regime has many exceptions based on necessity, practice, and policy. It cannot be reduced to “secondary source bad.” Agent workflows often need secondary materials, and a well-produced synthesis may be more usable than raw data. Literal exclusion would discard useful information.

**Consequence:** borrow lineage propagation and credibility inheritance, not categorical inadmissibility.

### 4.5 Standards of proof are not probabilities and do not specify sample sizes

Legal verbal standards allocate error risk but do not tell an orchestrator how many identically distributed workers make a fact “clear and convincing.” *Victor* expressly treats reasonable doubt as resistant to formulaic explication ([*Victor*](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/92-8894.ZO.html)). Agent confidence scores are commonly uncalibrated for a particular task distribution; this unit has no calibration evidence.

**Consequence:** define burdens as checkable evidence classes and tests. Do not label a 3–0 vote “beyond reasonable doubt” or convert it to 99.9%.

### 4.6 Trials usually admit or exclude; a harness can retain and discount

A trial must manage what reaches a jury and the time consumed in presentation. A harness can cheaply retain all outputs while declining to spawn successors or multiply confidence. Literal “exclusion” would damage auditability.

**Consequence:** use two planes: an immutable evidence log and a decision/launch plane. Cumulative material remains inspectable but has zero or discounted marginal launch weight.

### 4.7 The decision maker can itself become the correlated bottleneck

Evidence doctrine assumes a judge applies the gate and a factfinder weighs admitted proof. In an agent harness, the gatekeeper/synthesizer may itself be an LLM sharing the workers' biases. A sophisticated ledger cannot rescue a gatekeeper that misidentifies material claims.

**Confidence:** **likely (55-80%)** that extraction/gating error is the design's dominant new failure mode. Evidence: the gate requires semantic judgments about materiality, common lineage, and contradiction; these are not resolved by the legal sources or deterministic hashing. This ranking is **UNVERIFIED; experiment X077-E2 would settle it by scoring gate decisions against human labels**.

## 5. Decision — UNVERIFIED pending X077-E1–E4: adopt the structure, reject courtroom theater

The analogy survives because the structural match is real:

| Evidence-law problem | Orchestration problem | Valid transfer |
|---|---|---|
| Later testimony conforms to earlier testimony | Later worker anchors on peer answer | Seal first passes / label post-contact agreement |
| Many statements derive from one declarant | Many worker claims derive from one source/output | Propagate lineage; count source families |
| More relevant proof becomes needlessly cumulative | Extra workers repeat established claims | Gate on marginal decision-relevant delta |
| Consequences require different error allocation | Actions have different reversibility and harm | Require consequence-specific evidence classes |
| Authentication proves identity/process, not truth | Logs prove provenance, not correctness | Separate custody metadata from validation |

The analogy stops before jury voting, legal exclusion, numerical translations of burdens, or the claim that sequestration creates independence.

**Overall recommendation:** implement the envelope and decision rule as a small pilot on the existing orchestration bus, with all thresholds configurable. Do not build a new runtime.

It does not introduce a second orchestration runtime, vector database/knowledge graph, durable-execution engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, or guardrail framework. State can be per-run JSON and discarded or archived with the run. The gate should default to no intervention for single-worker tasks, preserving the deliberately minimal path.

It is **likely (55-80%)** that this small pilot is worth building. ## 6. Experiments required before promotion

### X077-E1 — sequestration ablation

Sample fan-out tasks with answers that can be independently adjudicated. Randomize among:

1. workers see the current leading answer;
2. workers are sealed until first-pass submission;
3. sealed workers also receive method-diverse assignments.

Measure final accuracy, pairwise error correlation, unique material contradictions, citation/source diversity, tokens, latency, and rate at which correct minority findings survive synthesis. Use identical task samples and adjudication across arms.

**Settlement criterion:** promote sealed first passes if they reduce pairwise shared-error rate or improve final accuracy at an acceptable cost, not merely if they increase textual diversity.

### X077-E2 — lineage-gate accuracy

Build a labeled set of worker bundles containing:

- same source through different URLs;
- peer-derived claims;
- independent execution reproductions;
- same conclusion from distinct tests;
- unique-source dissent;
- fabricated or unreachable citations.

Have the small gate classify source families, hearsay layers, contradictions, and material novelty. Compare against two human labels, adjudicating disagreements. Measure precision/recall separately for “cumulative” and “material dissent,” because a false cumulative label can suppress valuable evidence.

**Settlement criterion:** do not auto-stop on semantic classifications until false-cumulative errors are acceptably low; run advisory-only otherwise.

### X077-E3 — marginal worker-count replay

On historical or newly collected fan-out traces with correctness labels, replay after each worker and ask whether the next result changed:

- final correctness;
- a material proposition;
- an independent source family;
- an unresolved contradiction;
- risk-class coverage.

Compare fixed N, current policy, “stop after no delta,” and targeted-next-evidence policies on accuracy, tokens, wall time, and severe-error recall. Bootstrap by task, not by worker output, because outputs within a task are correlated.

**Settlement criterion:** select thresholds from the Pareto frontier; do not collapse high-consequence severe-error recall into average accuracy.

### X077-E4 — consequence/burden retrospective

Blindly label task consequences before viewing outcomes. Simulate each proposed burden using recorded evidence availability. Measure false-action and false-abstention costs by consequence class. Review misclassifications, especially allegedly low-consequence tasks that transitively affect external or destructive operations.

**Settlement criterion:** adopt only evidence-class requirements that reduce consequence-weighted loss relative to a single global policy.

## 7. Rejected translations and dead ends

### Rejected: “twelve agents make a jury”

Jury size is an institutional choice shaped by constitutional and procedural law, not an optimal ensemble size for correlated model replicas. It says nothing about token cost, model error dependence, or tool diversity. No proposal.

### Rejected: majority vote as burden of proof

A 9–1 vote can rest on one repeated error; a 1–9 dissent can contain the only direct observation. Legal proof standards govern persuasion and error allocation, not raw witness counts. The design counts evidence families and tests instead.

### Rejected: convert legal standards to 51%, 75%, and 95%

The examined authorities do not authorize these numerical conversions, and *Victor* emphasizes the difficulty of formulaically defining reasonable doubt ([opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/92-8894.ZO.html)). Such numbers would create false precision.

### Rejected: a permanent evidence knowledge graph

The task needs per-run provenance edges, which ordinary JSON can represent. A durable vector database or knowledge graph would violate the anti-overfit gate and add a second memory system without evidence of benefit.

### Rejected: always isolate every worker

Collaboration and iterative review can be valuable. Isolation is required only during the phase whose agreement will be counted as independent corroboration. After first-pass sealing, explicit synthesis, rebuttal, and targeted peer review are allowed and should be labeled as dependent stages.

### Rejected: automatic deletion of cumulative output

Legal exclusion manages what the factfinder receives; harness auditability benefits from retaining the raw record. The gate discounts decision weight and stops spending, while preserving output.

### Rejected: chain of custody as a truth score

Authentication establishes that an item/process is what it purports to be under Rule 901; it does not establish that the substantive assertion is correct ([FRE 901](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_901)). Provenance is necessary metadata, not correctness.

## 8. Evidence quality and gaps

The principal legal mechanisms are supported by primary or quasi-primary sources: the Judiciary's official Federal Rules publications, official U.S. Reports on GovInfo, and full Supreme Court opinions. Cornell LII is used for accessible current rule text and Committee Notes; each central rule is also anchored to the Judiciary's official rules page or amendment package.

**GAP — local prevalence:** Per unit instruction, no local repository or corpus grounding was performed. Therefore this artifact makes no claim that raw voting, peer-answer exposure, or redundant fan-out exists in the owner's current harness. Experiment X077-E3 would establish prevalence and cost.

**GAP — independence metric:** Provenance families are a causal bookkeeping approximation, not proof of statistical independence. Experiment X077-E1 would estimate pairwise shared-error behavior under actual models and tools.

**GAP — semantic gate reliability:** No evidence here establishes that an LLM or a small deterministic/LLM hybrid can reliably classify “material novelty.” Experiment X077-E2 would settle that premise. Until then, the gate should be advisory-only for high-consequence action.

**GAP — universal law survey:** This unit used U.S. federal evidence law as the source system. It does not claim that every jurisdiction handles cumulative evidence, sequestration, hearsay, authentication, or burdens identically. The target design needs only the cited mechanism, not a universal comparative-law proposition.

## 9. Bottom line for the neighboring build unit

Build a small **Evidence-Diversity Gate**, not a courtroom simulator:

1. seal first-pass workers when their agreement will count;
2. require claim/source/test/falsifier envelopes;
3. group dependent assertions into provenance families;
4. preserve unique contradictions and independent tests;
5. launch only a worker that can obtain a named missing evidence class;
6. stop when the last batch is cumulative and the consequence-specific burden is met—or stop without action when the burden cannot be met;
7. retain all raw outputs for audit;
8. calibrate the numeric defaults by X077-E1–E4.

The courtroom's numbers, burdens, exclusions, and institutional assumptions do not.
