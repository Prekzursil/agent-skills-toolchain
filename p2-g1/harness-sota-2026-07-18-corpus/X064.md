## Executive judgment

The useful borrow is **evaluated case surveillance**, not “memory as disease.” Public-health surveillance separates (1) a deliberately defined case, (2) noisy routes by which possible cases enter a registry, (3) classifications such as suspected/probable/confirmed, (4) active and passive case finding, and (5) recurring evaluation of sensitivity, predictive value, timeliness, representativeness, and data quality. That structure transfers to memory because an agent also observes noisy evidence about a latent proposition, must choose which observations merit operational use, and pays different costs for misses and false admissions.

**Transfer verdict:** **very likely (80-90%)** that this mechanism transfers usefully as a lightweight memory admission, revalidation, and evaluation protocol. Evidence: CDC explicitly treats a surveillance system as a pipeline whose case definition, reporting channels, data quality, sensitivity, positive predictive value, timeliness, and stability must be evaluated together; it also warns that improving one attribute can harm another ([CDC surveillance-system evaluation guideline](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5013a1.htm)). The structural match is strong at that pipeline level. It is not stronger than 90% because “useful memory” is task-relative and partly normative, whereas whether a person met a disease case definition at a given time is more nearly observer-independent.

**What does not transfer:** epidemiology does **not** justify a universal memory expiration interval, a single scalar confidence score, contagion/outbreak machinery, or automatic deletion of old records. **almost certain (90-99%)** that importing those elements literally would be a category error. Evidence: surveillance definitions are condition- and purpose-specific, can include person/place/time, and are distinct from ordinary clinical diagnosis ([WHO IHR glossary](https://ihrbenchmark.who.int/document/glossary)); CDC states that collection methods and cadence depend on the surveillance objective and the natural history of the condition ([CDC, Lesson 5 §4](https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/csels/dsepd/ss1978/lesson5/section4.html)). Agent-memory utility similarly varies by memory class and decision context, but has no biological natural history from which a universal TTL could be inferred.

The resulting recommendation is deliberately small: a versioned memory “case definition,” a quarantine/promote/revalidate state machine on the existing hook/skill bus, and a sampled audit. It adds no second orchestrator, vector database, knowledge graph, durable-execution engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, or guardrail framework.

## 1. The epidemiological mechanism in its own field

### 1.1 Surveillance is an operating loop, not a bag of records

CDC describes case surveillance as collection of information by public-health agencies about cases, followed by standardized reporting and use of those data to locate, monitor, and control health threats. Standard case definitions make reports comparable across sources and jurisdictions ([CDC, “What is Case Surveillance?”](https://www.cdc.gov/nndss/what-is-case-surveillance/index.html)). CDC’s teaching material is especially explicit that surveillance is the continued activity of watching a condition using collected data, not merely the data system or stored records ([CDC, Lesson 5 §4](https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/csels/dsepd/ss1978/lesson5/section4.html)).

This distinction matters. A registry can be perfectly durable while the surveillance process is insensitive, delayed, biased, or flooded with false positives. The field therefore evaluates the whole ascertainment loop.

### 1.2 A case definition makes inclusion reproducible

WHO defines a surveillance case definition as diagnostic criteria used to regard an individual as a case for surveillance/outbreak-investigation purposes; criteria can combine clinical and laboratory evidence with person, place, and time. WHO also notes that a surveillance definition need not be the ordinary clinical definition ([WHO IHR glossary](https://ihrbenchmark.who.int/document/glossary)). CDC likewise says a standard definition improves specificity and comparability and recommends using an established definition or explaining why one is not used ([CDC surveillance-system evaluation guideline](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5013a1.htm)).

Definitions can be tiered rather than binary. WHO’s yellow-fever example distinguishes suspected, probable, and confirmed cases using progressively stronger clinical, epidemiological, and laboratory evidence ([WHO yellow-fever surveillance case definitions](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/case-definitions-for-public-health-surveillance)). This lets a system notice weak signals early without pretending that every signal is confirmed.

Definitions are also versioned in practice. CDC’s hepatitis guidance says changes may be needed after clinical, laboratory, or surveillance-method advances, warns that observed trends can be affected by definition changes, and points to current and historical definitions plus implementation timelines ([CDC viral-hepatitis surveillance guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis/php/surveillance-guidance/general-surveillance.html)). Thus, a reclassification caused by a new definition is not silently treated as a change in the underlying disease.

### 1.3 Sensitivity, specificity, and positive predictive value answer different questions

For surveillance:

- **Sensitivity** asks what proportion of actual cases the system detects; CDC also applies it to outbreak detection.
- **Specificity** asks how well noncases are excluded by the ascertainment/definition process.
- **Predictive value positive (PVP/PPV)** asks what proportion of reported cases truly have the event.

CDC emphasizes that PPV depends jointly on the case definition’s sensitivity and specificity **and the prevalence of the event in the population under surveillance**. Low PPV wastes investigation effort and can create pseudo-outbreaks ([CDC surveillance-system evaluation guideline](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5013a1.htm)).

The base-rate consequence is mechanical. If only 1% of observations contain a genuinely durable fact, a screen with 90% sensitivity and 95% specificity has:

```text
PPV = (0.90 × 0.01) / ((0.90 × 0.01) + (0.05 × 0.99)) = 15.4%
```

So even a superficially good detector would make about 85% of its positive candidates false admissions in that low-prevalence stream. This is why “the model sounded confident” or “the fact appeared once” cannot by itself be a safe promotion rule.

The sensitivity/specificity trade-off is empirical, not merely textbook. During a 2016 Singapore Zika outbreak, one evaluated definition achieved 100% sensitivity but only 2% specificity and 42% PPV; other definitions traded lower sensitivity for much higher specificity ([Chow et al., *Emerging Infectious Diseases*, Table 2](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/23/4/16-1716-t2), [article and DOI](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/23/4/16-1716_article)). The lesson is not to reuse those numbers; it is to measure the operating point rather than label a definition “strict” or “good” in the abstract.

### 1.4 Passive, active, and sentinel surveillance cover different blind spots

CDC defines passive surveillance as provider-initiated reporting under known rules and active surveillance as health-department-initiated solicitation of reports, often for a bounded period or outbreak. It describes sentinel reporting as a stable, prearranged sample of providers committed to reporting all cases of selected conditions; such a sample may not represent the whole population, but can provide consistent high-quality reporting over time. Registries can also contain an active component that periodically updates records ([CDC, Lesson 5 §4](https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/csels/dsepd/ss1978/lesson5/section4.html)).

The field does not claim that one mode dominates. Passive reporting is cheap but can underreport; active finding is more complete but resource-intensive; sentinel sites offer a stable trend signal but may be unrepresentative. CDC accordingly says the source and method should follow the surveillance objective and that primary collection is warranted only for a high-priority problem with no adequate existing source ([CDC, Lesson 5 §4](https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/csels/dsepd/ss1978/lesson5/section4.html)).

### 1.5 Surveillance systems themselves are periodically evaluated

CDC’s evaluation framework includes usefulness, simplicity, flexibility, data quality, acceptability, sensitivity, PPV, representativeness, timeliness, and stability. It explicitly notes trade-offs: higher sensitivity can lower PPV; added requirements can reduce simplicity, timeliness, flexibility, or acceptability; improvements cost resources ([CDC surveillance-system evaluation guideline](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5013a1.htm)). This is the most valuable part of the borrow: the memory mechanism must be audited as an ascertainment system, not judged by how sophisticated its schema looks.

## 2. Translation: from case surveillance to memory surveillance

The mapping is structural, not rhetorical:

| Epidemiology | Agent-memory equivalent | Concrete implication |
|---|---|---|
| Condition under surveillance | A defined class of cross-session fact: owner preference, environment fact, project rule, episodic event, or inferred heuristic | Do not use one admission rule for every memory class. |
| Person/place/time criteria | Subject/scope/time criteria | Every operational memory states whom/what it concerns, where it applies, and when it was observed or last validated. |
| Suspected/probable/confirmed case | Candidate/provisional/verified memory | Weak signals enter quarantine; only stronger classes enter default retrieval. |
| Laboratory/reference confirmation | Owner assertion, authoritative artifact, or live environment probe appropriate to the claim | Verification method depends on claim class; two model paraphrases are not independent confirmation. |
| Passive reporting | Contradiction, tool failure, owner correction, or changed-file event automatically reports possible staleness | Cheap broad coverage, but absence of a report is not evidence of currency. |
| Active surveillance | Recheck a high-impact memory immediately before consequential use | Spend validation cost where stale use would hurt. |
| Sentinel surveillance | Regularly audit a fixed, stratified sample of memories and decisions | Detect drift trends without rescanning the full store. |
| Case-definition version | Memory-policy version | Reclassify records when the policy changes and distinguish policy drift from world drift. |
| Sensitivity | Fraction of genuinely useful durable facts captured and available when needed | Measures forgetting/under-capture. |
| Specificity | Fraction of nondurable, incorrect, or out-of-scope observations kept out of operational memory | Measures resistance to memory pollution. |
| PPV | Fraction of promoted/retrieved memories that are correct, current, scoped, and useful at the decision where retrieved | Measures whether memory retrieval deserves trust. |
| Timeliness | Delay from fact/change to capture, revalidation, quarantine, or retirement | Measures stale-exposure window. |
| Registry history | Append-only provenance, supersession, and reclassification history | “Stop retrieving” is distinct from “erase all evidence,” except where privacy/deletion requires erasure. |

The crucial translation is **two-stage ascertainment**:

1. Optimize the candidate lane for sensitivity. Capture explicit owner statements, repeated observations, corrections, and high-impact inferred facts into a non-operational quarantine.
2. Optimize the operational lane for PPV. Promote only when the class-specific definition is met; attach scope, provenance, observation time, validation time, and invalidation triggers.

That separation preserves the ability to notice a potentially useful fact without letting an unverified inference silently steer future sessions.

## 3. Concrete minimal design

### Proposal A — versioned memory case definitions **[UNVERIFIED — settle with the “definition replay” experiment in §5]**

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only)**

Define a small policy table by memory class. The exact thresholds below are hypotheses, not established facts about this harness. **likely (55-80%)** that class-specific rules outperform one global confidence threshold; evidence is WHO/CDC’s condition- and purpose-specific definitions and the sharp empirical differences among Zika definitions. **UNVERIFIED:** settle by replaying the same labeled decisions under a global rule and the class-specific rules, comparing PPV, sensitivity, and owner-correction burden.

| Memory class | Candidate definition | Promotion/reference standard | Revalidation and retirement rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner identity or durable preference | Direct owner statement, with exact provenance | Direct owner statement is primary evidence; inferred preference remains provisional | Recheck only on contradiction or explicit change; an explicit “forget” bypasses surveillance and invokes deletion policy. |
| Project/repository rule | Rule found in an authoritative scoped instruction artifact, or direct owner statement | Current authoritative artifact plus scope | Invalidate on artifact change, branch/worktree change where relevant, or conflict with a higher-priority instruction. |
| Environment/capability fact | Successful live observation or authoritative configuration | Current live probe suitable to the capability; record platform and scope | Revalidate before high-impact use after environment/version change; quarantine on contrary probe. |
| Episodic fact (“X happened”) | Tool/session evidence | Immutable event provenance | Event itself does not become false with age; its present relevance decays. Supersede interpretations, not the historical event. |
| Inferred heuristic/workaround | Repeated successful use plus explicit conditions | At least two mechanically independent success signals or owner confirmation | Short review interval; retire on contradictory outcome or changed dependency. Never present as an owner preference. |
| Secret, credential, transient token | Any observation | **Excluded from memory** | Do not store. Existing data follows security/deletion policy, not a freshness score. |

Every admitted record should minimally contain:

```text
memory_id
claim
class
subject_and_scope
observed_at
source_locator
source_kind
case_status = candidate | provisional | verified
definition_version
last_verified_at
verification_method
invalidators[]
operational_status = quarantined | active | stale_pending_review | superseded | retired
supersedes / superseded_by
decision_impact = low | medium | high
```

Do **not** store a model-generated scalar such as `confidence: 0.87` unless it has been calibrated against labeled outcomes for that class. **very likely (80-90%)** that an uncalibrated scalar would create false precision rather than sound base-rate reasoning; evidence is that CDC defines PPV from measured operating characteristics and prevalence, while the proposed harness would initially have neither calibrated likelihood ratios nor representative prevalence estimates. The actionable status should come from reproducible criteria and provenance.

### Proposal B — quarantine/promote/revalidate hook **[UNVERIFIED — settle with the shadow-mode and canary experiments in §5]**

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus)**

Implement one small hook or skill entry point, not a service:

```text
observe candidate
    -> classify memory type and attach source/scope/time
    -> if exclusion rule: discard securely
    -> if promotion definition met: active
    -> else: quarantine

before retrieval-assisted high-impact action
    -> filter by exact scope
    -> if invalidator fired or review due: active revalidation
    -> on success: refresh validation timestamp
    -> on contradiction/failure: quarantine and surface both claim and evidence

after owner correction/tool contradiction
    -> passive stale report
    -> quarantine implicated record immediately
    -> link replacement rather than silently overwrite
```

No background daemon is necessary. Revalidation can be event-driven at retrieval, on known invalidators, and during a bounded sampled audit. **very likely (80-90%)** that this catches stale environment facts before consequential reuse when an authoritative low-cost probe exists; evidence is direct structural coverage: the record carries invalidators and the retrieval path performs active verification. **UNVERIFIED:** settle with fault injection that changes versions, paths, permissions, branches, or tool availability and measures stale-use escapes.

**likely (55-80%)** that it catches scope leakage, where a true fact from one repository, branch, machine, owner, or time is reused elsewhere; evidence is the direct analogue of person/place/time criteria. Confidence is lower because scope inference itself can be wrong. **UNVERIFIED:** settle with a cross-scope replay set containing same-named projects, different branches, and conflicting local rules.

**very likely (80-90%)** that it prevents single-observation model inferences from becoming authoritative owner preferences, because the candidate and operational lanes are mechanically separate. **UNVERIFIED:** settle with adversarial transcripts containing implicatures, sarcasm, temporary exceptions, and later corrections.

**likely (55-80%)** that the sensitive candidate lane recovers more genuinely durable preferences and workarounds than a high-specificity write gate alone; evidence is the established sensitivity/specificity trade-off. **UNVERIFIED:** settle with a blinded annotation of durable facts that were and were not later needed. It is not appropriate to infer this from correction logs alone because users tend to correct harmful retrieved memories, not invisible missed captures.

### Proposal C — passive reports plus a sentinel memory audit **[UNVERIFIED — settle with the stratified audit experiment in §5]**

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus)**

Use both:

- **Passive reports:** owner correction, contradictory tool output, failed action whose premise came from memory, authoritative-file change, version change, or explicit preference change. These immediately quarantine the implicated memory.
- **Sentinel audit:** at a fixed cadence, sample a stable panel from high-change/high-impact strata (environment facts and inferred workarounds), plus a random sample from all active records. Validate against the class-appropriate reference source. Keep panel membership stable enough to measure drift over time, and report separately because it is not necessarily representative.

Do not rescan every memory every session. **likely (55-80%)** that the combined passive-plus-sentinel design finds staleness earlier per unit cost than passive correction alone; evidence is CDC’s rationale for active and sentinel surveillance when passive reporting underreports. **UNVERIFIED:** settle by seeding known-stale records across strata and comparing detection delay, probe cost, and stale uses under passive-only versus combined surveillance.

The sentinel audit must not be advertised as the global stale-memory rate. CDC explicitly cautions that sentinel samples may not represent the whole population ([CDC, Lesson 5 §4](https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/csels/dsepd/ss1978/lesson5/section4.html)). A separate random audit sample is required for a population estimate.

### Proposal D — forgetting as operational retirement, with a privacy override **[UNVERIFIED — settle with owner-policy review and retrieval tests in §5]**

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only)**

Use four distinct operations:

1. **Supersede:** retain old claim and provenance, but only the replacement is operational.
2. **Retire:** remove from default retrieval because it is no longer useful or its scope ended.
3. **Quarantine:** temporarily block operational use pending verification.
4. **Delete:** erase content when explicitly requested or required by privacy/security policy; if policy permits, retain only an opaque deletion receipt, never the deleted content.

Age alone should trigger review or lower retrieval priority, not assert falsehood. An episodic fact remains historically true; an environment claim may become stale in minutes; a stable owner preference may remain valid for years. **very likely (80-90%)** that class- and invalidator-based retirement makes fewer semantic errors than a universal TTL; evidence is CDC’s objective/natural-history-dependent surveillance cadence and its preservation of historical case-definition timelines. **UNVERIFIED:** settle by comparing stale-use and wrongful-retirement rates across fixed TTL, class TTL, and event-driven review on a labeled replay.

Privacy is a hard disanalogy: public-health interest in longitudinal records does not override an owner’s right or instruction to forget. An explicit deletion request must not be converted into “retired but still archived.”

## 4. What this would catch—and what I cannot claim about the current harness

The design targets these failure classes:

- a remembered tool/path/version fact that was correct last month but is wrong now;
- a repository-local instruction retrieved in another repository or branch;
- a temporary exception promoted as a durable owner preference;
- an agent inference promoted after one observation and later repeated as fact;
- a policy-definition change that makes old and new memory counts appear directly comparable;
- a contradiction that overwrites history, leaving no way to diagnose why earlier sessions acted differently;
- a memory system that appears “accurate” only because it stores almost nothing (high specificity, poor sensitivity);
- a memory system that appears “helpful” because it captures everything (high sensitivity, poor PPV);
- a low-base-rate candidate stream in which a plausible detector creates many more false than true admissions.

Whether the subject harness currently misses any particular item above is **UNVERIFIED**; the task forbids local grounding, and no absence/broken claim can be made from this lens. The settling experiment requires two mechanically independent signals: (1) an offline replay of previously adjudicated owner corrections and known environment changes, with a detector-positive control; and (2) a live shadow-mode canary measuring newly observed contradictions and owner adjudications. Agreement between those signals would support a current-gap claim; either alone would not.

## 5. Evaluation plan: make the borrow falsifiable

### 5.1 Establish case definitions before scoring

Create a blinded adjudication guide for each memory class. For each sampled candidate and retrieval event, label:

- whether the proposition was correct at observation time;
- whether it was meant to persist across sessions;
- exact subject/scope/time;
- whether it was current at retrieval time;
- whether it materially helped or harmed the decision;
- what source counts as the reference standard.

Use two independent adjudicators or one adjudicator plus an authoritative live probe for ambiguous, high-impact items. Disagreement is a result, not noise to hide: it exposes classes for which “ground truth” is normative or underspecified.

### 5.2 Definition replay experiment

Replay the same labeled corpus through:

1. current behavior/baseline;
2. one global admission threshold;
3. the class-specific case definitions;
4. an “everything remembered” high-sensitivity bound;
5. an “explicit owner statements only” high-specificity bound.

Report by class and source stream:

```text
capture sensitivity = durable useful facts captured / all labeled durable useful facts
promotion PPV       = valid promoted facts / all promoted facts
retrieval PPV       = correct + current + scoped + useful retrievals / all retrievals
stale-use rate      = stale memories used / memory-assisted decisions
wrong-scope rate    = out-of-scope retrievals / all retrievals
median quarantine delay
median change-to-detection delay
owner adjudications per 100 sessions
probe cost and latency per 100 sessions
```

“Specificity” needs careful denominator construction because the universe of nonmemorable utterances is enormous. Sample negatives from defined source streams and report the sampling scheme; do not publish a global specificity number from convenience negatives.

### 5.3 Fault-injection experiment

Seed synthetic but realistic changes after a memory is verified: version update, renamed path, revoked permission, branch switch, changed instruction artifact, preference reversal, and expired workaround. Include known-current controls to prove the detector does not mark everything stale. Measure which invalidators fire, whether active validation runs before use, and whether the old record remains diagnostically linked to its replacement.

This supplies the two independent signals required for a “staleness detector works” claim:

1. **State signal:** expected record transition (`active → stale_pending_review/quarantined → superseded`).
2. **Behavior signal:** the agent does not use the injected stale premise in the downstream action and does use a known-current control.

Re-running the same state query twice is not independence.

### 5.4 Shadow mode, then bounded canary

First run classification and audits without changing retrieval; compare predicted quarantines with actual later corrections. Then allow enforcement only for one high-impact, objectively verifiable class such as environment capabilities. Stop or revise if owner-correction burden, latency, or wrongful quarantine exceeds a predeclared threshold.

**likely (55-80%)** that a shadow-first rollout avoids more regressions than direct enforcement because it reveals base rates and detector errors before changing behavior; evidence is methodological rather than harness-specific: PPV cannot be inferred without prevalence/operating data, and CDC recommends evaluating interacting surveillance attributes. **UNVERIFIED:** settle by comparing shadow predictions with blinded outcomes before enabling enforcement.

### 5.5 Decision rule

Adopt only if the class-specific system reduces stale-use and wrong-scope rates without a material sensitivity loss or unacceptable owner/probe burden. Reject the borrow if:

- “useful durable fact” cannot be adjudicated reproducibly for major classes;
- reference checks cost more than the avoided errors;
- candidate quarantine creates an unreviewable backlog;
- retrieval PPV does not improve over a simple explicit-owner-statements-only baseline;
- scope and invalidators cannot be obtained reliably from the existing bus.

This is important: a negative experiment is evidence that the analogy does not transfer operationally, not a reason to add a daemon or learned critic.

## 6. Adversarial analysis of the analogy

### 6.1 Where the structure genuinely matches

1. **Latent state, noisy indicators.** A disease case is not identical to a report; a durable/current memory is not identical to a sentence or model inference.
2. **Asymmetric error costs.** Missing a harmless preference differs from injecting a false security or environment fact; surveillance likewise chooses different operating points by purpose.
3. **Multiple ascertainment channels.** Owner statements, artifacts, tool outcomes, and corrections resemble sources with different coverage and reliability.
4. **Scope and time are constitutive.** “True somewhere/once” is not the same as “true here/now,” just as case definitions can constrain place and time.
5. **The detector changes over time.** A policy/schema revision can change counts without an underlying-world change, analogous to a changed case definition changing apparent trends.
6. **Stable blind spots matter.** A system can track trends while missing many cases if sensitivity is stable, but it cannot claim completeness; CDC makes this distinction explicitly ([CDC surveillance-system evaluation guideline](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5013a1.htm)). Memory evaluation likewise needs both usefulness and ascertainment coverage.

### 6.2 Where the structure breaks

1. **Truth versus utility.** Disease status at a specified time is closer to a factual latent variable. Whether a fact deserves memory depends on future tasks, owner values, token budget, and opportunity cost. There is no task-independent “prevalence of memorable facts.” PPV must therefore be computed per defined class and use context.
2. **Reference-standard weakness.** Epidemiology may use laboratory evidence; memory often lacks a gold standard. Owner statements are authoritative for preferences but not necessarily for external facts. Live probes are authoritative for some environment claims but can themselves be transient or scoped incorrectly.
3. **Feedback effects.** Retrieved memories change what the agent asks, probes, and does, affecting which corrections become observable. That selection loop makes correction logs a biased surveillance source.
4. **Population versus individual decision.** Public-health surveillance often supports aggregate trends. A single stale high-impact memory can be catastrophic even when aggregate PPV is excellent. Metrics must be severity-stratified.
5. **Dependence among records.** Epidemiological formulas are easiest to interpret with defined units and sampling. Agent memories can entail, contradict, or derive from one another. Treating correlated paraphrases as independent confirmation would inflate evidence.
6. **Forgetting has a rights dimension.** Epidemiological registries value longitudinal retention. Agent memory must honor deletion, secrecy, and data-minimization requirements even when retention would improve evaluation.
7. **No meaningful contagion analogue.** A false memory can propagate through summaries, but that is copying/provenance, not biological transmission. Importing reproduction numbers or outbreak models would add vocabulary without a validated causal structure.
8. **Base rates drift with prompts and tasks.** The proportion of durable facts in an owner correction stream can differ radically from ordinary chat or tool logs. Pooling streams destroys the very base-rate reasoning being borrowed.

### 6.3 Guard against “Bayesian-looking” theater

Bayes’ rule is relevant only after estimating class-conditional detector performance and a meaningful local base rate. Until then, keep observable provenance and categorical case status. **almost certain (90-99%)** that multiplying invented priors and likelihood ratios would be worse than explicit rules because the resulting number would be precise but uncalibrated; evidence is the absence, by design in this ideation unit, of labeled harness-specific operating data and the CDC requirement that PPV depends on actual prevalence and test performance. This is not a claim that Bayesian updating is intrinsically unsuitable; it is a claim that inputs must be measured first.

## 7. Anti-overfit gate and alternatives rejected

### Accepted architecture

The accepted design rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus and consists of a JSON policy, a small state-transition hook, and an offline/scheduled audit script. **very likely (80-90%)** that this can remain below the complexity of a separate memory runtime if the existing bus exposes candidate capture, retrieval, and post-correction events. **UNVERIFIED:** settle with a bus-capability spike that proves those three event points using a known-present control. If the bus lacks them, this unit does not authorize a new daemon; return the design as blocked or reduce scope.

### Rejected alternatives

- **Second orchestration runtime / durable workflow engine:** rejected. Revalidation is bounded and event-driven; durable orchestration adds a failure domain without evidence of need.
- **Vector database or knowledge graph:** rejected. This proposal is about admission, provenance, scope, and staleness, not semantic-nearest-neighbor recall. A new retrieval substrate would confound the experiment.
- **Trained critic:** rejected. It would create a second noisy case definition whose calibration and drift must itself be surveilled.
- **Observability SaaS or guardrail framework:** rejected. Local counters and sampled adjudication are sufficient for the falsification plan.
- **Universal TTL:** rejected because it confuses age with invalidity and ignores claim classes.
- **Always-live-check everything:** rejected because CDC’s active-surveillance lesson is explicitly about targeted completeness at higher cost, not universal collection.
- **Automatic hard deletion on staleness:** rejected. Staleness should normally change operational status while preserving provenance; deletion follows explicit privacy/security policy.
- **One confidence number:** rejected until calibrated per class and source stream.

## 9. Bottom line

Epidemiology has already solved a narrower and more valuable problem than “how long should memory live?” It has solved how to operate a noisy, incomplete, changing ascertainment system without confusing a report with a case or a detector’s sensitivity with the truth of its positives. The concrete borrow is:

1. define memory classes and reproducible case definitions;
2. include subject/scope/time and definition version;
3. separate sensitive candidate capture from specific operational promotion;
4. combine passive contradiction reports with targeted active checks and a sentinel audit;
5. use measured PPV and sensitivity by source/class, respecting base rates;
6. quarantine, supersede, retire, and delete as distinct operations;
7. preserve definition-change history so policy drift is not mistaken for world drift;
8. reject the whole design if replay and canary experiments do not beat the simple baseline.

That borrow is ambitious in discipline but minimal in machinery. It targets stale use, false admission, scope leakage, and silent definition drift without creating another memory platform.
