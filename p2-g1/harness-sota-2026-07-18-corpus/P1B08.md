## Bottom line for later design/build units

Preserve the small append-only core and current lock/atomic projection; do not replace it with a service, database, vector store, or durable workflow engine. The radical change warranted by evidence is not a larger runtime—it is making the existing bus observable and mechanically wired. Restore the three keystone handlers, add one versioned privacy-bounded dispatcher receipt, validate/repair sequence identity offline, and keep adaptive/pruning claims inert until prospective evidence supports them.

The storage core is worth evolving. The current deployment and generic observability contract are not acceptable as “ground truth”: a ground-truth system that silently stopped recording its primary event on July 10 and cannot say whether its own hooks fired is operationally broken even if every JSON line parses.
