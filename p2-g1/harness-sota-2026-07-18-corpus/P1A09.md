## 7. Operational implications and proposals

### P1 — Failure-aware alias routing — NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus)

On an explicit `is not connected`, `transport dropped`, or registration loss, mark that exact route unhealthy for a bounded TTL and inject the already-configured alternative into subsequent context. Do not block the tool call at PreToolUse; the behavior contract reserves hard blocks for dangerous operations. Do not merge providers solely by suffix: alternatives must be declared (`claude_ai_Tavily -> tavily/search-hub`, not guessed).

This is **very likely (80-90%)** to reduce repeated dead calls.
