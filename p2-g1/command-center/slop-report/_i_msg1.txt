The SLOP lane carried 45 probe scripts that committed artifacts named while
.gitignore:76 kept every one of them untracked. It fixed the 45 instances. Nothing
stopped the 46th, and there already is one: two committed command-centre reports
publish `python platform/lib/_p12_refute_predicate.py` as "Executable proof", and
that file exists on exactly one disk.

check_published_commands.py is the class fix. For every tracked .md under
command-center/ and docs/ it extracts each `python <path>` invocation and requires
<path> to be TRACKED. 230 commands across 255 documents today.

COUNT THE FIELD, NOT THE DOCUMENT. The obvious version of this gate is refuted and
the refutation is why the scope is narrow. Grepping tracked artifacts for an
untracked script's BASENAME reports 103-158 "dangling citations" -- but 157 of the
citers are this lane's own census outputs (_slop_measure*.out.json), whose entire
job is to ENUMERATE untracked filenames. A census that lists a file is measuring it,
not depending on it. A JSON array element "_foo.py" cannot match `python <path>`, so
that whole noise class disappears by construction rather than by an exception list.
Measured in _i1_dangling_census.out.json and _i2_published_cmds.out.json.

Two resolution roots, because both conventions are live: repo-root-relative and
citer-dir-relative. Requiring repo-root alone reports ~83 false violations across
the skill packs.

The baseline is a ratchet, not a mute button. The two inherited violations belong to
the platform lane and are NOT carried here, because they cannot be carried
byte-unchanged: `ruff check` at the CI pin 0.15.17 reports F841 at
_p12_refute_predicate.py:107, so committing it as-is turns gate-lint-format RED for
every sibling PR. Both SLOP rounds carried evidence byte-unchanged on purpose -- the
round-1 provenance-lock detector failed its own positive control, so nobody can yet
prove a receipt does not pin those bytes. Each row therefore records its owner, why
it is open, and the one-line settling action; and a row that stops violating makes
the gate FAIL so the floor drops instead of drifting into slack.

Detector controls: fail-closed on zero commands found; --self-test proves both
states; the negative sentinel is uuid4-generated at runtime, not hardcoded, because
a fixed sentinel poisons its own corpus the moment the gate and its receipt are
committed -- measured on this lane's round-1 control, which ships asserting quiet==0
and yields quiet==2 to everyone who reruns it.
