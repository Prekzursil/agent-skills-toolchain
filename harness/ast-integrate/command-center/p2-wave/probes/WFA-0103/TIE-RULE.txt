WFA-0103 written tie rule (recorded before any fusion is applied).

When a stage slot has no benchmark separation — the measured scores of
two or more sources are equal — the operator MUST:

  1. Record that slot's resolution as TIED. It must not silently pick a
     favourite and leave the record looking like a strict-score win.
  2. Resolve the TIED slot by UTF-8 lexicographic order of source_id:
     the lesser source_id wins. This rule is written here, not chosen
     at fusion time by a model, a vendor reputation, or an LLM judgement.
  3. Never break a TIED slot by prefer_source, reputation, model
     preference, or llm_judgement.

Falsifier: a fusion that, on equal measured scores, either (a) records
resolution other than TIED, or (b) selects the lexicographically later
source_id, or (c) selects by an LLM judgement field.

When scores are not equal this rule is not applied; the higher measured
score wins (higher_is_better=true).
