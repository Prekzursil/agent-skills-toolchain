What is the use-versus-mention trap for transcript claims?

-----TURN 2 speaker=assistant-----
A document describing a defect necessarily contains that defect's text.
Example of the trap (quoted, not asserted as our finding):
"The fingerprint function accepts every confirmation including same-probe re-runs."
That quoted sentence is the defect being described; this turn does not assert it.
