docs(p2): P2 build round-1 report, blocker census, and a half-enforced-contract finding

Fills in the round-1 artifact (the predecessor died at skeleton stage) and adds
the blocker enumeration the DoD asks for.

TWO CORRECTIONS TO THE GROUNDING LANE, both executed rather than argued.

1. "6 units have a genuinely unresolvable output_schema" is REFUTED. Measured
   with the repo's own schema_resolver.validator_for() over all 2,007 units:
   ZERO unresolvable. The four it named individually (IMP-0006, IMP-0044,
   IMP-0051, RES-0001) all resolve.

   But the real defect is worse than the claimed one and is NOVEL. Those
   contracts are not unresolvable, they are HALF-ENFORCED. resolve() calls
   parse_schema_ref(), which takes the leading logical path and silently drops
   everything after the '+'. Executed:

     declared = 'command-center/schema/receipt.schema.json
                 + tools/plugin/schema/error-taxonomy.schema.json'
     resolved logical_id -> command-center/schema/receipt.schema.json
     a plain receipt validated against that expression -> 0 errors

   A plain receipt is certainly not a valid error-taxonomy document. 41 units
   declare a two-schema expression (not 6), 5 of them READY right now. Each will
   report a green contract check that only ever tested half its contract.

2. "drop reviewer -> VALID=True" was state-blind. On a SUCCESS receipt all three
   strictness conditions hold {sentinel, evidence, reviewer} = all True; on a
   BLOCKED receipt reviewer is optional, which is correct design because a
   blocked unit has no reviewer to cite. Covered in the IMP-0433 commit.

THE STRUCTURAL FINDING OF THE LANE: 449 of 449 READY units (100%) carry a
reviewer_requirement, and receipt_core requires reviewer.verdict=PASS with
independent=true for state SUCCESS. A build lane working alone can therefore
deliver ZERO units at SUCCESS without forging completion condition 9. That is
the schema working as designed -- it is the self-authored-verification result
encoded as a constraint -- but it means P2 needs a paired reviewer lane or no
unit in this program will ever legitimately reach SUCCESS.

Other measured blockers over the READY set: 55 cloud-batch units marked
NOT-CHECKED (no reachability probe was run, so unavailable is not claimed);
AUD-0001 skipped rather than narrowed because its declared inputs walk
~/Documents/GitHub, the parent of a directory this wave is forbidden to touch,
and its reviewer_requirement is cross-vendor-mandatory.

The report also records two detector failures of my own -- a false 12/12 from an
invalid base fixture, and an SCC detector that was inert on sibling imports and
would have shipped a confident "0 cycles" without a two-sided control -- plus the
partition reading dropping from 44 delivered to 1, which is not a regression but
the first honest number the tool has produced.
