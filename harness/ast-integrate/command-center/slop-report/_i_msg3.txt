SLOP-INTEGRATE-R1.md plus the seven probes behind it and every receipt they emitted.

The headline is not the gate, it is what the first measurement did to the gate's design.
I set out to gate "a committed artifact names an untracked file" and measured 103-158
violations -- then read the citer list and found 157 of them were this lane's OWN census
outputs, whose job is to enumerate untracked filenames. A census listing a file is
measuring it, not depending on it. Shipping that would have fired on the measurement
instead of the defect. Re-scoped to the field that IS a dependency -- a published
`python <path>` command -- which drops the census noise by construction rather than by an
exception list, and lands on 2 real violations across 230 commands in 261 documents.
