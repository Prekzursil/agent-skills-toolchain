Metabase SQLi zero-day exploited in customer data-theft attacks

TL;DR

Metabase disclosed a critical unauthenticated SQL injection zero-day in versions 1.58 and above that attackers exploited to breach Framework, Tally, and LexisNexis customer instances starting August 3. If you're running self-hosted Metabase for analytics or logging, update to patched versions immediately or block the /api/session/reset_password endpoint until you can patch, since attackers grabbed plaintext credentials for connected databases and full customer records.

A critical Metabase SQL injection vulnerability was exploited in zero-day attacks to breach customer instances in data theft attacks, known to impact Framework and Tally.
