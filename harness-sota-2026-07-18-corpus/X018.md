# X018 — Cross-domain borrow: intelligence tradecraft → orchestration

**Unit:** X018  
**Source field:** intelligence tradecraft (ACH, source evaluation, corroboration, Sherman-Kent/IC probability language)  
**Target decision:** how many parallel workers to run, and when another worker stops helping  
**Lens constraint:** external research and ideation only; no local-repository grounding  
**Research date:** 2026-07-18

## Executive judgment

The useful borrow is **not** “ask several analysts and take a vote.” It is the combination of:

1. **Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH):** judge an item by whether it discriminates among live alternatives, not by how much material it adds in support of the current favorite;
2. **source reliability versus information credibility:** keep the producer’s track record separate from the merits of this particular report;
3. **independent corroboration and provenance:** do not count several reports as several signals when they descend from the same underlying source; and
4. **calibrated uncertainty language:** communicate the residual uncertainty and its evidentiary basis rather than silently converting agreement into certainty.

This transfers **partially, not literally**. The structural match is strong: both domains allocate costly, imperfect collectors/analysts under uncertainty; outputs can be correlated; apparent agreement can be circular; and the decision-maker cares about whether the next collection path changes a decision. The structural mismatch is equally important: intelligence tradecraft does not provide a validated formula for the optimal number of analysts, ACH itself has mixed or adverse empirical results, and LLM workers share models, prompts, retrieved sources, tools, and coordinator framing much more tightly than nominally separate human sources often do.

**Bottom line:** it is **very likely (80-90%)** that a provenance-aware *marginal diagnostic contribution* gate is a better control variable than raw worker count for research, review, and alternative-generation tasks. Evidence: Heuer’s definition of diagnosticity, the NATO/Admiralty separation of source and report quality, and Google’s 180-configuration agent study showing task- and architecture-dependent gains and losses. It is **UNVERIFIED whether the proposed gate improves this owner’s end-to-end outcomes; the settling experiment is the “Adaptive Worker Gate Replay” defined below.** Consequently, this unit does **not** recommend a universal number such as 3, 5, or 8 workers.

The recommendation is deliberately small and rides the existing orchestration/event bus. It introduces no second runtime, vector database, knowledge graph, durable-execution engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, or guardrail framework.

## 1. What intelligence tradecraft solved in its own field

### 1.1 ACH solved “more evidence” versus “more discriminating evidence”

Richards Heuer defines an evidence item’s **diagnosticity** as the extent to which it changes the relative likelihood of alternative hypotheses. His canonical example is a high temperature: it is strong evidence that a patient is ill but weak evidence about *which* illness is present because it is consistent with many hypotheses. He states that evidence consistent with all hypotheses may have no diagnostic value at all. See CIA, *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*, pp. 72–73: <https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf>.

ACH operationalizes that idea as an evidence-by-hypothesis matrix. The procedure is to generate alternatives, list evidence and arguments, rate each item across every hypothesis, remove items with no diagnostic value, prefer attempts to disprove hypotheses over attempts to prove the favorite, and test sensitivity to critical evidence. Heuer also specifically advises using analysts with different perspectives during hypothesis generation. See the step-by-step outline at pp. 123–124 of the same CIA volume: <https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf>.

In its own field, this addresses a recurring analytic failure: analysts can accumulate a large pile of mutually repetitive facts that all fit the favored explanation, while failing to seek the one observation that would separate it from a plausible rival. The mechanism changes the unit of value from **report volume** to **discrimination among alternatives**.

What ACH did **not** solve is an analyst-team sizing problem. It offers a work discipline and an audit structure; it does not derive a stopping threshold, a cost-sensitive optimum, or a guarantee of judgment accuracy. That boundary is essential to the translation.

### 1.2 The Admiralty/NATO system solved “good source” versus “good report”

The Admiralty Code (used in NATO intelligence doctrine) assigns two separate attributes: a historical assessment of **source reliability** and a case-specific assessment of **information credibility**. A new source may have reliability that cannot yet be judged, while its particular report can still be evaluated on plausibility and corroboration. Conversely, a source with a strong record can issue a weakly supported report. A recent peer-reviewed account describes the 6×6 system and its doctrinal roots: Karvetski, Mandel, and colleagues, *The effect of source reliability and information credibility on judgments of information quality in intelligence analysis*, *Judgment and Decision Making* (2025), <https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/judgment-and-decision-making/article/effect-of-source-reliability-and-information-credibility-on-judgments-of-information-quality-in-intelligence-analysis/E67548E8010A47345C3439D45D9EC6B3>.

The field’s useful abstraction is two-dimensional:

- **Producer prior:** What has this source’s access and past reporting earned?
- **Report likelihood:** How credible is this specific item given its logic, directness, and genuinely independent corroboration?

The same paper also reports a serious implementation defect: users tend to collapse the axes, assigning diagonal pairs and allowing source reputation to cue report credibility. That is a warning for the harness, not a footnote. Merely adding two fields does not ensure the orchestrator or synthesizer will reason about them independently.

### 1.3 Independent corroboration solved “several reports” versus “several roots”

Within the Admiralty formulation, the strongest credibility category depends on confirmation by other **independent** sources. The cited peer-reviewed account explains that credibility is keyed in part to independent corroboration, rather than to repetition alone: <https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/judgment-and-decision-making/article/effect-of-source-reliability-and-information-credibility-on-judgments-of-information-quality-in-intelligence-analysis/E67548E8010A47345C3439D45D9EC6B3>.

This protects against circular reporting: five reports can be one fact copied through five intermediaries. The important object is therefore a **provenance root or collection path**, not a document count. Intelligence practice does not make independence magically observable; it makes the question mandatory and allows “cannot be judged” rather than false certainty.

### 1.4 Sherman Kent and ICD 203 solved ambiguous confidence communication

Sherman Kent’s “words of estimative probability” arose because the same phrase conveyed radically different odds to different readers. His proposal linked a small vocabulary to explicit probability regions so that a consumer could distinguish fact, estimate, and degree of uncertainty. Primary source: Sherman Kent, *Words of Estimative Probability*, CIA, <https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Words-of-Estimative-Probability.pdf>.

Current U.S. Intelligence Community analytic standards require products to explain uncertainty using the quality and quantity of source material, knowledge gaps, assumptions, and indicators that would alter the judgment. ICD 203 also supplies standardized probability bands and warns against confusing likelihood of an event with confidence in the basis for the judgment. See ODNI, ICD 203, pp. 3–4: <https://www.dni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203.pdf>.

This mechanism solves a communication problem rather than an accuracy problem. A band does not make a judgment correct. It makes the analyst’s intended uncertainty inspectable and therefore challengeable.

## 2. Concrete translation to an agent harness

### Recommendation — UNVERIFIED: Adaptive Marginal-Diagnostic Worker Gate

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

**UNVERIFIED premise:** compact worker result envelopes contain enough stable provenance and claim structure for a lightweight gate to predict when another worker has negligible value. **Settling experiment:** the “Adaptive Worker Gate Replay” in §5, comparing the gate with fixed worker counts on historical tasks using blinded outcome, cost, latency, and defect measurements.

Build one small dispatcher-side policy, not a new orchestration runtime. It controls whether to commission the *next* worker. It has five parts.

#### 2.1 Start from the work shape, not a preferred team size

Before spawning, the coordinator records a compact task card:

```text
decision_or_artifact: what final choice/output must be made
candidate_alternatives: current live solution/explanation set, including “unknown alternative”
independent_lanes: separable questions or collection paths
sequential_edges: outputs that must exist before other work is meaningful
tool_density: rough number of external tools/systems required
stakes: low | medium | high
budget: max workers, tokens/cost, and wall time
```

Use one worker by default for a strongly sequential, low-branching task. Use initial parallel workers only for genuinely separable lanes or deliberately different hypothesis-generation perspectives. This is **likely (55-80%)** to avoid some negative-scaling cases because Google’s controlled study found multi-agent gains on parallelizable work but 39–70% degradation across its tested multi-agent variants on a sequential planning task, plus disproportionate coordination tax on tool-dense tasks. Evidence: Google Research, *Towards a science of scaling agent systems* (180 configurations), <https://research.google/blog/towards-a-science-of-scaling-agent-systems-when-and-why-agent-systems-work/>. The wide band reflects limited benchmark coverage and the risk that production tasks differ materially.

#### 2.2 Commission workers for distinct information paths

Each worker receives a commission with an explicit lane, for example:

- generate rival explanations without seeing the incumbent answer;
- verify a named critical claim using a primary source;
- attempt a reproduction/test rather than another prose review;
- search for disconfirming evidence against the leading solution;
- inspect a distinct subsystem with a non-overlapping scope;
- synthesize only after evidence collection is complete.

Workers commissioned as independent corroborators must not see peers’ conclusions before their first submission. They may share the task statement and necessary artifacts, but the result envelope records shared prompt, model family, retrieved source roots, tool outputs, and upstream worker dependencies. Independence is not a boolean; it is an evidence lineage.

This would catch the current blind spot of **nominal diversity**: multiple workers that use the same model, framing, search results, and upstream assertion can converge while adding almost no independent evidence. It would also catch **circular corroboration**, where several worker reports cite different pages that all repeat one original claim.

It is **very likely (80-90%)** that provenance-root counting is more honest than worker-count agreement as a description of evidentiary independence. Evidence: the Admiralty system’s explicit requirement for independent corroboration and its “cannot be judged” states. It is only **likely (55-80%)** that the harness can infer useful independence automatically, because common training-data influence and latent model correlation are generally unobservable from a trace; the replay experiment must test observable proxies.

#### 2.3 Require a compact result envelope

Each worker returns normal prose/artifacts plus a machine-readable envelope:

```yaml
lane: <commission id>
claims:
  - claim: <atomic consequential statement>
    evidence_roots: [<URL/path/test-output ids>]
    upstream_claims: [<worker/claim ids or none>]
    result: supports | contradicts | unresolved
    alternatives_affected: [<ids>]
    disconfirming_test: <what observation would reverse this>
new_alternatives: [<ids/descriptions>]
gaps: [<what remains unknown>]
worker_confidence: <mandated band plus basis>
```

The dispatcher computes, without asking an LLM judge to “score quality”:

- count of new candidate alternatives;
- count of new provenance roots;
- count of roots independent of already-used roots under the observable lineage;
- count of prior claims contradicted or independently verified;
- count of alternatives whose status changed;
- unresolved critical gaps;
- cost and elapsed time.

This is intentionally an ACH-*inspired* coverage ledger, not a full ACH implementation. The harness should not turn qualitative “consistent/inconsistent” cells into fake probabilities or sum them as if evidence items were independent.

#### 2.4 Use marginal diagnostic contribution as the continuation signal

After each return, assign the result to one of four mechanically checkable contribution classes:

1. **D3 — decision-changing:** changes the selected alternative, exposes a blocking defect, or invalidates a critical assumption;
2. **D2 — discriminating:** adds independent evidence that changes the relative support for at least two live alternatives;
3. **D1 — coverage-extending:** adds a genuinely new alternative, provenance root, lane result, or unresolved critical gap, without yet changing the decision;
4. **D0 — repetitive:** adds none of the above.

Do not infer that D3 is “truer” than D1; this is a utility/novelty classification, not a truth score. Keep source/worker reliability metadata separate from this report’s contribution class, following the Admiralty distinction.

Continue by commissioning **one targeted next worker** when a D2/D3 result creates a new disputed critical claim, when a critical lane remains uncovered, or when the leading alternative depends on a single provenance root. Stop when all mandatory lanes are covered and either:

- two consecutive appropriately commissioned returns are D0; or
- the only remaining gaps are explicitly noncritical and the next worker’s cost exceeds the predeclared budget; or
- the task is sequential and the next useful action depends on synthesis/execution, not another parallel opinion.

The “two consecutive D0” threshold is **UNVERIFIED and likely (55-80%) to be a usable starting heuristic**, not a fact. Evidence for the direction is ACH diagnosticity and observed task-dependent diminishing returns; evidence for the number two does not exist. **The settling experiment is a threshold sweep over 1–4 consecutive D0 results in the Adaptive Worker Gate Replay.** Until that test, retain a hard maximum and log the counterfactual spawn decision.

#### 2.5 Synthesis must expose uncertainty, not erase dissent

The final synthesizer reports:

- chosen alternative and rejected live alternatives;
- the critical evidence roots, with dependency/circularity notes;
- unresolved contradictions;
- a likelihood band for uncertain external outcomes and a separate explanation of confidence in the evidence base;
- which new observation or test would change the result;
- why orchestration stopped (coverage complete, marginal contribution exhausted, budget exhausted, or task became sequential).

This adapts Kent/ICD 203’s communication discipline. It catches a common orchestration loss that simple voting misses: a minority worker may supply the only diagnostic contradiction, yet majority synthesis can smooth it away.

## 3. What this would catch that raw parallelism misses

| Missed failure | Signal from the borrowed mechanism | Harness response |
|---|---|---|
| Five workers repeat one web claim | Five reports, one provenance root | Do not upgrade corroboration; commission a different collection/test path or stop calling clones |
| Workers agree because they share the coordinator’s framing | Same candidate set and near-identical commission | Commission blind alternative generation before exposing the incumbent |
| More prose arrives but no decision changes | D0: no new alternative, root, contradiction, verification, or gap closure | Stop after the tested D0 threshold |
| One reputable worker/model gives a weakly supported claim | High producer prior, low case-specific credibility | Preserve both dimensions; target the claim for verification |
| A novel worker/source has no track record but brings executable proof | Reliability unknown, report directly testable | Do not discard it because the producer prior is absent; execute/inspect the proof |
| Majority is wrong; one worker finds a falsifier | Minority report is highly diagnostic across alternatives | Escalate contradiction instead of voting it away |
| Parallel workers fragment a sequential job | Task card has strong dependency edges; next action awaits prior state | Collapse to one owner/worker for the sequential segment |
| A worker invents a superficially novel alternative unsupported by evidence | New hypothesis (D1) but no credible evidence root | Record it without promoting it; commission a targeted discriminating test only if material |
| Apparent confidence rises merely because worker count rises | Confidence basis shows correlated roots/model/prompt | Keep the confidence band unchanged or lower it; state the dependence |

## 4. Adversarial transfer test

### 4.1 Structural properties that do transfer

1. **Costly sampling under partial observability.** Intelligence collectors and agent workers both reveal only a subset of relevant information and consume time/resources. This match is **very likely (80-90%)** to be operationally meaningful; evidence is the target problem itself plus Google’s finding that task properties and coordination costs change the optimal architecture.

2. **Correlated reporting masquerading as corroboration.** Both settings can contain copied, upstream-derived, or framing-correlated reports. The mapping is **almost certain (90-99%)** at the logical level: if outputs share an upstream source, report count is not independent evidence count. Evidence is the Admiralty emphasis on independent corroboration. The remaining 1–10% uncertainty concerns whether all relevant dependency is observable.

3. **Alternatives matter more than support volume.** A result useful under every candidate solution does not discriminate among them. This mapping is **almost certain (90-99%)** as a property of hypothesis comparison, supported directly by Heuer’s fever example. Whether every build task can be represented by mutually useful “hypotheses” is a separate issue addressed below.

4. **The next collection action can be targeted.** Once a critical assumption or provenance bottleneck is explicit, another worker can be commissioned to test it rather than repeat the whole task. This is **very likely (80-90%)** for research/review work; evidence is ACH’s sensitivity and disconfirmation steps. It is less applicable to indivisible generative work.

### 4.2 Structural properties that do not transfer cleanly

1. **Workers are not independent human sources.** Sampling the same model with different role prompts does not create the kind of independence implied by separate sensor modalities, access paths, or human networks. Shared pretraining, post-training, system prompts, retrieval index, and tools create hidden common causes. Therefore the proposal never equates “separate context window” with “independent source.”

2. **A solution is not always a hypothesis.** In coding, design, or writing, alternatives can be partially compatible, composable, or judged on multiple objectives. A classical ACH matrix can force false mutual exclusivity. The gate uses “alternatives affected” and coverage contribution, not ACH’s least-inconsistent winner calculation.

3. **Worker output is an action as well as a report.** An agent can mutate state, run tests, or consume scarce external side effects. Intelligence report grading assumes evaluation of information; it does not model write conflicts, tool contention, irreversible actions, or cascading environmental errors. Parallel mutation should therefore remain prohibited or separately serialized; this borrow does not solve transaction safety.

4. **Feedback is faster and ground truth is sometimes executable.** Many harness claims can be settled by a test, compiler, schema validator, or file inspection. In those cases direct verification dominates a panel of estimates. The gate must reward an executable independent check more than extra opinion.

5. **ACH is not strongly validated.** A randomized study of 50 intelligence analysts found mixed evidence on confirmation-bias reduction and observed that ACH may increase judgment inconsistency and error: Dhami et al., “The ‘analysis of competing hypotheses’ in intelligence analysis,” *Applied Cognitive Psychology* (2019), <https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/acp.3550>. A later experiment reports that ACH-style structure did not reduce confirmation bias in its tested setting: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11169332/>. This prevents any honest claim that “ACH works in intelligence, therefore it will work for agents.” The transferable pieces are diagnosticity, explicit alternatives, provenance independence, and uncertainty disclosure—not an unmodified ACH algorithm.

6. **Intelligence standards are normative, not optimal-stopping theorems.** Neither ICD 203 nor the Admiralty Code supplies an empirically calibrated marginal-value threshold for adding an analyst. The proposed D0 threshold is an engineering hypothesis and must be evaluated as such.

### 4.3 Verdict on the analogy

The analogy **does transfer as a measurement and commissioning discipline**, but **does not transfer as a numeric answer or full decision algorithm**. It tells the harness what a useful additional worker looks like: a new independent collection path, a new plausible alternative, a discriminating test, a contradiction, or closure of a critical gap. It does not tell the harness in advance that the correct team size is N.

This bounded conclusion is **very likely (80-90%)**. Evidence: the primary tradecraft sources establish the component mechanisms; empirical critiques reject confidence in full ACH; and current agent-system evidence shows that worker benefit depends on task decomposability, tool density, architecture, and error propagation rather than count alone.

## 5. Required validation before policy — Adaptive Worker Gate Replay

Because the core recommendation is UNVERIFIED, adoption beyond shadow logging requires this experiment.

### 5.1 Design

Take a stratified sample of completed tasks spanning research, review, debugging, implementation, sequential planning, and tool-heavy work. For each task, run or reconstruct blinded conditions:

- fixed 1 worker;
- fixed 2 workers;
- fixed 4 workers;
- current/default orchestration policy;
- adaptive gate with D0 thresholds of 1, 2, 3, and 4;
- optional oracle-after-the-fact curve that identifies the best observed stopping point, used only as an upper bound.

Randomize condition order where reruns are possible. Prevent workers in “independent” lanes from seeing other first-pass outputs. Preserve model, tool, and prompt lineage. Use direct validators where available and blinded human pairwise assessment only for irreducibly qualitative outcomes.

### 5.2 Outcomes

Primary:

- task success or defect-free acceptance;
- consequential defect recall (especially minority-found falsifiers);
- cost and tokens;
- wall-clock latency;
- unnecessary worker count after the last D2/D3 contribution.

Secondary:

- distinct provenance roots;
- circular-corroboration rate;
- new-alternative discovery rate;
- fraction of worker reports classed D0;
- calibration of stated confidence against eventual outcomes;
- write conflicts/tool errors caused by concurrency;
- false-stop rate: a later worker would have found a consequential defect;
- overrun rate: additional workers add no consequential information.

### 5.3 Decision rule

Promote the gate only if it is non-inferior on defect-free success and consequential-defect recall, while materially reducing median cost or latency, with results broken out by task shape. Do not average away a severe regression on sequential or high-stakes tasks.

The gate’s benefit is **roughly even (45-55%) until this experiment is run**. Evidence for upside is the fit of provenance/diagnosticity to correlated parallel output; evidence against is ACH’s mixed empirical record, classification overhead, noisy claim extraction, and hidden dependence among same-family models. This deliberately low pre-experiment confidence prevents the elegance of the analogy from being mistaken for validation.

## 6. Implementation sketch and size discipline

### Core policy proposal

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

Implement as:

- one result-envelope schema;
- one event-bus hook that updates an in-memory/per-run ledger;
- one deterministic contribution classifier based on IDs, lineage, coverage, contradictions, and verification events;
- one `continue | target-next | stop` decision with hard budget caps;
- one end-of-run orchestration rationale.

Avoid embeddings and semantic clustering in version 1. Require workers to name atomic claims and provenance. Exact IDs and explicit relationships are inspectable and cheap; a model can still produce the envelope, but the gate logic must not be another unconstrained LLM opinion.

The <200-line estimate is **likely (55-80%)** for the gate logic and schema validation alone, based on the deliberately narrow state machine above; it excludes any platform-specific adapter already supplied by the existing bus. It is **UNVERIFIED; the settling experiment is a time-boxed spike that implements envelope parsing, ledger update, and stop decision against 20 stored traces and records changed-line count.** If platform adapters push this beyond the bound, reclassify rather than hiding the size.

### Optional historical reliability prior — defer

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only)** if the harness already records outcome-calibrated worker/lane statistics; otherwise omit it in version 1.

An Admiralty-like producer prior could later be a small JSON table keyed by task lane and verifiable outcome—not a universal “agent reputation.” This proposal is **UNVERIFIED; the settling experiment is a prospective calibration study comparing prior bins with verified claim accuracy over at least 100 eligible claims.** Do not infer reliability from eloquence, self-confidence, or synthesizer acceptance.

### Explicitly rejected proposal: universal fixed swarm size

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only).** Rejected because it ignores task decomposability, tool density, sequential dependencies, cost, and observed marginal contribution. Google’s study directly reports different signs of multi-agent effects across task types: <https://research.google/blog/towards-a-science-of-scaling-agent-systems-when-and-why-agent-systems-work/>.

### Explicitly rejected proposal: majority vote as corroboration

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).** Rejected because votes from correlated workers are not independent evidence, and a minority falsifier can be more diagnostic than repeated majority support. Majority vote is appropriate only when outputs are exchangeable, independently erroring classifications with an objective aggregation rule; that premise is generally UNVERIFIED for long-horizon agent work. **Settling experiment:** measure pairwise error correlation and majority-vote accuracy against direct ground truth on the replay set.

### Explicitly rejected proposal: full Bayesian provenance/knowledge service

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-LARGE (new daemon/service/runtime -- needs its own justification).** Rejected. It would introduce a new durable reasoning service and likely drift toward a knowledge graph or probabilistic runtime, both contrary to the anti-overfit gate. Evidence dependencies are difficult to elicit correctly; false numerical precision would be worse than an explicit “independence cannot be judged.” No justification in this unit clears the complexity burden.

### Explicitly rejected proposal: trained orchestration critic

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-LARGE (new daemon/service/runtime -- needs its own justification).** Rejected. It reintroduces a previously rejected trained critic, requires a stable labeled distribution, adds opaque failure modes, and is unnecessary to test the basic hypothesis. A deterministic shadow-mode ledger is sufficient for the first experiment.

## 7. Anti-overfit gate

The core proposal passes the stated gate:

- it rides the existing orchestration/event/hook bus;
- it is a per-run ledger and policy, not a second orchestrator;
- it adds no vector database or knowledge graph;
- it adds no durable-execution engine;
- it adds no trained critic;
- it adds no observability SaaS;
- it adds no guardrail framework;
- it can run in shadow mode and be deleted without migrating durable state;
- it makes the smallest useful borrow: explicit alternatives, provenance roots, marginal diagnostic contribution, and a stopping rationale.

The most important anti-overfit feature is that it can choose **one worker**. A mechanism that always justifies more agents has failed before it starts.

## 8. Failure modes and safeguards

1. **Envelope theater:** workers fill required fields with vague claims. Safeguard: reject missing atomic claim IDs/evidence roots mechanically; do not let prose count as provenance.

2. **False independence:** different URLs repeat one upstream source. Safeguard: record claimed upstream root and allow `independence: cannot_be_judged`; sample-audit lineage during the replay.

3. **Novelty gaming:** workers invent alternatives to earn D1. Safeguard: D1 alone does not justify repeated expansion; the next commission must be a targeted materiality/discrimination test.

4. **Premature D0 stop:** a valuable worker happens to follow two repetitive ones. Safeguard: stratify by commission quality, require mandatory lane coverage, retain a hard high-stakes verification lane, and measure false stops in replay.

5. **Coordinator anchoring:** the task card’s initial alternatives constrain discovery. Safeguard: include “unknown alternative” and run at least one blind alternative-generation commission for contested/high-stakes analysis.

6. **Reliability halo:** a historically strong worker’s weak claim escapes scrutiny. Safeguard: never combine producer prior and current-report credibility into one score.

7. **Confidence laundering:** the synthesizer upgrades confidence because multiple workers agree. Safeguard: confidence basis reports distinct observable provenance roots and hidden-dependence caveat.

8. **Schema cost exceeds savings:** producing envelopes consumes tokens and attention. Safeguard: keep the envelope small, run shadow-mode measurement, and remove the mechanism if net cost/quality is unfavorable.

9. **Wrong abstraction for creative work:** divergent artifacts may not map to truth-valued hypotheses. Safeguard: use coverage lanes and explicit evaluation criteria, or disable the gate; do not force ACH vocabulary onto aesthetic generation.

10. **Action races:** workers mutate shared state concurrently. Safeguard: this proposal governs information gathering only; serialize mutations or use the harness’s existing isolation. Transaction safety is outside this unit.
