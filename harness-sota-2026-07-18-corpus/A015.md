### C04 — caret mechanism vs PowerShell behavior — STALE

**Artifacts and exact lines**

**Specific resolution — BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).** Retitle the rule “Quote revision expressions containing `{…}` in PowerShell.” State that the backtick is PowerShell's escape/continuation character; `HEAD^` itself is valid in the recorded host probe; brace expressions such as `tag^{commit}` must be single-quoted or replaced with `git rev-list -n 1 <tag>`. Add a disposable-host regression fixture that prints received argv for `HEAD^`, `HEAD^^`, `tag^{commit}`, and the quoted equivalents.
