# P1C03 — Normalized audit findings A09–A12

## Scope and method

This unit normalizes the 38 findings in `A09.md`, `A10.md`, `A11.md`, and `A12.md`. It does **not** inspect the harness files named by those audits. Consequently every underlying audit claim is **UNVERIFIED against the subject**; the later verification phase should settle each one by opening the cited snapshot and reproducing any cited command or probe. The judgement here is narrower: whether the evidence *as written and taken at face value* supports the one-sentence claim.

`claimed_severity` is the value component of each audit's `value x effort` field (`high` or `med`). `SUPPORTED` means the cited material coherently covers the material parts of the claim; `PARTIAL` means it supports only part of the wording or supports a narrower scope; `UNSUPPORTED` would mean the cited material does not establish the claim. The internal-coherence classifications are **almost certain (90-99%)**, because every record below was mapped directly from the finding's `what`, `evidence`, and `value x effort` fields; the remaining uncertainty is interpretive and would be settled by an independent normalization pass over these four audit files.
