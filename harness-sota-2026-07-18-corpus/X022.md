# X022 — Cross-domain borrow: intelligence tradecraft → harness observability without a baseline

**Unit:** X022  
**Lens:** intel-tradecraft (ACH, source reliability grading, indicators/signposts, Sherman-Kent probability language) → observability  
**Boundary honored:** This is an external-research and ideation unit. I did not inspect or make claims about the local harness, repository, or subject tree. The only local premise used is the task's stipulated target problem: deciding whether a harness change helped when no baseline exists.

## Executive judgment

The analogy transfers, but only partially.

Intelligence tradecraft has a strong answer to **“How should we reason and communicate when observations are incomplete, conflicting, differently trustworthy, and compatible with several explanations?”** It does this by (1) forcing competing explanations into view, (2) asking which observations discriminate among them, (3) grading the source separately from the content of each observation, (4) exposing linchpin assumptions, (5) naming future indicators that would change the judgment, and (6) communicating likelihood with bounded vocabulary. Heuer's canonical ACH procedure explicitly calls for a hypothesis/evidence matrix, diagnostic evidence, disconfirmation, sensitivity analysis, reporting alternatives, and future milestones; ICD 203 separately requires source-quality descriptions, explicit assumptions, alternatives, uncertainty, and change indicators ([Heuer, *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*, pp. 96–109](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf); [ODNI ICD 203, pp. 3–4](https://www.odni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203_TA_Analytic_Standards_21_Dec_2022.pdf)).

Intelligence tradecraft has **not** solved **“What was the causal effect of an intervention when nobody observed a counterfactual or baseline?”** ACH can organize explanations of a post-change observation, but it cannot create the missing comparison. Heuer explicitly says ACH does not guarantee a correct answer and remains fallible judgment over incomplete and ambiguous information ([Heuer, pp. 108–109](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf)). A 2024 critical review of six experiments reports little or no overall judgment-quality benefit from full ACH and possible harm, while allowing that individual elements may be useful ([Wilcox and Mandel, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1080/02684527.2024.2304934)). Therefore this unit does **not** recommend an “ACH engine” that scores telemetry and declares a winner.

The viable borrow is narrower: use an **ACH-derived prospective change-assessment card** as the epistemic control plane, then create a real comparison with a small control/canary or replay. Google defines canarying as a partial, time-limited deployment evaluated against a control and emphasizes separately identifiable canary and control telemetry; Microsoft describes randomized control/treatment assignment as the conventional way to answer a counterfactual question ([Google SRE, “Canarying Releases”](https://sre.google/workbook/canarying-releases/); [Yom-Tov, Mansour, and Rosenfeld, 2017](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Predicting-counterfactuals-WWW-2017.pdf)). Intelligence tradecraft supplies disciplined hypotheses, evidence provenance, and stopping/collection questions. The experiment supplies identification.

It is **very likely (80-90%)** that this combined mechanism would make post-change decisions more defensible than an unstructured before/after story, because ACH externalizes alternatives and an explicit concurrent control addresses temporal case-mix confounding; the evidence is Heuer's audit-trail rationale plus Google's requirement to separate canary and control signals ([Heuer, pp. 108–109](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf); [Google SRE](https://sre.google/workbook/canarying-releases/)). It is **UNVERIFIED** for this owner's harness; the settling experiment is the 50-change prospective pilot specified below, scored for conclusion reversals, telemetry defects found, and owner-validated outcomes.

## 1. The specific mechanisms in their own field

### 1.1 Analysis of Competing Hypotheses: diagnosticity, disconfirmation, and an audit trail

ACH is not “brainstorm causes” in generic form. Its specific mechanism is an evidence-by-hypothesis matrix coupled to an unusual direction of reasoning:

1. Enumerate plausible hypotheses before choosing among them.
2. List evidence and arguments for and against each.
3. Put hypotheses in columns and evidence in rows.
4. Judge **diagnosticity**: evidence is useful to the extent that it changes the relative standing of hypotheses. Evidence equally consistent with every hypothesis has little diagnostic value.
5. Try to disprove hypotheses instead of accumulating confirmations for a favorite.
6. Recheck the few diagnostic observations and linchpin assumptions on which the conclusion is sensitive.
7. Report the relative likelihood of alternatives, not merely the winner.
8. Name future milestones that would indicate a different trajectory.

These are Heuer's eight steps, not a reconstruction ([Heuer, pp. 96–105](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf)). His crucial diagnosticity example is that fever establishes illness but poorly discriminates among diseases; similarly, a telemetry change that accompanies every plausible explanation is not useful attribution evidence ([Heuer, pp. 101–102](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf)). Heuer also directs the analyst to ask, for every hypothesis, what should be visible or absent if it were true, and whether a missing observation is genuinely absent, unobservable, concealed, or simply not collected ([Heuer, pp. 98–99](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf)).

The field-level value is procedural: the matrix exposes where analysts disagree about an observation or its interpretation and leaves an audit trail. Heuer expressly limits the claim: the matrix aids judgment and must not dictate it ([Heuer, pp. 104, 108–109](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf)). That limitation matters in the harness translation.

### 1.2 Source reliability grading: separate the messenger from the message

The NATO Intelligence Grading System, often called the Admiralty Code, records two separate judgments in one digraph:

- source reliability: A (completely reliable), B (usually reliable), C (fairly reliable), D (not usually reliable), E (unreliable), F (cannot be judged);
- information credibility: 1 (confirmed by other sources), 2 (probably true), 3 (possibly true), 4 (doubtful), 5 (improbable), 6 (truth cannot be judged).

The UK Ministry of Defence's JDP 2-00 stresses that reliability and credibility must be evaluated separately: a historically reliable source can produce an inaccurate report, and credible information can come from an unreliable source. It also requires re-evaluation as more information arrives and warns against mistaking circular reporting for corroboration ([UK MOD, *JDP 2-00*, 4th ed., paras. 3.39–3.40 and table 3.1](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/653a4b0780884d0013f71bb0/JDP_2_00_Ed_4_web.pdf)).

That separation is the important mechanism. “The hook usually logs correctly” is a judgment about a producer. “This session ended successfully” is a judgment about one observation. Collapsing them into one “quality score” loses information in exactly the way the Admiralty scheme is designed to prevent.

### 1.3 Sherman-Kent bands: constrain what probabilistic words are allowed to mean

Sherman Kent's original problem was communication failure: intelligence writers and readers attached widely different odds to phrases such as “serious possibility.” His remedy was a small, standardized vocabulary tied to probability ranges, while leaving “possible” to mean only that an event was neither impossible nor certain when odds could not be assigned ([Kent, “Words of Estimative Probability”](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/studies-in-intelligence/archives/vol-8-no-4/words-of-estimative-probability/); [CIA PDF](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Words-of-Estimative-Probability.pdf)). Modern ICD 203 retains the mechanism: use one consistent set of likelihood terms, explain the evidentiary basis and causes of uncertainty, distinguish assumptions from observations, and identify indicators that would change a judgment ([ODNI ICD 203, pp. 3–4](https://www.odni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203_TA_Analytic_Standards_21_Dec_2022.pdf)).

The useful transfer is not Kent's historical numeric cut points; this program already mandates its own bands. The transfer is to forbid unbounded claims such as “seems better,” “probably fixed,” and “should work.” A harness change assessment must say exactly one of:

- almost certain (90-99%)
- very likely (80-90%)
- likely (55-80%)
- roughly even (45-55%)
- unlikely (20-45%)
- very unlikely (5-20%)
- almost certainly not (1-5%)

It must pair the band with the discriminating evidence. The probability statement and the quality of its evidence base are separate fields; this follows ICD 203's distinction between likelihood of a proposition and confidence in the basis for the judgment ([ODNI ICD 203, p. 4](https://www.odni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203_TA_Analytic_Standards_21_Dec_2022.pdf)).

### 1.4 Indicators and signposts: turn uncertainty into a collection plan

The forward-looking part of ACH is not passive monitoring. Step 8 requires milestones for future observation that signal events are taking a different course; ICD 203 likewise requires, where appropriate, indicators that would alter a judgment or the likelihood of alternatives ([Heuer, p. 96](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf); [ODNI ICD 203, pp. 3–4](https://www.odni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203_TA_Analytic_Standards_21_Dec_2022.pdf)). In intelligence, this converts “we do not know” into “these observable developments would discriminate among our explanations.” In a harness, it converts “watch the dashboard” into precommitted outcome, guardrail, telemetry-integrity, and stop indicators.

## 2. Translation to the harness problem

### 2.1 The decision question

Do not ask “Did metric M go down after release?” Ask:

> Among eligible tasks, did change C cause an owner-relevant improvement, without unacceptable displacement into cost, latency, correction burden, or hidden failure, compared with the best safe counterfactual available during the same evaluation window?

That wording forces five distinctions:

- **cause versus sequence:** post-change is not the same as because-of-change;
- **eligible population versus all traffic:** a targeted change can be diluted or masked globally;
- **owner-relevant outcome versus proxy:** fewer tool errors can coexist with lower completion quality;
- **benefit versus displacement:** the harness can trade errors for retries, latency, token cost, or user correction;
- **observed event versus trustworthy evidence:** missing or duplicated telemetry can manufacture a trend.

Google's canary guidance directly supports population separation and warns that a 20% canary error rate on 5% of traffic appears as only 1% globally. It also warns that mismatched aggregation windows can falsely condemn a canary ([Google SRE](https://sre.google/workbook/canarying-releases/)). Microsoft reports that telemetry loss can bias experiments, reduce power, and create erroneous conclusions ([Gupchup et al., 2018](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/trustworthy-experimentation-under-telemetry-loss/)).

### 2.2 Competing hypotheses for every material harness change

Use a stable default set, then add change-specific alternatives:

| ID | Hypothesis | Expected signature if true | Strong disconfirmation |
|---|---|---|---|
| H+ | The change causes a net owner-relevant improvement. | Treatment improves the primary outcome against a concurrent or paired control; guardrails remain acceptable; effect appears in the eligible slice. | No treatment-control separation, or benefit disappears on owner-blind review. |
| H0 | The change has no material effect; the observed movement is noise. | Treatment and control fluctuate together; interval includes the declared smallest useful effect. | Stable separation replicated across evaluation windows. |
| Hmix | Case mix or time changed. | Raw before/after moves, but concurrent arms and matched strata do not separate; assignment balance differs. | Randomized concurrent arms are balanced and still separate. |
| Htelemetry | Instrumentation changed, dropped, duplicated, or mislabeled events. | Event conservation fails; independent terminal outcomes disagree with logs; missingness differs by arm. | Two independent outcome channels reconcile and missingness is arm-balanced. |
| Hproxy | The optimized proxy improved while owner value did not. | Tool/error metric improves while blind task success, correction burden, or downstream acceptance is flat/worse. | Proxy and owner outcome both improve with acceptable guardrails. |
| Hshift | Failure moved elsewhere. | Target error falls while retries, fallbacks, latency, tokens, abandonment, or another error family rises. | End-to-end outcome improves and the predeclared displacement guardrails remain stable. |
| Hlearn | Operators or agents adapted, not the harness change itself. | Effect tracks exposure/order or decays; new and old variants converge with experience. | Effect survives balanced ordering and washout/repeated windows. |

The table is deliberately not a numeric ACH score. A consistency count is not a causal estimator, and dependent evidence must not be counted as independent corroboration. JDP 2-00 specifically warns about circular reporting ([UK MOD, paras. 3.39–3.40](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/653a4b0780884d0013f71bb0/JDP_2_00_Ed_4_web.pdf)); recent ACH scholarship also identifies weak handling of evidence relationships and uncertainty as a limitation ([Karvetski et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40070-013-0001-x); [Wilcox and Mandel, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1080/02684527.2024.2304934)).

### 2.3 Grade every evidentiary row, not every metric name

For each observation, record:

```yaml
evidence_id: E-017
claim: "eligible task completed without owner correction"
producer: owner_disposition_event
producer_reliability: B        # history: usually reconciles with terminal outcome
observation_credibility: 1     # independently confirmed for this task
lineage_root: owner-action-8f2 # shared root prevents fake corroboration
arm: treatment
task_stratum: repo_edit
window: 2026-07-19T10:00Z/2026-07-19T14:00Z
missingness: observed
supports_or_conflicts:
  H+: supports
  H0: conflicts
  Hmix: neutral
  Htelemetry: conflicts
  Hproxy: conflicts
  Hshift: neutral
diagnostic_reason: "owner outcome differs across balanced arms"
```

The crucial fields are `producer_reliability`, `observation_credibility`, and `lineage_root`. Two dashboard panels derived from the same hook event are one source, not two. A historically trustworthy hook can still emit a doubtful record. A new producer is “cannot be judged,” not “unreliable.” Those are direct translations of the Admiralty separation and JDP's circular-reporting warning ([UK MOD, paras. 3.39–3.40](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/653a4b0780884d0013f71bb0/JDP_2_00_Ed_4_web.pdf)).

The grade is metadata for review, never a multiplier such as `A1 = 1.0` or `C3 = 0.5`. The source scales are ordinal judgments, and converting them to pseudo-precision would add unsupported mathematics. The matrix must show why an evidence row is diagnostic and which lineage it derives from.

## 3. Recommendation — UNVERIFIED: Prospective Change Assessment Card

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

Build one thin, file-backed mechanism, not an observability platform:

1. A `change-card.yaml` template is completed before treatment outcomes are inspected.
2. A pre-change validation hook rejects incomplete cards for material changes or marks the release `NO-CAUSAL-CLAIM` rather than blocking low-risk work.
3. A deterministic assignment hook places eligible, safe units into control or treatment and stamps `change_id`, `arm`, `eligibility`, and `task_stratum` on existing events.
4. Where live control is unsafe or side effects cannot be duplicated, the card selects paired shadow replay with writes disabled. The result is explicitly limited to replay validity.
5. A closeout skill reads the existing event stream, performs event-conservation and assignment-balance checks, renders the evidence/hypothesis matrix, and requires a banded conclusion plus evidence-basis statement.
6. Output is append-only JSONL/YAML/Markdown beside existing artifacts; no daemon, database, durable workflow engine, trained critic, or SaaS is introduced.

The mechanism is **likely (55-80%)** to fit the BUILD-SMALL boundary because its required behavior is schema validation, deterministic hashing/labeling, reconciliation, and report rendering rather than collection or storage infrastructure; the evidence is the deliberately bounded interface above. This sizing premise is **UNVERIFIED**; the settling experiment is a spike implementing one hook, one template, and one closeout command, followed by a physical nonblank-line count excluding tests and schema data.

The mechanism is **very likely (80-90%)** to detect at least some false “improved” judgments that raw before/after dashboards miss, because it creates separate control/treatment populations and makes telemetry loss, case-mix imbalance, and proxy displacement explicit; the evidence is Google's control-population guidance and Microsoft's telemetry-loss results ([Google SRE](https://sre.google/workbook/canarying-releases/); [Gupchup et al., 2018](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/trustworthy-experimentation-under-telemetry-loss/)). This harness-specific yield is **UNVERIFIED**; the settling experiment is the 50-change pilot below.

### 3.1 Required card fields

```yaml
change_id: CHG-YYYY-NNN
decision: "Did C improve O for population P without violating G?"
owner_value: "observable outcome that matters"
eligibility: "machine-checkable predicate"
unit_of_assignment: session | task | replay_case
design: randomized_control | canary_control | paired_replay | no_causal_claim
control: "legacy behavior or replay comparator"
treatment: "exact changed behavior"
primary_outcome:
  name: task_success_without_correction
  direction: higher
  smallest_useful_effect: 0.03
guardrails:
  - tool_error_rate
  - owner_correction_rate
  - end_to_end_latency
  - token_or_compute_cost
hypotheses: [H+, H0, Hmix, Htelemetry, Hproxy, Hshift, Hlearn]
indicators:
  promote: "precommitted treatment-control and guardrail condition"
  stop: "harm or telemetry-integrity condition"
  extend: "insufficient information condition"
telemetry_controls:
  event_conservation: "started = terminal + explicitly_open"
  independent_check: "second channel not derived from the primary event"
  missingness_by_arm: true
analysis_freeze: "timestamp/hash before outcome read"
conclusion:
  likelihood_band: null
  basis_strength: null
  surviving_alternatives: []
  decisive_evidence: []
  collection_next: []
```

The numeric `smallest_useful_effect` is a decision threshold, not a forecast. It prevents a statistically detectable but operationally irrelevant movement from being called “help.” The exact threshold is owner/change specific and must be frozen before outcomes are read.

### 3.2 Control choice hierarchy

1. **Concurrent randomized control** when the legacy behavior remains safe. Random assignment supplies the strongest practical counterfactual. Microsoft describes random control/treatment assignment as yielding unbiased estimators of population means under the experiment's assumptions ([Yom-Tov et al., 2017](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Predicting-counterfactuals-WWW-2017.pdf)).
2. **Small canary plus concurrent control** when exposure must be limited. Keep a work unit entirely in one arm, and expose arm-specific metrics; Google explicitly recommends both properties ([Google SRE](https://sre.google/workbook/canarying-releases/)).
3. **Paired replay/shadow execution with writes disabled** when the same input can safely traverse both variants. Google describes dry-run canary processing against real data followed by comparison with the live pipeline ([Google SRE, “Data Processing Pipelines”](https://sre.google/workbook/data-processing/)).
4. **Matched historical comparison** only when the first three are impossible. Label the conclusion associational and keep Hmix alive.
5. **No causal claim** when no credible comparator or outcome exists. Continue collecting operational observations, but report “effect not identified,” not “no effect.”

A live legacy control is **unlikely (20-45%)** to be acceptable for every change because some changes remove a known harmful behavior or alter irreversible actions; the evidence is the control hierarchy's side-effect and safety constraints, not local-harness inspection. This premise is **UNVERIFIED**; the settling experiment is a prospective classification of the next 20 material changes by whether live control, dry replay, or neither passes an owner-approved safety checklist.

### 3.3 Decision protocol

The closeout report must answer in this order:

1. Did assignment and telemetry integrity pass? If not, Htelemetry remains live and no promotion claim is allowed.
2. Is the primary outcome owner-relevant and independently observable? If not, Hproxy remains live.
3. Does treatment separate from the concurrent/paired comparator within eligible strata?
4. Do guardrails reveal displacement?
5. Which evidence rows are diagnostic across the whole hypothesis set?
6. Which conclusion depends on a single source, interpretation, or linchpin assumption?
7. What observation would reverse the conclusion?
8. State the result with one mandated probability band and cite the decisive rows. State evidence-basis strength separately.

Example closeout language:

> It is **likely (55-80%)** that CHG-042 produced a net improvement for eligible repository-edit tasks. Evidence: treatment exceeded the frozen smallest-useful-effect threshold in the randomized concurrent comparison, owner-blind completion ratings agreed, assignment balance passed, and telemetry reconciled through an independent terminal channel. Basis strength: moderate because the sample excludes long-running tasks. Hlearn and generalization outside the eligible stratum remain unresolved. The judgment would move downward if the second window fails to replicate or correction burden rises above the frozen guardrail.

This is a format example, not a claim about any real change.

## 4. What this catches that ordinary post-change telemetry misses

### 4.1 The global-average dilution trap

A targeted treatment can fail badly inside a small eligible population while the fleet-wide rate barely moves. Arm and eligibility labels expose the relevant denominator. It is **almost certain (90-99%)** that stratification catches a deliberately constructed dilution case in which a 5% canary has a 20% error rate but the dashboard shows only a 1% fleet-wide increment, because Google documents exactly that arithmetic and the need for arm-specific breakdowns ([Google SRE](https://sre.google/workbook/canarying-releases/)). The harness-specific prevalence of this trap is **UNVERIFIED**; settle it by replaying historical change windows with and without eligibility/arm stratification and counting decision reversals.

### 4.2 The “dashboard got healthier because the detector went blind” trap

Event conservation, arm-specific missingness, and a mechanically independent terminal outcome test Htelemetry before interpreting success. It is **very likely (80-90%)** that these controls catch gross arm-asymmetric event loss, because Microsoft demonstrates that telemetry loss biases experiment results and proposes measuring its absolute level ([Gupchup et al., 2018](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/trustworthy-experimentation-under-telemetry-loss/)). Sensitivity to subtle correlated loss is **UNVERIFIED**; settle it with fault injection at 1%, 5%, and 20% loss, both symmetric and arm-dependent, and score detection and false-alarm rates.

### 4.3 The proxy-success / owner-failure trap

The card requires an owner-value outcome and separate guardrails, so a falling tool-error count cannot alone prove improvement. It is **likely (55-80%)** that this catches some proxy displacement, because the design requires evidence across H+ and Hproxy rather than confirmation from the optimized metric; the evidence for the reasoning mechanism is ACH diagnosticity and alternative analysis ([Heuer, pp. 101–104](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf)). Actual detection depends on having an observable owner outcome and is **UNVERIFIED**; settle it by seeding pilot changes known to lower a proxy while worsening blind task-quality ratings.

### 4.4 The failure-shifting trap

Predeclared guardrails test whether a target error became retries, latency, token cost, fallback use, abandonment, or owner correction. It is **likely (55-80%)** that the card reveals obvious displacement when the destination metric is instrumented, because Hshift forces cross-metric evidence and a frozen stop condition; the evidence is the structured alternatives and indicators requirement in ICD 203 ([ODNI ICD 203, pp. 3–4](https://www.odni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203_TA_Analytic_Standards_21_Dec_2022.pdf)). Unknown destinations remain a gap; settle coverage by red-team changes that intentionally move failure into one unlisted channel at a time.

### 4.5 False corroboration

Lineage roots reveal that a dashboard, summary, and alert all derive from one event producer. It is **very likely (80-90%)** that explicit lineage prevents reviewers from counting an obvious shared-root trio as three independent signals, because the record makes the common root machine-visible and JDP 2-00 explicitly warns against circular reporting ([UK MOD, paras. 3.39–3.40](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/653a4b0780884d0013f71bb0/JDP_2_00_Ed_4_web.pdf)). Whether automated lineage capture covers every transformation is **UNVERIFIED**; settle it by tracing 30 randomly selected evidence rows back to raw producers and measuring undocumented ancestry.

### 4.6 The “absence of evidence” trap

ACH asks whether an expected observation is absent because the event did not occur, because it is unobservable, because collection failed, or because nobody looked. It is **very likely (80-90%)** that a required detector-control row prevents some single-probe absence claims, because Heuer explicitly distinguishes these explanations and calls for conscious analysis of what should be present ([Heuer, pp. 98–99](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf)). The harness-specific reduction is **UNVERIFIED**; settle it with mutation tests that disable the detector while retaining a known-present positive control.

## 5. Why the analogy can fail

### 5.1 Intelligence targets are observed; harness changes are controlled interventions

An intelligence analyst usually cannot randomize an adversary. A harness owner often can control rollout, preserve a comparator, replay inputs, or disable writes. Importing only the intelligence reasoning layer would leave stronger experimental leverage unused. Therefore ACH must sit **after experimental design and before judgment**, not replace the experiment. This disanalogy is decisive.

### 5.2 ACH consistency is not effect estimation

“Least inconsistent” is not an effect size, uncertainty interval, or causal estimand. Full ACH also has contested empirical performance: the 2024 review found little-to-no aggregate benefit across the small experimental literature, and Karvetski et al. identify weak representation of dependence, assumptions, and what-if uncertainty ([Wilcox and Mandel, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1080/02684527.2024.2304934); [Karvetski et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40070-013-0001-x)). The matrix is therefore a review artifact and collection planner, never a formula that converts pluses and minuses into probability.

### 5.3 Source reliability is not metric validity

A perfectly reliable logger can faithfully report a useless proxy. An owner-relevant metric can be poorly captured. The two-axis grade improves provenance reasoning but does not validate the construct being measured. That is why the card separately records `owner_value`, primary outcome, producer reliability, observation credibility, and Hproxy.

### 5.4 Probability words do not calibrate themselves

Standardized bands reduce semantic ambiguity; they do not make the analyst statistically calibrated. Kent's mechanism controls language, while empirical calibration requires resolved forecasts or repeated decisions. It is **likely (55-80%)** that storing forecast bands and later outcomes would reveal systematic over- or underconfidence after enough comparable cases, because bands create scoreable predictions; the required sample size and comparability are **UNVERIFIED**. Settle it by accumulating at least 100 resolved, operationally comparable banded predictions and plotting observed frequency by band, without treating non-independent changes as separate trials.

### 5.5 Agent tasks violate simple experiment assumptions

Tasks differ in difficulty; variants can alter whether a task becomes eligible; sessions can interfere through shared state; evaluator judgments can know the arm; and repeated replays may be nondeterministic. Microsoft notes that complex randomization can violate independence assumptions and yield untrustworthy inference ([Deng, Lu, and Litz, 2017](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/trustworthy-analysis-of-online-a-b-tests-pitfalls-challenges-and-solutions/)). The card must record assignment unit, eligibility, strata, exposure, and shared-state risks. Statistical analysis beyond descriptive paired or arm-level comparisons requires a separately reviewed method; the ACH wrapper does not repair a broken design.

### 5.6 Adversarial deception only partly maps

Harness telemetry is not normally a strategic adversary, but optimization pressure can create Goodhart-like behavior and a changed logger can conceal its own failures. The useful mapping is “measurement can become endogenous to the intervention,” not “the harness is an enemy.” Treating every discrepancy as deception would waste attention and encourage elaborate defenses unsupported by risk.

## 6. Anti-overfit gate and rejected expansions

The proposal passes the stated anti-overfit gate:

- It rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus.
- It uses flat append-only artifacts and current telemetry rather than a second orchestration runtime.
- It introduces no vector database, knowledge graph, durable-execution engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, or guardrail framework.
- Its matrix is generated at change closeout, not continuously maintained by a service.
- It creates a minimal counterfactual label and evidence ledger; it does not duplicate collection, scheduling, or storage.

Rejected alternatives:

1. **Full ACH scoring engine:** rejected because consistency tallies are not causal effect estimates, evidence dependence is awkward, and empirical support for full ACH is weak ([Wilcox and Mandel, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1080/02684527.2024.2304934)).
2. **Bayesian-network observability service:** rejected for this unit. Bayesian networks can model evidence dependence and what-if sensitivity better than canonical ACH, but they add modeling and runtime complexity; Karvetski et al. present those benefits precisely as remedies for ACH limitations ([Karvetski et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40070-013-0001-x)). This would be BUILD-LARGE and would violate the anti-overfit gate absent evidence that flat lineage plus controlled comparison is insufficient.
3. **Retrospective synthetic baseline:** rejected as the default. Without strong stability and exchangeability assumptions, it can make an attractive counterfactual out of case-mix drift. Historical matching remains an explicitly associational last resort.
4. **Observability SaaS:** rejected because the missing component is experimental identification and evidence discipline, not another dashboard.
5. **Mandatory heavy ceremony for every edit:** rejected because ACH is time-consuming and full use has uncertain benefit. Apply the card to material harness changes; allow low-risk changes to carry `NO-CAUSAL-CLAIM` rather than blocking work.

## 7. Settling experiment: 50-change prospective pilot

This recommendation remains **UNVERIFIED** until this experiment is run.

### Phase A — detector validation and dry run (10 historical/replayable changes)

1. Create cards without viewing recorded post-change outcomes.
2. Inject known telemetry defects: missing terminal events, duplicated events, wrong arm label, shared-root “corroboration,” and aggregation-window mismatch.
3. Include known-present positive controls for every absence detector.
4. Require two mechanically independent signals before declaring an event absent or the instrumentation intact.
5. Measure defect-detection rate, false-positive rate, analyst time, and whether the evidence lineage is recoverable.

### Phase B — prospective deployment (50 material changes)

For each change:

1. Freeze the card and its hash before reading treatment outcomes.
2. Choose the highest feasible comparator in the control hierarchy.
3. Record the reason when live control or replay is unsafe.
4. Keep each unit entirely within an arm and preserve eligibility/assignment logs.
5. Blind owner/task-quality review to arm when feasible.
6. Produce both the ordinary dashboard judgment and the card judgment independently.
7. Record disagreements, decision reversals, surviving alternatives, telemetry defects, and time cost.
8. Revisit conclusions after a second window to assess stability.

### Success criteria, frozen before the pilot

- At least one seeded defect of every class is detected, with its known-present control also detected.
- Every “helped” claim cites a comparator, eligible population, owner-value outcome, guardrails, and evidence lineage.
- No shared-root evidence is presented as mechanically independent corroboration.
- The card changes or blocks at least one conclusion for a demonstrably valid reason, without creating an unacceptable false-stop rate.
- Median authoring/review overhead stays within an owner-selected budget.
- Reviewers can reproduce the conclusion from stored artifacts without querying a new service.

The pilot's success is **roughly even (45-55%)** under these criteria because the epistemic mechanisms are well motivated but the harness-specific event model, safe-control rate, owner-outcome availability, and workflow cost are unknown; evidence for the upside comes from intelligence diagnosticity and production canary practice, while the downside is supported by ACH's mixed empirical record and experiment-design pitfalls ([Heuer, pp. 96–109](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf); [Google SRE](https://sre.google/workbook/canarying-releases/); [Wilcox and Mandel, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1080/02684527.2024.2304934); [Deng et al., 2017](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/trustworthy-analysis-of-online-a-b-tests-pitfalls-challenges-and-solutions/)).

## 8. Final transfer verdict

**Transfer:** yes, with a hard boundary.

Borrow **diagnostic competing hypotheses, source/content separation, lineage-aware corroboration, linchpin sensitivity, precommitted indicators, and bounded probability language**. Do not borrow the fiction that a matrix can replace a baseline, the tally of inconsistencies can estimate treatment effect, or standardized words create calibration.

The concrete harness change is one prospective change card plus a thin assignment/reconciliation/report hook. Its most important output is sometimes a refusal: **“effect not identified.”** That is a better observability result than a precise-looking causal story built from an unlabelled post-change trend.

The recommendation is **likely (55-80%)** to deliver net epistemic value if applied only to material changes and paired with a genuine comparator; the evidence is the fit between ACH's diagnosticity/audit trail and canary control requirements ([Heuer, pp. 101–109](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf); [Google SRE](https://sre.google/workbook/canarying-releases/)). It is **UNVERIFIED** for this harness; the 50-change prospective pilot above is the experiment that settles the recommendation.

## GAPS

- No local harness interfaces, event schemas, hook semantics, or safe replay capability were inspected, by instruction. Therefore the exact integration point and physical line count are UNVERIFIED; the BUILD-SMALL spike settles both.
- The public sources establish the tradecraft mechanisms and their limitations, not their efficacy in this owner's harness. The 50-change pilot settles local yield.
- The pilot needs an owner-approved definition of “material change,” an overhead budget, and owner-value outcomes. Those are policy choices, not facts recoverable from external research.
- No claim is made that 50 changes provide universal statistical power. The pilot is a mechanism and workflow validation; each treatment comparison needs an analysis appropriate to its assignment unit, outcome distribution, and interference risks.
