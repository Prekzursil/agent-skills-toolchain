## Intentional-collision safety criteria

No collision can be declared intentional and safe from the packaged evidence. A future sweep may classify one as safe only when all of the following are evidenced from mounted files and runtime resolution:

* provider scopes are disjoint, or a deterministic precedence rule always selects the intended definition;
* trigger text makes the distinction legible to the agent;
* same-name variants have compatible side effects and dependency requirements;
* redirects have no stale consumers before tombstone removal; and
* a routing fixture demonstrates the intended choice for ambiguous prompts and exact slash/name invocations.

The provider-scoped `imagegen` pattern described by the prior audit is a plausible intentional collision, but safety remains **UNVERIFIED** until the experiment stated above is run. Confidence that provider-scoped duplicates can be safe in principle is **almost certain (90-99%)**, because distinct catalogs can expose distinct native capabilities; this does not establish that this installation's catalogs are distinct or deterministic.
