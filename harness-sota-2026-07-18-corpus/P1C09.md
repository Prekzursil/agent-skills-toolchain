It performs internal-coherence review only: it asks whether the evidence text, accepted at face value, supports the claim text. It does not inspect the cited subject files, replay commands, follow URLs, or determine whether the source-world assertions are true.

- **SUPPORTED** — the evidence as written directly addresses every material part of the claim.
- **PARTIAL** — the evidence addresses the core claim but leaves at least one material qualifier, comparison, scope, or causal step unsupported.
- **UNSUPPORTED** — the evidence does not establish the claim’s core proposition.
