# P1C02 — Normalized audit findings A05–A08

## Scope and method

This unit normalizes the findings in `A05.md`, `A06.md`, `A07.md`, and `A08.md`. It does **not** verify any cited statement against the subject harness. `SUPPORTED` means the evidence as written, taken at face value, directly supports the material claim; `PARTIAL` means it supports only part of a compound claim, recommendation, causal attribution, or runtime consequence; `UNSUPPORTED` means the cited evidence does not support the claim. The source files expose `value x effort`, not a separate severity field, so `claimed_severity` preserves the value half and the complete source notation is retained in parentheses.

Stable IDs have the form `P1C02-<source>-F<n>`. There are no exact duplicates across these four files. `related_ids` records umbrella/detail relationships and substantial topical overlap without mislabeling distinct findings as duplicates.

No proposal is introduced by this normalization unit, so a NATIVE-vs-BUILD tag is not applicable. Recommendations quoted inside claims remain claims of the source audit, not new proposals by this unit. Every support judgement includes an honesty confidence band paired with its basis.
