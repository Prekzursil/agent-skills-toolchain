# P1C30 — Normalized audit findings E07–E10

## Scope and method

This unit normalizes every finding in `E07.md`, `E08.md`, `E09.md`, and `E10.md`. It does **not** verify cited paths, commands, inventories, papers, or URLs against the subject. `SUPPORTED`, `PARTIAL`, and `UNSUPPORTED` therefore mean only whether the evidence *as written and taken at face value* entails or reasonably supports the one-sentence claim.

All subject-level claims below are **UNVERIFIED**; the settling experiment is the later verification phase replaying every cited command and opening every cited path/range or URL, with detector-positive controls before accepting absence claims. This qualification is repeated in each record's assessment so it remains inline with the judgment it limits.

Judgment rubric:

- `SUPPORTED`: the cited material, if accurate, directly establishes the material parts of the claim; modest interpretation may remain.
- `PARTIAL`: the evidence establishes an important premise or local observation, but the claim includes an extrapolation, prescriptive architecture, comparative conclusion, or absence stronger than the evidence shown.
- `UNSUPPORTED`: the cited evidence does not establish the claim's material premise even if accepted at face value.

Claimed severity is normalized from the source's `value x effort` value component (`high` or `med`), because these audit files do not contain a separate severity field. The source's own action tag is retained separately. `NATIVE-vs-BUILD` is my normalization of proposals under the program's mandatory taxonomy; descriptive claims without a proposal are marked `—`. Confidence bands apply to the **internal-coherence judgment**, not to real-world truth.
