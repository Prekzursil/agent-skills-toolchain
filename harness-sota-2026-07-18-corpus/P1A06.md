## Bottom line

The measured problem is not simply “277 of 355 skills were unused.” That subtraction mixes at least four populations: (1) capabilities with demonstrated demand, (2) local skills whose filesystem timestamp is the corpus cut-off date and therefore have almost no fair observation window, (3) seasonal/domain tools, and (4) duplicated deployment copies and provider aliases. The strongest safe reduction is therefore consolidation, not deletion.

The roster below recommends:

1. **KEEP 43 locally installed skill names with observed invocations**, plus time-boxed retention of new or domain-seasonal skills. The 43 count is a normalized local-name count; the corpus itself records 78 invocation keys because plugin namespaces, aliases, and skills no longer present locally are also represented (`corpus/corpus.json:13266-13345`).
2. **MERGE 54 same-name skill deployments across `.claude/skills` and `.agents/skills`**: 29 pairs are byte-identical by SHA-256; 25 have drifted and require reconciliation, not blind deletion.
3. **MERGE semantic entrypoints** for grilling, TDD, verification, Codex routing, monitoring, and research routing while retaining narrow modules behind those entrypoints.
4. **KEEP the heavily used specialized agents**, but merge the zero-use TDD/reviewer/judge surfaces into modes of existing agents or skills rather than adding another critic tier.
5. **MERGE MCP aliases into one logical route per provider** and **PRUNE from eager production discovery** the dead Claude.ai Tavily/Exa endpoints, failed duplicate bridges, 100%-failure low-sample endpoints, and test probes. Preserve seasonal installs and test fixtures; pruning here means removing from the eager production registry, not deleting packages.

The consolidation-first direction is **very likely (80-90%)** to reduce selection ambiguity and dead-route retries because it is supported by two independent signals: exact duplicate-content hashes/current configuration inspection, and the corpus’s invocation/error counts. It remains **UNVERIFIED for end-to-end task success; settle it with a 30-day shadow-routing A/B that records route chosen, task completion, fallback count, and owner correction rate before deleting any source capability.**

## Decision rules used for every item

Each comma-delimited roster below is item-level: every named item inherits the status and all checks stated immediately above its list.

- **U(n):** observed invocation count after mapping both bare `name` and namespaced `provider:name` forms to the installed local name.
- **N1:** local winning copy has `LastWriteTime=2026-07-18`. This is evidence of a new/copied-on-cutoff candidate, not provenance proof. **UNVERIFIED newness: settle with installer/package ledger or source-control introduction commit.**
- **O(date):** latest local copy predates the corpus cut-off.
- **D(n):** token-bounded references from other skill/agent definitions; `D0` means none found. This is the dependency check, not a claim of hard dependency.
- **S-domain:** the item’s own name/frontmatter scopes it to a domain or event-driven workflow; it is treated as seasonal unless trigger-opportunity data disproves that.
- **S-general:** general-purpose/infrastructure capability; seasonality does not explain zero use.
- **Current-config:** currently declared in `.mcp.json` or `.claude.json`.

The corpus cannot supply N, D, S, or current-config status; those are independent current-state probes. No item is pruned merely because `U=0`.
