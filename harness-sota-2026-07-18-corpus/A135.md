## Required comparison and why the missing side is decisive

The requested finding schema requires all of the following for every conflict:

1. the command text verbatim with `file:line`;
2. the MCP-side text verbatim with `file:line`;
3. a concrete situation in which both authorities apply and disagree;
4. which authority wins at runtime, verified rather than inferred; and
5. the minimal edit that removes the independently drifting authority.

Items 2–5 cannot be satisfied from command files alone. Configuration names, endpoints, enabled/disabled state, and scope precedence are exactly the facts held on the missing side. A command-only overlap is merely a candidate, not a conflict. Reporting one as a finding would violate both “a conflict that never actually arises is not a finding” and “evidence or silence.”
