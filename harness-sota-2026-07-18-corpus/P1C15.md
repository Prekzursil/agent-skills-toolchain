# P1C15 — normalized audit findings B17–B20

## Scope and method

This unit normalizes every `### F…` record in `B17.md`, `B18.md`, `B19.md`, and `B20.md`: 8 + 9 + 10 + 9 = 36 records. The assessment is deliberately limited to **internal coherence**. `SUPPORTED` means that, if the cited evidence is accepted at face value, it entails the central claim; `PARTIAL` means that it supports a material portion but not the full scope, comparative superlative, threshold, absence, or predicted benefit; `UNSUPPORTED` would mean the cited evidence does not entail the claim. No subject file was checked to establish whether any citation is true.

“Claimed severity” is the value half of each source finding’s `value x effort`; effort is retained parenthetically. “NATIVE-vs-BUILD” is supplied only when the normalized claim contains a proposal. Descriptive findings use an em dash rather than inventing a proposal classification. The permitted proposal values are used exactly: `NATIVE`, `CONFIG`, `BUILD-SMALL`, or `BUILD-LARGE`.

Assessment confidence is attached to every evaluative judgement using the required bands. These bands concern only the internal-coherence classification, not the truth of the underlying audit claim.
