# P1C18 — Normalized audit findings C09–C12

## Scope and method

This artifact normalizes every numbered finding in `C09.md`, `C10.md`, `C11.md`, and `C12.md`: 34 records in total. The assessment is deliberately limited to internal coherence. `SUPPORTED` means the evidence as described, taken at face value, supports the full material claim; `PARTIAL` means it supports a premise or narrower version but not the full inference, generalization, absence, causal statement, or proposed mechanism; `UNSUPPORTED` would mean the cited evidence does not establish the claim. I did not inspect the cited subject files, execute the cited searches, or validate the cited publications, because the unit explicitly reserves real verification for a later phase.

The confidence bands below concern only my support-status judgments. They do not upgrade an audit claim into verified fact. Any statement below that a cited search found nothing is therefore `UNVERIFIED` as a real-world absence; the settling experiment is to rerun the stated search with a known-present positive control and a second mechanically independent inventory/code-path probe.

Duplicate notation is conservative. `C10-F07` and `C12-F06` are marked partial duplicates because both identify the same exact-third-repeat anti-degeneration behavior, but each uses it for a different higher-level claim. No other pair states materially the same claim.

This normalization makes no new harness proposal and does not adopt the source findings' proposals; the NATIVE-vs-BUILD proposal tag is therefore not applicable to this artifact.
