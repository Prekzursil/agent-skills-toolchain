# X135 — Cross-domain borrow from systems safety into finding synthesis

## Unit scope and conclusion

This unit asks one narrow question: what mechanism from systems safety can collapse thousands of findings into a defensible decision without requiring an agent to read the entire source corpus? It intentionally does **not** inspect or characterize the local harness. The remote override says the subject tree is available read-only, but the unit brief explicitly says not to ground this ideation lens in the local repo; I followed the narrower instruction. Therefore, statements below about what a proposed mechanism catches are properties of the design, not claims that a named local implementation lacks them.

**Conclusion:** a **modular assurance case with explicit defeaters**, fed by a small STPA-derived coverage matrix, transfers well enough to prototype. The transferable mechanism is not the safety vocabulary and not a graphical “Swiss cheese” picture. It is a typed, auditable argument graph that separates (1) the decision claim, (2) the reasoning that makes lower-level claims support it, (3) evidence with scope and provenance, (4) assumptions/context, and (5) unresolved counterarguments. Modules expose small contracts, allowing a decision agent to read the argument spine and only open evidence behind contested or high-impact nodes.

I judge this transfer **very likely (80-90%)** to reduce decision-context volume while retaining materially better traceability than prose summarization. Evidence: the UK Ministry of Defence’s safety-case guidance defines a safety case as a structured argument supported by evidence and separately defines the report as its summary; OMG SACM specifies machine-readable structures for auditable claims, arguments, evidence, context, and assumptions; and the University of York work explicitly developed modular case contracts because monolithic cases lose maintainability and compositional benefits ([UK MOD ASEMS, SMP12](https://www.asems.mod.uk/guidance/posms/smp12), [OMG SACM 2.3](https://www.omg.org/spec/SACM/2.3), [Bate et al., 2003](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/static/papers/R%3ABate%3A2003e.pdf)). This remains a forward-looking estimate because none of those sources evaluates this owner’s finding corpus.

The end-to-end effectiveness on this workload is **UNVERIFIED; the settling experiment is a blinded replay in which independent agents decide the same held-out audit question from (A) raw findings, (B) an ordinary prose digest, and (C) the proposed case, then compare decision agreement with an expert adjudication, missed critical contradictions, citation precision, tokens, and elapsed time.**

## 1. What systems safety solved in its own field

### 1.1 Primary mechanism: modular assurance cases with defeaters

A safety/assurance case does not merely collect evidence. It states a top-level proposition, decomposes it into subclaims through explicit reasoning, attaches evidence at the leaves, and records the scope, assumptions, and context in which the inference is intended to hold. The UK MOD guidance quotes Def Stan 00-056’s definition: a safety case is a “structured argument” supported by evidence for a particular application and environment; it distinguishes that case from a safety-case report, which summarizes the argument and evidence ([UK MOD ASEMS, §12.1](https://www.asems.mod.uk/guidance/posms/smp12)). This distinction is the first crucial borrow: the compact decision surface is not a lossy replacement for the evidence base. It is a navigable argument over that base.

Goal Structuring Notation (GSN) is one established surface form. The UK MOD’s GSN guidance says an argument links documented evidence to the goal it is intended to satisfy and may be built top-down from a goal or bottom-up from available evidence ([UK MOD ASEMS, “Goal Structuring Notation and Claim Trees”](https://www.asems.mod.uk/node/165)). The useful semantics are claim/evidence linkage and explicit reasoning, not the diagram shapes.

OMG’s Structured Assurance Case Metamodel (SACM) standard makes the same idea machine-addressable. The official specification page provides both a normative specification and a normative machine-readable model ([OMG SACM 2.3 landing page](https://www.omg.org/spec/SACM/About-SACM)). Its stated scope is representing auditable claims, arguments, evidence, operational context, and assumptions so assurance knowledge can be exchanged among stakeholders ([OMG SACM scope summary](https://www.omgwiki.org/dido/doku.php?id=dido%3Apublic%3Ara%3Axapend%3Axapend.b_stds%3Atech%3Aomg%3Asacm)). This directly addresses scale through structure: consumers traverse relevant relationships instead of rereading every source document.

Large cases need boundaries. Bate, Bates, Hawkins, Kelly, and McDermid describe modular safety cases in which modules have contracts recording how one module’s guarantees satisfy another module’s needs. Their motivation is that a monolithic case discards the maintenance and reuse benefits of modular system architecture ([Bate et al., 2003](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/publications/Bate2003e.html)). The transferable idea is an **assume/guarantee interface for findings**: a domain packet exposes a small set of supported claims, dependencies, exceptions, and unresolved objections while retaining its internal evidence graph.

A positive case alone is dangerous because it can become a confirmation machine. Assurance 2.0 therefore represents **defeaters**: doubts that rebut a claim, undermine its evidence, or challenge its inference, with subcases that resolve or sustain the doubt. Bloomfield, Netkachova, and Rushby explicitly motivate defeaters as protection against fallible judgment and confirmation bias and describe recording skeptical debate for later developers and assessors ([Bloomfield, Netkachova & Rushby, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15800)). NASA’s analysis of Baconian confidence similarly describes identifying defeaters for propositions and determining whether each can be dismissed, while warning that counts of eliminated defeaters are ordinal and must not be subjected to unjustified probability arithmetic ([NASA/TM-2016-219341](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20160013333/downloads/20160013333.pdf)).

In its own field, then, the complete mechanism is:

1. Define the consequential top claim and its operating context.
2. Decompose it into reviewable subclaims using explicit inference strategies.
3. Bind each leaf to traceable evidence, not to another summary whose provenance has disappeared.
4. Partition the case into modules with declared assumptions and guarantees.
5. Attach rebutting and undermining defeaters, rather than hiding contrary evidence in prose.
6. Permit a compact case report while preserving drill-down to the complete case and original evidence.

NASA’s tool-support survey independently depicts assurance-case work as claim definition, argument architecture, evidence selection, counterevidence/defeaters, property validation, and argument improvement ([NASA, “Tool Support for Assurance Case Development”](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/tool_support_for_assurance_case_development.pdf)). That independent convergence is why this unit treats the structure as an engineering mechanism rather than a diagramming fashion.

### 1.2 Supporting mechanism: STPA’s loss-driven control/coverage analysis

STAMP models accidents as failures to enforce safety constraints through a control structure, including inadequate feedback, unsafe actions, and mismatched controller process models—not only failed components. The STPA procedure begins by defining unacceptable losses, hazards, and system constraints; models controllers, controlled processes, control actions, and feedback; identifies unsafe control actions; and then constructs causal scenarios and requirements. MIT’s partnership describes its handbook as the definitive step-by-step guide and lists purpose, control structures, and unsafe control actions as the basic sequence ([MIT PSASS books and handbooks](https://psas.scripts.mit.edu/home/books-and-handbooks/)). The STAMP Institute gives the same loss → control structure → unsafe action → causal scenario → requirement chain ([STAMP Institute, “About STAMP”](https://home.stamp-institute.com/about-stamp/)).

STPA’s bounded guidewords are especially relevant to synthesis completeness. For each control action, the analyst asks whether danger results when the action is not provided, is provided unsafely, occurs too early/late or in the wrong order, or lasts too long/stops too soon ([STPA Handbook, Leveson & Thomas, 2018](https://www.flighttestsafety.setp.org/images/STPA_Handbook.pdf); [Japan IPA STPA procedure](https://www.ipa.go.jp/en/digital/complex_systems/stamp.html)). The deeper lesson is not to copy aviation terms into an audit. It is to define a finite cross-product that makes omission visible.

For synthesis, the corresponding finite questions are: was a relevant finding omitted; included under the wrong decision or scope; incorporated too late to affect the decision; or allowed to persist after its evidence became stale? A second dimension asks whether the decision received adequate feedback: coverage, contradiction, provenance, freshness, and independence. This matrix does not decide truth; it identifies where the case lacks an argument or feedback edge.

STPA is only an adjunct here. I judge a full STPA model of the agent harness **unlikely (20-45%)** to be the minimal solution for document synthesis because STPA assumes a system with control actions, feedback, and hazardous state transitions, while finding aggregation is primarily an epistemic argument problem. Evidence: the STPA procedure is explicitly organized around control structures and unsafe control actions, whereas SACM is explicitly organized around claims, arguments, evidence, and context ([Japan IPA](https://www.ipa.go.jp/en/digital/complex_systems/stamp.html), [OMG SACM scope](https://www.omgwiki.org/dido/doku.php?id=dido%3Apublic%3Ara%3Axapend%3Axapend.b_stds%3Atech%3Aomg%3Asacm)). Therefore this proposal borrows only STPA’s loss-first coverage discipline and guideword pattern.

### 1.3 Swiss cheese and normal accidents: warnings, not the architecture

Reason’s Swiss-cheese model treats defenses, barriers, and safeguards as imperfect layers. Harm occurs when weaknesses across layers align into a trajectory; Reason distinguishes active failures near operations from latent conditions introduced by design and management ([Reason, “Human error: models and management,” BMJ 2000](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1117770/)). This usefully reminds a synthesis designer that “three agents agree” is not three independent barriers if all consumed the same summary, shared the same prompt-induced framing, or inherited the same faulty extractor.

However, Swiss cheese does **not** solve evidence synthesis. It is a causal metaphor for defenses and accident trajectories, not a semantics for whether evidence supports a claim. EUROCONTROL’s critical review notes both the model’s usefulness and its drawbacks and lack of uncritical acceptance ([EUROCONTROL, 2006](https://www.eurocontrol.int/publication/revisiting-swiss-cheese-model-accidents)). A healthcare analysis likewise found ambiguity about what the slices, holes, and arrows represent and warns that complex system failure is not easily represented as a simple linear sequence ([Perneger, 2005](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1298298/)).

I judge “stack more summarizers/reviewers as independent cheese slices” **very unlikely (5-20%)** to produce trustworthy synthesis without explicit provenance and dependence modeling. Evidence: Reason’s model depends on distinct barriers while the assurance literature requires explicit claims, evidence, context, and assumptions; duplicated agents over a common input are structurally capable of common-mode failure ([Reason 2000](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1117770/), [OMG SACM scope](https://www.omgwiki.org/dido/doku.php?id=dido%3Apublic%3Ara%3Axapend%3Axapend.b_stds%3Atech%3Aomg%3Asacm)). The exact magnitude is **UNVERIFIED; the settling experiment is to inject correlated extractor errors and compare majority voting with a provenance-aware defeater case.**

Normal-accident theory adds a second warning: modularization does not make unmodeled interactions disappear. Modular safety-case research itself says compositional arguments require attention to independence, non-interference, and coupling ([University of York, “Architectural Considerations in the Certification…”](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/static/papers/R%3ABate%3A2002a.pdf)). For synthesis, shared sources, shared derived metrics, duplicated fingerprints, or one claim reused across several branches create coupling. The design must show those dependencies rather than numerically treating every evidence edge as independent.

## 2. Does the analogy actually transfer?

### 2.1 Structural correspondences

| Systems-safety structure | Synthesis structure | Why correspondence is substantive |
|---|---|---|
| Top safety claim scoped to system/environment | Decision claim scoped to corpus snapshot, decision owner, and acceptance criteria | Both are propositions whose validity depends on explicit context, not free-floating summaries. |
| Hazard/loss | Decision loss: adopting a harmful change, rejecting a valuable one, or asserting completion with missing evidence | Both provide a consequence-oriented reason for prioritizing branches. |
| Evidence artifact | Finding with source locator, detector identity, timestamp, and excerpt/hash | Both require provenance and a statement of what the artifact actually demonstrates. |
| Argument strategy | Explicit aggregation rule: exhaustiveness, risk threshold, causal explanation, comparison, or trade-off | In both domains, evidence does not entail a conclusion without an inference. |
| Context/assumption | Corpus limits, detector recall, branch/version, measurement window, definition | Hidden scope changes can invalidate a conclusion in both domains. |
| Defeater | Contradiction, counterexample, stale source, detector blind spot, non-independent evidence, or alternative causal account | Both need negative evidence to remain first-class and capable of blocking acceptance. |
| Module contract | Domain synthesis packet exposing supported claims, dependencies, exceptions, and unresolved defeaters | Both allow a consumer to inspect an interface before opening internals. |

These mappings are about the structure of justified decisions, not about pretending software-maintenance findings can cause physical accidents. That difference matters: the proposal borrows assurance-case epistemology, not safety certification authority.

### 2.2 Disanalogies and failure conditions

1. **No regulator or accepted risk threshold.** Safety cases often target a defined acceptance regime; harness design may have contested goals such as speed, autonomy, cost, and owner preference. A case can expose that trade-off but cannot invent the owner’s utility function. Any top claim must therefore name acceptance criteria and the human decision right.

2. **Findings are often claims, not observations.** Audit prose can contain interpretation layered on interpretation. Treating each document as “evidence” would launder assertions into facts. Leaf nodes need evidence-kind labels (`measurement`, `source-observation`, `expert-judgment`, `inference`, `proposal`) and must not silently promote one kind to another.

3. **Completeness is open-world.** In a bounded component inventory an analyst can sometimes enumerate interfaces; frontier research can always contain unknown unknowns. A green case means “no unresolved blocker under declared scope and detectors,” never “all relevant truth has been found.”

4. **Evidence can be highly correlated.** Thousands of files may restate one detector output or copy a common claim. Numeric vote counts without provenance collapse are misleading. Evidence clusters must share an origin identifier, and no confidence arithmetic should assume independence merely because paths differ. NASA specifically warns against unjustified arithmetic over ordinal defeater assessments ([NASA/TM-2016-219341](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20160013333/downloads/20160013333.pdf)).

5. **An LLM is both analyst and failure source.** The case cannot use the same unconstrained model judgment as extractor, supporter, and independent challenger and then call the outputs independent. Mechanical checks and source-address verification must be separate from semantic judgment.

6. **Argument structure can create false authority.** A polished graph may look rigorous while its decomposition is circular, incomplete, or supported by weak evidence. Defeaters and lint rules reduce this risk but do not eliminate it. The top-level decision must expose unresolved objections, not merely a color or score.

The analogy survives these disanalogies because each one can be represented as context, evidence type, dependency, or defeater. It would fail if the desired output were a creative brainstorm with no acceptance claim, or if source artifacts lacked stable locators. In those cases there is no testable proposition or auditable evidence relation, and an assurance case would add ceremony without epistemic leverage.

## 3. Concrete harness translation

### Proposal A — Finding Assurance Packet (FAP) compiler

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus)**

Build one deterministic compiler/linter, invoked as an existing skill or completion hook, that consumes newline-delimited finding records and emits:

- `case.json`: the complete typed graph;
- `decision.md`: a bounded argument spine suitable for the deciding agent;
- `open-defeaters.md`: only unresolved challenges, ranked by potential decision impact;
- `evidence-map.json`: reverse links from source locators and origin clusters to every claim that depends on them.

Do **not** implement SACM in full. A deliberately small subset is enough:

```yaml
case:
  id: CASE-...
  decision: "Adopt proposal P for snapshot S under criteria C"
  scope: {snapshot: ..., included_domains: [...], excluded_domains: [...]}
  acceptance: [criterion_ids]

nodes:
  - id: C-...
    type: claim | evidence | context | assumption | defeater | decision
    statement: "one falsifiable proposition"
    status: supported | contested | unsupported | out_of_scope
    source: {uri: ..., locator: ..., content_hash: ...}   # evidence only
    evidence_kind: measurement | observation | judgment | inference | proposal
    origin_cluster: ORIGIN-...                            # correlation control
    owner: ...
    freshness: ...

edges:
  - from: ...
    to: ...
    type: supports | rebuts | undermines | assumes | contextualizes | depends_on
    rationale: "why this relation holds"

modules:
  - id: M-...
    guarantees: [claim_ids]
    assumes: [assumption_ids]
    imports: [module_ids]
    open_defeaters: [defeater_ids]
```

The compiler is deterministic plumbing, not an autonomous truth oracle. Existing research/audit agents may produce candidate nodes, but linting enforces graph invariants:

- every decision criterion has at least one claim branch;
- every supported leaf reaches at least one source-addressed evidence node;
- every evidence locator is syntactically resolvable and content-hashed;
- every inference edge has a rationale;
- every imported module declares assumptions and unresolved defeaters;
- every contradiction remains reachable from the claim it challenges;
- an unresolved high-impact defeater forces the parent claim to `contested`;
- cycles in `supports` are rejected;
- evidence with the same `origin_cluster` is displayed as one provenance family, not many votes;
- freshness/scope mismatch creates an undermining defeater rather than silently dropping the evidence;
- no scalar “confidence score” is synthesized from counts.

The deciding agent receives `decision.md`, whose fixed budget is an argument spine rather than a generic summary:

1. exact decision and scope;
2. acceptance criteria;
3. one sentence per top subclaim;
4. strongest supporting provenance family per subclaim;
5. all unresolved decision-relevant defeaters;
6. assumptions on which the recommendation changes;
7. drill-down node IDs and source locators.

It opens full modules only when a node is contested, carries high consequence, rests on a single origin, or violates a lint invariant. This is how the mechanism avoids requiring a read of the entire corpus while preserving a path to it.

I judge a deterministic schema/compiler/linter **very likely (80-90%)** to fit within BUILD-SMALL if it only parses JSONL, validates the graph, and renders Markdown. Evidence: the proposed representation uses a small subset of SACM concepts and no storage service; OMG supplies a much larger normative metamodel, demonstrating that the object relations are representable ([OMG SACM 2.3](https://www.omg.org/spec/SACM/2.3)). The line-count estimate is **UNVERIFIED; the settling experiment is a time-boxed implementation spike with a 200 logical-line cap and fixtures for all listed invariants.** If the spike exceeds the cap because ingestion requires format-specific extraction, keep the compiler small and require producers to emit the interchange schema; do not grow a new runtime.

### Proposal B — STPA-derived synthesis coverage matrix

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only)**

Add a required manifest that defines decision losses and a finite set of coverage cells. This is configuration consumed by Proposal A, not a second engine:

```yaml
losses:
  - L1: "harmful recommendation accepted"
  - L2: "valuable replacement rejected due to omitted evidence"
  - L3: "completion asserted despite unresolved critical uncertainty"

coverage_dimensions:
  acceptance: [reliability, capability, maintainability, cost, safety]
  evidence_kind: [measured, observed, claimed, proposed]
  polarity: [supporting, contradicting]
  timing: [current, stale, superseded]
  provenance: [independent, shared-origin, unknown-origin]

guidewords:
  - omitted
  - included_under_wrong_scope
  - arrived_too_late
  - retained_after_invalidation
```

An empty cell is not automatically a defect; it produces a visible `coverage-gap` node requiring one of three dispositions: `evidence-added`, `explicitly-out-of-scope`, or `accepted-unknown`. This preserves the open-world limitation while preventing silent omission.

I judge this matrix **likely (55-80%)** to surface omissions that prose summaries hide because STPA’s guideword method systematically queries absence, incorrect provision, timing/order, and duration for each control action ([STPA Handbook](https://www.flighttestsafety.setp.org/images/STPA_Handbook.pdf), [Japan IPA](https://www.ipa.go.jp/en/digital/complex_systems/stamp.html)). Transfer effectiveness is **UNVERIFIED; the settling experiment is mutation testing: delete, mis-scope, stale, and duplicate selected findings in a gold-case fixture and measure which mutations create a coverage gap or defeater.**

### Proposal C — Independence-aware review rule

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only)**

Require every evidence node and reviewer output to declare an `origin_cluster` and `method_family`. A claim may be described as having multiple corroborating signals only when at least two evidence nodes differ in both origin and method. Shared prompt, shared intermediate summary, copied source paragraph, or common generated metric keeps outputs in the same cluster. This operationalizes the useful part of Swiss cheese without pretending that more layers guarantee safety.

I judge this rule **likely (55-80%)** to prevent false corroboration in multi-agent synthesis, because Swiss-cheese protection relies on multiple barriers while common-mode dependence makes nominally separate layers fail together; the assurance architecture literature explicitly identifies independence/non-interference as a prerequisite for compositional reasoning ([Reason 2000](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1117770/), [Bate et al., 2002](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/static/papers/R%3ABate%3A2002a.pdf)). The harness effect is **UNVERIFIED; the settling experiment is a fixture with ten restatements of one false source plus two mechanically independent counter-signals, comparing naive vote aggregation to origin-cluster aggregation.**

## 4. What the mechanism would catch

The following are design-detectable failure classes, not claims about the local subject:

| Failure that ordinary summarization can hide | Case mechanism that exposes it | Mechanical symptom |
|---|---|---|
| A high-volume finding dominates despite weak provenance | Evidence-kind and origin-cluster fields | Many support edges collapse to one source family or judgment-only leaf. |
| Two reports contradict each other but the digest selects one | `rebuts` edge plus contested-status propagation | Decision branch cannot render as supported while the defeater is open. |
| A recommendation has evidence but no stated inference | Required edge rationale | Linter rejects an unsupported reasoning jump. |
| A conclusion is true only for one branch, version, or time window | Context and freshness binding | Scope mismatch creates an undermining defeater. |
| A domain produced no findings because its detector failed | Coverage matrix plus detector-status evidence | Cell is `unknown`, not silently interpreted as “no defects.” |
| Several agents agree because they consumed one generated digest | Origin/method clustering | Corroboration count remains one independent family. |
| A module’s conclusion depends on a hidden assumption made elsewhere | Module `assumes`/`imports` contract | Missing or contested imported assumption blocks its guarantee. |
| A critical objection was “resolved” only by another assertion | Defeater must terminate in evidence or accepted-risk disposition | Defeater subgraph remains open. |
| A proposed replacement optimizes one criterion while violating another | Top claim decomposed by explicit acceptance criteria | Missing or contested criterion branch is visible in the decision spine. |
| The final digest is too large | Fixed argument-spine renderer with drill-down IDs | Render fails budget rather than arbitrarily truncating evidence. |

I judge the deterministic checks in this table **almost certain (90-99%)** to catch their corresponding deliberately constructed fixtures because each maps to an explicit required field or graph invariant. Evidence is the schema and linter specification above, not empirical deployment. Real-world recall is **UNVERIFIED; the settling experiment is the mutation suite plus retrospective replay on a stratified sample of prior decisions with expert-labeled omissions and contradictions.**

The mechanism will **not** catch a fabricated but internally consistent source, a universally shared false assumption, a missing research domain not represented in the coverage manifest, or a semantically invalid inference that passes the structural rationale check. Those require source verification, adversarial domain review, and owner judgment. The case makes those responsibilities locatable; it does not automate truth.

## 5. Decision semantics: avoid a fake master score

The compiler should not turn thousands of claims into a weighted “truth score.” Evidence volume is not evidential independence, severity is not probability, and owner preferences are not sensor measurements. NASA’s Baconian-confidence memorandum specifically cautions that ordinal rankings of defeated doubts do not license arithmetic manipulation ([NASA/TM-2016-219341](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20160013333/downloads/20160013333.pdf)).

Use a four-state decision surface instead:

- **SUPPORTED:** every mandatory criterion has a supported branch and no open blocker.
- **CONTESTED:** at least one decision-relevant defeater remains open.
- **INSUFFICIENT:** a mandatory criterion lacks evidence under the declared scope.
- **OUT-OF-SCOPE:** the case explicitly declines the decision.

Severity and confidence stay separate. A finding may have consequence (`critical/high/medium/low`) and epistemic status (`measured/corroborated/single-source/judgment/unknown`). The renderer orders review by high consequence plus weak epistemic status; it does not multiply the labels.

I judge a non-numeric state machine **very likely (80-90%)** to be safer than an invented aggregate score for initial adoption because it preserves unresolved doubts and avoids unsupported probability arithmetic; NASA documents precisely that arithmetic problem ([NASA/TM-2016-219341](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20160013333/downloads/20160013333.pdf)). Whether owners find it usable is **UNVERIFIED; the settling experiment is an A/B decision review measuring calibration, reversal after source drill-down, and reviewer explanation quality for the state machine versus a scalar dashboard.**

## 6. Validation and falsification plan

### Stage 1 — structural fixtures

Create small hand-authored cases containing known cycles, missing source locators, shared-origin duplicates, stale evidence, contradicted claims, hidden imports, and unresolved defeaters. The compiler must reject or downgrade each case exactly as specified. Include known-valid controls so detector failure is distinguishable from true absence.

Pass gate: every injected structural defect is detected, every known-valid control passes, and diagnostics identify the affected node. This validates mechanics only, not decision quality.

### Stage 2 — mutation test on real-shaped packets

Take a resolved packet and independently apply one mutation at a time: remove a critical finding; change its scope; replace direct evidence with a judgment; clone one source across ten paths; reverse polarity; mark evidence stale; disconnect a criterion branch. Measure mutation detection and false alarms. Use separate mutation generation and case compilation mechanisms so the detector is not tested against its own assumptions.

Pass gate: all high-consequence omissions, contradictions, and provenance clones become visible in `decision.md` or `open-defeaters.md`; benign formatting changes do not.

### Stage 3 — blinded comparative replay

Randomize previously adjudicated synthesis tasks across raw corpus, prose digest, and assurance packet. Blind decision agents to condition and expected answer. Record:

- agreement with expert-adjudicated decision;
- recall of adjudicated critical objections;
- unsupported-claim rate;
- source-locator precision;
- tokens read and elapsed time;
- decision reversal after targeted drill-down;
- inter-reviewer agreement.

The proposal earns adoption only if it improves critical-objection recall and traceability without unacceptable decision degradation, and materially reduces context. “Prettier output” is not a pass condition.

### Stage 4 — adversarial dependence test

Seed one plausible false claim into a common upstream summary consumed by several agents, while placing contrary primary evidence in a separate method family. Compare naive majority synthesis, prose digest, and origin-aware defeater case. This directly tests the Swiss-cheese/common-mode warning.

### Stage 5 — shadow operation and kill criteria

Run the packet beside, not in control of, several live decisions. Kill or redesign it if any of the following occurs: reviewers treat `SUPPORTED` as proof; source drill-down frequently fails; module contracts hide material cross-domain interactions; case maintenance costs approach rereading the corpus; or the compiler expands into its own orchestration/runtime layer.

I judge this staged test plan **very likely (80-90%)** to falsify the most important structural claims because it combines known-present controls, injected faults, independent expert adjudication, and a common-mode attack. Evidence is the direct mapping between each experiment and the claimed mechanism. It remains **UNVERIFIED; execution of all five stages is the settling experiment.**

## 7. Anti-overfit gate and rejected expansions

The accepted design rides the existing skill/hook/MCP bus and adds only a tiny interchange format, deterministic validation/rendering, and configuration. It does not add a second orchestration runtime, vector database, knowledge graph service, durable-execution engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, or guardrail framework. Although the case is a graph mathematically, `case.json` is an artifact, not a graph database or memory service.

Rejected expansions:

- **Full SACM implementation:** rejected as unnecessary standards surface. The subset needs compatibility of ideas, not a new modeling platform.
- **Assurance-case daemon:** rejected. Batch compilation at a normal lifecycle hook is sufficient until measured evidence proves otherwise.
- **LLM debate swarm:** rejected as the core mechanism because participants may share common-mode errors and token cost grows with the evidence volume. A challenger may propose defeaters, but deterministic provenance and a human decision remain authoritative.
- **Bayesian master score:** rejected because dependence and priors are not available, and ordinal assurance judgments do not license arithmetic.
- **Vector/KG memory:** explicitly rejected as a previously disallowed component. Stable IDs and reverse indexes in ordinary JSON are enough for this bounded artifact.
- **Swiss-cheese “more agents” layering:** rejected because barrier independence is unproven and the metaphor supplies no evidence semantics.
- **Full STPA of the harness for this problem:** rejected because it models more than the synthesis bottleneck requires.

I judge the minimal design **likely (55-80%)** to retain its benefit as base models improve because its value is mechanical provenance, explicit scope, contradiction preservation, and bounded drill-down rather than compensating for a particular model’s reasoning weakness. Evidence: SACM formalizes these relations independently of any model, and the safety-case report/case distinction is an information architecture property ([OMG SACM 2.3](https://www.omg.org/spec/SACM/2.3), [UK MOD ASEMS](https://www.asems.mod.uk/guidance/posms/smp12)). Long-term value is **UNVERIFIED; the settling experiment is to replay the benchmark whenever the base model changes and remove any component whose marginal gain disappears.**

## 8. Implementation order and stop conditions

1. Define ten to twenty representative fixture cases and the acceptance criteria before code.
2. Implement the minimal JSONL-to-graph compiler and lint invariants under the BUILD-SMALL cap.
3. Add the coverage manifest as configuration.
4. Produce the fixed-budget argument spine and open-defeater report.
5. Run mutation tests.
6. Run blinded replay against raw evidence and ordinary prose summaries.
7. Adopt only if it clears the empirical gate; otherwise retain the finding schema/provenance lessons and discard the case renderer.

Stop immediately if stable source locators cannot be produced, because the graph would then be a structured collection of hearsay. Stop if module boundaries cannot expose cross-domain dependencies without routinely opening all internals, because the promised compression would be illusory.
