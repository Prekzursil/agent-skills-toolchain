## Quality rubric and verdict semantics

- **Excellent**: unusually actionable, scoped, evidence-gated, and unique; current corpus use strengthens but is not required.
- **Healthy**: useful and adequately scoped; minor thinness or maintenance cost does not impair use.
- **Improve**: keep the capability, but a concrete defect, drift, excess size, missing asset, or over-broad scope degrades it.
- **Merge**: useful content exists, but another entry is the better front door/canonical home.
- **Retire**: not a live capability, explicitly deprecated/archived, a tombstone/container, or cost-asymmetric dead weight.
- Defect grade `cosmetic` does not change expected task outcome; `degrading` can harm routing, context, consistency, or execution; `breaking` can block legitimate work or routes to a known-nonexistent surface.

All per-item qualitative verdicts below are **likely (55-80%)** unless the note names mechanically conclusive evidence. `Use` is exact corpus-label count, not recency or success. Every name denotes `<root>\<name>\SKILL.md`.
