# B001 — Groundedness: citation resolver

Date: 2026-07-19  
Unit: B001  
Scope: design only; the live harness, subject configuration, and repository were read but not modified.

## Executive decision

Build a conservative citation resolver as a skill and small receipt-producing module on the existing harness bus. For every factual claim carrying a local `file:line` citation or URL, it must (1) parse the claim/citation relationship, (2) open the exact target under bounded security rules, (3) preserve the fetched bytes and a content hash, (4) locate the cited evidence, and (5) issue exactly one of:

- `RESOLVES+SUPPORTS`
- `RESOLVES+CONTRADICTS`
- `RESOLVES+IRRELEVANT`
- `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE`

The resolver must never equate “the path/URL opened” with “the citation supports the claim.” Resolution is deterministic; support is a separate, claim-level judgment. A valid citation can point to irrelevant prose, a contradictory value, or a source that supports only half of a compound sentence.

**Proposal tag: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).** The production core should be one bounded Python resolver plus a `citation-resolver` skill; semantic adjudication should use an existing agent/model invocation rather than a new daemon, vector store, knowledge graph, trained critic, or orchestration runtime. Tests and fixtures will be larger than the production core. This proposal passes the anti-overfit gate because it composes with the current dispatcher, skill surface, and additive ledger rather than replacing them: the dispatcher already runs a list of modules (`C:\Users\Prekzursil\.agents\harness\dispatcher.py:237-279`), `Stop` is already a registered event with a five-second budget (`C:\Users\Prekzursil\.agents\harness\registration.py:17-54`), and ledger events accept sanitized additive fields (`C:\Users\Prekzursil\.agents\harness\ledger.py:851-880`).

It is **very likely (80-90%)** that this mechanism will catch a material class of fabricated, stale, off-by-line, and merely-topical citations before a grounded artifact is accepted. Evidence: the known-defects file records two orchestrator-authored false premises that propagated downstream, including a hook-telemetry absence claim later disproved by two independent transcript probes and a fabricated Write-tool constraint (`INPUT-known-harness-defects.md:133-162,182-192`); the proposed resolver directly re-opens cited exhibits instead of trusting the brief. The exact local catch rate is **UNVERIFIED**; the settling experiment is the balanced, blinded mutation benchmark in §12.

It is **almost certainly not (1-5%)** capable of eliminating hallucination. Evidence: it sees only extracted claims with citations; a model still performs claim decomposition and semantic entailment; sources themselves can be wrong; and even SAFE agreed with crowd labels only 72% across roughly 16,000 facts ([NeurIPS 2024 SAFE paper](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2024/hash/937ae0e83eb08d2cb8627fe1def8c751-Abstract-Conference.html)). The correct promise is detection with explicit abstention, not truth by construction.

## 2. Research basis and design implications

The research converges on four useful principles, while also showing a ceiling:

1. **Atomize before judging.** FActScore defines an atomic fact as one short statement carrying one piece of information and validates each against a knowledge source; it reports that a single sentence averaged 4.4 facts in its ChatGPT sample and could mix supported and unsupported content ([FActScore, lines 11-20](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14251)). FactLens likewise argues that holistic labels obscure errors, while warning that subclaim generation can lose context or semantic equivalence ([ACL Findings 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.929/)). Implication: claim extraction must preserve a parent span and context, and compound claims cannot pass because one clause is supported.

2. **Measure citation correctness separately from answer correctness.** ALCE evaluates fluency, correctness, and citation quality separately, and reports incomplete citation support even among its best systems ([EMNLP 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/)). Implication: B001 reports resolution, evidence location, and support as separate fields rather than one “grounded” score.

3. **Deterministic span existence is stronger than generic model scoring, but narrower.** The 2026 ResearchQA preprint uses a deterministic matcher to check that a cited passage appears in the claimed paper and reports that citation-based metrics separated systems more clearly than compressed LLM-evaluator scores ([ResearchQA preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11074)). This is recent non-peer-reviewed evidence, so transfer to local code artifacts is **likely (55-80%)**, not established; evidence is the method’s direct structural match to `file:line`, and the settling experiment is the §12 local benchmark.

4. **Semantic judges require local calibration.** A July 2026 preprint evaluating eight citation judges on 1,248 human-reviewed decisions found meaningful differences in false-positive and false-negative drift even when F1 was similar ([citation-verifier calibration preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08700)). SAFE’s 72% agreement with crowd labels also rules out treating an LLM verifier as an oracle ([NeurIPS 2024](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2024/hash/937ae0e83eb08d2cb8627fe1def8c751-Abstract-Conference.html)). Implication: semantic verdicts start advisory and expose their evidence quote; only deterministic resolution failures can graduate to enforcement early.

VeriFact additionally finds that decompose/decontextualize/verify pipelines can drop essential context and relational facts ([EMNLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.905/)). Therefore each atom retains `parent_claim`, antecedent-expanded context, qualifiers, modality, units, dates, entity names, and the exact artifact byte span.

## 3. Threat model: what B001 catches and what it cannot

### Caught classes

The resolver is designed to catch:

- nonexistent files, out-of-range lines, malformed URLs, redirect loops, retrieval failures, unsupported content types, missing anchors, and blocked targets;
- line drift where a quoted exhibit moved, while clearly marking that the original location is stale;
- a real target whose cited lines are merely on-topic;
- explicit numeric, date, entity, polarity, or status contradictions;
- partial support hidden inside compound prose;
- citation laundering, where several claims share one broad source that supports only some of them;
- quote fabrication, because the returned supporting excerpt must round-trip back into the fetched bytes;
- source mutation between review and use, because the receipt records SHA-256, byte length, final URL/path, and access time.

It is **very likely (80-90%)** that exact local-path and line-range failures can be detected with near-zero ambiguity, because existence, canonical path, line count, and byte hashing are deterministic operations. The numeric false-positive rate is **UNVERIFIED**; replay all gold `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE` and `RESOLVES+*` fixtures across NTFS paths, symlinks, Git revisions, CRLF/LF, UTF-8/UTF-16, and concurrent edits to settle it.

### Not caught or not proven

B001 does not establish that a source is authoritative, complete, current, unbiased, or itself true. It cannot see uncited hallucinations unless a separate coverage pass first identifies citation-worthy claims. It cannot reliably adjudicate normative claims, causal inference, nuanced synthesis, images/tables lost during extraction, or claims requiring several documents unless those dependencies are explicitly represented. It cannot prove that a URL will serve the same bytes tomorrow. It cannot turn correlated model agreement into ground truth.

An adversarial artifact can also cite a passage that literally contains the same words while quoting a negation, table header, example, deprecated section, or third-party quotation. Exact matching catches fabricated quotes but not misleading context. Semantic review and a surrounding evidence window are mandatory.

## 4. Input and output contract

Invocation:

```text
/citation-resolver <artifact> [--root <allowed-root> ...] [--mode shadow|advise|enforce-resolution]
```

The artifact is read once and hashed before parsing. If it changes during the run, the run terminates `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE` for every pending claim with `reason=artifact_changed_during_review`; it must not combine claims from one version with citations from another.

## 5. Citation extraction and claim association

### 5.1 Parse structure, not regex alone

Use `markdown-it-py` for Markdown block/inline structure; it is present in the current Python environment (read-only import probe on 2026-07-19 returned `FOUND markdown_it`). A bounded fallback lexer handles plain text. Regex is used only inside text/code tokens to recognize citation syntax, never to infer semantics. This directly answers the current use-versus-mention defect: the defects file shows four scanners misclassifying prose, negation, JSON field names, and quoted commands because they ignored semantic role (`INPUT-known-harness-defects.md:9-82`).

Recognized local forms:

```text
C:\path\file.py:123
C:\path\file.py:123-131
C:\path\file.py:123,127-131
relative/path.md:44
`path with spaces/file.md:8-12`
[label](/absolute/path/file.py:77)
git:origin/main:path/to/file.py#L77-L89
```

Windows drive colons are consumed before the final line suffix. Trailing punctuation is removed only if it is outside matching backticks, brackets, or parentheses. A path without a line is recorded as a target citation but cannot satisfy a `file:line` requirement; it is `RESOLVES+IRRELEVANT` unless the claim is explicitly about whole-file existence/hash.

Recognized URL forms are Markdown links and bare `http://` or `https://` URLs. Link text is never treated as evidence. `file:`, `javascript:`, `data:`, credential-bearing URLs, and non-HTTP schemes are rejected as `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE`.

### 5.2 Exclusions and mentions

By default, citations in headings, paragraphs, list items, tables, blockquotes, and footnotes are candidates. URLs or `file:line` strings inside fenced code are classified as examples/mentions unless the immediately preceding prose says that the block itself is the evidence list. The artifact’s own source section is parsed, but bibliography entries with no adjacent factual claim are marked `non_claim_reference` and excluded from the claim denominator.

This behavior is **likely (55-80%)** to reduce the known use-versus-mention false-positive class, because it uses Markdown node type and adjacency rather than a payload-wide substring. The local rate is **UNVERIFIED**; the settling experiment is to replay all recorded D1 examples plus 100 citation-bearing code/prose pairs through the parser.

### 5.3 Associate citations before atomization

Association boundaries, in order:

1. inline citation → smallest containing sentence or semicolon-delimited independent clause;
2. citation-only tail after a colon → preceding clause in the same list item/table cell;
3. citation-only line → immediately preceding nonblank paragraph/list item, stopping at a heading or blank-list boundary;
4. footnote → every claim that references that footnote, independently;
5. citation after a paragraph → that paragraph only, never the whole section.

If one citation is syntactically equidistant from two claims, emit two records with `association_ambiguous=true`; neither may automatically support. If a factual sentence has no citation, the resolver emits `UNCITED` in a separate coverage report, not one of the four citation verdicts. This preserves the required four-way contract while making citation coverage visible.

### 5.4 Atomic claim decomposition

First split deterministically on Markdown blocks and sentence boundaries. Then ask the existing model lane to decompose only sentences containing conjunctions, multiple numbers/entities, or multiple independent verbs. Every atom must be entailed by the parent sentence; the parent text and byte span remain attached. A second prompt performs a reconstruction check: all atoms joined together must neither add nor drop a parent proposition. Failure yields one unsplit parent record with `atomization_failed=true` and semantic authority `advisory`.

The model must expand pronouns only from the immediately preceding sentence and must retain negation, hedges (`may`, `likely`), time, units, comparison direction, and source attribution. FactLens and VeriFact both identify subclaim/context loss as a verification risk ([FactLens](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.929/), [VeriFact](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.905/)).

## 6. Target resolution and opening algorithm

### 6.1 Local `file:line`

For each local citation:

1. Parse the raw path and line/range without shell interpolation.
2. Expand environment variables only from an explicit allowlist (`USERPROFILE` is not needed for absolute artifact citations); never execute command substitutions.
3. Resolve relative paths against, in order: artifact directory, declared repository root, then explicitly supplied `--root` values. Zero matches → `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE`. More than one distinct match → `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE` with `ambiguous_path`.
4. Canonicalize with `Path.resolve(strict=True)` and confirm it remains under an allowed root. Symlinks/junctions are checked after resolution.
5. Refuse credential/key stores and device paths even if under an allowed root. The default deny patterns include `.env`, `auth.json`, credential files, SSH keys, browser profiles, and Windows device namespaces.
6. Open read-only in binary mode, cap at 10 MiB by default, hash raw bytes, detect BOM/encoding, and normalize newlines only for line addressing. Preserve a byte-to-normalized-line map.
7. Confirm every requested line is within `1..line_count`. A partly out-of-range range does not truncate; it is `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE`.
8. Re-stat after read. Size/mtime change or hash mismatch on a second bounded read yields `target_changed_during_review`.

Git citations use `git show <rev> -- <path>` with an argv array, never a shell string. The requested revision and resolved commit SHA are recorded. If a path exists only on `origin/main`, this form preserves that fact rather than falsely mapping it to a working-tree file.

### 6.2 URLs

URL extraction follows URI structure, not a whitespace-only regex. RFC 3986 defines relative-reference resolution and separates fragments from the primary resource ([RFC 3986](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc3986/)). The resolver therefore records both the fetched URL and fragment outcome; HTTP 200 alone does not prove that `#section` exists.

Opening rules:

1. Allow only `http` and `https`, with no embedded username/password.
2. Resolve DNS and reject loopback, private, link-local, multicast, unspecified, and metadata-service addresses before each request and after every redirect. Re-check the connected peer where the client exposes it; otherwise mark `dns_rebinding_check=partial`.
3. Use `GET` with TLS verification, a descriptive user agent, 10-second connect and 20-second total timeout, five redirects maximum, and 10 MiB decompressed maximum. `HEAD` is not authoritative because servers implement it inconsistently.
4. Record status, redirect chain, final URL, content type, ETag/Last-Modified when supplied, raw-byte SHA-256, byte count, and access timestamp.
5. HTML is converted to visible text while retaining heading/anchor offsets. JSON and plain text retain JSON pointers/line maps. PDF uses the already-present `pdftotext` executable in a temporary directory and records tool version plus page boundaries. Unsupported binary/media → `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE`.
6. A fragment must map to an HTML `id`/`name`, a text-fragment selector, or a supported PDF page. Missing fragment → `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE` even when the page opens.
7. Authentication wall, robot denial, anti-bot challenge, empty JavaScript shell, or truncated payload → `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE`, not “irrelevant.” A browser-backed retry may be attached as a second resolution backend, but it must hash the rendered text and state that it differs from raw HTTP.

Opening arbitrary cited URLs creates SSRF and data-exfiltration risk. The resolver must not send cookies, ambient authorization headers, repository contents, or claim text to the cited host. Semantic judging receives the already-fetched bounded evidence locally. This security boundary is deterministic and should be enforced from the first release.

## 7. Evidence-span matching: exact, fuzzy, semantic

These are sequential layers with different authority. Fuzzy and semantic similarity must never upgrade a broken target into a resolved one.

### 7.1 Exact location and exact quote

For `file:line`, the primary evidence span is exactly the requested lines. Add up to three lines of surrounding context for interpretation, but visually distinguish cited from context lines. For anchored URLs, use the anchored section; for unanchored pages, candidate passages are lexical retrieval windows, and the absence of a precise locator is a warning.

If the artifact contains a quoted passage, perform:

1. raw UTF-8/decoded substring match;
2. normalized match with CRLF→LF, Unicode NFC, and runs of horizontal whitespace collapsed;
3. case remains significant; punctuation and negation are not removed.

An exact quote match proves only `quote_present=true`. It does not prove support. False-positive behavior: duplicate boilerplate or a quote appearing inside a negated/example context can match exactly. The surrounding-window semantic stage must still label it. The local exact-locator false-positive rate is **UNVERIFIED**; a corpus of repeated headers, examples, negations, and quote-within-quote fixtures settles it. Expected direction is **very likely (80-90%)** lower false support than semantic-only matching because byte/line identity is mechanically constrained; evidence is the deterministic passage-existence design used by ResearchQA, not a local measurement ([ResearchQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11074)).

### 7.2 Fuzzy span relocation

Fuzzy matching is a recovery aid for stale line numbers, not evidence authority. Use Python’s standard-library `difflib.SequenceMatcher` over normalized token sequences, avoiding a new dependency (the current environment probe found no `rapidfuzz`). Concrete algorithm:

- require a quoted anchor of at least 8 non-stopword tokens or 40 characters;
- generate source windows from 0.8× to 1.2× the anchor token length;
- search first within ±50 lines of the cited location, then the whole target only if local search fails;
- score `2*M/(len(anchor)+len(window))` using the contiguous matching blocks returned by `SequenceMatcher`;
- accept relocation only at score `>=0.92`, with exactly one best window and a margin `>=0.05` over runner-up;
- record original and actual locator; never rewrite the artifact automatically;
- if thresholds fail or candidates tie, the original locator remains `RESOLVES+IRRELEVANT` when it exists but does not support, or `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE` when the requested line is invalid.

False-positive behavior: templated prose, license text, repeated table rows, short quotes, reordered clauses, and punctuation-stripped negation can create high similarity. The minimum length, uniqueness margin, and preservation of negation reduce but do not eliminate it. Both thresholds are **UNVERIFIED**; tune them only on a held-out mutation set that includes repeated boilerplate and negation, optimizing first for false relocation rather than recall. Until that experiment, fuzzy relocation has `authority=locator-advisory` and cannot cause `RESOLVES+SUPPORTS` by itself.

### 7.3 Semantic support/contradiction

The semantic input is exactly `{atomic_claim, parent_claim, cited_span, context_span, source_metadata}`. The judge returns one of `entailed`, `contradicted`, `neutral`, plus the shortest decisive evidence quote and a reason. The quote must then exact-round-trip into the fetched target. If it does not, the semantic verdict is discarded as `RESOLVES+IRRELEVANT` with `judge_quote_not_found`.

Decision rules:

- `entailed`: every entity, number, unit, date, qualifier, polarity, relation, and scope in the atom follows from the evidence without unstated domain knowledge;
- `contradicted`: the evidence directly asserts an incompatible value/status/relation or explicitly negates the atom;
- `neutral`: on-topic, incomplete, ambiguous, silent, merely recommended, or requiring external inference.

Run the same prompt twice with claim/evidence order varied. Agreement is not truth, but disagreement is a useful abstention signal. A cross-vendor second judge is reserved for load-bearing contradictions or contested support; universal debate is unnecessary.

False-positive behavior: semantic models can accept topical overlap, fill missing premises, overlook negation/units, trust source prestige, or become biased by the parent artifact’s wording. The round-trip quote and strict-neutral default constrain this, but the local false-support rate is **UNVERIFIED**; the §12 blinded benchmark settles it. The 2026 calibration preprint reports that citation judges with similar F1 still differ in directional false-positive drift, so no uncalibrated semantic judge may block or certify completion ([source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08700)).

### 7.4 Why not embeddings alone

Embedding similarity is not an entailment test: a contradiction is usually semantically close to the claim. ResearchQA explicitly reports that embedding similarity can pass fabricated-but-on-topic text ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11074)). No vector database or knowledge graph is proposed. Lexical candidate selection plus explicit entail/contradict/neutral judgment is smaller and safer for this harness.

## 8. Four verdicts, exactly defined

### `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE`

Use when the target or requested locator cannot be opened and reproduced: nonexistent/ambiguous path, invalid/out-of-range lines, disallowed target, failed URL, redirect/size/timeout limit, missing fragment, unsupported content, extraction failure, or mutation during read. It says nothing about whether the underlying claim is true.

### `RESOLVES+SUPPORTS`

Use only when at least one target resolves, every atomic proposition is entailed by the cited evidence (individually or by an explicitly recorded union of citations), no resolved evidence directly contradicts it, and the decisive evidence quote round-trips. Exact quote presence alone is insufficient.

### `RESOLVES+CONTRADICTS`

Use when resolved evidence directly negates or supplies an incompatible fact for the atom. Mere absence is not contradiction. If sources conflict, retain this verdict with `source_conflict=true`, list both sides, and require human review; do not silently pick the preferred source.

### `RESOLVES+IRRELEVANT`

Use for every resolved but non-supporting/non-contradicting case: topical only, partial support, ambiguous scope, source is a bibliography/home page rather than evidence, citation covers another nearby claim, judge abstains, or decisive quote does not round-trip. This is the conservative “insufficient” bucket required by the four-label interface.

### Aggregation across citations

Evaluate every claim–citation pair first, then aggregate:

1. all citations fail → `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE`;
2. any direct contradiction → `RESOLVES+CONTRADICTS` (set conflict if support also exists);
3. union of resolved citations covers every atom, with no contradiction → `RESOLVES+SUPPORTS`;
4. otherwise → `RESOLVES+IRRELEVANT`.

This ordering deliberately treats contradictory evidence as load-bearing. It is **likely (55-80%)** to surface more dangerous failures than support-first aggregation because conflicts remain visible; the local precision/annoyance tradeoff is **UNVERIFIED**, settled by comparing both policies on the §12 conflict subset.

## 9. Pipeline pseudocode

```python
artifact_bytes = stable_read(artifact)
artifact_hash = sha256(artifact_bytes)
nodes = parse_markdown(artifact_bytes)
pairs = associate_claims_and_citations(nodes)
atoms = decompose_with_reconstruction_check(pairs)

for atom in atoms:
    pair_results = []
    for citation in atom.citations:
        target = resolve_under_policy(citation, artifact, allowed_roots)
        if not target.ok:
            pair_results.append(unresolved(target.reason))
            continue
        exhibit = stable_open_hash_extract(target)
        span = exact_locator(exhibit, citation.locator)
        relocation = fuzzy_relocate_only_if_quoted(span, atom.parent_claim)
        semantic = judge(atom, span.with_context())
        semantic = require_quote_roundtrip(semantic, exhibit)
        pair_results.append(classify_pair(target, span, relocation, semantic))
    emit_jsonl(aggregate(atom, pair_results))

assert sha256(read_bytes(artifact)) == artifact_hash
render_markdown_from_jsonl()
emit_terminal_summary()
```

## 11. Cost controls and failure behavior

Cheap-first order:

1. parse and deduplicate identical citations;
2. open each unique local target once;
3. cache URL bytes only for the current run, keyed by final URL plus content hash;
4. exact location/quote;
5. fuzzy relocation only on failed quoted anchors;
6. semantic review only for resolved claim–evidence pairs;
7. second judge only for high-stakes contradictions/disagreement.

Batch semantic records by source section, not by the entire artifact, to bound context and prevent one malformed record from losing the batch. Preserve one JSONL line per claim even when later claims fail. This choice directly responds to the 22.9% observed `StructuredOutput` error rate (`corpus/corpus.json:102-108`).

Expected performance is **UNVERIFIED**. Measure p50/p95 wall time separately for local-text, HTML, and PDF; URL bytes; model input/output tokens; cache hit rate; and number of second-judge escalations. A reasonable pilot budget is not asserted as fact. The rollout should set limits from the first 50 real artifacts, then preregister them before evaluation.

Fail closed for the individual claim, fail open for the parent session: an internal parser/fetch/judge error yields `DOES-NOT-RESOLVE` or `RESOLVES+IRRELEVANT` with a reason and a `FAILED:` summary, but the hook itself injects rather than crashes. Never synthesize a support verdict from missing evidence.

## 12. Evaluation and calibration plan

### 12.1 Gold set

Construct at least 240 claim–citation examples, balanced 60 per required verdict:

- 60 genuine supports from current harness/audit artifacts;
- 60 contradictions created by controlled number/date/entity/polarity/status mutations;
- 60 irrelevant citations using same-topic wrong sections, abstracts, home pages, examples, or neighboring claims;
- 60 non-resolving cases covering missing file, out-of-range/off-by-one lines, ambiguous relative path, moved Git content, 404, redirect loop, missing fragment, auth wall, oversized content, and unsupported binary.

Add dedicated slices for Windows drive colons, spaces, parentheses, CRLF/LF, UTF-16, symlinks/junctions, Git revisions, PDF pages/tables, multiple citations jointly supporting one claim, conflicting sources, code fences, negation, and duplicated boilerplate.

At least two humans independently label each example while blinded to resolver output; adjudicate disagreements and retain rationale plus decisive source span. Raw `owner_correction_candidates` are not labels because their own corpus field reports 41/41 smoke-sample false positives (`corpus/corpus.json:10371-10374`).

### 12.2 Metrics

Report:

- four-way confusion matrix and per-class precision/recall/F1;
- **false-support rate**: gold contradiction/irrelevant/unresolved labeled `SUPPORTS` (primary safety metric);
- false-contradiction rate;
- resolution false-positive and false-negative rates separately from semantics;
- quote round-trip success;
- exact/fuzzy/semantic contribution and error rate by tier;
- citation coverage (citation-worthy claims with at least one citation);
- p50/p95 latency, bytes fetched, model tokens, and cost;
- abstention/irrelevant rate;
- inter-annotator agreement and adjudication rate;
- source mutation reproducibility by stored hash.

Do not report accuracy alone. A resolver that calls everything irrelevant can appear safe while providing no value.

### 12.3 Ablations

Run the same held-out set with:

1. URL/path reachability only;
2. exact locator/quote only;
3. exact + fuzzy;
4. exact + semantic;
5. full cascade;
6. one judge versus order-swapped repeated judge;
7. parent sentence versus context-preserving atoms.

This directly measures whether fuzzy matching or model decomposition adds catch rate worth its false positives. No component graduates merely because literature suggests it.

### 12.4 Graduation gates

- `SHADOW → ADVISE`: all output schema/receipt invariants pass; every positive control resolves; no internal error becomes support; qualitative inspection of all false supports.
- `ADVISE → ENFORCE-RESOLUTION`: zero false blocks on the positive-control suite and two weeks of real artifacts; Wilson interval and raw numerator/denominator reported, not “0%” alone.
- semantic support remains advisory until at least 500 blinded, representative claim–citation pairs show a preregistered false-support bound acceptable to the owner. Even then it may fail a citation-quality artifact gate but never supersede deterministic execution receipts.

The exact acceptable false-support bound is **UNVERIFIED** and owner-dependent; preregister it before seeing held-out results. The experiment is the 500-pair calibration plus cost-of-error review by artifact class.

## 15. Implementation acceptance criteria

The implementation is not done until all are true:

1. Every recognized claim/citation pair emits exactly one of the four required labels.
2. Every result includes artifact hash, target hash or explicit failure reason, requested locator, actual locator, accessed time, decisive evidence quote, and authority.

9. Semantic disagreements abstain to `IRRELEVANT` or surface a source conflict; they do not silently choose.
10. The resolver detects artifact/target mutation during review.
11. JSONL survives partial failures and renders the Markdown summary deterministically.
12. The known D1 use-versus-mention examples do not trigger as real citations unless explicitly marked evidence.
13. Positive controls demonstrate the detector can find known-present file, line, URL, fragment, quote, support, contradiction, and irrelevant cases before any absence conclusion.
