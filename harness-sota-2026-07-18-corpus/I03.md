# I03 — Initiative / anticipation calibration: when an unprompted improvement is welcome

Date: 2026-07-19  
Unit: I03  
Family: initiative / anticipation calibration  
Scope: design and research only; the unpacked harness subject was read-only and was not modified.

## Executive answer

The discrimination rule should be **discover freely, preserve automatically, deliver sparingly**.
An agent may notice any number of opportunities, but a noticed opportunity is not permission to
interrupt, ask, or implement. Every opportunity first becomes a typed, deduplicated event on the
existing event-ledger. A deterministic policy then chooses one of five dispositions:

1. **DO-NOW** — execute without asking only when the addition is directly instrumental to the
   current accepted outcome, reversible, bounded, within already-authorized files/systems, and has
   an objective post-action verifier.
2. **SURFACE-NOW** — show one compact card only when an external signal establishes urgency or a
   material correctness/safety consequence and delay would make the main outcome worse.
3. **ASK-NOW** — use one owner question only when the proposal is high-value but would expand
   authority, spend materially more time/money, make an external commitment, or resolve genuinely
   outcome-changing ambiguity.
4. **ESCROW** — preserve and deduplicate the opportunity without interrupting; surface at a
   receptive boundary or in a batch digest.
5. **DROP** — suppress duplicates, unsupported intuitions, self-certified benefits, stale items,
   and ideas whose expected benefit does not clear their interruption/verification cost.

This is a **lattice, not a scalar “proactivity score.”** A high-value idea cannot override an
authority boundary; high model confidence cannot substitute for evidence; and an idea that is not
urgent cannot pre-empt the active thread. The same model that proposed an idea is never allowed to
certify it. Promotion requires a different-context or objective signal: a test/build/run receipt,
current tool output, a fresh-context verifier, a later owner action, or an owner decision.

The policy is **very likely (80-90%)** to fit this owner better than either “never suggest” or
“suggest everything,” because the local corpus contains both a repeated invitation to implement
“ideas you get along the way” and repeated corrections to continue/finish the main thread, while
the best controlled coding study found benefit from moderate proactive suggestions but sharply
lower preference for a persistent variant. Local evidence:
`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/corpus.json:10377-10394,10425-10426`.
External evidence: Chen et al., CHI 2025,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04596 and DOI https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3714002.

## The discrimination contract

Each candidate must carry this minimal record before it can be delivered:

```text
opportunity_id      stable hash(category, target, normalized proposal)
goal_ref            active ledger goal identifier
relationship        REQUIRED | ENABLES | IMPROVES | ADJACENT
external_trigger    receipt/event/tool-result/owner-message identifier
authority           IN_SCOPE | EXPANDS_SCOPE | EXTERNAL_COMMITMENT
reversibility       TRIVIAL | CHECKPOINTED | COSTLY | IRREVERSIBLE
verification        exact command/tool/owner decision that can falsify benefit
urgency             NOW | NEXT_BOUNDARY | LATER, derived from external event
effort_class        MINUTES | SUBTASK | PROJECT
category            tests/security/reliability/ux/capability/research/etc.
status              CAPTURED | VERIFIED | SURFACED | ACCEPTED | DEFERRED |
                    DISMISSED | EXECUTED | VERIFIED_DONE | STALE
```

The fields are intentionally categorical. A 294-field or fan-out schema would overfit and be
fragile in a workspace where StructuredOutput failed 846 of 3,694 calls (22.9%)
(`corpus.json:103-108`). The compact record can be JSONL on the current ledger bus and does not
require StructuredOutput from the conversational model.

Disposition is deterministic:

```text
if no external_trigger or no exact falsifier: DROP
elif duplicate or contradicted or stale: DROP/TAINT
elif authority != IN_SCOPE or reversibility in {COSTLY, IRREVERSIBLE}:
    ASK-NOW only at a receptive boundary; otherwise ESCROW
elif relationship == REQUIRED:
    DO-NOW under the original task authority, then require external receipt
elif urgency == NOW and delay_cost is externally demonstrated:
    SURFACE-NOW (one card); ASK-NOW only if owner authority is required
elif relationship in {ENABLES, IMPROVES}:
    DO-NOW only inside the bounded autonomy envelope; otherwise ESCROW
else: ESCROW
```

The “bounded autonomy envelope” is conjunctive: same accepted outcome; same repo/system; no new
credential, publication, purchase, account, remote deployment, or human communication; checkpoint
or trivial undo exists; estimated effort is a small minority of the active task; and an executable
verifier exists. The exact effort cutoff is **UNVERIFIED**. Start in shadow with a candidate cutoff
of 10% of the main task’s observed elapsed time; compare false promotions and owner undo/defer rates
across 5%, 10%, and 20% in Finding 8. The model's own elapsed-time estimate is forbidden—the clock
and task events are the external signal.

## Findings (inferred-surprise first)

### Finding 1 — UNVERIFIED IN THIS WORKSPACE: opportunity escrow before notification

- **Improvement:** Introduce a silent, typed “opportunity escrow.” The surprising move is to make
  *capture* maximally proactive while making *delivery* conservative. An idea noticed during work is
  automatically preserved, deduplicated, and attached to the active goal; it does not immediately
  become a message or question.
- **What it is:** A new `kind="opportunity"` event in the existing event-ledger plus a projection
  into the existing `OPEN-THREADS.md` under a clearly non-committed `PROPOSED` state. This is not a
  second backlog or memory system. Accepted items become normal open threads; dismissed/stale items
  remain terminal ledger outcomes and disappear from the active projection.
- **Why-inferred:** The corpus simultaneously contains repeated invitations to implement ideas found
  along the way (`corpus.json:10380-10390`) and repeated “why did you stop/finish everything” signals
  (`corpus.json:10424-10426,10565-10570`). Escrow reconciles the two instead of averaging them.
  It is **very likely (80-90%)** that preserving an idea without interrupting is preferable to either
  forgetting it or immediately detouring, because the owner expressly sets both “never drop a
  thread” and prompt-fatigue as hard constraints; the live trial below settles preference.
- **How-to-build:** **NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**
  Add `EventLedger.append_opportunity(...)`, stable dedupe keys, state transitions, and an
  `OPEN-THREADS.md` projection function to the existing `ledger.py`/`note.py` ownership surface.
  Candidate capture is a normal tool/helper call from the agent; it adds no hook. Fold delivery into
  the existing persist-nudge/Stop aggregation so this proposal **replaces no hook and adds no hook**.
  Wrap parsing/projection in `try/except`; on any error, preserve the main task path and exit 0.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** A hermetic test writes one opportunity, writes its duplicate
  from a second session, compacts/resumes, and verifies one projected item with the same ID. Then a
  second process marks it accepted/dismissed and verifies the projection changes. Effectiveness is
  **UNVERIFIED** until a 20-session shadow/advise run shows (a) zero discussed items lost across
  compaction, (b) owner can retrieve every deferred item by ID, and (c) lower immediate-interruption
  count than the current baseline.
- **SOTA:** Horvitz explicitly calls for working memory, attention-aware timing, cheap rejection,
  and learning from observation (principles 3, 7, 11, 12),
  https://erichorvitz.com/chi99horvitz.pdf. ProAgentBench separately evaluates *when* to intervene
  and *what* assistance to give using continuous real-world traces rather than isolated synthetic
  tasks, https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04482.
- **Value × effort:** **transformative × low**.
- **Novelty:** **inferred-surprise**.
- **Autonomy:** **just-do-it** for silent capture/dedupe; **confirm-first** before converting an
  escrowed adjacent idea into committed work.

### Finding 2 — UNVERIFIED IN THIS WORKSPACE: authority-first initiative lattice, not a confidence score

- **Improvement:** Implement the five-way DO-NOW / SURFACE-NOW / ASK-NOW / ESCROW / DROP rule above.
  Authority, reversibility, goal relationship, and verifier availability are hard gates; expected
  value only ranks candidates that survive them.
- **What it is:** A pure policy function over typed external facts. It does not ask the proposer to
  “reflect whether this is a detour.” Same-context reflection is rejected by construction. In
  particular, verbal confidence can never promote a candidate; absence of a receipt/tool/owner
  trigger means DROP or ESCROW.
- **Why-inferred:** The owner welcomes valuable additions, but observed corrections also stop wrong
  assumptions and reroute approaches (`corpus.json:10573-10574,10705-10706`). The known-defects file
  records six confident wrong calls from single probes and a seventh caught by a known-positive
  control (`INPUT-known-harness-defects.md:111-129`). A hard authority/evidence lattice is therefore
  **almost certain (90-99%)** to be safer than “act when confident”; evidence is the measured local
  detector failure class plus the law binding this unit.
- **How-to-build:** **NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**
  Add a pure `disposition(opportunity, task_state, preference_state)` function beside the ledger
  reducer, with table-driven tests for every boundary. Consume existing goal IDs, typed receipts,
  checkpoints, and owner-state transitions. Keep it advisory/shadow first through the harness's
  existing `stage()` ladder (`modules.py:414-417`); do not add a gate process.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** For every fixture, the result must derive from a recorded
  external trigger. Known-positive controls: a failed test blocking the requested deliverable must
  yield REQUIRED/DO-NOW; a passing test plus unrelated refactor must yield ESCROW; a proposed remote
  publish must yield ASK-NOW. A separate process runs the table and compares actual dispositions.
  Live correctness is **UNVERIFIED** until owner accept/undo/dismiss outcomes show no unauthorized
  DO-NOW action across the shadow and advise phases.
- **SOTA:** Horvitz frames initiative as expected utility under uncertainty while requiring cheap
  termination, cost-sensitive dialogue, and “doing less” as uncertainty rises,
  https://erichorvitz.com/chi99horvitz.pdf. PROPER explicitly scores both initiative
  appropriateness and intent alignment rather than generic helpfulness,
  https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.2082/.
- **Value × effort:** **transformative × medium**.
- **Novelty:** **inferred-surprise**.
- **Autonomy:** **just-do-it** to implement and shadow the pure classifier; **confirm-first** before
  enabling DO-NOW for the first live session.

### Finding 3 — UNVERIFIED IN THIS WORKSPACE: receptive-boundary scheduler with one interruption slot

- **Improvement:** Decouple idea urgency from message timing. Deliver at most one proactive card at a
  natural, externally observed boundary; queue all others.
- **What it is:** Receptive boundaries are not inferred from tone by the proposing model. They are
  objective events: `TaskCompleted`; `Stop` after a fresh receipt; an explicit owner request for
  ideas/options; a failed execution whose remediation is on the critical path; or an idle boundary
  reported by the provider/task runner. Active tool execution, a pending owner question, and an
  unverified main deliverable close the slot. Critical safety/correctness evidence can pre-empt once;
  ordinary enhancements cannot.
- **Why-inferred:** Prompt-fatigue is an explicit hard constraint, while the corpus contains cases
  where the owner wants an opportunity examined before the main task (`corpus.json:10713-10714`). A
  boundary scheduler preserves exceptional promotion without continuous interruption. It is
  **likely (55-80%)** that a single-slot boundary policy will reduce distraction without suppressing
  valuable initiative; evidence is the CHI 2025 moderate-versus-persistent result, and the local
  A/B trial settles transfer to this owner.
- **How-to-build:** **NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only).** Extend the opportunity policy
  configuration with `max_cards_per_boundary: 1`, eligible existing events, cooldown by goal, and
  priority order `REQUIRED > time-sensitive risk > ENABLES > IMPROVES`. Delivery folds into the
  existing persist-nudge aggregation; this is explicitly **zero additional hooks**. If the existing
  normalized envelope does not expose a reliable idle/task boundary, that premise is **UNVERIFIED**;
  replaying captured envelopes for known TaskCompleted/Stop/idle cases settles it. Until then, use
  only TaskCompleted and receipt-backed Stop.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** Provider-event fixtures prove a known TaskCompleted is
  detected and an active-tool event is suppressed. Session telemetry records opportunity capture
  time, surface time, active-task completion time, and explicit disposition. Success requires no
  increase in median main-task completion time and no more than one unprompted card per boundary.
- **SOTA:** Chen et al. designed for contextual timing and experimentally compared ordinary and
  persistent suggestion regimes, https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04596. Attention-sensitive alerting
  explicitly balances interruption cost against deferral cost,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.6707. ProAgentBench's timing/content decomposition is direct frontier
  support, https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04482.
- **Value × effort:** **high × low**.
- **Novelty:** **inferred-surprise**.
- **Autonomy:** **just-do-it** for shadow logging; **confirm-first** for enabling visible delivery.

### Finding 4 — UNVERIFIED IN THIS WORKSPACE: evidence-first progressive-disclosure cards

- **Improvement:** Make a proactive suggestion cheap to judge and cheap to reject. Show one sentence
  first, not a mini-report or a multi-question form.
- **What it is:** The card has exactly four visible fields: `(type) opportunity`, `why now` with a
  clickable/inspectable external signal, `cost + scope`, and one recommended disposition. Details,
  patch preview, and research stay collapsed. Actions are `do`, `later`, `dismiss`; only `do` on an
  authority-expanding item opens AskUserQuestion. “Later” must record a resurfacing condition, not
  become forgotten prose.
- **Why-inferred:** The owner values focus, and the corpus shows both use of AskUserQuestion
  (`corpus.json:135-138`) and extensive tool/capability use. The best coding-assistant study made
  suggestions rapidly evaluable through one-sentence summaries and previews. It is **likely
  (55-80%)** that one evidence-first card lowers cognitive load relative to a prose bundle, based on
  that interface study; the owner's actual scan time and dismiss rate settle it.
- **How-to-build:** **NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only).** Reuse native
  AskUserQuestion only for the confirm-first branch and the existing edit-checkpoint preview/rollback
  for patch details. Add one rendering template and a maximum character budget to persist-nudge.
  Do not add a dashboard, button surface, or duplicate workflow. A plain text fallback with stable
  opportunity ID is complete and wired even if rich UI is unavailable.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** Log card expand/accept/later/dismiss actions and time from
  surface to action; join accepted patch cards to diff/test receipts. A fresh-context verifier checks
  that `why now` cites an existing event and that no card exceeds the rendering contract. Owner
  cognitive-load improvement is **UNVERIFIED** until median decision latency and “ignore/no response”
  rates beat the current multi-option-message baseline without lowering accepted-and-verified ideas.
- **SOTA:** Chen et al.'s proactive coding UI uses a one-sentence summary, expandable details, and a
  preview with accept/revert, https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3714002 and
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04596. In a 155-conversation user study, users valued core functionality
  and disliked stylistic components without it; first-person, expert, and LM evaluations also
  disagreed, https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1017/.
- **Value × effort:** **high × low**.
- **Novelty:** **inferred-surprise**.
- **Autonomy:** **just-do-it** to render evidence for already-authorized work; **confirm-first** for
  scope expansion.

### Finding 5 — UNVERIFIED IN THIS WORKSPACE: receipt-bounded “welcome addition” autonomy envelope

- **Improvement:** Permit truly welcome additions to be implemented without a permission round-trip,
  but only inside a narrow envelope and only with objective verification plus rollback.
- **What it is:** A DO-NOW improvement must be same-goal, same-authority, checkpointed, mechanically
  verifiable, non-secret, non-publishing, non-purchasing, and small. Examples: add the missing test
  that directly verifies the requested fix; repair a lint error introduced by the current patch;
  update adjacent documentation whose assertion is falsified by the changed interface. Examples that
  always require confirmation: dependency adoption, broad refactor, remote deploy, account/config
  mutation outside the stated target, recurring cost, or a new product direction.
- **Why-inferred:** A recurring corpus pattern explicitly invites “all logical steps” and additions
  (`corpus.json:10380-10394`), while edit-validate feedback shows objective lint defects left by work
  (`corpus.json:10685-10686,10737-10738`). It is **very likely (80-90%)** that automatically fixing
  current-patch breakage is welcome; it is only **roughly even (45-55%)** that opportunistic adjacent
  refactors are welcome, because local signals support both initiative and redirection. Therefore
  only the first class starts eligible.
- **How-to-build:** **NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only).** Build on B1 edit checkpoints and
  rollback, the current goal ID, diff scope, and typed receipts. Configure eligible categories and
  deny authority-expanding effects. Require a before/after diff plus a fresh test/lint/build/run
  receipt from a different tool/environment. On verifier error, fail open toward *not claiming or
  expanding*: preserve the checkpoint and return to the main task; do not block the provider.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** The git diff proves scope, checkpoint receipt proves undo,
  and a test/build/lint/run receipt proves outcome. A second process compares the final diff against
  allowed paths and operation classes. Owner undo/revert or “why did you do this?” marks the category
  negative. No same-context prose review can certify eligibility or completion.
- **SOTA:** Horvitz recommends matching service precision to uncertainty and doing less correctly
  when uncertain, https://erichorvitz.com/chi99horvitz.pdf. Microsoft’s appropriate-reliance work
  defines success as accepting correct output and rejecting incorrect output, and warns that
  mitigations can backfire without user research,
  https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/fostering-appropriate-reliance-on-genai-lessons-learned-from-early-research/.
- **Value × effort:** **high × low**.
- **Novelty:** **inferred-surprise**.
- **Autonomy:** **just-do-it** only inside the envelope; **confirm-first** everywhere else.

### Finding 6 — UNVERIFIED IN THIS WORKSPACE: learn initiative preferences from dispositions, not language mining

- **Improvement:** Personalize *which categories, timings, and autonomy levels* are welcome using
  explicit/behavioral outcomes, never the recall-biased “correction” detector and never the agent's
  self-rating.
- **What it is:** Keep tiny decayed counters per `(category, goal relationship, disposition,
  boundary type)`: accepted-and-verified, deferred-then-accepted, dismissed, ignored, owner-undone,
  and verifier-failed. Promote only after repeated externally successful outcomes; demote quickly on
  undo or explicit dismissal. Store provenance and expiry on the existing ledger/basic-memory rail.
  This is not a user embedding, vector DB, trained critic, or personality dossier.
- **Why-inferred:** The correction miner's 41/41 false-positive smoke sample
  (`corpus.json:10371-10374`) makes language-only preference inference unsafe, while the owner has
  heterogeneous behavior—sometimes “do them now,” sometimes stop/reroute
  (`corpus.json:10705-10706,10781-10782`). It is **almost certain (90-99%)** that explicit disposition
  is a better label than this detector; evidence is the detector's own failed precision control.
  It is **likely (55-80%)** that category-specific decay will improve timing/category fit; PersonalAlign
  and ProAgentBench support long-term contextual personalization, but only the local trial settles it.
- **How-to-build:** **NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**
  Extend the ledger reducer with bounded counts and timestamps. Use conservative Beta-binomial or
  Wilson intervals computed from actual dispositions; no reinforcement-learning service. Require at
  least three accepted-and-verified outcomes before increasing autonomy one step, and one owner undo
  to demote immediately. Those thresholds are **UNVERIFIED**; replay 3/1, 5/1, and 5/2 policies on
  collected outcomes to settle calibration before enforcement.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** Native card actions, owner follow-up directives, git undo,
  and typed execution receipts are labels. A separate batch job recomputes counters from raw ledger
  events and must match the projection. It must also prove a known-positive accepted item and a
  known-negative dismissed item before reporting either class absent.
- **SOTA:** PersonalAlign evaluates implicit-intent resolution and proactive assistance from 20k
  long-term records with 775 preferences and 215 routines, reporting improvements from structured
  user memory, https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1669/. ProAgentBench reports that real workflow
  history outperforms synthetic context for proactive prediction, https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04482.
  These are frontier research results, not proof of transfer to this owner.
- **Value × effort:** **high × medium**.
- **Novelty:** **inferred-surprise**.
- **Autonomy:** **just-do-it** for logging and shadow personalization; **confirm-first** before any
  learned increase in mutation autonomy.

### Finding 7 — UNVERIFIED IN THIS WORKSPACE: independent “detour challenge” only for ambiguous promotions

- **Improvement:** When a candidate is valuable but its relationship to the active goal is
  ambiguous, send a minimal evidence packet to a fresh-context verifier. Do not ask the proposing
  model to critique itself, and do not burden every small candidate with a second model.
- **What it is:** The verifier receives only active-goal text, proposed outcome, claimed relationship,
  authority/reversibility fields, and raw external-signal references—not the proposer’s persuasive
  rationale. It must return `required`, `enables`, `adjacent`, or `insufficient-evidence` with the
  external observation that would falsify its classification. Disagreement forces ESCROW or owner
  confirmation; it never silently promotes.
- **Why-inferred:** The known-defects file records six wrong single-probe conclusions, a known-positive
  control that isolated only the actually broken detector, and a fabricated orchestration constraint
  propagated into about 15 prompts (`INPUT-known-harness-defects.md:111-129,151-160`). The harness
  already records fresh-context verification use (`corpus.json:13290-13292`). It is **almost certain
  (90-99%)** that same-context self-approval violates the local evidence; a fresh context is mandated
  by the unit's binding law. Whether second-context adjudication is accurate enough for this specific
  classification is **UNVERIFIED**; the labeled fixture experiment settles it.
- **How-to-build:** **NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only).** Reuse the existing
  `fresh-context-verify`/Codex review surface only when the pure lattice returns
  ambiguous-high-value. Cap at one verifier call per boundary and pass a strict small schema. This is
  a normal tool/model call, not a hook or orchestration runtime. A verifier timeout/error fails open
  to ESCROW and exit 0; it never blocks the main task.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** Create a balanced fixture set from owner-labeled accepted,
  deferred, and rejected candidates plus known REQUIRED test failures. Run the verifier in a new
  context with shuffled ordering. Measure confusion by class and disagreement with objective
  critical-path receipts. Promote only if false-DO-NOW is zero in the safety fixture set and owner
  agreement improves over the shadow lattice alone.
- **SOTA:** Research on intrinsic self-correction finds that models often fail without reliable
  external feedback; the critical survey recommends reliable external signals rather than prompted

## Prompt-fatigue and thread-preservation invariants

These are acceptance tests, not suggestions:

1. **No lost thread:** every surfaced or explicitly discussed item has a stable ID and terminal state
   or a resurfacing condition. Compaction/resume and provider switches preserve it through the
   event-ledger/OPEN-THREADS projection.
2. **No question cascade:** at most one proactive card at a receptive boundary and at most one owner
   question opened from that card. Additional candidates remain escrowed.
3. **No self-certification:** model confidence, repeated agent agreement, or same-context critique can
   neither promote nor close a candidate.
4. **No silent authority expansion:** scope, remote effects, credentials, purchases, publication, and
   irreversible actions always require confirmation.
5. **No “later” black hole:** `later` must contain an external resurfacing condition (task completion,
   date, dependency receipt, or owner request). It is not a prose note.
6. **No hook proliferation:** candidate capture is a helper/tool action; scheduling folds into
   event-ledger/persist-nudge; verification uses existing tools/fresh context. Net hooks: zero.
7. **Fail open:** any candidate parser, preference reducer, renderer, verifier, or projection error
   returns the main task to its normal path, records a sanitized failure receipt where possible, and
   exits 0. It may lose an uncommitted suggestion, never wedge work or claim success.
8. **No half-built UI:** the complete minimum is ledger schema + reducer + projection + plain-text
   card + transitions + tests. Rich controls are optional only after the plain path works end-to-end.

## Anti-overfit gate

All eight proposals ride existing buses. None introduces a second orchestrator, vector-DB/KG
memory, durable execution engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, or general guardrail framework.
The policy is deliberately a small categorical lattice and a ledger reducer, not an initiative
agent. Candidate generation remains model-native; correctness and promotion remain externally
grounded. This is compatible with the lesson that capable base models reduce the value of elaborate
scaffolding: the harness supplies only state, authority, timing, and falsification—the parts a model
cannot reliably certify inside its own context.

The most radical alternative considered was a learned contextual bandit that automatically tunes
timing and intervention content. It is rejected for now: it would add a trained decision component,
needs clean counterfactual rewards this corpus does not contain, and could optimize engagement at
the expense of focus. A bounded offline analysis of categorical counters captures most of the
personalization value without creating another runtime. It is **unlikely (20-45%)** that a learned
bandit would outperform the proposed lattice during initial deployment, because labels are sparse
and the correction candidates are demonstrably noisy; a future randomized trial with sufficient
accepted/dismissed outcomes could settle that rather than assumption.

## Bottom line

The welcome addition is not “an idea the agent thinks is good.” It is an opportunity that survives
five gates: **goal relationship, external evidence, authority, reversibility, and interruption
economics**. If it is directly required and objectively verifiable, do it. If it is urgent but needs
owner authority, ask once. If it is promising but non-urgent, escrow it and protect the current
thread. If it cannot be falsified outside the proposing context, do not promote it.

This design is **very likely (80-90%)** to meet the owner's stated combination of ambition,
autonomy, low cognitive load, and never-forget continuity better than a generic “be proactive” rule,
because it makes discovery broad while making action evidence- and authority-bounded. The remaining
uncertainty is explicitly assigned to shadow telemetry, executable receipts, a fresh verifier, and
the owner's observed choices—not to same-context self-correction.
