# N01 — The discovery gap: classify the zero before fixing it

## Executive verdict

The pinned headline is 78 distinct skill invocation labels out of approximately 355 installed labels: 22.0% observed and approximately 277 labels (78.0%) with no recorded invocation. The same corpus records 403 total skill invocations; 29 of the 78 observed labels appear once and 46 appear at most twice. These are exact computations from `capability_use.skills_invoked` in `/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/corpus.json:1`. The corpus is unusually complete for transcript mining—11,633 files seen, zero failed, one bad line—but it records positive invocations, not the installed universe or missed opportunities (`_meta` and `capability_use`, same file and line). Therefore “zero” is a censored outcome, not a diagnosis.

It is **almost certain (90-99%)**, based on the positive-only corpus field plus the independently measured divergence among the 75-skill Claude daily set, 280-skill shared vault, and provider/plugin catalogs, that the 277-label subtraction mixes at least the five requested causes and provider reachability. The inventory audit reports those differing denominators and a symlink/tombstone at `/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/audit-findings/A19.md:9-19`; this is an audit claim rather than re-measured fact because the remote bundle omits the actual skill trees. The local settings independently prove that the active Claude surface has a 2% listing budget and native tool search (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/settings.json:2-18`).

The requested per-capability adjudication cannot honestly be completed on this job node. The unpacked subject contains one incidental `SKILL.md`, not the installed manifests; `corpus.json` contains no zero-use names. Consequently, assigning any of the 277 names to “unknown”, “never applicable”, “superseded”, “broken”, or “badly named” here would fabricate both identities and causes. The settling experiment is the Windows-side `capability-disposition audit` specified in Finding 1, supplied with the canonical manifest, corpus, router exposure records, execution receipts, and a stratified owner adjudication sample. This is an inline **UNVERIFIED** premise wherever a future population count appears below.

What can be established now is that naive “unused = prune” is invalid. The current manual prune report ranks only capabilities that fired at least once—zero-use capabilities never enter its `Counter`—yet labels the bottom as prune candidates (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/event-ledger/prune-report.py:65-90,151-167`). Separately, selection telemetry captures actual tool selections and success/failure without persisting inputs, and is explicitly fail-open (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/selection-events/emitter.py:1-29`). Those existing rails are enough to close the discovery loop externally.

## Required causal contract

The disposition must be mutually exclusive at a given catalog version and evidence-bearing:

| Cause | Meaning | Required external settling signal | Correct fix | Forbidden shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `unknown_to_owner` | Applicable opportunity existed; capability was reachable and healthy; owner/agent did not know or recall it | Historical opportunity judged independently, plus shadow exposure or owner recognition | Contextual exposure at a decision point | Global catalog dump or tutorial spam |
| `never_applicable` | No representative owner task fell inside the capability’s declared trigger boundary | Two independent task-opportunity classifiers, calibrated on known positives, with owner audit of disagreements | Cold-library/archive; preserve explicit access | Rename, advertise, or delete from zero alone |
| `superseded` | Another live capability covers the trigger and required outcome at least as well | Declared replacement plus deterministic contract comparison/smoke receipts | Alias then dependency-aware prune | Keep both names in implicit catalog |
| `broken` | A representative invocation cannot load or produce its declared minimal receipt | Parser/load check plus execution/smoke receipt | Quarantine, repair, or remove | More routing exposure |
| `badly_named` | Applicable, reachable, healthy capability is missed under its current routing metadata but found under a blinded alternate description/name | Paired retrieval test on held-out real prompts, then actual selection/outcome lift | Rename/short-description rewrite with alias | Assume semantic embeddings cure metadata loss |

Order is load-bearing: `superseded -> broken -> opportunity -> name/exposure`. A broken old wrapper may also be badly named, but its operational disposition is `superseded` or `broken`; otherwise the same label gets multiple incompatible fixes. Every record must include `capability_id`, provider-visible invocation ID, source hash, catalog version, disposition, evidence IDs, adjudicator, confidence band, expiry/recheck date, replacement ID if any, and an unresolved thread ID. No model may mint its own final disposition from the same context that proposed it.

## Findings — inferred surprises first

### Finding 1 — Build a falsifiable capability-disposition ledger, not an “unused” list

- **what it is:** A manual subcommand of the existing event-ledger/report bus joins (a) canonical manifest rows, (b) provider visibility, (c) corpus invocation counts, (d) router exposure/selection events, (e) health receipts, (f) supersession declarations, and (g) opportunity-audit labels. It refuses to emit a cause without its cause-specific evidence and emits `unresolved` instead. It canonicalizes aliases before counts, which matters because the corpus itself contains both `reframe`/`Reframe`, `brainstorm`/`brainstorming`, and plugin-qualified/bare forms (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/corpus.json:1`). The current semantic observer already tries both namespaced and bare skill keys (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/semantic-router/observe.py:20-40`), showing canonicalization belongs on this bus.
- **why-inferred:** The owner asked “why per capability,” not for a popularity chart, and has a measured history of false absence conclusions. The known-defects record says a detector returned zero skills/agents/hooks until a known-positive control showed only hooks were broken (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/INPUT-known-harness-defects.md:111-129`). A typed refusal to classify is therefore more valuable than another confident dashboard.
- **how-to-build:** Add a `disposition` mode beside `prune-report.py`. Read an explicit manifest, not recursive Markdown. Fail the report—not the agent session—if the known-positive controls `deep-research` invocation and one known supersession cannot be recovered. Apply the ordered decision rules above. Emit JSONL records into the existing ledger plus a Markdown projection. Require two distinct `evidence_id` types for `broken`, `superseded`, or any absence. Retain prior dispositions as versioned events rather than overwriting them. Wrap the CLI in `try/except`; a reporting error prints `UNRESOLVED` and exits 0 when invoked from harness automation.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** A deterministic fixture catalog containing one known example of each cause and one ambiguous example must yield five correct dispositions plus one `unresolved`; a separate fresh-context model must reproduce the result using only evidence receipts, and the fixture’s executable smoke checks must agree. Production acceptance is `manifest canonical count == disposition rows`, zero orphan evidence IDs, and no cause minted from invocation count alone.
- **SOTA citation:** NIST says accuracy measurement should use realistic test sets, disaggregated segments, human-AI teaming, and external validity rather than a single aggregate ([NIST AI RMF characteristics](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/3-sec-characteristics/)). ToolRet shows ordinary retrieval strength does not imply tool-retrieval strength and that poor retrieval lowers task pass rate ([ToolRet, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01763)).
- **value x effort:** transformative x medium.
- **novelty:** inferred-surprise.
- **autonomy:** just-do-it for the read-only report and fixtures; confirm-first before any disable/prune action.
- **confidence:** It is **very likely (80-90%)** that this prevents false pruning and turns the 277-label headline into actionable populations, because it forces the five different evidence paths and uses existing telemetry; the remaining uncertainty is whether the Windows manifest can canonicalize every plugin/provider alias. The production dry run named above settles that.
- **anti-overfit gate:** Pass. It replaces an existing report path and writes additive ledger events; no second runtime, vector DB/KG, durable engine, trained critic, SaaS, or guardrail framework.

### Finding 2 — Measure “never applicable” with a counterfactual opportunity census

- **improvement:** A batch opportunity audit over historical owner tasks, explicitly separated from invocation. **NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).** No live hook and no user interruption.
- **what it is:** For each canonical skill, generate a compact trigger contract from its name, short description, exclusions, required environment, and one positive/one negative example. Sample real user turns by project/domain/time, then ask two mechanically independent judges whether the skill could materially improve the requested outcome. One judge is a fresh-context second model; the other is a deterministic lexical/domain rule or executable prerequisite check. Calibrate both on known-present positives before accepting any absence. Disagreements remain `unresolved` and a small stratified sample goes to the owner in one batch.
- **why-inferred:** “Never applicable” is the only cause for which silence is healthy. The corpus correction miner itself warns that 41/41 smoke-sampled correction candidates were false positives (`owner_correction_candidates.precision_warning`, `/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/corpus.json:1`). Reusing raw corrections or invocation zeros as opportunity labels would repeat that category error.
- **how-to-build:** Add `capability-disposition audit-opportunities --manifest ... --corpus ...`. Deduplicate templated hook/meta messages, since the corpus’s top correction patterns are mostly Stop feedback and agent-authored traffic (same corpus field). Stratify at minimum by project, month, and task family; cap evidence to hashed turn IDs plus bounded redacted text. For every skill require `eligible_turns`, `ineligible_turns`, judge agreement, prerequisite result, and sample coverage. Mark `never_applicable` only when both signals find zero opportunity and the detectors recover their seeded positives. Re-run quarterly or after material domain change; do not continually scan inside sessions.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** Blind 50 manually labelled opportunities/non-opportunities, including known-used skills and intentionally irrelevant mobile/Apple/game cases. Require predeclared precision/recall and report confidence intervals; the owner adjudicates only disagreements and 10 random agreements. A capability becomes `never_applicable` only after the owner audit has no discovered positive in its sample.
- **SOTA citation:** PersonaFeedback deliberately separates persona inference from response personalization and uses 8,298 human-annotated cases, warning that retrieval augmentation is not automatically sufficient ([PersonaFeedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12915)). ETAPP evaluates personalized tool invocation and proactivity in a sandbox across 800 cases rather than inferring quality from use frequency ([ACL 2025 ETAPP](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1064/)).
- **value x effort:** high x medium.
- **novelty:** inferred-surprise.
- **autonomy:** just-do-it for offline scoring; confirm-first for owner adjudication batch and any cold-storage decision.
- **confidence:** It is **likely (55-80%)** that the census can confidently isolate a meaningful `never_applicable` population, because 10.62 GB spans 1,964 sessions and diverse task domains, but rare future-relevant capabilities may have no historical opportunity. Quarterly expiry and owner sampling are the external checks against that survivorship error.
- **anti-overfit gate:** Pass. It is a bounded offline report using the corpus and existing ledger; it does not become a service or memory substrate.

### Finding 3 — Use blinded description tournaments to distinguish “badly named” from “unknown”

- **improvement:** Per-capability paired retrieval experiments using real eligible prompts. **NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).** Fold it into the existing semantic-router evaluator, replacing its coarse set-hit benchmark; do not add a hook.
- **what it is:** For a healthy, reachable skill with historical opportunities, compare current metadata against two independently authored routing cards: a terse `object + action + boundary + not-for` card and an owner-vocabulary card mined from eligible turns. Blind the names and test rank@k/MRR plus abstention. If alternates reliably retrieve while current metadata misses, disposition is `badly_named`; if all descriptions retrieve but the owner/agent never selects after contextual exposure, it is `unknown_to_owner` (or low value), not a naming failure.
- **why-inferred:** Local code doubles name tokens and reads only the one-line `description:` scalar (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/skill-router/route.py:76-106`); folded YAML loses its body. It requires at least two shared tokens and emits only six total candidates/four skills (`route.py:173-198`). Separately, the audit measured 290/292 Codex descriptions truncated to roughly 15 characters (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/audit-findings/A24.md:15-21`). Names and descriptions therefore have observable, separable failure modes.
- **how-to-build:** Extend the router eval with corpus-derived held-out turns, positives, hard sibling negatives, and no-skill cases. YAML-parse metadata with a real parser. Compare current vs candidate cards with identical retrieval code and catalog. Require a statistically predeclared lift, no material negative-fire increase, and executable downstream outcome. Keep an alias for renamed invocations for one release. Store tournament receipts in the disposition ledger; never allow the model that wrote a description to judge it.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** A different model generates candidates; deterministic retrieval computes rank; actual fresh sandbox sessions execute a stratified winning-card sample; deterministic task receipts or owner choice decide whether the surfaced skill helped. Promote only if held-out rank and task outcomes improve. This explicitly rejects same-context self-approval.
- **SOTA citation:** ToolRet’s 43,000-tool benchmark finds that conventional strong retrievers can perform poorly on tool retrieval ([ToolRet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01763)). Multi-Field Tool Retrieval identifies documentation inconsistency, query/document granularity mismatch, and multiple utility dimensions as distinct retrieval challenges ([MFTR, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05366)).
- **value x effort:** high x medium.
- **novelty:** inferred-surprise.
- **autonomy:** just-do-it for offline tournaments; confirm-first for public names or deletion of aliases.
- **confidence:** It is **very likely (80-90%)** that this finds real metadata defects, because current parsing and truncation provide two independent mechanisms that destroy routing signal; it is only **likely (55-80%)** that metadata repair improves end-task outcomes, because retrieval is necessary but not sufficient. The fresh execution receipts settle the second claim.
- **anti-overfit gate:** Pass. Deterministic evaluation extends an existing router package; no learned router or trained critic is proposed.

### Finding 4 — Explore unknown capabilities through bounded, contextual trials—not a digest

- **improvement:** An “exploration slot” that exposes at most one unfamiliar, high-fit capability at a natural decision point and measures acceptance. **NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only).** Configure the existing router/catalog policy and selection-event fields; do not add a hook.
- **what it is:** After health, reachability, opportunity, and naming checks pass, reserve one of the existing router’s four skill slots for an `unknown_to_owner` candidate only when predicted fit is high, the action is reversible/read-only, and no equivalent known skill already covers the task. Phrase it as a silent agent instruction (“use if apt”) rather than a question to the owner. Ask only when execution would spend money, mutate external state, or encode a consequential preference. Cool down rejected candidates and never show the same trial twice without new evidence.
- **why-inferred:** The owner wants expansion but explicitly treats focus as a scarce resource. The current capability catalog dumps broad prose once per session (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/capability-catalog/inject.py:42-83`), while the lexical router already threshold-gates, caps suggestions, and deduplicates per session (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/skill-router/route.py:157-215`). The surprise is that discovery should consume an existing candidate slot, not owner attention.
- **how-to-build:** Add catalog fields `exposure_state`, `risk`, `cooldown_until`, and `exploration_eligible`; sort one eligible candidate into the current top-four only at task boundaries/decision-shaped prompts. Record `exposed`, `selected`, `succeeded`, `ignored`, or `owner_rejected` through the selection-event/event-ledger path. Use `just-do-it` only for read-only/reversible invocations already authorized; otherwise use the existing AskUserQuestion once, with one recommendation and one alternative. Set a global budget such as one owner-visible question per session; **UNVERIFIED** whether one is the right cap—the experiment is a four-week crossover measuring acceptance, interruption count, and owner corrections.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** Randomize eligible sessions between exploitation-only and one exploration slot. Compare accepted useful invocations, deterministic task receipts, owner interruptions, and explicit rejection/correction rate. Owner can disable the lane; rollback is a JSON toggle.
- **SOTA citation:** Horvitz’s mixed-initiative principles frame assistance as decision-making under uncertainty and expected utility ([CHI 1999 paper](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/chi99horvitz.pdf)). A CHI 2026 study found proactive help worked better when delivered at observed struggle rather than too early, late, or misaligned, and bounded responses to 160 tokens ([Sensing What Surveys Miss](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791191)).
- **value x effort:** high x low.
- **novelty:** inferred-surprise.
- **autonomy:** just-do-it for read-only/reversible trials; confirm-first for external mutation, cost, credentials, or durable preference.
- **confidence:** It is **likely (55-80%)** that bounded exploration uncovers useful unknown skills without raising prompt fatigue, because it reuses a capped/deduped slot and intervention timing matters; the owner-specific interruption threshold is **UNVERIFIED** until the crossover above.
- **anti-overfit gate:** Pass. Configuration on existing router and ledger buses; no additional runtime.

### Finding 5 — Treat broken skills as quarantined executable contracts, not routing misses

- **improvement:** Minimal skill health contracts and quarantine state, folded into the existing B1 liveness/catalog lane. **NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).** Fold into `mcp-liveness` plus capability-catalog maintenance; no seventh hook.
- **what it is:** Each skill declares `load_probe` and, where possible, a side-effect-free `smoke_probe` with timeout and expected exit/schema. Static health means standard YAML parses, referenced files exist, invocation ID resolves, and required tool family is registered. Runtime health means a fresh subprocess/tool result satisfies the probe. Failure changes disposition to `broken`, removes it from automatic exposure, and names the repair prerequisite; explicit invocation remains possible with a warning if safe.
- **why-inferred:** Routing a dead capability creates retries and erodes trust. The corpus’s worst MCP has 135 “not connected” failures despite repeated use (pinned ground truth; underlying fingerprints at `/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/corpus.json:1`). The router intentionally swallows harvest and runtime exceptions (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/skill-router/route.py:91-110,216-217`), so disappearance and breakage are currently observationally similar. The audit also reports four standard-YAML-invalid skill descriptions (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/audit-findings/A24.md:55-61`), but their live parser impact remains an audit claim.
- **how-to-build:** Extend the catalog schema with probe commands and expected receipts. Run probes manually/on sync in an isolated temp directory; never on every prompt. Seed a known-good and known-bad fixture so the detector proves sensitivity. Require parser plus execution signals before `broken`; if only one fails, record `unresolved`. Write health receipts with source hash and expiry to the event ledger. Filter quarantined IDs from router candidates. All automation wraps errors and exits 0; stale/unknown health does not block ordinary work.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** CI/temp-copy probe suite with known-good, missing-reference, invalid-YAML, missing-backend, timeout, and wrong-schema fixtures; a separate direct invocation confirms every quarantined production candidate before the label is accepted. Repair is complete only when both probes pass on the same source hash.
- **SOTA citation:** Amershi et al.’s human-AI guidelines include making clear what the system can do, supporting efficient correction, and updating behavior from user feedback ([Microsoft HAI Guidelines project](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/publications/)). NIST requires validity/reliability under expected and unexpected conditions, not presentation alone ([NIST AI RMF](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/3-sec-characteristics/)).
- **value x effort:** high x medium.
- **novelty:** inferred-surprise.
- **autonomy:** just-do-it for safe probes/quarantine; confirm-first for deleting, installing, starting licensed backends, or changing credentials.
- **confidence:** It is **very likely (80-90%)** that quarantine reduces futile selections and makes `broken` distinguishable from unknown, given the measured dead-MCP retry pattern and swallowed router exceptions. Coverage of skills without safe smoke probes is **UNVERIFIED**; the manifest audit and fixture suite settle how many remain static-only.
- **anti-overfit gate:** Pass. This extends existing liveness/catalog rails and uses executable receipts; it is not a generic guardrail framework.

### Finding 6 — Supersession must be a dependency-checked alias migration

- **what it is:** A superseded skill stays as a non-implicit alias for one migration window. Its replacement must declare trigger coverage, required-tool equivalence, and migration references. The sync validator rejects cycles, missing targets, multiple active definitions of one canonical ID, and deletion while references remain. After a fresh smoke receipt and zero unresolved consumers, prune the body but preserve an invocation alias/tombstone where provider behavior requires it.
- **why-inferred:** An earlier audit found a `design-review-loop` tombstone while repo and router consumers still referenced the old name (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/inputs/audit-findings/A23.md:44-50`), and separately claimed 43 stale/superseded legacy entries (`A23.md:71-77`). The active router deduplicates only exact `(type,name)` pairs (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/skill-router/route.py:91-106`), so synonyms compete rather than consolidate. Block publication of two implicit definitions for one canonical ID. Emit unresolved references into the existing OPEN-THREADS/event-ledger lane so migration cannot be discussed then forgotten.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** Two independent signals: reference scan across declared roots plus a replacement smoke/golden-trigger receipt. A second shell/provider catalog verifies the old invocation resolves to the new ID before pruning. Completion requires zero consumers and passing replacement receipts on the published hash.
- **SOTA citation:** Tool retrieval research identifies structurally inconsistent documentation as a core source of failure ([MFTR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05366)). It is **likely (55-80%)** that every provider honors alias tombstones uniformly; provider catalog probes settle that before deletion.
- **anti-overfit gate:** Pass. Declarative manifest plus existing sync/validator and open-thread rails.

### Finding 8 — Calibrate proactive autonomy from outcomes and preserve every unresolved classification thread

- **improvement:** A risk/attention policy and durable unresolved-thread projection attached to every disposition. **NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only).** Reuse selection events, event-ledger, open-loops, AskUserQuestion, and OPEN-THREADS.md.
- **what it is:** Discovery confidence controls whether the harness silently routes, offers a compact suggestion, asks once, or abstains. Confidence is not prose self-confidence: it derives from independent retrieval rank, health receipt freshness, reversibility, and prior owner acceptance. Every `unresolved`, rejected exposure, missing prerequisite, and pending alias migration gets a stable thread ID, owner/action/settling experiment, and expiry. The existing open-loop SessionStart path resurfaces only actionable items, not the whole backlog.
- **why-inferred:** Appropriate reliance requires avoiding both over-trust and under-use. The owner’s “never drop a thread” requirement makes a silent unresolved classification a product defect. Existing infrastructure already resurfaces open loops in the SessionStart dispatcher (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/_dispatch/manifest.json:6-70`) and the project card reads open failures/milestones without writing (`/local_disk0/codexwork/bundle/claude/hooks/capability-catalog/project_card.py:124-164`). Building another memory system would duplicate both.
- **how-to-build:** Add policy fields to the manifest: `risk=read_only|reversible_write|external_write|costly`, `attention=ambient|suggest|ask`, `min_receipt_age`, and `thread_id`. Rules: ambient/just-do only for high-fit healthy read-only actions; suggestion for reversible work with measured benefit; AskUserQuestion for external mutation, money, credentials, or an enduring preference; abstain/quarantine when evidence conflicts. Append outcomes to selection events. Project unresolved disposition threads into OPEN-THREADS.md/basic-memory via existing note/ledger tooling, deduped by ID. Auto-close only on an external receipt or owner decision, never because the same agent says it is done.
- **EXTERNAL signal that verifies it:** Four-week report: helpful-selection rate, ignored/rejected exposure rate, owner-visible questions per session, correction rate, task receipts, unresolved-thread age, and zero “closed without evidence” records.
