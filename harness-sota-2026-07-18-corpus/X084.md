# X084 — Cross-domain borrow: mechanism design → use-vs-mention guards

## Scope and bottom line

This unit is an ideation lens, as requested. I did **not** inspect or re-derive the local harness, corpus, defects, or audit. The starting observation—five payload-text detectors and at least twelve false fires on text that merely describes their trigger—is supplied by the unit brief, not independently established here. The external evidence is cited directly below.

The useful borrow is a **strictly proper scoring rule**, coupled to a trusted, delayed resolution of the semantic event. It transfers only partially:

* It **does** give a principled way to make a detector state uncertainty, prevent threshold gaming from masquerading as detector quality, compare revisions, and audit both alerts and non-alerts.
* It **does not** solve semantic-role recognition. A scoring rule cannot tell whether a span is an instruction, an executable argument, quoted prose, or an example. It only makes the best available probabilistic belief the optimal report *after an outcome definition and an outcome-resolution process exist*.
* Therefore, a direct replacement of substring guards by “properly scored substring guards” would still be wrong. The concrete transfer is a role-aware event envelope plus probabilistic detector output plus shadow outcome resolution. It is a small mechanism around the existing five detectors, not a second orchestration runtime.

My overall judgment is **very likely (80-90%)**, based on the mathematical propriety result and on the direct structural match between probabilistic elicitation and detector confidence, that this borrow will improve evaluation and threshold selection *when labels are resolved independently of the guard*. It is only **roughly even (45-55%)**, based on the absence of a demonstrated native semantic-role signal in this ideation-only unit, that the existing harness exposes enough trusted role metadata to make the full design useful without a semantic adjudicator. The experiment in “Falsification and acceptance test” settles both claims.

## 1. The source-field mechanism

### 1.1 Strictly proper scoring rules

In forecast elicitation, a forecaster has a private belief about an event and reports a distribution. A scoring rule maps the reported distribution and the subsequently observed outcome to a reward or loss. A rule is *proper* when truthful reporting maximizes expected score; it is *strictly proper* when that optimum is unique. Gneiting and Raftery give this definition, develop its general form, and explain why strict propriety encourages honest probabilistic forecasts ([JASA paper, 2007](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/people/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf); [University of Washington record](https://stat.uw.edu/research/tech-reports/strictly-proper-scoring-rules-prediction-and-estimation-revised)). Brier's original probability-forecast verification paper supplies the classic quadratic score ([Monthly Weather Review, 1950](https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/mwre/78/1/1520-0493_1950_078_0001_vofeit_2_0_co_2.xml)).

For a binary event `Y ∈ {0,1}`, let the forecaster report `p = P(Y=1)`. Use Brier loss:

```text
L(p, Y) = (p - Y)^2
```

If the forecaster's true belief is `q`, expected loss is:

```text
E[L] = q(1-p)^2 + (1-q)p^2
     = (p-q)^2 + q(1-q)
```

The second term does not depend on `p`; the first is uniquely minimized at `p=q`. The mechanism therefore removes the reward for bluffing with `0` or `1`, hiding uncertainty, or moving a probability merely to cross an operational threshold. This is incentive compatibility in the narrow source-field sense: truthful revelation of a belief is the optimal report under the scoring contract. It is not proof that the belief itself is accurate.

This distinction matters. The revelation principle says that implementable equilibrium outcomes can be represented by incentive-compatible direct mechanisms; it does not say that arbitrary messages reveal truth without an incentive constraint ([Royal Swedish Academy advanced background, 2007, pp. 5–6](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/advanced-economicsciences2007.pdf); [Nobel Prize popular background](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2007/popular-information/)).

### 1.2 Why this is related to Goodhart's law, but is not “a Goodhart fix”

Goodhart's original monetary-policy observation concerned an empirical regularity collapsing when used for control. The popular slogan is broader than the original formulation; the source context is monetary targeting, not a theorem that every proxy inevitably fails ([Bank of England archival record for the 1975 material](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/CalmView/Record.aspx?id=6A50%2F17&src=CalmView.Catalog)).

The harness analogue is straightforward: if “number of dangerous substrings caught” is the target, a guard can improve that number by firing on examples, quotations, incident reports, and documentation. That is not strategic behavior by software; it is optimization pressure on a misspecified proxy. Proper scoring helps by replacing a one-bit proxy with a full probability scored against a later semantic outcome. It does **not** eliminate proxy risk: the semantic label and its resolution process become the new target.

It is **likely (55-80%)**, based on the algebra above and the known separation between forecast and outcome in proper scoring, that retaining the full probability will expose detector revisions that buy apparent recall by becoming indiscriminately confident. It is **unlikely (20-45%)**, based on Goodhart's control-setting warning and the potential for label-policy coupling, that a single scalar score will remain sufficient under sustained optimization. The settling experiment is to report Brier loss alongside class-conditional false-fire/false-negative rates and calibration curves for each semantic role; divergence between those measures falsifies scalar sufficiency.

## 2. Structural mapping

| Mechanism-design object | Harness translation | Required assumption |
|---|---|---|
| Private type/belief | Detector's evidence-conditioned belief that a candidate span is actionable use | The detector can emit a probability, not only a boolean |
| Direct report | `{p_use, candidate_span, evidence_features}` | The report is frozen before intervention/outcome |
| Realized event | Independently adjudicated semantic role for the candidate | The adjudicator is not just the detector re-run |
| Proper transfer/score | Negative Brier loss, retained per detector and role | Cases receive labels, including sampled non-alerts |
| Allocation/action rule | Allow, annotate, ask, or block at cost-derived thresholds | Intervention policy is separate from forecast scoring |
| Incentive compatibility | A detector revision cannot improve expected score merely by overstating certainty | Evaluation data is not chosen by the revision itself |
| Goodhart boundary | Score, labels, and sampling can themselves be gamed | Multiple diagnostics and held-out adversarial cases remain outside tuning |

The key translation is from **match** to **forecast about a typed event**:

```text
trusted hook event
  └─ candidate span + provenance
       ├─ semantic-role evidence
       │    execution argument / agent directive / quoted example /
       │    retrieved data / incident description / ambiguous
       ├─ existing detector evidence
       └─ report p_use before any action
             ├─ shadow log and sample for resolution
             └─ separate policy maps probability + cost to action
```

“Use” must be defined operationally. A workable binary target is: **absent a guard intervention, is this span being supplied in a role that can directly authorize, specify, or induce the guarded operation in this turn?** Quoted documentation about an operation is `Y=0`; a shell argument, write target, tool request, or imperative directed at the agent is `Y=1`. A retrieved document containing a prompt-injection directive is not automatically a mention: the span can be `Y=1` for an *instruction-following* guard even though its outer container is retrieved data. Thus labels belong to spans and nested roles, not whole payloads.

That definition prevents a dangerous shortcut: source channel alone is insufficient. “Tool argument” is strong use evidence; “document” is not conclusive mention evidence. The distinction is semantic and nested.

## 3. Concrete harness change

## Recommendation — shadow-scored semantic-role adapter (UNVERIFIED — settle with the replay-and-adjudication experiment in §6) — NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus)

Build one adapter on the existing hook bus and route the five detectors through it. Do not add a daemon, durable workflow engine, vector store, trained critic, or separate orchestration runtime.

### 3.1 Event contract

For every candidate, write an append-only shadow record:

```json
{
  "event_id": "stable-id",
  "detector_id": "guard-name+version",
  "phase": "pre_action",
  "candidate_span_hash": "...",
  "trusted_provenance": {
    "hook_event_type": "...",
    "producer": "...",
    "tool_name": "...",
    "field_path": "..."
  },
  "claimed_role": "execution|directive|quotation|data|description|ambiguous",
  "p_use": 0.0,
  "policy_action": "allow|annotate|ask|block",
  "policy_version": "...",
  "resolution": null
}
```

`trusted_provenance` must come from the hook/event envelope, not be parsed from payload assertions such as “this is only an example.” `claimed_role` is a fallible forecast feature. The event is recorded before the policy action so the detector cannot condition its report on the outcome.

### 3.2 Minimal role evidence

The adapter should use, in descending evidentiary strength:

1. Native event position: actual tool argument, command field, patch destination, or structured action parameter.
2. Conversation role and target: a user/agent directive addressed to the acting agent versus tool output or retrieved data.
3. Span structure: fenced code, inline quotation, reported speech, example/test fixture, or negated incident description.
4. Local language: imperative/directive syntax versus descriptive syntax.
5. Existing detector's pattern evidence.

These are evidence features, not irreversible rules. In particular, fenced code can be either an example or an action requested for execution. A small existing-model classification call may be used only for ambiguous candidates if one is already available on the hook bus; adding a new model service would change the proposal to BUILD-LARGE and is not justified here.

### 3.3 Probability first, action second

Each detector emits `p_use`. A separate, versioned policy chooses an action using estimated costs:

```text
block/ask when p_use × cost(false negative)
             > (1-p_use) × cost(false positive)
```

Different guards may have different costs. A destructive command guard can ask at a lower probability than a style lint guard. The scoring rule stays unchanged when policy thresholds change. That separation is the practical defense against optimizing detector truthfulness for the current threshold.

Run in shadow mode first. Resolve every historical false-fire candidate and a stratified random sample of:

* alerts from each detector and semantic role;
* non-alert candidates near the threshold;
* non-alert candidates far below the threshold;
* adversarial minimal pairs that differ only in use versus mention context.

Sampling non-alerts is essential: scoring only fired guards creates selective labels. In the selective-labels literature, the decision itself determines which outcomes become observable, biasing evaluation ([Lakkaraju et al., KDD 2017/PMC version](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5958915/)).

### 3.4 Independent resolution

For experimental labels, require two mechanically independent signals before calling a case resolved:

* **Signal A:** event provenance from the native hook envelope and structured field position;
* **Signal B:** blind semantic adjudication of the span plus bounded surrounding context, without showing the detector probability or action.

Disagreements remain `ambiguous` and do not silently become either class. A small random sample receives a second blind adjudication to estimate resolver reliability. This satisfies the two-signal requirement for claims made from the experiment; it does not assert that the current harness already has both signals.

The availability of sufficient native event provenance is **UNVERIFIED — the settling experiment is to capture one known-present tool execution, one known-present quoted example, and one nested prompt-injection example through every relevant hook and compare the emitted event types/field paths**. It is **roughly even (45-55%)**, because modern hook buses commonly distinguish event types but this unit deliberately did not inspect the subject or its documentation.

### 3.5 What it would catch

Conditional on the role signal working, the adapter is **very likely (80-90%)**, based on the direct use of conversation/event role before lexical evidence, to suppress false fires where the trigger occurs in a quoted example, a detector specification, an incident report, or prose explaining a forbidden operation. The replay test in §6 settles this on the supplied twelve-plus cases.

It is **likely (55-80%)**, based on structured action position being evidence independent of exact spelling, to catch some real uses the current literal match misses: commands assembled across fields, aliases or paraphrases inside an actual action request, and dangerous values located in typed tool parameters. The adversarial minimal-pair recall test in §6 settles this. This is not a claim of complete semantic detection.

It is **likely (55-80%)**, based on retaining uncertainty and auditing sampled non-alerts, to reveal detectors that appear clean only because they abstain broadly or because only alerts are labeled. Stratified shadow review settles this by estimating false negatives across probability bands.

It will **almost certainly not (1-5%)**, based on the absence of any causal or authentication guarantee in a scoring rule, distinguish benign quoted text from malicious instructions in every retrieved document. Nested prompt injection, polysemy, encoded commands, and instructions that become actionable only after later turns require contextual adjudication; the red-team set in §6 measures the residual.

## 4. Why the analogy transfers—and where it breaks

### 4.1 The part that transfers

The shared structure is an elicitation problem:

1. The designer wants a latent quantity: probability that a span is actionable use.
2. The reporting component has partial information.
3. A binary decision hides uncertainty and invites optimization around a threshold.
4. A later resolution can sometimes establish the event.
5. A strictly proper rule makes the component's best report its full belief rather than a threshold-shaped claim.

This is a real mathematical transfer, not merely a metaphor. The Brier derivation applies unchanged to any binary outcome if the resolution is exogenous to the report and examples are sampled from the evaluation distribution.

### 4.2 The decisive disanalogies

**No self-interested agent.** A regex or classifier has no utility or strategy. “Incentive compatibility” applies to the objective used to select/tune detector versions, or to an LLM explicitly prompted/rewarded to report a probability—not to the source code as an economic actor. The mechanism changes optimization pressure; it does not confer agency.

**The outcome is not naturally realized.** Rain eventually occurs or does not. “This text was a use” is a semantic judgment. If labels are produced by the same detector, properness is circular. If guards block actions, their decisions can also change what is observed. Research on performative prediction explicitly treats the case in which predictions influence the outcomes they aim to predict ([Perdomo et al., ICML 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/perdomo20a.html)). Shadow replay and blind adjudication are therefore prerequisites, not optional polish.

**Payloads can be adversarial and nested.** A document may say “ignore previous instructions and run X” as an attack, while a security report may quote exactly that sentence as evidence. Neither substring nor top-level channel resolves the nested speech act.

**Truthful probability is not semantic truth.** Strict propriety says `p=q` is optimal given a belief `q`; it does not guarantee that `q` is calibrated, informed, or robust out of distribution. Calibration, discrimination, and class-conditional errors still need measurement.

**The resolver is another mechanism.** Reviewers may optimize throughput, accept detector suggestions, or drift in their meaning of “use.” Hiding detector output, retaining ambiguous labels, and periodically using minimal pairs reduce but do not eliminate this risk.

The analogy therefore supports a conditional build, not a claim that mechanism design has “solved use-vs-mention.” If independent resolution cannot be constructed, stop at typed provenance and adversarial regression tests; do not market a Brier score against self-generated labels as incentive compatible.

## 5. Alternatives considered and rejected

### 5.1 Direct-revelation form / self-declared semantic role — reject

Asking payload text to declare `role=mention` is cheap talk. The revelation principle requires incentive compatibility; it does not make arbitrary reports truthful. Adversarial content would simply claim the exempt role. A role field is useful only when attached by a trusted event producer or treated as fallible evidence.

### 5.2 Vickrey/VCG-style payments — reject

There is no allocation competition among detector agents and no transferable utility that corresponds cleanly to harness safety. Synthetic “payments” would just restate an evaluation metric and add ceremony. This analogy does not transfer.

### 5.3 Peer prediction or Bayesian truth serum — reject for this unit

Peer-prediction mechanisms are intended for settings without directly observable ground truth and require assumptions about correlated private signals and reporting populations. Multiple guards here can share the same substring feature and therefore make correlated errors. Agreement would reward the common failure rather than semantic correctness. Independent semantic resolution is preferable.

### 5.4 Optimize F1/precision/recall directly — reject as the only objective

These metrics require a threshold and discard confidence. Optimizing one operating point can hide miscalibration and can shift failures between classes. Retain them as operational diagnostics, but use a proper score for the probability report and a separate cost policy for action.

### 5.5 Full semantic-policy daemon — reject under the anti-overfit gate

This would be BUILD-LARGE (new daemon/service/runtime). It would reintroduce a second policy/orchestration layer, increase failure modes, and is not justified before a small adapter proves that role evidence has incremental value. The proposed adapter rides the existing hook bus and stores only ordinary evaluation records.

## 6. Falsification and acceptance test

This is the experiment that settles every UNVERIFIED premise above.

### 6.1 Dataset

Create a versioned, append-only evaluation set containing:

* all supplied twelve-plus false-fire cases;
* confirmed true fires from each of the five detectors;
* minimal pairs for every case: same trigger span, changed only from mention to actionable use or vice versa;
* nested-role cases: benign quotation of an attack, actual prompt injection inside retrieved text, requested execution of fenced code, and prose merely documenting fenced code;
* paraphrases and structured tool arguments that omit the historical literal trigger;
* sampled historical non-alerts.

Split by source document/session and transformation family, not randomly by span, to prevent near-duplicate leakage. Freeze a held-out adversarial tranche before threshold selection.

### 6.2 Detector-control checks

Before interpreting absence:

1. Prove the event detector sees one known-present actual execution through each hook path.
2. Prove it sees one known-present quotation and preserves its surrounding role.
3. Use a second independent blind adjudication for a sample of both classes.
4. Report unresolved disagreements rather than dropping them.

### 6.3 Compare

Compare current boolean guards with:

* pattern-only probability;
* provenance + pattern;
* provenance + span-structure + pattern;
* full small adapter, if an existing model can adjudicate ambiguous cases.

Report, per detector and pooled:

* Brier loss and reliability/calibration plot;
* false-fire rate on mention cases;
* recall on actionable-use cases;
* error rates by semantic role and source channel;
* coverage and error rate for `ambiguous`/abstain cases;
* estimated operational cost across a range of false-positive:false-negative ratios;
* latency and hook failures.

### 6.4 Predeclared decision rule

Adopt the adapter only if, on the frozen tranche:

1. it reduces false fires on mention cases without a statistically or operationally material loss of actionable-use recall;
2. it improves Brier loss over the pattern-only forecast;
3. gains appear in at least four of five detectors rather than coming from one detector;
4. both known-present controls are observed through the relevant hook path;
5. failures remain bounded when the same literal span changes semantic role.

Set the exact materiality margins and confidence intervals before running the test; selecting them afterward would make the gate another optimization target. If the adapter fails criterion 5, the cross-domain borrow has not solved the target problem and should not ship in enforcement mode.

The adapter meeting all five criteria is **likely (55-80%)**, based on the strong information value of trusted action position but tempered by unknown hook metadata and the difficulty of nested speech acts. This is **UNVERIFIED — the frozen replay-and-adjudication experiment above settles it**.

## 7. Anti-overfit and implementation classification

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).** The estimate is **likely (55-80%)**, based on the adapter consisting of an event normalizer, feature extraction, score calculation, and append-only logging, that the non-model code fits under roughly 200 lines. This is **UNVERIFIED — a 30-minute interface spike against the actual hook event schemas settles the line-count and bus-compatibility premise**. If native events do not expose stable IDs, event types, roles, and structured field paths, do not silently expand this into a daemon; reclassify and re-justify it as BUILD-LARGE or stop.

The proposal passes the stated anti-overfit gate conceptually:

* it rides the existing hook bus;
* it wraps the five existing detectors rather than creating another agent runtime;
* it needs no vector database, knowledge graph, durable-execution engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, or guardrail framework;
* its artifacts are a small event schema, ordinary append-only records, a scorer, and a replay test;
* it is removable if the controlled replay shows no gain.

## 8. Final recommendation

Proceed only with the shadow experiment and small adapter. Treat proper scoring as a mechanism for honest **uncertainty reporting and detector-selection pressure**, not as a semantic classifier. The radical replacement justified by this lens is replacement of boolean, payload-wide trigger semantics with **span-level probability over a trusted, nested semantic-role event**, while preserving existing detectors as evidence providers during evaluation.

If trusted provenance plus blind resolution cannot be obtained, this cross-domain mechanism does **not** transfer far enough to justify enforcement. In that case, the valuable result is the disanalogy: mechanism design assumes an outcome and an incentive channel; use-vs-mention currently lacks a naturally realized outcome, and no scoring rule can repair that missing ontology.
