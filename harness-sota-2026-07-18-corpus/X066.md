# X066 — Cross-domain borrow: epidemiology → orchestration

**Unit:** X066  
**Lens:** epidemiologic surveillance, case definitions, sensitivity/specificity, predictive value, base-rate reasoning, and multiple-source capture–recapture  
**Target decision:** how many parallel workers to run, and when another worker stops being worth running  
**Scope discipline:** This is an ideation unit. Per the task, I did not inspect or make claims about the local harness/repository. The only write is this artifact.

## Executive conclusion

The borrow transfers, but only as a **bounded decision aid for discovery/enumeration work**, not as a proof that a task is complete.

Epidemiology's useful mechanism is a surveillance pipeline with four coupled parts:

1. a stable **case definition** so sources report comparable things;
2. separate measurement of **sensitivity** (how much of the true burden is found) and **positive predictive value** (how much of what is reported is real);
3. **multiple-source capture–recapture** or multiple-systems estimation, which uses the overlap pattern among independently ascertained case lists to estimate the unobserved cell; and
4. **active/sentinel follow-up** when routine ascertainment is plausibly incomplete.

The concrete harness translation is to treat workers as case-ascertainment sources and validated, deduplicated findings as cases. Start with a small number of deliberately heterogeneous workers, construct a capture-history matrix, measure each worker's validated unique yield and false-positive burden, and add workers in waves. Do not stop merely because workers agree. Stop only when (a) a cautious upper bound on remaining *actionable* cases is below a task-specific tolerance, (b) a mechanically independent sentinel worker finds no material new confirmed cases, and (c) an external task oracle passes where one exists.

This would **very likely (80-90%)** improve worker-count decisions on bounded audit, search, review, and enumeration tasks because those tasks have a reasonably stable case universe, findings can be linked across sources, and overlap/unique-yield data directly expose redundancy; the evidence is the structural match to multi-list surveillance and CDC's use of combinations of data sources to assess sensitivity. It is **unlikely (20-45%)** to give trustworthy completeness estimates for implementation, open-ended research, or agents that mutate shared state, because the population is not closed and capture probabilities change as workers alter the environment. Both predictions are **UNVERIFIED for this harness; the settling experiment is the replay study in “Experiment that can falsify the recommendation.”**

The most dangerous failure is correlated workers. Agents that share a base model, prompt, context, tools, or retrieval ranking are not independent sources. Positive dependence increases overlap and therefore can make a two-list estimator understate the unseen residue: exactly the wrong direction for a stopping rule. Consequently, epidemiology supplies a disciplined vocabulary, measurements, and escalation protocol; it does not license an automatic “estimated complete” badge.

## 1. The mechanism in its own field

### 1.1 Case definitions make cases commensurable

Public-health surveillance begins by specifying the health event under surveillance. CDC's *Updated Guidelines for Evaluating Public Health Surveillance Systems* says a system depends on a clear case definition; definitions may combine clinical manifestations, laboratory results, epidemiologic information, and levels of certainty such as confirmed, probable, and possible. It also says standard definitions improve specificity and comparability across data sources ([CDC, 2001](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5013a1.htm)).

This is not clerical decoration. If one hospital counts a suggestive symptom while another requires a laboratory result, their lists do not represent the same event. An overlap calculation on those lists confounds ascertainment with semantics. Tiered definitions let surveillance retain sensitivity at the “suspected” tier while reserving interventions or burden counts for confirmed cases.

### 1.2 Sensitivity and predictive value answer different questions

CDC defines surveillance sensitivity at the case-reporting level as the proportion of actual cases detected, and at another level as the ability to detect outbreaks or changes over time. It defines predictive value positive (PVP, commonly PPV) as the proportion of reported cases that actually have the event. CDC explicitly warns that low PVP misdirects investigation resources and that PVP depends on the case definition's sensitivity and specificity **and on prevalence** ([CDC, 2001](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5013a1.htm)).

The base-rate point is mathematical, not rhetorical. For prevalence \(\pi\), sensitivity \(Se\), and specificity \(Sp\):

\[
PPV = \frac{Se\pi}{Se\pi + (1-Sp)(1-\pi)}.
\]

At \(Se=0.90\), \(Sp=0.95\), and \(\pi=0.01\), PPV is only about 15.4%. Thus a high-sensitivity detector can produce mostly false alarms when true cases are rare. In surveillance, the appropriate operating point depends on the use: broad screening can tolerate suspected cases, while an expensive intervention needs a more specific confirmation layer.

CDC also notes a useful nuance: a surveillance system with incomplete sensitivity can still monitor trends if sensitivity stays reasonably constant; changes in tests, awareness, or collection methods can instead create artifacts ([CDC, 2001](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5013a1.htm)). This supports trend monitoring but not an exhaustive-completeness claim.

### 1.3 Capture–recapture estimates what all observed sources missed

The epidemiologic adaptation of capture–recapture links records for the same person or event across two or more ascertainment sources. In the simplest two-source case:

- source 1 finds \(n_1\) cases;
- source 2 finds \(n_2\) cases;
- \(m\) cases occur in both lists.

Under strong assumptions, the Lincoln–Petersen estimate is

\[
\hat N = \frac{n_1 n_2}{m},
\]

and a common small-sample correction is the Chapman estimate

\[
\hat N_C = \frac{(n_1+1)(n_2+1)}{m+1} - 1.
\]

The estimated unseen burden is \(\hat N - |L_1 \cup L_2|\). Intuitively, high overlap suggests that the sources are repeatedly sampling the same cases and the unseen pool may be small; low overlap suggests substantial unseen mass.

With three or more sources, each case receives a capture history such as 101 (found by sources 1 and 3, not 2). Log-linear multiple-systems models use counts in the observed capture-history cells and model list interactions to estimate the unobserved all-zero cell. The International Working Group for Disease Monitoring and Forecasting formalized the history/theory and human-disease applications in paired papers ([theory paper, 1995](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.aje.a117558); [applications paper, 1995](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.aje.a117559)). Hook and Regal's epidemiologic review is explicitly about both methods and limitations ([Hook & Regal, 1995](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.epirev.a036192)).

CDC names capture–recapture as one way to estimate total cases and hence assess surveillance sensitivity. It also recommends calculating sensitivity for individual data sources and combinations of sources to judge whether adding or removing a source changes surveillance results ([CDC, 2001](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5013a1.htm)). That add/remove-source question is the closest direct analogue to deciding whether another worker helps.

### 1.4 The assumptions are part of the mechanism

The estimator is meaningful only to the extent that several assumptions are defensible:

- **Same case universe / closure:** lists concern the same population in the same bounded period; membership does not change materially during observation.
- **Record linkage is accurate:** the same case can be recognized across lists, while different cases are not falsely merged.
- **Capture probabilities are adequately modeled:** cases are not silently partitioned into easy and impossible-to-detect groups.
- **Source dependence is absent or modeled:** in the basic two-source estimator, captures are independent; with more lists, selected dependencies may be modeled, but model choice cannot reveal every unobserved dependence.
- **Case classification is valid:** false positives are not treated as genuine population members.

A recent malaria population-size study gives concrete evidence for these fragilities rather than treating them as footnotes. It defines multi-source capture–recapture as using overlap across three or more samples, compares open and closed population assumptions, evaluates record-linkage sensitivity/specificity across 24 linkage settings, and reports that more flexible linkage created more matches and smaller population estimates. It concludes that linkage quality needs thorough assessment and transparent reporting ([Rerolle et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-56810-y)).

There is also direct evidence that low overlap can make estimates unusably large. A 2025 idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis study using two health systems reported that low patient overlap produced a highly inflated capture–recapture incidence estimate ([Linkov et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12931-025-03164-2)). This is a useful warning for agent runs: zero or tiny overlap is not proof of extraordinary hidden burden; it can mean that the lists do not sample a shared universe or cannot support the estimator.

### 1.5 Active surveillance is selective escalation, not permanent maximal effort

CDC describes active surveillance as contacting all providers and institutions responsible for reporting and lists it among ways to improve sensitivity. It also recommends external standards, tracking suspected cases investigated and ruled out, and monitoring diagnostic effort ([CDC, 2001](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5013a1.htm)). The transferable idea is to escalate ascertainment when routine passive sources look incomplete, while explicitly recording the cost and ruled-out candidates. It is not “always use every source.”

## 2. Structural mapping to an agent harness

| Epidemiology | Orchestration translation | Required operational definition |
|---|---|---|
| Event under surveillance | Actionable finding relevant to the task | A task-specific case definition fixed before workers see results |
| Confirmed / probable / possible case | Verified / plausible / candidate finding | Evidence threshold and adjudication state |
| Ascertainment source | Worker with a distinct search method, role, model/tool route, or evidence source | A declared ascertainment stratum, not merely a different worker ID |
| Same individual on two lists | Same underlying finding reported by two workers | Stable fingerprint plus reviewed fuzzy-link fallback |
| Capture history | Bit vector of workers that found a case | One row per deduplicated finding |
| Sensitivity | Fraction of true actionable findings detected | Usually estimated, because truth is rarely fully observed |
| PPV | Fraction of worker reports that validate as genuine cases | Confirmed unique findings / adjudicated candidate reports |
| Prevalence / base rate | Density of remaining real findings among examined candidates | Falls as an audit saturates; must be estimated per wave |
| Active surveillance | Launch a targeted, expensive specialist or exhaustive scan | Triggered escalation rather than default fan-out |
| Sentinel source | Independent holdout worker/procedure used to test residual yield | Hidden from earlier worker outputs and materially different in method |
| Unobserved all-zero cell | Real findings missed by every worker | Estimand, never directly observed |

The key conceptual correction is that **worker count is not the exposure variable that matters**. The effective number of ascertainment sources depends on diversity and dependence. Ten clones may act like one list. Three workers using distinct evidence channels may offer more coverage than ten prompt variants.

The analogy also changes the objective. The controller should not maximize raw findings or consensus. It should minimize expected residual harm plus investigation and compute cost, using *validated novel cases per incremental source* as the marginal-yield signal.

## 3. Recommended harness mechanism — UNVERIFIED: surveillance-gated adaptive fan-out

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

This recommendation is **UNVERIFIED because no replay has measured capture dependence, linkage error, or stopping regret in this harness; the settling experiment is the replay study below.** It deliberately avoids a second orchestration runtime, vector database/knowledge graph, durable-execution engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, and guardrail framework.

### 3.1 Applicability gate

Enable the mechanism only when all of the following are declared:

1. **Bounded snapshot:** workers inspect the same immutable or version-pinned subject during a wave.
2. **Enumerable event:** a finding can be expressed as a stable object with evidence and location.
3. **Linkable reports:** two descriptions of the same underlying issue can be matched with reviewable rules.
4. **Action threshold:** the owner can state what severity/count of unseen cases is tolerable.
5. **At least two genuinely different ascertainment routes:** for example static search versus execution trace, or documentation/specification versus behavior test.

If any condition fails, record “capture estimate not applicable” and use ordinary budget/oracle-driven orchestration. This negative gate is part of the proposal, not a fallback embarrassment.

### 3.2 Fix the case definition before fan-out

For each task, store a compact case-definition record:

```json
{
  "unit": "finding identity boundary",
  "eligible": "what counts",
  "excluded": "what does not count",
  "evidence_minimum": "path+line, command output, test, or URL",
  "states": ["candidate", "probable", "confirmed", "ruled_out"],
  "actionable_severity": ["critical", "high"],
  "snapshot": "immutable subject/version identifier"
}
```

Changing the definition starts a new surveillance epoch. Do not compare yield curves across epochs as though sensitivity stayed constant.

### 3.3 Run heterogeneous sources in waves

Start with three workers when three materially different ascertainment strata exist; otherwise start with two and do **not** compute a strong completeness claim. Workers in a wave must not see one another's outputs before submitting, because exposure would make later capture conditional on earlier capture.

This initial count of three is **likely (55-80%)** to be a useful default for bounded discovery tasks because three lists expose more dependence structure than two while keeping the first wave small; the evidence is the epidemiologic use of three-or-more-list log-linear models and the severe identifiability weakness of a single overlap count. It is **UNVERIFIED; the settling experiment is a randomized replay comparing starts of 2, 3, and 5 workers on tasks with an exhaustive reference set.** It must remain configurable, not universal.

Each worker emits candidate records with:

- canonical subject coordinates;
- concise claim;
- evidence;
- severity;
- ascertainment stratum;
- cost and elapsed time;
- no peer-derived identifiers.

### 3.4 Link, adjudicate, and validate the detector

Normalize obvious exact identities first (same test, symbol, file/line, error fingerprint, or requirement). Apply fuzzy matching only as a proposed link, with an adjudication reason retained. Over-merging increases apparent recapture and falsely signals saturation; under-merging decreases recapture and exaggerates unseen burden.

Before trusting the matcher or case classifier, seed known-present controls:

- two differently worded reports of the same synthetic finding, which must merge;
- two nearby but distinct findings, which must remain separate;
- one evidence-backed confirmed case and one unsupported candidate, which must classify differently.

These controls validate that the detector can find a known-present item before it reports absence. A second, mechanically independent signal is manual review of a stratified sample of matched and unmatched pairs. Re-running the same matcher is not independent validation.

### 3.5 Compute surveillance statistics, not a single magic score

After each wave, report at least:

- candidate reports;
- confirmed/probable/ruled-out reports;
- PPV by worker and ascertainment stratum;
- deduplicated confirmed cases;
- confirmed cases unique to each worker;
- capture-history cell counts;
- pairwise overlap/Jaccard matrix;
- validated novel actionable cases per unit cost;
- a capture–recapture estimate and interval **only when assumptions and overlap support it**;
- explicit assumption diagnostics: closure, linkage audit, source dependence, and capture heterogeneity.

Never pool candidate and confirmed cases in the capture matrix used for stopping. A permissive case definition is useful for screening, but low-PPV candidate volume is not residual burden.

### 3.6 Continue or stop using two independent signals

Add one new ascertainment stratum, or a small wave if latency dominates, when any of these holds:

- the upper uncertainty bound on unseen actionable cases exceeds tolerance;
- the newest source still contributes material validated unique cases per cost;
- overlap is too low to identify the estimate;
- dependence diagnostics say existing sources are clones;
- a high-severity stratum has not had an independent source;
- the external oracle fails.

Permit “surveillance saturation” only when both mechanically independent conditions hold:

1. **Overlap/yield signal:** under explicitly recorded assumptions, the conservative upper bound on remaining actionable cases is below tolerance and the last wave's validated marginal yield is below its cost threshold.
2. **Sentinel signal:** a held-out worker using a materially different method, with no access to prior reports, returns no material *new confirmed* case (or a predeclared sub-threshold yield).

If an objective oracle exists—tests, schema validation, exhaustive enumerator, proof obligation—it is a third required gate. The status must say **“surveillance saturated under stated assumptions,” not “complete.”**

The combined gate is **very likely (80-90%)** safer than stopping on duplicate rate alone because positive dependence can fool overlap but does not automatically fool a genuinely different holdout or an external oracle; the evidence is the direction of bias in the two-list formula and CDC's recommendation to use external standards and multiple measurements of sensitivity. It remains **UNVERIFIED; the experiment is to compare premature-stop rates with overlap-only, sentinel-only, and combined gates against exhaustive reference sets.**

### 3.7 Minimal event record and controller output

The existing bus needs only append-only structured events and a small scoring hook, for example:

```json
{
  "task_epoch": "...",
  "worker": "...",
  "stratum": "runtime-trace",
  "case_id": "stable-or-reviewed-link-id",
  "state": "confirmed",
  "severity": "high",
  "evidence": ["..."],
  "cost": 1.0,
  "capture_history": "1010"
}
```

Controller output should be advice with reasons:

```text
CONTINUE: high unique confirmed yield from runtime-trace; static sources positively correlated.
NEXT STRATUM: specification-conformance.
DO NOT ESTIMATE COMPLETENESS: only 2 overlaps; linkage audit failed.
```

or:

```text
SATURATED-UNDER-ASSUMPTIONS: actionable unseen upper bound < tolerance;
independent sentinel added 0 confirmed high-severity cases; oracle passed.
```

No daemon or persistent semantic-memory service is needed. A per-task JSONL ledger and end-of-wave hook are enough for the experiment.

## 4. What this catches that ordinary fan-out misses

These are predicted capabilities, not claims that the current local harness lacks them.

### 4.1 Clone inflation

Raw parallelism reports “ten workers ran.” The capture matrix reports that all ten behave as one highly dependent source. This is **very likely (80-90%)** to catch some waste from prompt/model/tool homogeneity because pairwise overlap and unique validated yield directly measure redundant ascertainment; the evidence is the same-list overlap structure used in multiple-source surveillance. It is **UNVERIFIED; settle it by clustering worker capture vectors and testing whether clusters predict zero marginal discoveries on held-out tasks.**

### 4.2 False saturation and hidden residue

If each new source continues to contribute many confirmed unique cases, observed agreement is not saturation. Low overlap or persistent singleton capture histories warn that substantial residue may remain. This is **likely (55-80%)** to detect under-exploration on bounded tasks because unique-cell counts drive unseen-population estimates; the evidence is the capture–recapture mechanism. It is **UNVERIFIED; settle it by hiding known defects from selected evidence channels and testing whether the controller continues until a different channel finds them.**

### 4.3 False progress from low-PPV output

A worker producing many plausible claims may look productive while consuming review time. Tracking confirmed unique cases and ruled-out candidates exposes the cost. This is **very likely (80-90%)** to improve allocation where findings are adjudicable because PPV directly measures the share of reports that validate; CDC links low PVP to misdirected investigation resources. It is **UNVERIFIED in this harness; settle it by measuring reviewer time and accepted findings per worker stratum.**

### 4.4 Base-rate collapse late in a run

As obvious cases are removed from the remaining pool, the prevalence of real undiscovered findings among speculative candidates usually falls; fixed-specificity searches then lose PPV. A falling confirmed-unique-per-candidate curve can stop waste earlier than a fixed worker cap. This is **likely (55-80%)** for finite audits because depletion lowers the remaining base rate, and PPV depends on prevalence; the evidence is the PPV identity and CDC guidance. It is **UNVERIFIED; settle it by plotting wave-indexed candidate PPV and confirmed unique yield on reference-set replays.**

### 4.5 Definition and detector drift

A sudden yield change can come from a new case definition, matcher, tool, or worker method rather than a changed subject. Epoching definitions and running linkage canaries prevents a measurement artifact from being called improvement or completion. This is **very likely (80-90%)** to catch some measurement drift because the proposed record explicitly versions the definition and validates known positive/negative links; the evidence is CDC's warning that diagnostic and method changes alter sensitivity and can create surveillance artifacts. It is **UNVERIFIED; settle it by intentionally changing matcher thresholds and verifying that the system opens a new epoch and blocks cross-epoch trend claims.**

### 4.6 Uncovered high-risk strata

An overall low residual estimate can hide a rare, severe subgroup with low capture probability. Stratifying capture histories by severity/domain/evidence route makes “no independent coverage for this high-risk stratum” visible. This is **likely (55-80%)** to catch some pockets of risk because heterogeneous capture probability is a known threat and stratification is a standard remedy, but sparse strata also make estimates unstable; the evidence is the methodological literature and Rerolle et al.'s explicit modeling of temporal and individual heterogeneity. It is **UNVERIFIED; settle it with seeded rare severe cases accessible only to one evidence stratum.**

## 5. Adversarial analysis: where the analogy breaks

### 5.1 Agents are not independent surveillance sources

Two hospitals may have different referral patterns; two agents using the same foundation model often share training priors, attention failures, tool affordances, prompt framing, and retrieval rank. Their errors can be much more correlated than worker IDs suggest.

For the two-list estimator, positive dependence makes \(m\) too large relative to independence and drives \(\hat N=n_1n_2/m\) downward, creating false confidence. Negative dependence makes \(m\) small and can explode \(\hat N\), as the low-overlap IPF example illustrates. Multi-list log-linear models can represent selected interactions, but sparse data and shared latent failure modes leave the all-zero cell weakly identified.

**Consequence:** overlap is a triage signal, never sufficient evidence to stop. “Different prompt” does not qualify as mechanical independence without empirical capture-vector evidence.

### 5.2 The population of findings may be open or constructed by the search

Disease cases exist before surveillance observes them. In agent work, one worker may edit code, create a design, reveal a dependency, or change requirements; this creates and destroys possible findings. Research agents also expand the question as they learn. The population is then open and path-dependent.

**Consequence:** apply the method only to immutable snapshots and fixed epochs. For build tasks, use tests and acceptance criteria rather than an unseen-case estimator.

### 5.3 “Case identity” is semantically contestable

One defect may have five symptoms; five defects may share one error string. Epidemiologic record linkage already matters, but agent findings can be more abstract than persons with identifiers. LLM-based deduplication also shares the same correlated judgment problems as the workers.

**Consequence:** keep match evidence, validate known merge/separate controls, audit both matched and unmatched samples, and sensitivity-test linkage thresholds. Never silently collapse by embedding similarity.

### 5.4 Capture probabilities are extremely heterogeneous

Trivial syntax issues and deep distributed-state bugs do not have equal probability of discovery. A universal blind spot produces no observed cases and therefore no recaptures from which to infer it. Capture–recapture extrapolates from observed capture patterns; it cannot reliably quantify a completely unreachable stratum.

**Consequence:** enumerate risk strata from an external taxonomy/specification and require at least one purpose-built ascertainment route per critical stratum. The method cannot certify that the taxonomy itself is complete.

### 5.5 False positives violate the simple population model

Classic capture–recapture commonly treats list members as real population members. Agent workers hallucinate, overstate evidence, and split speculation into multiple reports. A shared hallucination can even appear as a high-confidence recapture.

**Consequence:** adjudicate before estimating and track PPV separately. Consensus is not confirmation; evidence must be checked against the subject or an oracle.

### 5.6 Sequential stopping changes worker behavior and inference

If later workers see prior outputs, they are deliberately conditioned on earlier captures. Adaptive assignment also chooses the next source based on observed yield. This is useful orchestration but invalidates a naive fixed-design interpretation.

**Consequence:** keep within-wave workers blinded; call the output a decision heuristic unless the adaptive design's inferential properties have been simulated. Do not report textbook confidence intervals as calibrated after arbitrary adaptive stopping.

### 5.7 The cost of missing a case is not uniform

One unseen security defect can dominate twenty minor style findings. A scalar “estimated remaining count” obscures severity and asymmetric loss.

**Consequence:** estimate and stop by actionable severity stratum, and retain hard coverage/oracle gates for catastrophic categories even when aggregate marginal yield is low.

### 5.8 Sentinel surveillance is not exhaustive surveillance

Epidemiology permits incomplete but stable systems for trend monitoring. That maps to monitoring repeated harness runs, not to claiming a single task is finished. Stable sensitivity can preserve trend direction while missing a constant fraction of findings.

**Consequence:** label outputs precisely: “trend stable,” “surveillance saturated,” or “oracle complete” are different states.

## 6. Decision rule in operational form

The following is intentionally a policy skeleton, not a statistically validated theorem:

```text
if subject is mutable or case definition/linkage is unavailable:
    do not use capture-based stopping
    use acceptance oracle + explicit budget

run initial blinded heterogeneous wave
adjudicate and link cases
validate matcher on known merge/separate controls
audit a stratified linkage sample with a different procedure/person

if overlap is tiny, sources are highly correlated, or linkage audit fails:
    report estimate as non-identifiable
    launch a different evidence stratum if expected harm justifies cost
else:
    estimate unseen actionable residue with uncertainty and sensitivity analyses

continue if:
    unseen-actionable upper bound > tolerance
    OR last wave adds material confirmed unique yield
    OR critical risk stratum lacks independent coverage
    OR oracle fails

otherwise run blinded independent sentinel

stop as SATURATED-UNDER-ASSUMPTIONS only if:
    overlap/yield gate passes
    AND sentinel gate passes
    AND available oracle passes
```

No fixed number such as 3, 5, or 20 is “optimal” in isolation. The answer is a sequential number of **effective independent ascertainment strata**, constrained by marginal validated yield, uncertainty, task loss, and cost.

## 7. Experiment that can falsify the recommendation

This experiment is necessary because the central premise—worker capture histories contain enough independent information to improve stopping—is **UNVERIFIED**.

### 7.1 Dataset

Construct at least 30 bounded historical tasks for which an exhaustive or near-exhaustive reference set can be obtained by an independent process. Suitable tasks include finite static audits, requirement-to-implementation conformance checks, error-log fingerprint enumeration, and repository searches with a known snapshot. Exclude implementation tasks from the primary analysis.

Build the reference set from two mechanically independent signals:

1. an exhaustive machine enumerator/test oracle where possible; and
2. blinded expert adjudication of the union of all worker findings plus deliberately seeded cases.

The reference set must include seeded rare/high-severity cases and known duplicate paraphrases to validate the case detector and linker.

### 7.2 Arms

Randomize replay order and compare:

- fixed 2 workers;
- fixed 5 workers;
- fixed 10 workers;
- stop on raw duplicate-rate threshold;
- stop on capture estimate alone;
- stop on independent sentinel alone;
- the proposed combined surveillance gate.

Cross worker diversity conditions:

- same model + prompt variants;
- same model + distinct tool/evidence routes;
- different model families where available;
- specialist role with exclusive evidence source.

Workers remain blinded within waves. Replay against immutable snapshots.

### 7.3 Measurements

Primary outcomes:

- missed confirmed high/critical cases at stop;
- total confirmed reference cases missed;
- compute/time/reviewer cost;
- stopping regret relative to the cheapest run that meets the miss tolerance.

Calibration and diagnostic outcomes:

- coverage of unseen-case upper bounds;
- pairwise and higher-order capture dependence;
- validated unique yield by wave;
- PPV by source and wave;
- matcher sensitivity and specificity on seeded and reviewed pairs;
- how often the method correctly refuses to estimate;
- result sensitivity to linkage threshold and case-definition tier;
- effective-source count versus nominal worker count.

### 7.4 Predeclared success and rejection criteria

Adopt the mechanism only if the combined gate reduces median cost relative to fixed-10 while remaining non-inferior on missed high/critical cases, and if its upper bounds have acceptable empirical coverage under at least the distinct-evidence-route condition. Exact non-inferiority margins must be set from owner loss preferences before seeing results.

Reject capture-based stopping if any of these occurs:

- positive dependence causes more premature stops than fixed-5 on high/critical cases;
- nominal intervals substantially undercover the unseen reference count;
- linkage sensitivity/specificity changes reverse stop decisions frequently;
- “different” worker strata do not produce measurably different capture vectors;
- the controller rarely refuses estimation despite violated assumptions;
- reviewer cost of adjudication exceeds saved worker cost.

If capture estimation fails but sentinel marginal yield remains predictive, retain only the simpler sentinel-and-oracle controller. That outcome would still validate the epidemiologic lesson about independent ascertainment without retaining a fragile population estimator.

## 8. Anti-overfit gate

The proposed experiment and mechanism pass the specified architectural gate:

- They ride an existing hook/skill/MCP event bus.
- The experimental implementation is an append-only per-task ledger plus an end-of-wave scorer.
- They do not require a new orchestration runtime.
- They do not require vector-DB/KG memory.
- They do not require durable execution.
- They do not train a critic.
- They do not require observability SaaS.
- They do not introduce a guardrail framework.

The smallest useful outcome may be smaller still: a capture-history report and a mandatory independent sentinel, with no automatic estimator. It is **roughly even (45-55%)** whether the numerical unseen-case estimator will add enough calibrated value beyond simple confirmed-unique-yield plus sentinel gating, because agent-source dependence is much stronger than the textbook two-list assumptions while bounded audit tasks still supply useful overlap data. The evidence on the positive side is epidemiology's successful use of multi-source overlap; the evidence on the negative side is the documented sensitivity to source dependence, closure, linkage, and low overlap. This is **UNVERIFIED; the ablation arms above settle it directly.**

## 9. Alternatives considered and rejected

### Fixed worker count

Rejected as the main policy because it ignores task base rate, source diversity, marginal validated yield, and miss cost. Keep it as a safe cap and experimental baseline.

### Stop when workers agree

Rejected because agreement can be induced by shared model bias and positive source dependence. It can be evidence of redundancy without being evidence of coverage.

### Stop when no worker reports anything new

Rejected unless “new” means adjudicated, link-audited, and evaluated over a predeclared independent sentinel. Shared blind spots can yield zero novelty immediately.

### Use only pairwise Jaccard similarity

Rejected as a completeness estimator. It is a useful dependence/redundancy diagnostic but lacks a case universe, case validity, uncertainty model, and severity-aware loss.

### Full Bayesian orchestration service

Rejected at this stage. It would be **BUILD-LARGE (new daemon/service/runtime -- needs its own justification)** and would violate the anti-overfit preference before the minimal signal has been validated. A richer hierarchical model might represent source and case heterogeneity, but proposing it now would add model risk and infrastructure ahead of evidence.

### Vector database or knowledge graph for semantic deduplication

Rejected because it reintroduces a specifically excluded component and does not solve identity adjudication or shared hallucination. Exact coordinates, explicit fingerprints, a reviewable fuzzy-link proposal, and sensitivity analysis are enough for the experiment.

### Make every worker exhaustive

Rejected because active surveillance is expensive and still does not eliminate shared blind spots. Targeted escalation based on uncovered strata and residual harm is the transferable epidemiologic principle.
