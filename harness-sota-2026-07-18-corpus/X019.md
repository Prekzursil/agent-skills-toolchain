# X019 — Cross-domain borrow: intelligence tradecraft → high-volume synthesis

## Executive determination

**Verdict: transfer the intelligence-estimate product architecture, source/evidence separation, calibrated estimative language, explicit alternatives, and audit trail; do not transfer full Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH) as the universal synthesis algorithm.**

The transferable mechanism is not “make a giant ACH spreadsheet.” It is a **layered estimative product**: a very small set of decision-relevant key judgments at the front; a visible assessment base beneath each judgment; explicit separation of observation, assumption, inference, and forecast; calibrated likelihood and a separate account of evidential confidence; dissent and contradictory information preserved rather than averaged away; indicators that would change the judgment; and a fully referenced annex that makes the compression reversible. UK intelligence standards explicitly recommend key judgments or an executive summary for rapid extraction, require contradictory information when it materially affects judgments, distinguish information from assumptions, require uncertainty and confidence to be explained, and retain a fully referenced audit version ([PHIA Common Analytical Standards](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/phia-common-analytical-standards/phia-common-analytical-standards)). US ICD 203 similarly requires source quality, uncertainty, information/assumption/judgment separation, alternatives, relevance, and logical argumentation ([ODNI, ICD 203, 2022 PDF](https://www.odni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203_TA_Analytic_Standards_21_Dec_2022.pdf)). This is directly a solution to “a decision-maker cannot read the underlying volume,” not merely a metaphor borrowed from an adjacent field.

I assess it as **very likely (80-90%)**, because the source field and target field share the core structural problem: many heterogeneous, sometimes duplicated or contradictory reports must be reduced to a few consequential judgments without destroying provenance or uncertainty. The evidence is the explicit role of all-source assessment as adding the “so what?” while information is incomplete, described by the UK Joint Intelligence Organisation ([JIO](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/joint-intelligence-organisation)), and the requirements for key judgments plus auditability in the PHIA standards above. The residual uncertainty is material: intelligence products are written by trained analysts in governed institutions, whereas agent-produced findings may have noisier semantics, repeated derivation from the same upstream source, and automated extraction errors.

I assess mandatory, full-matrix ACH as **unlikely (20-45%)** to improve bulk synthesis quality, because controlled studies do not support treating ACH as a generally effective debiasing or accuracy mechanism. A randomized study of 50 intelligence analysts found mixed evidence on confirmation bias and observed that ACH could increase inconsistency and error ([Dhami, Belton & Mandel 2019](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/acp.3550)). A 2024 experiment found the conventional ACH matrix orientation did not reduce confirmation bias, while a transposed layout did in that task ([Dhami et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11169332/)). ACH remains useful as a *selective prompt* to enumerate alternatives, locate disconfirming evidence, and expose what would change a judgment; it is not justified as the scoring engine for thousands of findings.

This unit used the ideation lens requested and did not inspect or draw conclusions about the local harness repository. The artifact therefore specifies a buildable interface and the experiments that would validate it, but makes no claim that a named local hook, skill, or file already exists.

## 1. What intelligence tradecraft has already solved

### 1.1 Layered estimative products: key judgments backed by a reversible audit trail

In its own field, intelligence assessment exists because collecting and arranging facts is insufficient for decision support. The UK JIO describes analysis as fitting together pieces and assessment as adding the “so what?” even when pieces are missing ([JIO](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/joint-intelligence-organisation)). The corresponding publication convention is progressive disclosure:

1. State the customer question.
2. Put a small set of key judgments or an executive summary first.
3. Explain the evidence and reasoning that support each judgment.
4. Include contradictory information that materially affects it.
5. expose the assessment base: source coverage, quality, credibility, currency, deception risk, and important gaps.
6. Preserve a fully cited version and the methods used, so reviewers can descend from conclusion to evidence.

Those are not inferred customs; they are stated in the UK PHIA standards under Clear, Comprehensive, and Auditable ([PHIA](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/phia-common-analytical-standards/phia-common-analytical-standards)). ODNI’s source-citation specification gives the same traceability goal: source citations are meant to improve analytic integrity and connect collected information to judgments and conclusions ([ODNI Source Citations](https://www.odni.gov/index.php/who-we-are/organizations/ic-cio/ic-technical-specifications/source-citations)).

The key innovation for the target is **loss-bounded compression**. The short document is not a substitute for the evidence store. It is an index into it, with every important judgment carrying enough metadata to tell the reader when to drill down. A summary paragraph alone is lossy and hard to audit; an estimate is intentionally layered.

### 1.2 The Admiralty/NATO distinction: source reliability is not information credibility

In intelligence collection, a familiar grading mechanism uses two independent axes: reliability of the source (A–F) and credibility of the particular information (1–6). The UK Ministry of Defence’s Joint Doctrine Publication 2-00 states that the axes need not match: a usually reliable source may supply improbable information, and an unreliable source may supply information confirmed elsewhere. It also warns that “cannot be judged” is not synonymous with useless ([UK MOD JDP 2-00, table 3.1 and accompanying text](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/653a4b0780884d0013f71bb0/JDP_2_00_Ed_4_web.pdf)).

How the mechanism works in its own field:

- **Source reliability** is about the reporter or collection channel: access, competence, authenticity, history, bias, and possible deception.
- **Information credibility** is about the specific report: internal coherence, plausibility, directness, and independent corroboration.
- The analyst evaluates both rather than letting a trusted source automatically make every report true or letting a poor source automatically make every report false.

The useful transferable principle is the separation, not the literal A1–F6 labels. In an agent program, “source” has at least three layers that must not be collapsed: the upstream evidence (command output, file, URL, measurement); the producing unit or agent; and the synthesized claim. Ten agents repeating one audit claim are not ten independent confirmations if all ten cite the same document. Conversely, a historically noisy unit can still surface a claim confirmed by direct execution and an independent source.

I assess this separation as **almost certain (90-99%)** to expose false consensus in multi-agent synthesis, because provenance graphs can mechanically show common ancestry while vote/count summaries cannot. The evidence for the mechanism is the official two-axis doctrine above; the remaining uncertainty is how often the target corpus actually contains duplicated ancestry, which this ideation unit intentionally did not measure.

### 1.3 Sherman Kent’s estimative language: make likelihood words mean the same thing

Sherman Kent’s “Words of Estimative Probability” argued that a small, consistently understood vocabulary was necessary because readers otherwise attach different numerical meanings to terms such as “likely” or “almost certain” ([CIA, Kent](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/studies-in-intelligence/archives/vol-8-no-4/words-of-estimative-probability/)). Modern ICD 203 operationalizes this with ordered probability terms and requires consistency; it separately requires explanation of the analyst’s confidence in the basis for a judgment ([ODNI ICD 203](https://www.odni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203_TA_Analytic_Standards_21_Dec_2022.pdf)). The UK likewise distinguishes a Probability Yardstick from Analytical Confidence Ratings: likelihood concerns whether a statement/event is true or occurs; analytical confidence concerns the strengths and limitations of the judgment base and its susceptibility to change ([UK, Explaining Uncertainty](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/explaining-uncertainty-in-uk-intelligence-assessment/explaining-uncertainty-in-uk-intelligence-assessment)).

This solves two recurring synthesis failures in principle:

- vague modal language (“could,” “might,” “should”) that looks like a conclusion but expresses no comparable probability; and
- conflation of **likelihood** with **confidence**. A forecast can be “very likely” while based on a fragile evidence base; those answer different questions.

For this program, the mandated seven bands are the vocabulary. The implementation should retain the mandated exact text and numeric interval together, and require one-line evidence for the band. It should not invent pseudo-precise point probabilities.

### 1.4 ACH: test alternatives by diagnostic evidence and inconsistency

Richards Heuer’s ACH asks analysts to enumerate reasonable hypotheses, list significant evidence and arguments, assess each evidence item against every hypothesis, identify which evidence is most diagnostic, refine the matrix, compare hypotheses by inconsistency rather than simply counting confirmation, test sensitivity to pivotal evidence, report conclusions, and identify future indicators ([CIA, *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*, chapter 8](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf)). Its central insight is that evidence consistent with several hypotheses has little power to discriminate among them. Heuer illustrates this with a fever: it is evidence of illness but weak evidence for which illness.

That maps to synthesis when multiple explanations or architectures fit the same observation. For example, “tool errors are frequent” may be consistent with a bad tool, bad prompting, a platform constraint, or corrupted environment state. Repeating that observation does not choose among fixes. Evidence such as an error fingerprint tied to a platform boundary may be highly diagnostic.

But ACH’s own-field mechanism contains subjective judgments about evidence consistency, credibility, and weight. It is not Bayesian inference, it can mishandle priors and dependent evidence, and analysts do not reliably execute all its steps. The 2019 randomized study explicitly found that ACH-trained analysts did not follow all steps and that results could be worse; the 2024 study found matrix orientation itself altered performance ([2019 study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/acp.3550), [2024 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11169332/)). Therefore, the defensible borrow is a narrow **alternatives-and-disconfirmers worksheet** for disputed, high-impact judgments—not universal ACH scoring.

### 1.5 Explicit assumptions, dissent, and indicators

ICD 203 requires analytic products to distinguish intelligence information from assumptions and judgments, state linchpin assumptions, explain implications if they fail, incorporate alternatives, explain uncertainty, and identify indicators that would alter uncertainty where appropriate ([ODNI ICD 203](https://www.odni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203_TA_Analytic_Standards_21_Dec_2022.pdf)). PHIA likewise requires changes from earlier assessments and unresolved inter-organization differences to be communicated, while judgments should be falsifiable and reconsidered in light of new information ([PHIA](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/phia-common-analytical-standards/phia-common-analytical-standards)).

The important synthesis move is to treat disagreement and change conditions as first-class outputs. Consensus text is not a faithful collapse when the decision hinges on a contested assumption. A minority view should be short, but it should survive compression with its strongest evidence and the observation that would resolve it.

## 2. Adversarial analogy test

### 2.1 Structural matches

The analogy transfers on five structural properties:

| Intelligence assessment problem | Agent-program synthesis problem | Transferable mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Many reports exceed a decision-maker’s attention | Thousands of findings exceed a model’s context/attention | Key judgments plus linked annex |
| Reports vary in source quality and corroboration | Agent claims vary in evidence quality and often share ancestry | Separate producer reliability, claim credibility, and independence |
| Evidence is incomplete or contradictory | Audits conflict, use different scopes, or omit decisive probes | Contradictions, gaps, and assumptions stay visible |
| Decision requires judgment, not a document count | Architecture choice cannot be selected by majority wording | Decision relevance, diagnosticity, alternatives, implications |
| Future state may change | Harness/platform/model behavior changes | Indicators, expiry/review dates, delta from prior judgment |

These matches are about information topology and decision constraints, not about spies or secrecy. That is why the borrow is substantive.

### 2.2 Structural mismatches and failure modes

The analogy fails if copied literally:

1. **Agent “sources” are not human sources.** Agent identity is a weak reliability unit because agents may share the same model, prompt, corpus, and mistake. Reliability must be attached to evidence-generation methods and upstream provenance, not merely to an agent name.
2. **Corroboration can be circular at machine speed.** A hundred generated findings may paraphrase one audit file. Intelligence tradecraft recognizes liaison/circular reporting problems, but a harness can detect shared citation ancestry mechanically and should do so before counting support.
3. **The hypotheses are often not mutually exclusive.** Several harness changes can be simultaneously useful, and several root causes can jointly produce an error. A forced winner-take-all ACH matrix would create false exclusivity. The worksheet must allow `compatible`, `joint cause`, and `decision alternative` relations.
4. **The target often has testable artifacts.** Unlike many geopolitical questions, harness claims can often be settled by running a command, replaying a trace, or conducting an A/B task. The synthesis must privilege decisive experiments over continued estimative debate.
5. **Volume is more regular but extraction is fallible.** Findings can be machine-parsed, yet automated claim normalization can merge distinct scopes or split duplicates. Human-readable citations and reconciliation queues remain necessary.
6. **Policy/analysis separation only partly transfers.** Intelligence analysis traditionally informs rather than chooses policy. A harness design unit is explicitly expected to recommend. The useful constraint is to distinguish evidence/judgment from the owner’s value trade-offs, not to prohibit recommendations.
7. **Security classification and compartmentation do not map by default.** Importing handling markings or a dissemination bureaucracy would add friction without solving this task.

I assess the overall selective transfer as **very likely (80-90%)** to be structurally valid, supported by the five matches and bounded by the seven mismatches above. I assess a literal Admiralty-code-plus-ACH bureaucracy as **very unlikely (5-20%)** to be net beneficial, because it would turn compact epistemic distinctions into form filling, while empirical ACH results are mixed and agent provenance differs sharply from human-source reporting.

## 3. Concrete harness translation

### Proposal A — UNVERIFIED: Decision Estimate Compiler (experiment: retrospective blind reconstruction plus seeded-loss test)

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

Build a small compiler/skill that consumes newline-delimited **judgment cards** and emits two artifacts:

- `DECISION-ESTIMATE.md`: customer question, at most 5–9 key judgments, recommendation implications, material dissent, and the highest-value next experiments;
- `EVIDENCE-ANNEX.md` (or JSONL): every input finding assigned to a judgment, marked duplicate, deferred, out of scope, or unresolved, with citations intact.

Do not introduce a daemon, vector database, knowledge graph, durable workflow engine, critic model, or observability service. The compiler should be a deterministic transformation invoked by an existing skill or completion hook. Storage is ordinary JSONL/Markdown in the run artifact directory. This passes the anti-overfit gate: it rides the existing bus and introduces none of the rejected components.

Minimal input schema (required fields deliberately few):

```json
{
  "id": "X123-K4",
  "claim": "One atomic declarative judgment",
  "claim_type": "observed|inferred|forecast|proposal",
  "decision_question": "Which decision this informs",
  "evidence": [{"ref": "URL or path:line/command output", "method": "how obtained"}],
  "status": "supports|contradicts|qualifies|gap"
}
```

Optional enrichment belongs in a nested object rather than making ingestion fail: probability band and evidence sentence; confidence rationale; linchpin assumptions; source-method history; upstream ancestry IDs; independence group; falsifier/indicator; expiry; and proposal build tag. This “small required core, optional epistemic envelope” matters because a universal strict schema can reject useful partial findings. Invalid cards go to a visible reconciliation section rather than disappearing.

The estimate compiler performs deterministic checks:

1. Every input ID has exactly one disposition: attached, duplicate-of, deferred, out-of-scope, or unresolved.
2. Every key judgment links at least one evidence reference.
3. Contradictions and qualifiers cannot be omitted from the judgment’s drill-down.
4. Repeated evidence ancestry counts once for independence, even when cited by many cards.
5. Forecasts and uncertain claims use exactly one approved probability band with an evidence sentence.
6. Proposals carry exactly one NATIVE-vs-BUILD value.
7. An absence/broken/done claim cannot be promoted unless it names two mechanically independent signals and a known-present detector control.
8. The compiler reports counts before and after collapse: total findings, unique atomic claims, evidence families, contradictions, unresolved cards, and disposition totals. The totals must balance.

An LLM synthesis pass then writes prose only from the grouped cards. Deterministic validation runs after prose generation and blocks “complete” if a cited key judgment has no backing card, if material contradiction cards are absent, or if mass balance fails. It should warn—not hard-fail—on optional metadata omissions.

I assess it as **likely (55-80%)** that this will reduce silent evidence loss and false consensus, because the mass-balance and ancestry checks are mechanical consequences of the design, while clarity gains depend on claim extraction quality. **UNVERIFIED:** whether it improves real owner decisions remains unsettled; the named experiment is (a) take completed historical decisions with known source sets, (b) have one blinded evaluator use the compiler and another use the current synthesis process under equal time/context, (c) seed rare contradictions, duplicated ancestry, a high-impact minority claim, and malformed partial findings, and (d) score recovery, citation correctness, decision agreement, time, and false promotion. Promotion requires non-inferior decision accuracy and materially higher seeded-item recovery.

### Proposal B — UNVERIFIED: two-axis evidence grading plus independence families (experiment: provenance-injection calibration)

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only).**

Adopt the Admiralty distinction without copying its opaque letters. Each evidence item may carry:

- `method_reliability`: history and capability of the collection method (direct execution, static read, secondary claim, model inference, etc.);
- `item_credibility`: directness, internal coherence, currency, and confirmation for this item;
- `independence_family`: the earliest common upstream evidence or derivation family;
- `grade_basis`: one sentence, never a naked score;
- `unknown`: explicit when history or credibility cannot be judged.

The synthesis must not numerically multiply or average these fields. They are review cues. Directly reproduced command output can be credible even when its producing unit has a poor history; a normally strong unit’s uncited assertion can remain weak. Support is counted by independent evidence families, not agents or documents.

I assess as **very likely (80-90%)** that provenance-family grouping will catch circular corroboration when upstream references are present, because equality/ancestry is mechanically inspectable. **UNVERIFIED:** extraction of ancestry from free-form citations may be unreliable; settle it with a provenance-injection calibration set containing exact duplicates, paraphrases with one upstream URL, truly independent replications, and two sources that merely happen to agree. Measure family precision/recall, and require manual review rather than auto-merge below the validated threshold.

### Proposal C — UNVERIFIED: selective alternatives/disconfirmers worksheet (experiment: randomized contested-decision trial)

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: NATIVE (Claude Code already does this; we just enable it).**

Use a prompt/skill template only when a judgment is high-impact and one of these triggers is present: material contradiction; low confidence; a linchpin assumption; multiple plausible causes; or a low-probability/high-impact alternative. The worksheet is ACH-inspired but intentionally not full ACH:

| Alternative | Evidence inconsistent with it | Evidence uniquely diagnostic for/against it | Shared/non-independent evidence | Decisive experiment | Indicator that changes judgment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Place alternatives in rows and evidence in columns when presenting a matrix to a human/model reviewer. This orientation choice is informed by the 2024 experiment in which that layout, unlike conventional ACH orientation, reduced confirmation bias in the tested task ([Dhami et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11169332/)); transfer to agent reasoning is **UNVERIFIED**, and the randomized trial below settles it. Do not calculate a winner by counting cells. Do not assume alternatives are exclusive. Mark joint causes and compatible proposals explicitly. Prefer a runnable decisive experiment over further argument.

I assess the worksheet as **roughly even (45-55%)** to improve final decision accuracy, because explicit alternatives and disconfirmers have strong face validity and are required by analytic standards, but ACH-like procedures have mixed empirical results. Settle it with a randomized trial across held-out contested harness decisions: concise free-form synthesis versus the worksheet, equal model/token budget, outcome scored against executable ground truth where available. Track accuracy, overlooked alternatives, invented alternatives, time/tokens, and calibration. Retain only fields that show benefit.

### Proposal D — UNVERIFIED: judgment cards with separate likelihood, confidence basis, assumptions, and change indicators (experiment: reader-interpretation and update test)

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: CONFIG (settings/JSON only).**

Each published key judgment should render as:

```text
KJ-3 — [one decision-relevant declarative sentence]
Likelihood: likely (55-80%).
Evidence for band: [specific cited evidence].
Confidence basis: [coverage/quality/independence/currency and important weakness].
Why it matters: [decision implication].
Linchpin assumptions: [explicit, or “none identified”].
Contrary/minority case: [strongest one, not a straw man].
Would change this judgment: [observable indicator or decisive experiment].
Evidence: [card IDs/citations].
```

Likelihood is about the proposition. “Confidence basis” is prose about evidential strength, not a second probability and not a substitute for the required band. The judgment must state whether it changed from the preceding estimate and why. Forecast cards require an expiry or review trigger so that a once-valid summary does not silently become current truth.

I assess it as **likely (55-80%)** to reduce reader disagreement about what the synthesizer meant, supported by Kent’s diagnosis of inconsistent probability language and modern US/UK standards separating likelihood from confidence. **UNVERIFIED:** model and owner interpretation could still diverge; settle it by giving blinded readers paired cards with and without the structure, asking them to recover proposition likelihood, evidence weakness, assumptions, and update triggers. Then inject new evidence and measure whether the structured condition updates the correct judgments without rewriting unrelated ones.

### Proposal E — UNVERIFIED: dissent and tail-risk preservation rule (experiment: rare-critical-finding seed test)

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

Add a synthesis lint rule: frequency never by itself determines inclusion. A claim is promoted when it is decision-relevant and any of the following holds: strong independent evidence; it contradicts a key judgment; it invalidates a linchpin assumption; it is a plausible low-probability/high-impact outcome; or it identifies a decisive cheap experiment. The brief must include the strongest material minority view and why it did not prevail. Duplicate paraphrases collapse; a unique disconfirming signal does not.

This catches “consensus by token mass,” where repeated mainstream claims crowd out one decisive counterexample. I assess this as **very likely (80-90%)** to improve recovery of seeded rare critical findings because the rule explicitly prevents frequency-only dropping. **UNVERIFIED:** it may also elevate distracting edge cases; settle it with a seeded corpus varying rarity, impact, and evidence strength, and score critical-find recall together with false escalation and reader time.

## 4. What the design catches that ordinary summarization misses

| Missed failure | Mechanism that catches it | Concrete visible output |
|---|---|---|
| Twenty units repeat one upstream claim and appear to corroborate it | Independence-family grouping | “20 reports, 1 evidence family” |
| A trusted unit makes an uncited inference | Separate method reliability from item credibility | Strong producer history, weak item basis |
| A low-frequency contradiction is compressed away | Dissent/tail-risk preservation | Contrary case attached to key judgment |
| Observation becomes recommendation without a reasoning bridge | Claim-type separation and linked cards | Observation → inference → implication chain |
| “Likely” is used as confidence or rhetorical emphasis | Kent bands plus separate confidence basis | Probability range and evidence weakness both visible |
| A hidden assumption holds the architecture together | Linchpin assumption field | Failure implication and resolving experiment |
| A judgment stays in the summary after its basis expires | Indicator/expiry and delta field | Review queue and change log |
| Summary silently drops malformed or inconvenient findings | Mass-balance dispositions | Unresolved/reconciliation count prevents “done” |
| An absence claim results from a broken detector | Two-signal plus known-present-control promotion rule | Claim remains unresolved, with missing control named |
| Evidence supports every hypothesis and therefore distinguishes none | Diagnosticity worksheet | Evidence labeled non-diagnostic/shared |
| Two root causes are forced into an artificial contest | Joint-cause/compatibility relation | Both survive; experiment isolates contribution |
| A syntactically polished key judgment has no underlying support | Post-generation citation/card validator | Validation failure linked to the orphan judgment |

I assess the first, seventh, eighth, and twelfth catches as **almost certain (90-99%)** *when their required metadata is present*, because they are deterministic validation properties. I assess the remaining catches as **likely (55-80%)**, because they require correct semantic classification by an agent or reviewer. Evidence is the specified invariant for the former and the cited tradecraft plus explicit human/model judgment dependency for the latter.

## 5. End-to-end operating procedure

1. **Tasking:** Put the decision question and customer constraints at the top. A synthesis without a decision question becomes a topic summary.
2. **Atomization:** Each research unit emits atomic claim cards. Preserve original citations and wording alongside normalized claims.
3. **Mechanical normalization:** Canonicalize exact references, calculate content hashes, and propose—not silently apply—semantic duplicate clusters.
4. **Evidence evaluation:** Record method reliability, item credibility, and independence family with a reason. Unknown is valid and must not be converted to low quality.
5. **Disposition:** Attach every card to a candidate judgment or explicitly mark duplicate, deferred, out of scope, or unresolved. Check totals.
6. **Challenge only where warranted:** Run the selective alternatives/disconfirmers worksheet on disputed/high-impact candidate judgments. Seek decisive executable experiments.
7. **Estimate drafting:** Produce 5–9 key judgments, each with probability band, evidence sentence, confidence basis, implication, assumptions, contrary case, change indicator, and links.
8. **Adversarial lint:** Verify source ancestry, contradiction retention, two independent signals for absence/broken/done, proposal build tag, exact uncertainty vocabulary, and evidence links.
9. **Progressive disclosure:** Deliver the short decision estimate first, then judgment drill-downs, then full annex/reconciliation queue. The decision-maker need not read all material, but can audit any consequential statement.
10. **Update rather than regenerate:** New evidence changes affected cards and emits a judgment delta. Unchanged judgments remain stable; changed likelihood, confidence basis, or assumptions are stated.

The design intentionally avoids an extra orchestration runtime. The existing agent/skill/hook bus performs collection and invokes a small compiler/linter at synthesis boundaries. The format is inspectable text, not durable hidden state. This preserves the anti-overfit principle: the model does judgment; code enforces bookkeeping invariants that models routinely lose under compression.

## 6. Experiments and adoption gates

The recommendations are all **UNVERIFIED** for this harness until these experiments settle them.

### Experiment 1: retrospective decision reconstruction

Select at least 20 completed, heterogeneous harness decisions whose underlying findings and eventual outcomes are available. Randomize equal-context syntheses between current practice and the proposed estimate format. Blind evaluators to condition. Score:

- correctness of the chosen decision;
- citation precision and entailment;
- recovery of contradictions and minority views;
- calibration of mandated bands;
- time/tokens to a decision;
- evaluator ability to locate the evidence for a challenged judgment.

Adopt only if decision correctness is non-inferior and traceability/contradiction recovery materially improve without prohibitive time cost. I assess reaching that gate as **likely (55-80%)**, based on the structural match and deterministic audit checks, but the experiment is necessary because structured methods can impose cognitive and token costs.

### Experiment 2: seeded-loss and circularity challenge

Create corpora with known seeds: one rare decisive contradiction, ten paraphrases from one source, two genuinely independent confirmations, one stale source, one plausible unsupported claim from a historically good unit, one malformed card, and one absence claim produced by a deliberately broken detector. Required outcomes are exact:

- the contradiction appears in the decision layer;
- ten paraphrases are displayed as one evidence family;
- independent confirmations remain distinct;
- stale/unsupported/malformed items are caveated or reconciled rather than dropped;
- the broken-detector absence claim is not promoted.

This is the fastest falsification test for the compiler invariants.

### Experiment 3: ACH-lite randomized trial

Use held-out tasks with executable or subsequently observed ground truth. Compare concise free-form synthesis, conventional ACH, and the proposed row-alternative worksheet. Match model, context, and token budget. Measure accuracy, calibration, overlooked alternatives, dependence errors, and cost. Full ACH should not be adopted without a clear advantage; published evidence currently argues against assuming one.

### Experiment 4: longitudinal calibration

For falsifiable judgments, store band, date, outcome window, and eventual resolution. Score bands in aggregate with a proper scoring rule and inspect calibration by claim type. Do not “train a critic”; use simple tabulation and feed the observed calibration back into prompt examples. I assess this as **likely (55-80%)** to reveal systematic over/underconfidence if enough resolvable judgments accrue; the uncertainty is sample size and outcome observability.

### Experiment 5: compression frontier

Vary the key-judgment cap (3, 5, 7, 9, 12) and annex depth. Measure decision quality, critical-find recall, reader time, and drill-down frequency. The number 5–9 is a starting constraint, not doctrine. Select the smallest front layer that preserves decision quality on held-out tasks.

## 7. Rejected transfers and dead ends

### Rejected: literal A1–F6 everywhere

The two-axis distinction transfers; the literal code is opaque and invites false precision. Plain-language fields with a basis sentence are more auditable. Do not numerically rank agents into permanent trust castes.

### Rejected: giant universal ACH matrix

At thousands of findings by dozens of candidate proposals, the matrix becomes quadratic, forces subjective cell filling, encourages false exclusivity, and consumes the context it is meant to save. Empirical ACH evidence is mixed to negative for blanket use ([Dhami et al. 2019](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/acp.3550); [Dhami et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11169332/)). Use alternatives analysis only at high-impact contested nodes.

### Rejected: support-vote winner

Document or agent counts are not evidence counts. They ignore shared ancestry, source quality, diagnosticity, and decisive contradictions.

### Rejected: probability arithmetic over subjective grades

Multiplying a “source score” by an “information score” would imply calibrated quantities that the grades do not supply. Bands and confidence rationales communicate judgment; experiments supply numbers when available.

### Rejected: vector database or knowledge-graph memory

This would reintroduce an explicitly rejected component and a second retrieval substrate. The needed relation set is small enough for JSONL cards, explicit IDs, reference hashes, and a generated Markdown index. No BUILD-LARGE proposal is justified here.

### Rejected: trained critic or second orchestration runtime

The empirical concern is loss of bookkeeping and alternatives under compression, not proof that another model role can reliably judge the first. Deterministic checks plus selective challenge ride the existing bus and remain inspectable.

### Rejected: summaries without a reconciliation queue

A prose summary can look complete while silently excluding malformed, conflicting, or uncategorizable findings. Completion requires mass balance, not fluency.

## 8. Minimal acceptance specification

A synthesis is publishable only when:

- its decision question is explicit;
- it contains no more than the empirically selected key-judgment cap;
- every key judgment is atomic, decision-relevant, cited, and linked to cards;
- uncertain/forward-looking judgments use the exact approved band plus evidence;
- likelihood and confidence basis are separate;
- linchpin assumptions and material contrary evidence are visible;
- upstream evidence ancestry is represented or explicitly unknown;
- absence/broken/done claims show two mechanically independent signals and a validated known-present control;
- every proposal has exactly one required NATIVE-vs-BUILD value;
- every input card has exactly one disposition and totals balance;
- unresolved and invalid inputs remain visible;
- an annex permits a reviewer to reconstruct the chain from decision to original evidence;
- no second runtime, vector/KG memory, durable engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, or guardrail framework has been introduced.

## 9. Bottom line

Intelligence tradecraft’s strongest contribution is an **epistemic compression protocol**, not a special reasoning algorithm. It makes a short decision product safe by preserving the ability to descend into evidence, showing what is uncertain and why, keeping dissent and assumptions visible, and stating what would change the conclusion. That structure transfers well.

The harness change is correspondingly modest: atomic judgment cards, provenance/independence families, a deterministic mass-balance compiler and linter, an estimate template, and selective alternatives analysis. It is deliberately not a new platform.
