# X020 — Cross-domain borrow: intelligence tradecraft → failure recovery

**Unit:** X020  
**Lens:** intel-tradecraft (ACH, source reliability grading, Sherman-Kent bands, structured analytic techniques)  
**Target:** resuming useful work after a worker, session, or machine dies mid-task  
**Scope discipline:** external research and design only. Per the unit brief, I did not inspect or make claims from the local harness repository.

## Executive judgment

The analogy transfers, but only partially and at a precise boundary.

Intelligence tradecraft has already developed a disciplined way to hand uncertain, incomplete, potentially contradictory evidence from one analyst to another without collapsing observations, assumptions, and conclusions into one narrative. The most useful borrow is therefore **not** “make every agent run full Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH).” It is an **analytic recovery dossier**: a compact, machine-readable evidence ledger plus a recovery triage that (1) distinguishes observations from assumptions and judgments, (2) records provenance, freshness, source reliability, and claim credibility separately, (3) considers a small fixed set of rival crash states, (4) looks for disconfirming evidence and discriminating tests, and (5) states calibrated uncertainty and the next observation that would change the recovery decision.

**Transfer judgment:** it is **very likely (80-90%)** that this borrow will reduce unsafe replay, false declarations of completion, and needless restart relative to recovery from prose summaries or raw transcripts alone. Evidence: CIA tradecraft explicitly externalizes competing hypotheses, evidence diagnosticity, sensitivity to critical evidence, and indicators that would change a conclusion; ODNI requires separation of information, assumptions, and judgments and description of source quality and uncertainty. This is nevertheless a forward-looking harness claim, not a measured result. **UNVERIFIED — settle with the crash-injection A/B experiment specified in “Acceptance experiment.”**

The borrow does **not** solve persistence or transaction semantics. It is **almost certain (90-99%)** that an analytic dossier by itself cannot guarantee durable writes, atomic state transitions, idempotent tool calls, or exactly-once external effects, because those are properties of storage and protocols, whereas ACH and source grading are methods for evaluating evidence. Evidence for the disanalogy is in the mechanisms’ own definitions: CIA describes ACH as evaluation of alternative explanations and evidence; UK doctrine describes intelligence grading as judgments about source reliability and report credibility, not an execution protocol ([CIA Tradecraft Primer, pp. 14–15](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/955180a45afe3f5013772c313b16face/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf); [UK Ministry of Defence, JDP 2-00, §3.40 and Table 3.1](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/653a4b0780884d0013f71bb0/JDP_2_00_Ed_4_web.pdf)).

Accordingly, the recommendation is deliberately small and rides the existing hook/skill/tool bus. It is not a second orchestrator, durable-execution engine, vector database, knowledge graph, trained critic, observability service, or guardrail framework.

## 1. What intelligence tradecraft solved in its own field

### 1.1 The “analytic record,” not merely the final answer

Intelligence work often ends with a judgment under incomplete and ambiguous information. A successor must be able to see not only the answer, but the evidentiary basis and the assumptions connecting evidence to judgment. ICD 203 therefore requires analytic products to:

- describe the quality and credibility of underlying sources, data, and methodologies, including accuracy, completeness, age, access, validation, motivation, bias, and expertise;
- explain uncertainty, its causes, and dependence on assumptions;
- clearly distinguish underlying information from assumptions and judgments;
- state linchpin assumptions and explain how a judgment changes if they are wrong; and
- identify indicators that would alter judgments ([ODNI, ICD 203, pp. 3–4](https://www.odni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203_TA_Analytic_Standards_21_Dec_2022.pdf)).

The CIA’s “Quality of Information Check” makes this operational: store information by source type and date, annotate source strengths and weaknesses, systematically review accuracy, identify critical sources, check corroboration, and reconsider previously dismissed information when new facts arrive ([CIA Tradecraft Primer, pp. 10–11](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/955180a45afe3f5013772c313b16face/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf)).

What this solves in intelligence is **handoff without narrative laundering**. The next analyst can distinguish “a sensor reported X” from “we assume X is current” from “therefore we judge Y,” inspect which sources carry the conclusion, and revise the judgment when a source ages or an assumption fails. This is a direct reading of ICD 203’s required distinctions, not a claim that the process guarantees correct intelligence.

### 1.2 Source reliability and information credibility are separate axes

The NATO/Admiralty grading system records two judgments separately: reliability of the source (A–F) and credibility of the particular information (1–6). UK doctrine gives the deliberately counterintuitive examples B5 (a usually reliable source reports improbable information) and E1 (an unreliable source provides information confirmed by other sources). It also warns that “unknown” grades do not mean worthless; they mean evidence is insufficient to judge. Ratings are revisited as new intelligence arrives ([UK Ministry of Defence, JDP 2-00, §3.40 and Table 3.1](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/653a4b0780884d0013f71bb0/JDP_2_00_Ed_4_web.pdf)).

This two-axis separation prevents a common category error: believing a claim merely because its producer is usually reliable, or discarding independently corroborated evidence merely because its producer is not. The doctrine additionally warns about circular reporting: apparent corroboration is not independent when reports ultimately derive from the same original source ([UK Ministry of Defence, JDP 2-00, §3.39–3.40](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/653a4b0780884d0013f71bb0/JDP_2_00_Ed_4_web.pdf)).

### 1.3 ACH preserves rivals and searches for diagnostic evidence

Richards Heuer’s ACH starts by identifying reasonable alternative hypotheses, listing significant evidence for and against them, and placing evidence against every hypothesis in a matrix. The analyst asks which evidence is diagnostic—consistent with one hypothesis but not all—and emphasizes inconsistency and disconfirmation rather than accumulating generic support for a favorite explanation. The method then tests sensitivity to critical evidence, asks what expected evidence is missing, reports weaker hypotheses that still merit monitoring, and identifies indicators consistent or inconsistent with the alternatives ([Heuer, *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*, Chapter 8, pp. 95–97](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf); [CIA Tradecraft Primer, pp. 14–15](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/955180a45afe3f5013772c313b16face/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf)).

The specific mechanism is important. Ten facts that occur under every hypothesis add little. One durable receipt that can exist only after a remote commit can be decisive. ACH calls attention to that difference instead of treating evidence as a pile of confirmations.

### 1.4 Calibrated estimative language makes uncertainty portable

Sherman Kent argued for a small, consistent vocabulary of estimative probability because ordinary phrases conveyed radically different odds to different readers. His historical account reports that the phrase “serious possibility” was interpreted by colleagues across a range from roughly 20% to 80%, motivating defined probability bands ([Sherman Kent, “Words of Estimative Probability”](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/studies-in-intelligence/archives/vol-8-no-4/words-of-estimative-probability/)). ICD 203 now requires standardized likelihood terms and explicitly separates likelihood of an event from confidence in the evidentiary basis ([ODNI, ICD 203, p. 4](https://www.odni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203_TA_Analytic_Standards_21_Dec_2022.pdf)).

For recovery, the portability is the value: “probably committed” is not actionable unless the successor knows what probability language means, why the judgment was made, and what could overturn it.

## 2. Concrete translation: the Recovery Intelligence Packet (RIP)

### Proposal — Recovery Intelligence Packet and triage

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

Add one append-oriented, per-task recovery file and one resume skill. Hooks emit compact records at task start, before and after side-effecting operations, at material decision points, and at turn/session boundaries. The resume skill reads the latest valid records, makes fresh observations, evaluates a bounded hypothesis set, and selects the safest informative next action.

It is **likely (55-80%)** that a minimal implementation can remain below roughly 200 lines if it uses the harness’s existing hook and skill dispatch and stores JSON Lines rather than introducing a database. Evidence: the required behavior is a small record emitter, validator, and rule table; no scheduling, RPC, or new storage service is proposed. **UNVERIFIED — settle by implementing a spike with line count and integration tests.**

#### 2.1 Record schema

Each record contains only fields needed to resume or audit:

```json
{
  "task_id": "...",
  "seq": 42,
  "recorded_at": "RFC3339 timestamp",
  "kind": "observation | assumption | judgment | intent | operation",
  "claim": "remote issue creation committed",
  "source_ref": "tool-call-id / file path+hash / remote receipt / user turn",
  "source_family": "worker-log | filesystem | remote-system | user",
  "source_reliability": "known-good | mixed | unknown",
  "claim_credibility": "confirmed | probable | possible | doubtful | unknown",
  "freshness": "timestamp or age bound",
  "operation_id": "stable idempotency/correlation key if available",
  "phase": "intent-recorded | dispatched | acknowledged | postcondition-verified",
  "alternatives": ["committed", "not-committed", "outcome-unknown"],
  "confidence_band": "likely (55-80%)",
  "basis": ["receipt:...", "remote-read:..."],
  "discriminator": "read issue by operation_id",
  "next_safe_action": "verify remote state; do not replay yet",
  "supersedes_seq": null
}
```

Do not copy the Admiralty A–F/1–6 labels literally. Human-source “reliability” does not cleanly map to tools, and an unexplained B2 would make an operational record harder to use. Preserve the **independence of axes**, but use harness-native descriptors and concrete provenance. A remote system’s signed/durable receipt may be strong claim evidence even when the worker that paraphrased it has a poor history; conversely, a normally reliable worker can make an unsupported completion claim.

The record is append-oriented: later observations supersede earlier judgments rather than erasing them. A sequence number and per-record checksum detect truncation or a torn final entry; they do not purport to provide power-loss durability.

#### 2.2 Fixed recovery hypotheses

At resume, do not brainstorm an open-ended scenario universe. Evaluate this bounded set, extending it only when evidence demands:

1. **Worker alive or result merely delayed.** A lease expired or session disconnected, but work may still be running.
2. **No side effect occurred.** Intent was recorded, but dispatch never happened or failed before acceptance.
3. **Side effect committed; acknowledgement was lost.** Replaying may duplicate or overwrite.
4. **Partial/local-only progress exists.** A file, patch, build artifact, or sub-result exists but is incomplete or unvalidated.
5. **Recorded completion is false or stale.** A success message exists, but the intended postcondition is absent or later state invalidated it.
6. **Original plan is no longer valid.** User intent, dependencies, branch state, credentials, or external reality changed while the worker was unavailable.
7. **Task was superseded or cancelled.** Resuming the old intent would itself be an error.

These are recovery hypotheses, not diagnoses inferred from one missing heartbeat. The successor gathers independent observations: process/lease state, durable filesystem state, remote-system receipts or reads, hashes, tests, version-control state, and newer user instructions. Two fields copied from the same worker log remain one source family, not corroboration.

#### 2.3 Evidence matrix reduced to a triage table

Full ACH is unnecessary for routine cases. A compact rule table captures the diagnostic core:

| Evidence state | Hypotheses weakened | Recovery action |
|---|---|---|
| Intent record exists; no dispatch record; target postcondition absent | committed | Execute with stable operation ID if the action is still authorized. |
| Dispatch exists; no acknowledgement; remote outcome unobserved | neither committed nor uncommitted | Do not blindly replay a non-idempotent action; query by operation ID or inspect the postcondition. |
| Durable receipt and independent remote read agree | not-committed | Mark committed, then continue from the verified postcondition. |
| “Success” appears only in worker prose; target postcondition absent | committed/completed | Treat success as an unsupported judgment; repair or re-execute only after classifying side-effect risk. |
| Local artifact exists and parses/tests against its stated checkpoint | restart-from-zero | Continue from verified artifact state. |
| Local artifact exists but checksum/parse fails | clean partial progress | Quarantine the tail/partial output and recover from the last valid boundary. |
| New user instruction conflicts with saved plan | original-plan-valid | Stop old execution and replan from the newer authority. |
| Lease expired but independent process or remote job remains live | worker-dead | Observe or reattach; do not create a competing worker yet. |

The triage must prefer **information-gaining, reversible probes** over effectful guesses. For an ambiguous remote write, “read by operation ID” is more diagnostic than “retry create.” For a local edit, parse/test/hash is more diagnostic than trusting the last prose summary.

#### 2.4 Confidence and action gates

Use the owner-mandated probability bands on uncertain recovery judgments, always with evidence. Do not turn the band into permission by itself. Action also depends on consequence and reversibility:

- A **very likely (80-90%)** “remote write committed” judgment may still be insufficient to replay if duplication is costly; evidence must include the exact receipt or postcondition, and the safe action is verification.
- A **likely (55-80%)** “local scratch computation did not finish” judgment may be sufficient to rerun when rerun is deterministic, cheap, and has no external effect; evidence might be a missing output plus an invalid partial checksum.
- An “unknown” source or claim is not false. It creates a collection requirement: the next discriminating probe.

This follows intelligence tradecraft’s separation of probability, evidentiary confidence, and source quality, but adapts it to operational loss functions.

## 3. What this catches that transcript/checkpoint recovery misses

The following are design targets, not verified defects in the owner’s current harness. **Current-harness presence is UNVERIFIED — settle by sampling 100 real interrupted-task handoffs and scoring whether each contains independent evidence for operation phase, provenance, assumptions, rival states, and a discriminating next probe.**

1. **Acknowledgement-loss duplicates.** A transcript ends after dispatch, so a successor equates “no success response” with “no effect” and repeats a non-idempotent operation. The packet preserves the ambiguous phase and demands a remote read or stable operation-ID lookup.

2. **Phantom completion.** A worker says “done,” but the file was not persisted, the test never ran, or the remote postcondition is absent. The packet labels worker prose as a judgment and keeps verification evidence separate.

3. **Needless restart.** Useful local output exists, but the successor sees only an incomplete narrative. Hash/parse/test evidence identifies a valid continuation boundary.

4. **False death and split-brain work.** A session disappears while a remote job or old worker remains active. Independent liveness signals preserve “delayed/alive” as a rival to “dead.”

5. **Stale-plan continuation.** A technically valid checkpoint predates a user correction or external-state change. Freshness and supersession records force the old assumption to be rechecked.

6. **Assumption laundering.** “The migration has not run” begins as an inference and reappears in the handoff as fact. Typed records preserve the statement as an assumption until an independent observation promotes or supersedes it.

7. **Circular corroboration.** Tool output, worker summary, and checkpoint all repeat the same event and appear to be three confirmations. `source_family` exposes their shared origin.

8. **Torn recovery state.** The final record is partially written. Sequence/checksum validation identifies the last parseable boundary instead of treating a malformed tail as the entire task state.

It is **very likely (80-90%)** that items 1, 2, 5, and 6 are detectable when the proposed records and independent probes exist, because each failure produces a contradiction between a recorded judgment and a separately observable phase, postcondition, freshness marker, or provenance chain. **UNVERIFIED — the crash-injection experiment below must demonstrate detection rates.** It is only **likely (55-80%)** that false death is consistently detectable, because some workers and remote systems expose no independent liveness/operation lookup. Evidence: the method requires an observation channel that may not exist. **UNVERIFIED — inventory tool APIs for job status and operation-ID lookup, then inject network/session loss while jobs continue.**

## 4. Adversarial analysis of the analogy

### 4.1 Where the structures genuinely match

Both domains involve a successor deciding under incomplete evidence, stale reports, multiple plausible explanations, and potentially costly premature closure. Both benefit from externalizing reasoning because the original analyst/worker may be unavailable. Both also have evidence whose producer quality and claim truth are not identical. These structural correspondences are supported by the intelligence mechanisms above and by the target’s definition (a worker/session/machine dies before it can provide a complete handoff).

The fit is strongest after an interruption has created **epistemic ambiguity**: “What state is the task actually in?” It is weaker during ordinary, fully observed execution.

### 4.2 Where the structures do not match

1. **Truth assessment is not state transition control.** ACH ranks explanations; it does not make writes atomic. A recovery packet can warn that a write may have committed, but only an idempotency key, transaction, conditional write, or target-side lookup can make replay safe.

2. **Software state often has a knowable ground truth.** Intelligence may never reveal an adversary’s intent. A file hash, transaction receipt, or remote object lookup can settle many recovery questions exactly. Running a probabilistic ritual when a deterministic read exists wastes time and can obscure the answer.

3. **Evidence is highly endogenous.** Intelligence source grading assumes analysts can reason about sources and corroboration. Agent logs, summaries, status fields, and checkpoints may all be generated from one event stream. Without provenance families, “multiple sources” is an illusion.

4. **The hypothesis set changes with tool semantics.** “Committed but acknowledgement lost” matters for a remote create; it may be irrelevant for a pure read. A universal matrix risks boilerplate and false precision.

5. **Recovery has asymmetric loss.** A 10% chance of duplicate payment is not equivalent to a 10% chance of repeating a local search. Intelligence likelihood bands alone do not encode impact, reversibility, or authorization.

6. **The original method’s efficacy is contested.** In a randomized study of 50 intelligence analysts, ACH-trained analysts did not follow all ACH steps; evidence of reduced confirmation bias was mixed, and ACH could increase judgment inconsistency and error ([Dhami, Belton & Mandel, 2019 abstract, ERIC/US Institute of Education Sciences](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1262011)). A later experimental paper likewise reports that structuring information in an ACH-style matrix did not reduce confirmation bias in its study ([PLOS ONE / PMC, “Effects of task structure and confirmation bias in alternative hypotheses evaluation”](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11169332/)). Therefore the design borrows traceability, disconfirmation, and diagnostic probes—not ACH’s tallying algorithm or a claim that a matrix improves judgment by itself.

7. **Tradecraft can become clerical drag.** Requiring a matrix and probability estimate after every harmless tool call would consume context and encourage formulaic entries. The packet belongs at side-effect boundaries, material decisions, and resumable checkpoints, with full rival-state triage only when outcome is ambiguous.

### 4.3 Bottom-line transfer decision

The analogy **does transfer** as a format and discipline for uncertain handoff. It **does not transfer** as the recovery engine itself.

It is **very likely (80-90%)** that a narrow, event-triggered dossier passes the anti-overfit gate better than a general recovery service, because it rides existing hooks and skills, stores plain records, and activates only at consequential boundaries. Evidence is architectural: the proposal contains no scheduler, queue, database, daemon, or alternate agent runtime. **UNVERIFIED — settle with the implementation spike plus overhead measurements.**

## 5. Acceptance experiment

### Recommendation — UNVERIFIED until crash-injection A/B passes

**NATIVE-vs-BUILD: BUILD-SMALL (<~200 lines, rides the existing hook/skill/MCP bus).**

Do not deploy from analogy alone. Compare baseline recovery (existing transcript/checkpoint) against the Recovery Intelligence Packet on a seeded suite. Kill the worker, session, or host at each phase:

1. before intent persistence;
2. after intent but before dispatch;
3. after dispatch but before target acceptance;
4. after target commit but before acknowledgement;
5. during local artifact write;
6. after worker completion claim but before postcondition verification;
7. after checkpoint while user intent or dependency state changes; and
8. during a long-running remote job while the initiating session disappears.

Exercise at least four effect classes: pure read, deterministic local write, idempotent remote write, and non-idempotent remote write. Include a target that supports operation-ID lookup and one that does not.

Score mechanically:

- duplicate external effects;
- omitted required effects;
- unsafe replay attempts blocked;
- false “done” declarations;
- valid partial work recovered;
- stale/superseded plans incorrectly continued;
- time and token cost to first useful action;
- dossier parse/torn-tail recovery rate;
- whether purported corroborating signals are truly independent; and
- false abstentions where recovery was safe but the agent refused.

Predeclare an acceptance threshold rather than choosing one after results. A reasonable initial gate is zero duplicate non-idempotent effects, zero continuation after explicit supersession, at least a 50% reduction in false completion versus baseline, at least 90% recovery of seeded valid local checkpoints, and median overhead below 2% of task tokens and wall time. These thresholds are design choices, not empirical facts.

It is **likely (55-80%)** that this experiment will show the largest gain specifically at “commit succeeded, acknowledgement lost,” because that case sharply rewards preserved operation phase and a diagnostic target read. Evidence: baseline absence of a response is compatible with both commit and non-commit, while a receipt/postcondition probe can discriminate them. **UNVERIFIED — the phase-4 fault injections settle it.** It is **roughly even (45-55%)** that the packet will improve ordinary local-write recovery enough to justify its overhead, because existing filesystem state and version control may already make those cases easy. Evidence: local state is directly observable, reducing the value of probabilistic tradecraft. **UNVERIFIED — compare valid-checkpoint recovery and overhead in phases 5–6.**

## 6. Rejected expansions

- **Full ACH on every resume — rejected.** The empirical evidence for ACH reducing bias is mixed, and software recovery usually offers deterministic probes. Preserve alternatives and diagnosticity; omit weighted inconsistency tallies unless a genuinely complex incident warrants them.
- **A second orchestration runtime — rejected by the anti-overfit gate.** The proposal needs event records and a resume procedure, not a scheduler or workflow engine.
- **Vector database or knowledge graph for recovery memory — rejected.** Recovery correctness depends on exact recent operations, provenance, and postconditions; semantic retrieval adds machinery without providing transaction truth.
- **Learned critic or confidence model — rejected.** Calibrated words without concrete evidence are decoration. The first version should use explicit evidence and deterministic action gates.
- **Literal Admiralty grades — rejected.** The two-axis principle transfers; opaque military codes and human-source assumptions do not.
- **Probability as authorization — rejected.** A likelihood judgment never broadens the user’s authorization and cannot make a destructive or external action safe.

## 7. Gaps and limits

1. **Current harness behavior is UNVERIFIED — experiment:** sample actual interrupted sessions and inspect their recovery artifacts using the rubric in §3. The unit explicitly forbade local grounding, so I make no claim that the current harness lacks or already has any proposed field.
2. **Hook timing and durability are UNVERIFIED — experiment:** kill the process and machine during record append, then measure the last valid record under each filesystem/platform. Checksums detect some torn records but do not prove flush-to-disk.
3. **Tool-side operation lookup/idempotency coverage is UNVERIFIED — experiment:** inventory every side-effecting tool’s support for stable operation IDs, conditional writes, receipts, and read-after-write verification.
4. **Source-family independence rules are UNVERIFIED — experiment:** trace generated summaries/status/checkpoints back to raw events and measure how often apparent corroboration shares one origin.
5. **Probability calibration by agents is UNVERIFIED — experiment:** score recovery forecasts against seeded ground truth using Brier score or calibration plots. The action gates must remain evidence- and consequence-based even if verbal calibration is poor.
6. **The proposed <2% overhead target is UNVERIFIED — experiment:** instrument tokens, bytes, and latency in the A/B suite. Reduce checkpoint frequency or fields if overhead exceeds the predeclared gate.

## 8. Final recommendation

Build the smallest version of the Recovery Intelligence Packet behind an experiment flag: typed append-oriented evidence records at consequential boundaries, explicit provenance families, separate source/claim grading, a fixed rival-state recovery checklist, and discriminating read-only probes before replay. Keep transaction safety in the tools and targets where it belongs. Do not build a new runtime, and do not ship until crash injection demonstrates fewer duplicate/omitted effects and false completions at acceptable overhead.

The net recommendation is **very likely (80-90%)** to be directionally correct: borrow intelligence tradecraft’s inspectable reasoning and disciplined uncertainty, but reject its heavier ritual and any implication that analysis substitutes for durable systems engineering. Evidence: the recommended subset is directly supported by ICD 203, the CIA Quality of Information Check, ACH diagnosticity, and Admiralty separation of source from claim; the rejection is supported by the domain disanalogies and experimental challenges to ACH cited above. **UNVERIFIED — only the specified fault-injection A/B can establish harness value.**
