# P1C05 — Normalized audit findings A17–A20

## Scope and method

This artifact normalizes all 32 findings in `A17.md`, `A18.md`, `A19.md`, and `A20.md`. Stable IDs preserve the source audit ID and finding number (`A17-F1`, etc.). “Claimed severity” is the value half of each source finding’s `value x effort` field; it is not an independently verified risk rating.

The support judgment is deliberately limited to internal coherence. It asks whether the cited evidence, taken at face value, supports the one-sentence normalized claim. It does **not** verify the cited files, commands, counts, runtime behavior, or source author’s search coverage against the real system. `SUPPORTED` means the cited paths/outputs directly cover the material parts of the claim; `PARTIAL` means they cover a narrower, conditional, or less categorical version; `UNSUPPORTED` would mean the evidence does not establish the claim. Confidence bands below describe confidence in this internal-coherence judgment, not confidence that the underlying system claim is true.

No proposals are introduced by this normalization, so NATIVE-vs-BUILD and anti-overfit proposal gates are not applicable. Source `tag` values such as `FIX`, `BUILD`, and `ALREADY-BUILT` are dispositions from the audited files, not proposals endorsed here.
