The coherence judgements are **very likely (80-90%)** to be reproducible by a fresh-context reviewer because they apply one explicit entailment rule to the evidence strings quoted below; the remaining uncertainty comes from compound findings that mix a descriptive diagnosis with a normative proposal. `Claimed severity` is normalized from each source finding's `value x effort` field (`high` or `med` value); it is not independently reassessed. `NATIVE-vs-BUILD` classifies the proposed disposition, where present, under the required vocabulary. Every proposed change rides an existing skill/hook/ledger/config bus; none introduces a previously rejected second runtime, vector/KG memory, durable-execution engine, trained critic, observability SaaS, or guardrail framework. Therefore all proposals pass the anti-overfit gate as written, though their efficacy remains UNVERIFIED pending the experiment named in their source finding.
