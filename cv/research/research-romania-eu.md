## 1. Photo on the CV

### Romania
- No Romanian law requires or forbids a photo on a CV. Nothing in the Labour Code (Legea 53/2003, republished; art. 5, 27, 29 read verbatim) or in HG 1336/2022 mentions photos. VERIFIED (absence in primary texts).
- Practice is genuinely split and moving away from photos in tech and multinational hiring:
  - eJobs career article (updated 14 Oct 2025) quotes Victoria Brulea (Adecco Romania): "Alege o poză recentă, cât mai profesionist realizată" (pro-photo) but also says the Europass address/nationality/sex fields "țin de discriminare". VERIFIED (page read).
  - Job'Or / verandamall.ro (2025 guide): "părerile sunt împărțite, deși mai mulți recrutori consideră că nu este nevoie. În final, decizia îți aparține." VERIFIED (snippet).
  - upgrade.education (2025 guide): "Fotografie opțională, dar profesională dacă e inclusă". VERIFIED (snippet).
  - LinkedIn, Florin Andrei Dumitru (25 Jun 2025): in Romania a photo is "o practică comună și adesea așteptată", but lists as disadvantages that ATS "pot avea dificultăți" and that it invites subjective judgement; recommends a LinkedIn link as the alternative. VERIFIED (post read via Exa).
  - LinkedIn, Andreea Nicolae (30 Oct 2025) and its comment thread: recruiters openly disagree; one commenter: "Nu e indicat sa ai poza in CV doar pentru ca majoritatea companiilor au ATS-uri care intampina probleme la parsing"; another: "Poza din CV contează dacă aplici ca model, actor, prezentator TV... dacă aplici pentru un post de HR, contabil, inginer, analist... ar trebui să conteze capul, nu poza din el." VERIFIED (post read).
  - relocate.me (tech job board, 7 Apr 2025): "The photo is not required for most of the tech roles... By cutting it from your resume you can save some space as well as avoid any subjective judgments." VERIFIED.
  - BestJobs blog (6 Dec 2019): "în multe țări europene, printre care și România, recrutorii spun că CV-urile cu fotografie transmit o impresie mai umană" (no data behind it). VERIFIED (snippet); the claim itself is unevidenced.
  - Outliers: CVMaker.ro (2026) claims "angajatorii din România nu sunt obișnuiți cu fotografii în CV" (contradicts almost everyone else); jobera.com (2023) and resumeflex (2025) claim a photo is "standard/expected". Both directions come from CV-tool marketing.
- Independent-source count for "optional, split, declining in tech": 9 (eJobs/Adecco, Job'Or, upgrade.education, two LinkedIn recruiter posts, relocate.me, Libertatea, BestJobs 2019, SNSPA career centre). Confidence: HIGH that it is optional and contested; LIKELY that omitting it costs nothing for developer roles at multinationals or remote employers.

### EU (for comparison)
- Country guides written in 2026 (lumicv 28 May 2026, resumematchlab 6 Jun 2026, nomadcareers, locitra 8 Aug 2026) converge: photo still common in Germany/Austria/Switzerland traditional sectors and declining in German tech; optional/declining in France; discouraged in the Netherlands, Nordics, UK, Ireland. LIKELY (4 convergent secondary sources, all CV-tool genre).
- The Michael Page / Page Personnel survey (2017; 4,245 candidates, 619 consultants) reported 100% of German consultants and 79% of Polish consultants expecting a photo. VERIFIED as a source, but nine years old; do not treat as current.

### US-headquartered employers
- EEOC "Prohibited Employment Policies/Practices" page, read verbatim: pre-employment inquiries touching race, sex, national origin, religion or age "may be used as evidence of an employer's intent to discriminate", and "employers should not ask for a photograph of an applicant. If needed for identification purposes, a photograph may be obtained after an offer of employment is made and accepted." VERIFIED (primary, US federal agency).
- Greenhouse "Anonymize resumes" (Pro tier): its ML model blurs identifying details, explicitly including "gender, photo, and related information". Workable "Anonymized resume screening" obscures name, "candidate photo", social profiles, address, email, phone in the Sourced/Applied stages. VERIFIED (vendor support docs). Note what this shows: photos are blurred, not rejected; and the vendors treat a photo as a bias vector, not a plus.
- Eight 2026 guides for international applicants to US roles (neatstack 22 Jul 2026, markdownresume 29 Mar 2026, dev.to/czechdevusa 26 Jun 2026, altercv 30 Apr 2026, Seabra/Medium 23 Jun 2026, lumicv, nomadcareers, locitra) all say: no photo, no DOB, no marital status, no nationality line unless it is a work-authorisation statement. LIKELY (convergent; genre is low quality but the EEOC page anchors it).

What it means for the owner: default to no photo. Put the photo on LinkedIn instead and link it. Add a plain headshot only when a Romanian job ad asks for one or the employer is a traditional local firm; never for US-HQ or remote-first employers.

## 2. Date of birth, age

- Romanian law: age ("vârstă") is a protected criterion in Labour Code art. 5(2) (verbatim: "Orice discriminare directă sau indirectă... bazată pe criteriul de rasă, cetățenie, etnie, culoare, limbă, religie, origine socială, trăsături genetice, sex, orientare sexuală, vârstă, handicap, boală cronică necontagioasă, infectare cu HIV, opțiune politică, situație sau responsabilitate familială, apartenență ori activitate sindicală, apartenență la o categorie defavorizată, este interzisă"). OG 137/2000 art. 7(2) makes it a contravention to condition a post "prin anunț sau concurs" on age, sex, etc.; the CNCD has sanctioned age-conditioned job ads (e.g., "vârsta cuprinsă între 30-35 de ani") and the Notaries' Union for a 35-year age cap (SIPHD training material citing CNCD case law; CNCD decision 429/2009 quoting art. 7). VERIFIED (Labour Code and OG 137/2000 art. 7 text read; CNCD examples from a secondary training document).
- Nothing requires a candidate to state DOB. Romanian HR professionals advise against it: Marga Radu (Microsoft, EMEA talent acquisition) in Libertatea: "Ce n-ar trebui să își treacă: data nașterii. Nu ar trebui să își treacă statutul - căsătorit sau nu, câți copii are"; Adecco's Victoria Brulea in the same piece. VERIFIED (snippet). eJobs, Romania's largest job board, hides age from employers by design: its 2023 tutorial says "Angajatorilor nu le arătăm vârsta ta atunci când aplici." VERIFIED (video transcript snippet).
- Legacy convention still exists: SNSPA's career centre page tells students the recruiter "va trebui să știe naționalitatea, adresa curentă, data nașterii" while marital status is "nu obligatorii"; headhunter George Butunoiu (mediastory.ro) argues that hiding age reads as evasive. VERIFIED (snippets). These are the minority view in the 2025-2026 material.
- Research nuance: Derous & Decoster (2017, Frontiers in Psychology, PMC5554369), experiment with 610 HR professionals: job-suitability ratings were lowest when the explicit DOB was omitted, and recruiters inferred age from names and hobbies anyway. VERIFIED (paper read via Exa highlights). This is one experiment (Belgian/Dutch sample), so treat "omitting DOB always helps" as UNCERTAIN; for a 2025 graduate the graduation year already reveals approximate age, so the question is moot.
- Source count: 8 for "omit DOB is lawful and increasingly standard"; 2 for "some Romanian recruiters still expect it"; 1 experiment complicating the picture.

What it means for the owner: omit DOB everywhere. Keep degree years (2022-2025) because they are the honest early-career signal recruiters look for, and they make DOB redundant.

## 3. Marital status, children, gender, religion, nationality, CNP, address

- Marital status / family responsibilities: "situație sau responsabilitate familială" is a protected criterion (Labour Code art. 5(2), verbatim above); Dentons' Romania guide lists "family status or responsibility" among protected characteristics. Every Romanian career source read (Libertatea, Job'Or, SNSPA, model-cv.ro, cvmaker.ro) says leave it out. VERIFIED (law) + 5 secondary. Confidence HIGH.
- Pregnancy: Labour Code art. 27(5) prohibits requesting pregnancy tests at hiring (paraphrased by DLA Piper's Romania guide and larinconsult's 2026 hiring guide; art. 27(1)-(2) read verbatim on codulmuncii.ro). LIKELY-to-VERIFIED.
- Gender: the Europass editor has an optional gender field; ResumeGenius and Adecco (via eJobs) both say the sex/gender field invites discrimination. Leave blank. LIKELY.
- Nationality/citizenship: "cetățenie" is a protected criterion in art. 5(2); the only legitimate use is as a work-authorisation signal. Decalex (Romanian privacy consultancy) notes an employer may check work/residence permits but that collecting parents' nationality or naturalisation details "is disproportionate to the purpose of recruitment". For EU and UK employers, a one-line "EU citizen, no sponsorship required" is recommended by lumicv, Seabra, markdownresume, nomadcareers. LIKELY (4+ sources).
- CNP (personal numeric code), ID copies, full home address: Law 190/2018 art. 4 allows processing of a national ID number on the basis of legitimate interest only with extra safeguards (data-minimisation measures, a DPO, defined storage periods, staff training) (PrivacyOne legal alert on the law; lege5 text of the law's structure). The ANSPDCP has sanctioned employers for mishandling employees' CNPs (revistalace.ro practice review). Career sources (model-cv.ro: "Nu include vârstă, stare civilă, CNP decât dacă e cerut explicit"; Job'Or: home address unnecessary) agree. VERIFIED (law structure) + 3 secondary. Confidence HIGH that a CNP never belongs on a CV.
- Sensitive data (health, religion, union membership, sexual orientation, political opinion): GDPR art. 9 special categories; Decalex: "It is prohibited to collect personal data from candidates on their parents, siblings, political opinions or health status." Putting it on a CV creates data the employer is not allowed to hold. VERIFIED (GDPR) + 2 secondary.

What it means for the owner: header = name, city ("Bucharest, Romania"), phone with +40, email, LinkedIn, GitHub. No CNP, no street address, no marital status, no gender, no religion. Nationality appears only as "EU citizen (Romanian), no visa sponsorship required" on international versions.

## 4. Europass: who requires it, who dislikes it, what the template actually contains

- Public sector in Romania REQUIRES it. HG 1336/2022 (Regulament-cadru for contractual staff paid from public funds) art. 35(1)(i): the competition file must contain "curriculum vitae, model comun european". HG 611/2008 (civil servants) has the same wording ("curriculum vitae, modelul comun european"). Verified verbatim on lege5.ro and in the Government decision PDF, and mirrored in dozens of institutional announcements (Ministry of Education 2022, DNSC January 2025, county councils, hospitals). VERIFIED. Confidence HIGH.
- Private sector and tech in Romania do not want it. eJobs (article updated 14 Oct 2025) quotes Marga Radu (Microsoft): "CV Europass este un format ușor învechit. Se caută altceva"; Victoria Brulea (Adecco): "CV Europass nu este necesar, nu este o regulă general valabilă"; Oana Bolocan Datki (CTeam): CV must be scannable "pe diagonală". Other Romanian sources: solutiiresurseumane.ro ("recruiterii, și chiar candidații, preferă un model de cv mai ușor de parcurs"), anuntul.ro ("de regulă, acestea preferă un CV personalizat"), cvapp.ro ("mulți angajatori strâmbă din nas când îl văd"), 8ore.ro (2026: use it when requested, keep a shorter version otherwise), LinkedIn Ioana M. (23 Apr 2026: "formatele standardizate, precum Europass... nu mai livrează rezultate", with a dissenting PHP developer in the comments who found a job with an eJobs-template CV), LinkedIn Miruna Nedelcu (Sep 2025: "Europass is dead", with a strong dissent in the comments: "Employers reject bad content, not a file format"), Lucian Vilcea (Dec 2025) pushing back on HR "rules". Europe-wide: relocate.me (2025), candycv (Jun 2026), a 2015 MSC HRBP post, a 2023 recruiter thread. VERIFIED (all read). Independent sources: 10+. Confidence HIGH that Europass is a net negative for private-sector tech applications in Romania and Western Europe, with the honest caveat that content matters more than template (several recruiters say so).
- What the official Europass site claims: "The best-known CV format in Europe... easy-to-use and familiar to employers and education institutions." It does not claim employers prefer it. VERIFIED (page read).
- Template fields: photo, date of birth, nationality and gender are OPTIONAL. Sources: Monster's Europass guide ("optional fields for your nationality, date of birth, gender, and photo"), the Europass CV instructions PDF hosted by europass.at ("a picture is not essential in a CV, unless requested by the employer"; nationality, DOB, gender each marked "(optional)"), and the official Europass FAQ ("You are required to fill only mandatory fields (name and surname)"). VERIFIED (3 sources, one official).
- Usage: Europass statistics page (official, 2026): 155 million visits to date, 10.4 million accounts, 8.8 million CV downloads "in 2026"; the "10 million registered users" milestone was announced on the Europass site. Cedefop counted 10 million CVs generated online by Oct 2010. VERIFIED (official pages). The frequently repeated "Adecco: 70% of candidates applying by email in Romania use Europass" appears only in the eJobs article, undated, as an "estimate". UNVERIFIED (single source, likely years old).
- The eJobs article also states Europass is "cerut în mod expres" only for jobs in German companies/universities. No other source supports that; German-market guides (resumematchlab, lumicv) describe a tabular Lebenslauf with photo/DOB, not Europass. UNCERTAIN, probably wrong.

What it means for the owner: keep one Europass export for Romanian public-sector or academic competitions (a DNSC or university posting will demand "model comun european"); everywhere else use a modern one-to-two page CV. If forced to use Europass, leave photo/DOB/gender/nationality blank; only name is mandatory.

## 5. GDPR and Romanian data-protection practice around CVs

- Legal basis: Romanian practitioners (Pachiu & Associates 2017; Grecu Partners 2018; WeAreHR May 2025; larinconsult 2026 guide; Decalex) converge that recruitment processing rests on GDPR art. 6(1)(b) (steps at the candidate's request before a contract) and/or legitimate interest, and that consent is a poor default because of the power imbalance; larinconsult (2026): "Consent from the data subject should not be mechanically included in the document package as a mandatory condition of hiring." LIKELY-to-HIGH (5 sources, consistent with WP29 Opinion 2/2017 which Grecu cites).
- Information duty: candidates must be told purpose, legal basis, retention, recipients and rights at the application stage (Decalex list; AmCham Romania Aug 2025 says the note should be "available from the application stage or published in the recruitment notice"). The ANSPDCP's 2019 annual report addressed candidates' rights explicitly (right to be informed from the start of the hiring process, to object to legitimate-interest processing, to erasure, to withdraw consent) per avocatnet (5 Oct 2020). VERIFIED (avocatnet summary of the regulator's report) + 2.
- Retention: no statutory number. Guidance ranges: delete when the process ends unless a documented legitimate interest exists (Grecu 2018); "6-12 months for future recruitment" (AmCham Romania, Aug 2025); up to the 3-year limitation period for discrimination claims as an outer bound (Pachiu 2017); "keeping it for 5 years is excessive" (Decalex). Talent-pool storage needs either documented legitimate interest or specific, revocable consent limited to similar roles (WeAreHR 2025; portalprotectiadatelor.ro 2022). LIKELY (5 sources agree on the shape, differ on the number).
- Enforcement examples: ANSPDCP fined an operator EUR 2,000 in 2024 after an employee emailed a candidate's data to the wrong person during recruitment (avocatnet, 22 Aug 2024); fines of EUR 2,000-5,000 for GPS/biometric employee monitoring and CNP mishandling (revistalace.ro; juridice.ro); NTT DATA Romania EUR 25,000 for security failures (ANSPDCP press release 25 Mar 2025); 448 fines from 2019 to May 2026 per apsap.ro. VERIFIED (regulator press releases and law-portal summaries). Recruitment-specific fines are rare and small.
- Automated decisions: GDPR art. 22 right not to be subject to solely automated decisions; Romanian commentary (WeAreHR 2025; AmCham 2025; Pachiu 2017) treats fully automated CV rejection without human intervention as not permitted and requires a DPIA before deploying AI screening. LIKELY (3 sources).
- Labour Code art. 29(3)-(4), verbatim from the Ministry of Labour's own May 2026 PDF of the republished code: "(3) Informaţiile cerute, sub orice formă, de către angajator persoanei care solicită angajarea cu ocazia verificării prealabile a aptitudinilor nu pot avea un alt scop decât acela de a aprecia capacitatea de a ocupa postul respectiv, precum şi aptitudinile profesionale. (4) Angajatorul poate cere informaţii în legătură cu persoana care solicită angajarea de la foştii săi angajatori, dar numai cu privire la activităţile îndeplinite şi la durata angajării şi numai cu încunoştinţarea prealabilă a celui în cauză." VERIFIED (3 primary copies: mmuncii.gov.ro PDF, lege5.ro, FAO's FAOLEX copy).

What it means for the owner: everything he writes becomes data the employer must justify holding, so minimal is also safer; he can ask what basis and retention apply, request erasure after a rejection, and object to being pooled; questions about family plans or health at interview are outside art. 29(3); reference checks require that he be told first, so "References available on request" is the correct approach rather than listing referees.

## 6. 2026-specific legal changes that touch applications

### EU AI Act
- Annex III point 4(a), verbatim: "AI systems intended to be used for the recruitment or selection of natural persons, in particular to place targeted job advertisements, to analyse and filter job applications, and to evaluate candidates" are high-risk. VERIFIED (artificialintelligenceact.eu annex text).
- Timeline change: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (the "Digital Omnibus on AI") was published in the Official Journal on 24 July 2026 and entered into force on 27 July 2026; it moved the Annex III high-risk obligations from 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027 (Annex I embedded systems to 2 August 2028). Article 50 transparency duties (disclose AI interaction, mark synthetic content) still applied from 2 August 2026; prohibited practices (including workplace emotion recognition) have applied since 2 February 2025; the AI-literacy duty (art. 4) applies since Feb 2025 in softened form. Sources: artificialintelligenceact.eu implementation timeline (updated 31 Aug 2026), scrut.io, Usercentrics, Cloud Security Alliance research note, Jones Walker (16 Jul 2026), Punter Southall Law, jobful.io (2027 deferral guide), Gavn Software, a Digital Omnibus explainer by a Spanish law firm. VERIFIED (8+ independent sources; the official tracker is one of them). Everything published before late July 2026 (jobful May 2026, pin.com, Celper, HeyMilo, Truffle, Octagon, AmCham Romania Aug 2025) states the now-superseded 2 August 2026 date.
- Extraterritorial reach: several sources (pin.com, Octagon, jobful) state that a US company using AI to screen EU-based candidates is a deployer in scope regardless of HQ. LIKELY (consistent with art. 2 of the Act; not read verbatim in this session).

### Pay Transparency Directive (EU) 2023/970
- Article 5, verbatim (EUR-Lex via Tavily extract): "1. Applicants for employment shall have the right to receive, from the prospective employer, information about: (a) the initial pay or its range, based on objective, gender-neutral criteria... Such information shall be provided in a manner such as to ensure an informed and transparent negotiation on pay, such as in a published job vacancy notice, prior to the job interview or otherwise. 2. An employer shall not ask applicants about their pay history during their current or previous employment relationships." Recital 33 adds that vacancy notices and job titles should be gender neutral. VERIFIED (primary).
- Transposition deadline 7 June 2026 (stated by every source: juridice.ro, Deloitte Romania, Forvis Mazars, partnerd.ro, salariile.ro, selectsoft, teamed.global, DCBusiness). Romania missed it. Status on 23 Sep 2026: Ministry of Labour draft published 30 March 2026; bill L445/2026 tabled in the Senate 17 June 2026 under urgency procedure; Legislative Council negative opinion 29 June; Senate committees gave favourable opinions on 2 and 15 September 2026; on 21 September 2026 the Senate plenary extended the examination term from 45 to 60 days, so the tacit-adoption date moved to 17 October 2026, after which the Chamber of Deputies decides; nothing is in force yet. Sources: Senate file page (senat.ro L445/2026), DCBusiness (Sep 2026), teamed.global tracker (checked weekly), gps-law.ro, partnerd.ro (status as of 8 Aug 2026), salariile.ro (checked 5 Aug 2026). VERIFIED (official file exists; procedural details from 3 convergent trackers). The draft: pay or range in the ad or in writing before the interview (partnerd claims "direct în anunț"; DCBusiness and teamed say ad OR written notice before interview, which matches the directive's wording); salary-history questions banned; sanctions 3-5 (repeat: 5-10) gross minimum wages (partnerd). LIKELY.

### Romanian employment/tax context relevant to how foreign employers hire
- The IT income-tax exemption ended for income from January 2025 (OUG 156/2024). VERIFIED (PwC Romania and Crowe tax alerts; multiple calculators). 
- From 1 January 2026 the micro-enterprise regime has a single 1% rate with the threshold lowered to EUR 100,000; e-Factura is mandatory for all B2B invoicing; VAT registration threshold 395,000 lei; single-client PFAs risk reclassification as employment. LIKELY (3 Romanian accounting sources: contapp.ro, catramai.ro, infiintaresrl-nou.ro; not checked against the Fiscal Code text).
- REGES-Online replaced REVISAL from 1 January 2026; non-registration fine RON 40,000 (Oyster, Aug 2026; larinconsult 2026). LIKELY.

What it means for the owner: as of today a Romanian employer may still legally ask his salary history and omit pay from ads; once L445/2026 passes (plausibly late 2026 or 2027) both become unlawful. Never write current or expected salary on the CV. Expect AI screening to be legal and common through 2027; write for parsers. He has no AI-Act-specific candidate rights before December 2027, only GDPR art. 22.

## 7. Romanian tech employers versus US-headquartered remote employers hiring into Romania

### Romanian tech employers (local companies, Romanian offices of multinationals)
- Language: English is the safe default for IT. r/programare thread "CV-urile din IT" (2023): a recruiter-side poster: "Am primit zeci de CV-uri... Deja nici nu mai contează formatul. Fă-l în engleza."; r/programare "CV în română sau engleză": "Depinde cum e scris anunțul... deși cred că cu cel în engleză n-ai cum să dai greș."; LinkedIn thread by recruiter Camelia Macsim (103 comments): an Endava engineer sends English CVs "pentru toate joburile din domeniul IT... indiferent de anunț", an executive-search founder recommends keeping both versions, and a recruiter complains that "sunt foarte puține anunțuri de job în limba română" even for Romanian roles; cvexpert.ro (2026): send the CV in the language of the ad; lexis.ro (Jul 2025): multinationals, tech start-ups and IT prefer English CVs; Hipo/Brainspotting "Romania IT Talent Map": ~90% of IT specialists are good English speakers. LIKELY (7 sources; no hard statistic on the share of IT ads in English).
- Format: 1-2 pages; Adecco says never more than 2; a Bucharest IT recruiter (Iulia Bădăuță, Oct 2024) argues against forcing seniors onto one page; CVMaker's IT guide says one page for graduates. Recruiters at multinationals run ATS: Brainspotting's tool stack lists Greenhouse, SmartRecruiters and Workday; taurusid claims "all multinationals" use ATS (unsourced). LIKELY.
- Local conventions tolerate photo/DOB/nationality; public sector demands Europass and a full "dosar de concurs" (ID copy, diplomas, medical certificate, criminal record) per HG 1336/2022 art. 35. VERIFIED for public sector.
- Anti-discrimination: job ads may not condition on age/sex/etc. (OG 137/2000 art. 7(2)); Pebl's 2026 guide tells foreign employers "make sure your job ad is clear, specific, and discrimination-free. The law requires it." VERIFIED (law) + 1.

### US-headquartered employers hiring into Romania
- Legal structures: (1) B2B contractor (Romanian PFA or SRL invoicing in USD/EUR, W-8BEN for the US client), (2) Employer of Record (Deel, Remote, Oyster, Rippling, Remofirst; Oyster says US, UK and Spain are its top sources of Romania hires and warns about REGES-Online deadlines and the lapsed IT exemption), (3) own Romanian entity. Sources: ceehire (May 2026), roamjobs (Apr 2026, Poland but same pattern), learnpython.academy (names Romania's PFA), Deel/Remote/Oyster/Rippling/Remofirst country pages, PILnet/Eversheds compass (foreign entities rarely employ directly), DLA Piper guide. LIKELY (8 sources). CONTESTED: Dentons' Romania guide states EOR/PEO "are not legally recognised in Romania and it is not lawful or possible to use them" (only licensed temporary work agencies), while Oyster, Remote, Deel and Pebl assert EOR is "fully legal". Not resolved in this session; the practical observation is that EOR hiring into Romania happens at scale.
- Document expectations: US resume, one page under ~10 years' experience, no photo/DOB/marital status/nationality, US spelling, single column, quantified bullets, standard headings; a work-authorisation/engagement line near the top. The dev.to "czechdevusa" pieces (Jun 2026) describe a ten-year backend engineer who stopped being ghosted within two weeks after adding: "Engagement: Independent contractor (B2B). I invoice US companies directly through my own entity... I provide a W-8BEN. No visa sponsorship or work authorization required." Time zone and overlap window stated explicitly (markdownresume, roamjobs, ceehire, duskolicanin). LIKELY (10+ convergent sources; anecdotal but consistent).
- Compliance signals that hurt: mentioning EU VAT handling, sole-proprietorship paperwork or local payroll structures inside the experience section reads as "extra work for legal and finance" (dev.to, Jun 2026). Put the engagement line in the header instead. UNCERTAIN (single practitioner source, plausible).

## 8. How Romanian and CEE candidates handle the format conflict (practitioner consensus)

- Keep one master document and render two or three targeted versions: a US-style one-page resume, a European two-page CV, and a Europass export only when an institution demands it (nomadcareers; candycv; 8ore.ro; resumematchlab; altercv; locitra). Choose by the employer's HQ country and the recruiter's location; when the signals conflict, send the two-page tailored CV because it "works in more places than a 1-page US resume" (nomadcareers). LIKELY (6+ sources, all CV-tool genre but consistent with the recruiter quotes above).
- Concrete deltas between the versions (convergent across lumicv, altercv, resumematchlab, Seabra, markdownresume, cvmaker.ro EN guide): photo (EU optional / US never), DOB and nationality (EU optional / US never), languages with CEFR levels (EU expected / US "fluent/professional"), references (EU sometimes listed / US never), length (EU 2 / US 1), personal profile paragraph (EU short profile / US punchy summary), spelling and date formats.
- Romanian-specific: Romanian degree and university presented in English with the international-equivalent title; remove "Curriculum Vitae" as a title; for RO-language ads keep a Romanian version (cvexpert.ro 2026; Camelia Macsim thread).

## 9. Length, file format and reviewer attention: what the only sizeable 2025 dataset says

- Jobseeker survey (2025; 1,013 HR professionals in 59 countries; run by a CV-builder company, so treat as industry data, not academic): 51% prefer two pages, 31.1% one page, 82.1% say one to two pages is ideal; 76.8% want PDF, 12% Word; 49.9% accept images "if relevant", 46.7% prefer text-only; 76.3% use an ATS at least occasionally; 81.6% have seen AI-written cover letters and 54% are less willing to proceed with unedited AI applications; the typical first-pass review is about 30 seconds. VERIFIED (methodology and figures read on the Romanian-language report page).

## 10. Contested or unevidenced claims found in this space

1. "ATS rejects/cannot parse CVs with photos." Unsupported. Parser vendors (Textkernel, Affinda, RChilli) extract text from PDF/DOCX and OCR images; nothing in their documentation says an embedded photo causes rejection. Greenhouse and Workable blur photos rather than reject them. An Enhancv study of 25 US recruiters (10+ ATS platforms): 92% said their ATS does not auto-reject for formatting or missing keywords; the real filters are knockout questions (84%). A May 2026 parser test (jobloo) found the genuine breakage is multi-column layouts and text inside graphics, not photos. The bias argument against photos stands; the "the robot rejects it" argument does not.
2. "Recruiters spend 6-7 seconds per CV." Repeated by eJobs/Forbes (5-7 s), Nedelcu (6 s), relocate.me (7-10 s), Simplify (6-7 s); the origin is a single vendor eye-tracking study (Ladders). The 2025 Jobseeker survey reports about 30 seconds; an MSC HR business partner says 25 seconds to a minute. Order of magnitude, not a rule.
3. "72% of BestJobs recruiters consider a photo important" and "over 60% of eJobs private-sector recruiters prefer a concise CV over Europass" (taurusid.com, 14 Sep 2026, a resume-tool site): no link, date, sample or methodology; no such figures appear on BestJobs' or eJobs' own pages found in this session. Treat as fabricated until BestJobs or eJobs publish them.
4. "70% of candidates applying by email in Romania send Europass" (Adecco estimate quoted by eJobs): single, undated estimate.
5. "Germany requires Europass" (eJobs): contradicted by German-market guides describing a tabular Lebenslauf; no support found.
6. "German recruiters 100% expect a photo" (Michael Page 2017): nine years old; 2026 guides report decline in German tech.
7. "Photos make CVs more human and get more positive responses" (BestJobs 2019; mediastory): asserted without data; recruiter comment threads in 2025 show the opposite view is common.
8. "Omitting DOB is always safe": the one controlled experiment found (Derous & Decoster 2017) reported lower ratings when DOB was absent, and age inference from names/hobbies regardless. Moot for a 2025 graduate.
9. "90%+ / 98% of employers use ATS": Robert Half (>95% of large companies, about half of mid-sized) versus Jobseeker 2025 (76.3% at least occasionally). Depends on company size and the definition of "use".
10. "Only 29% of Romanians speak fluent English" (Rippling) versus "almost 90% of IT specialists are good English speakers" (Brainspotting/Hipo): different populations, neither sourced.
11. EOR legality in Romania: Dentons (unlawful; only temporary work agencies) versus Oyster/Remote/Deel/Pebl (fully legal). Unresolved.
12. Romanian pay-transparency draft: "salary must be in the ad" (partnerd, Aug 2026) versus "in the ad or in writing before the interview" (DCBusiness Sep 2026, teamed). The later, more specific sources and the directive's own wording favour the second.
13. "Europass is ATS-compatible" (CVMaker, 8ore) versus "Europass tables and odd formatting are risky for ATS" (candycv): no parser test found either way.
14. "The AI Act's recruitment obligations apply from 2 August 2026": true until 27 July 2026, false since; every pre-July-2026 guide is now wrong on the date.

## 11. Consolidated recommendation for this owner

- Build a single English master; export (a) a one-page US resume: no photo/DOB/nationality/marital status; header with "Bucharest, Romania (EET, UTC+2/+3)", phone, email, LinkedIn, GitHub; one line "EU citizen (Romania). Open to B2B contractor engagement via Romanian PFA/SRL (W-8BEN available) or EOR; no visa sponsorship required"; quantified bullets; single column; text-based PDF; US spelling. (b) A one-to-two page EU/RO CV: same content plus CEFR language levels (Romanian native, English level stated honestly), optional photo omitted by default and added only if the ad asks; Romanian-language variant for Romanian-language ads. (c) A Europass export only for public-sector or academic competitions, with photo/DOB/gender/nationality left blank.
- Never include: CNP, full street address, marital status, children, religion, gender, health, ID scans, salary history or expectations, a "Curriculum Vitae" title, referees' contact details (write "available on request", consistent with Labour Code art. 29(4)).
- Include: BSc Computer Science, University of Bucharest, 2022-2025 with the international-equivalent title; a curated GitHub (pin the strongest of the 68 repos; recruiters look for live projects, not repo counts); LinkedIn URL; ORCID only on academic applications (it currently lists zero works, so it adds nothing for industry).
- Know the rules that protect him: art. 29(3) limits interview questions to job capacity; art. 27(5) bans pregnancy tests; OG 137/2000 art. 7 bans age/sex-conditioned ads; GDPR gives him access/erasure/objection rights and art. 22 human review; salary-history questions remain legal in Romania until L445/2026 is enacted.
