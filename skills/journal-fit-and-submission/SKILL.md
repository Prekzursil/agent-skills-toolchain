# journal-fit-and-submission

This skill evaluates venue fit and submission readiness. It balances scope, audience, evidence strength, and practical acceptance factors.

## When to use

- A manuscript or thesis chapter is heading toward journal submission.
- The user needs target-venue triage, cover-letter positioning, or submission readiness.

## Workflow

1. Classify the manuscript contribution, field, and likely audience.
2. Compare candidate journals by scope, article type, evidence depth, and realism.
3. Identify disqualifiers early: mismatch in methods, topic, novelty, or format.
4. Prepare the submission package: title, abstract, cover-letter angle, and checklist of missing pieces.
5. Return ranked venue options with explicit reasons.

## Configuration and access

- Use official author instructions and recent issues when available.
- Save only venue lists, scope notes, and checklist paths; no account credentials.

## Output standard

- Provide a ranked short list, not an undifferentiated catalogue.
- State the strongest reason each venue does or does not fit.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise acceptance odds as fact.
- Do not oversell novelty beyond what the evidence supports.
