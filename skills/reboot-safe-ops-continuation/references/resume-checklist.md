# Resume Checklist

1. Reconfirm host health after boot.
2. Reattach only the devices or mounts needed for the next step.
3. Compare the staged artifacts against the pre-reboot record.
4. Resume from the next safe command, not from memory.
5. Revalidate the phase boundary before proceeding further.
