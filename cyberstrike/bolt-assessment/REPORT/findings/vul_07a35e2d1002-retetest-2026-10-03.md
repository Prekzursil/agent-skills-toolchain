## Final severity rationale (MEDIUM, not LOW / not HIGH)
UPGRADE from LOW: surface is confirmed multi-vhost multi-tenant (2 vhosts, 2 pipelines), with unbounded
single-report size (>=66 KB) and no content-type/origin enforcement — the storage-cost ingestion DoS and
telemetry-integrity (poisoned CSP-report data, alert-noise masking) abuse ceiling is materially higher than
the single-sink LOW baseline implied.
CAP below HIGH: csp-report schema validation present on the beehive plane (400/702), no reflection, no data
exposure, no code-execution path, CSP collectors semi-public by design, CDN-fronted (Cloudflare DYNAMIC),
and no measured absence of upstream rate limiting.

## Remediation (updated)
Per-app ingestion attestation (origin allowlist or signed report submission) across BOTH vhosts; enforce
csp-report schema validation on node.bolt.eu (parity with beehive-csp 702 behavior); cap report size
(e.g. <=32 KB); rate-limit per source at the ingest handler, not only at CDN.
